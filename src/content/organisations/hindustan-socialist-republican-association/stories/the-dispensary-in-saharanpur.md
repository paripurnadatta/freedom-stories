In May 1929 a dispensary opened in the town of Saharanpur. Two young men worked there as compounders, the helpers who mix medicines. But they were not there to treat anyone. They were Shiv Verma and Jaidev Kapoor, and they were making bombs.

## Two friends from Kanpur

Shiv Verma was born in 1904 in the village of Khateli, in Hardoi district. At seventeen he joined the Non-Cooperation Movement. He studied at the D.A.V. College in Kanpur, where he took the party name "Prabhat". Jaidev Kapoor was also from Hardoi, and he too studied at that college. The two joined the Hindustan Republican Association together.

Both men were at the meeting in the ruins of Kotla in September 1928. Both were chosen for work that needed trust. Verma became the organiser for the United Provinces. Kapoor was placed in the military wing. In November 1928 they learned how to make bombs in Agra.

<aside class="note"><b>Born when?</b> The page on Shiv Verma gives 1904. An older note in this site's files says 1907. This story follows the page.</aside>

## A plan that stopped

Early in 1929, Verma, Kapoor and Shivaram Rajguru made a plan against Lord Irwin, the Viceroy. Kapoor was to throw the bomb. Verma was to be the second thrower, in case the first one failed. Rajguru was to watch the road and give the signal.

The Viceroy's car came. Rajguru did not give the signal. There were three women in the car. The plan was called off.

## A factory with a sign on the door

Then came April 1929. Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt threw their bombs in the Assembly and gave themselves up. Delhi was no longer safe for the party. The bomb factory moved to Saharanpur.

In May 1929, Verma and Kapoor set it up as a dispensary. They worked there as compounders. That was their cover.

It did not work for long. The party had no money, and people in the town noticed. The police noticed too. A police officer, Mathura Dutt Joshi, ordered a raid. The young men were taken. Shiv Verma was arrested on 13 May 1929. A comrade, Gaya Prasad, was arrested two days later.

<aside class="note"><b>Compounder</b>: a person who mixes and gives out medicines in a dispensary, under a doctor.</aside>

## On the way to Lahore

The prisoners were taken to Lahore, where they were put on trial in the Lahore Conspiracy Case. On 13 July 1929, Verma joined the hunger strike that Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt had begun in jail. He became very weak.

## Why it matters

The HSRA is often remembered through a few famous names. But a party is also made of people like Verma and Kapoor, who stood behind a counter. They were in their twenties. When the raid came, they were not famous. Within a few years they would be in the Andaman Islands.
