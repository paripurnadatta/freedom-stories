Kartar Singh Sarabha was born on 24 May 1896, in the village of Sarabha near Ludhiana. His father, Mangal Singh, died when Kartar was very young. His grandfather brought him up. He went to the Malwa Khalsa school in Ludhiana and studied up to the eighth class.

## To America

In July 1912 he sailed for San Francisco. He was sixteen. The plan was to study at the University of California in Berkeley. The university has no record of him, so no one can say whether he studied there. He joined a club of Indian students, the Nalanda club, which made his love for India stronger.

By December 1912, one account says, he was working at a timber mill in Astoria, Oregon. Sohan Singh Bhakna inspired him to work for India's freedom, and called him "Baba Gernal". He learned from Americans how to shoot and how to make detonators. He also took flying lessons.

## The paper

When the Ghadar Party began its newspaper in November 1913, Kartar Singh left his university work to help. He ran the Gurmukhi edition. He wrote articles and poems. One page says he also helped set up the press and run it.

Back then he was seventeen. When the First World War began in 1914, the paper called for revolt. He sailed home in October 1914.

## Home

He travelled by way of Colombo to Calcutta, with two friends, Vishnu Ganesh Pingle and Satyen Sen. They met Rash Behari Bose at Banaras. With them Kartar Singh took part in planning the rising of 21 February 1915. You can read about it in "The rising set for the twenty-first".

## The trial

After the plan failed, he was arrested. At his trial, he told the court that he took responsibility for his acts. The judge urged him to change his statement. He would not. He did not appeal. He said he would gladly give more than one life for his country.

In jail he tried to cut through the bars of his window, but guards took the tools. One page says he put on fourteen pounds in weight.

He was hanged at Lahore Central Jail in November 1915. He was nineteen.

<aside class="note"><b>The date</b>: one page gives 16 November 1915. Another says 17 November. One reference gives 1916 as the year, but all the other pages say 1915.</aside>

## Remembered

Bhagat Singh admired him. When Bhagat Singh was arrested, the police found a photograph of Kartar Singh Sarabha on him. Bhagat Singh's mother later spoke of him as a hero.

A Punjabi film about his life, *Shaheed Kartar Singh Sarabha*, was made in 1977.

## Why it matters

Kartar Singh is remembered because he gave up school, and then his life, for an idea. People still argue about the method he chose. But the man at the centre of this story was a teenager when he died.
