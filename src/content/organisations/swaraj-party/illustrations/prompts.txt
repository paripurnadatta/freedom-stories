ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Swaraj Party
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Do not show clear faces of real people in any of these. Show them from behind or at a distance.

==============================================================================
1. The split that made a new party  (flagship story)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: the-splits-that-made-a-new-party
Save it as the-splits-that-made-a-new-party.png (or .jpg / .webp) in this folder.
Shape: extra wide, 16:6. Generate at 2400 x 900 pixels if your tool lets you choose.
Where it goes: the top of the story "The split that made a new party", and its card everywhere on the site

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A large 1920s Indian conference hall at night, rows of wooden benches with men in white suits and round caps seen from the far end, their faces not shown, a small stage at the front with a single microphone stand, warm lamplight, the hall's high ceiling in shadow. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, warm amber light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green. Historically accurate 1920s Indian clothing, architecture and objects. Faces calm and dignified. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no borders. No gore, no blood, no weapons. Do not include the Indian national flag or the Ashoka Chakra.
