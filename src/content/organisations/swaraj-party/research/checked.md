# Checked ledger: Swaraj Party

From the shared `four-freedom-organisations-dossier.pdf` (another AI's research). Checked against
sources opened in this session (23 October 2026).

## What held up
- At the December 1922 Gaya session, a resolution moved by C. Rajagopalachari against entry into
  the legislative councils was carried by 1,740 votes against 880 (the Congress Sandesh tribute
  page would not open from here; the vote is held from the dossier's direct quote, and the
  stories use it with the other versions noted).
- On 31 December 1922 a meeting at the Gaya residence of the Maharaja of Tikari decided to form a
  new party; the original name was the "Congress Khilafat Swaraj Party", later changed to the
  "Swarajya Party" at a general meeting in Allahabad on 20 February 1923 (the name change and
  "1 January 1923" founding wording come from the government timeline and Wikipedia; the two
  dates coexist as a "Two versions" note).
- C. R. Das (Deshbandhu) and Motilal Nehru founded and led it; Das took the leadership in Bengal,
  Motilal led the party in the Central Assembly; in the 1923 elections the party won seats
  (45 of 145 in the central council per Wikipedia) and Vithalbhai Patel became its president in
  the Central Legislative Assembly.
- The "No-Changers" were the Gandhi followers who opposed council entry (Vallabhbhai Patel,
  Rajagopalachari); in September 1923 a special Congress session allowed the Swarajists to
  contest; the party's policy was to "carry the fight into the enemy's camp" (the Publications
  Division government timeline).
- C. R. Das died on 16 June 1925 at Darjeeling (Wikipedia, born 5 November 1870 in Calcutta;
  his closing speech for Aurobindo at the Alipore case is also there).
- By 1928 the party was "deeply disillusioned" as defections weakened it; it later dissolved
  quietly into Congress (the government timeline; Wikipedia).

## What could not be checked
- The Congress Sandesh page (P1) and the Motilal Nehru presidents page (P2) both returned HTTP
  403 from this environment; their facts are carried only from the dossier's direct quotes.
- Motilal Nehru's "one-man secretariat at Anand Bhawan" detail (dossier P1) could not be
  re-opened here, so it is used sparingly and marked as coming from that tribute page.
- The exact "1,740 against 880" figures are not on the government timeline I could open; they
  come from the blocked Congress Sandesh page, so the main text says the vote was close and puts
  the figures in a margin note labelled as coming from the Congress tribute page.

## What was left out
- No invented council scenes. The "enemy's camp" phrase is kept as the published government
  phrase with the source named, not dramatized.


## Rewrite of 27 October 2026 (Claude)

The six earlier stories (padded, failing the check) were deleted. Seven new stories were written
from sources opened in this session: Wikipedia (Swaraj Party, Chittaranjan Das, Motilal Nehru,
Vithalbhai Patel, Basanti Devi, Chauri Chaura incident, Central Legislative Assembly, Government
of India Act 1919, Kolkata Municipal Corporation, Huseyn Shaheed Suhrawardy), Banglapedia (Das;
Calcutta Corporation), GKToday (two pages), and the mkgandhi.org pictorial biography
("Reaction and Recovery", identical to the Publications Division timeline).

### Real disagreements between sources (each has a "Two versions" note in the stories)
- Founding date: 1 January 1923 (Wikipedia infobox, GKToday) against December 1922 (Wikipedia text,
  Banglapedia). The index now says "End of December 1922".
- Seats won in 1923: 45 of 145 (Wikipedia) against 42 of 101 elected (GKToday). The 1919 Act gives
  145 members, 104 elected, so the lists are counted differently.
- When Vithalbhai Patel became Assembly president: after the 1923 election (Swaraj Party page)
  against 1925 (his own page and GKToday, 24 August 1925).
- First mayor / deputy mayor / chief executive: Das, Bose, Suhrawardy in that order (Banglapedia)
  against Das, Suhrawardy (deputy mayor), Bose (CEO) (Wikipedia pages).
- March 1926: "walked out" (Central Legislative Assembly page) against "resigned their seats and
  returned to the Congress" (Motilal Nehru page).
- End of the party: 1935 (infobox), "defunct 1929-1937" (text), walk-out of 1930 (GKToday).
- Photo at Darjeeling: Wikipedia names Das and Gandhi; the Commons file page adds Annie Besant.

### What could not be checked, and was left out
- The Gaya vote count (1,740 against 880, from the dossier): the source page would not open. Not stated.
- The Public Safety Bill: GKToday gives 1924 on one page and 1928 on another. Left out.
- Wikipedia says Das "gave up his law practice in 1894"; Banglapedia says he enrolled at the bar
  that year. Left out. The Middle Temple (Wikipedia) and Inner Temple (Banglapedia) also differ; left out.
- "Gandhi's advice" to the two groups to stay together in the Congress (Swaraj Party page): Gandhi was
  in jail until February 1924, so this was not used.
- The terms of the Bengal Pact are not given by any source opened; they are not stated.
- Motilal Nehru's first walk-out in the United Provinces council (Motilal page only): not used.
- Names of Muslim members on the Bengal side beyond Suhrawardy: none given.
