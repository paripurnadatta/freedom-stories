# Checked ledger: Swaraj Party

From the shared `four-freedom-organisations-dossier.pdf` (another AI's research). Checked against
sources opened in this session (23 October 2026).

## What held up
- At the December 1922 Gaya session, a resolution moved by C. Rajagopalachari against entry into
  the legislative councils was carried by 1,740 votes against 880 (the Congress Sandesh tribute
  page would not open from here; the vote is held from the dossier's direct quote, and the
  stories use it with the other versions noted).
- On 31 December 1922 a meeting at the Gaya residence of the Maharaja of Tikari decided to form a
  new party; the original name was the "Congress Khilafat Swaraj Party", later changed to the
  "Swarajya Party" at a general meeting in Allahabad on 20 February 1923 (the name change and
  "1 January 1923" founding wording come from the government timeline and Wikipedia; the two
  dates coexist as a "Two versions" note).
- C. R. Das (Deshbandhu) and Motilal Nehru founded and led it; Das took the leadership in Bengal,
  Motilal led the party in the Central Assembly; in the 1923 elections the party won seats
  (45 of 145 in the central council per Wikipedia) and Vithalbhai Patel became its president in
  the Central Legislative Assembly.
- The "No-Changers" were the Gandhi followers who opposed council entry (Vallabhbhai Patel,
  Rajagopalachari); in September 1923 a special Congress session allowed the Swarajists to
  contest; the party's policy was to "carry the fight into the enemy's camp" (the Publications
  Division government timeline).
- C. R. Das died on 16 June 1925 at Darjeeling (Wikipedia, born 5 November 1870 in Calcutta;
  his closing speech for Aurobindo at the Alipore case is also there).
- By 1928 the party was "deeply disillusioned" as defections weakened it; it later dissolved
  quietly into Congress (the government timeline; Wikipedia).
