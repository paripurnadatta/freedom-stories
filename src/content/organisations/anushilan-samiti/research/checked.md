# Checked ledger: Anushilan Samiti

From the shared `four-freedom-organisations-dossier.pdf` (another AI's research). Checked against
sources opened in this session (23 October 2026).

## What held up
- Founded in Calcutta in 1902 as a social/fitness club that doubled as an underground network for
  anti-British revolutionaries (The Wire / Shukla Sanyal; Wikipedia: "24 March 1902").
- Two founder names in circulation: the barrister P. Mitter / Pramathanath Mitra (The Wire) and
  Satish Chandra Basu (a Commons portrait caption). Treated as a "Two versions" margin note;
  never asserted as one single founder.
- Aurobindo Ghosh and his brother Barindra Kumar Ghosh were linked to the society; a group led by
  Barin Ghose broke away in March 1906 to found the newspaper Jugantar (The Wire; Wikipedia:
  "Jugantar, founded in March 1906").
- The Dhaka branch led by Pulin Behari Das; the Alipore bomb case ran 1908-1910 (The Wire).
- Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chaki threw a bomb in Muzaffarpur on 30 April 1908; the bomb killed
  Mrs and Ms Kennedy; Chaki died and Bose was hanged on 11 August 1908 (The Wire; Wikipedia
  confirms the Alipore trial; the "chief presidency magistrate" detail is marked unverified).
- The 48 Bidhan Sarani building, the Anushilan symbol (1902-1936) and the Pramathanath Mitra
  portrait: all from the 1950 book `Anushilan Samitir Itihas` by Jibantara Haldar, on Commons as
  public domain (PD-India / PD-Bangladesh). Already in the picture library.

## What could not be checked
- The Indian Express page (A1) is blocked from this environment, so its facts (the "building with
  no sign", the "wrong carriage" detail) are held from The Wire / Wikipedia instead; the plaque /
  signage point is left out of the stories.
- The exact original photograph date of the 1950-book scans is not known (Commons marks them
  public domain but records no shutter date).
- The "chief presidency magistrate" as the bomb's intended target is not confirmed by a source I
  opened; the main text says a carriage, and the note carries the other version.

## What was left out
- No invented dialogue, weather or crowd size. The "two teenagers" phrasing is kept only as the
  source's own words inside a margin note, not restated in the main text.

## Rewritten 24 October 2026 (reviewer)

The six stories were rewritten from sources opened in this session: Wikipedia (Anushilan Samiti,
Alipore Bomb case, Khudiram Bose, Prafulla Chaki, Barindra Ghosh, Jugantar, Pulin Behari Das),
Banglapedia (Anushilan Samiti, Jugantar Party) and The Wire (Shukla Sanyal, 2018).

Real disagreements between sources, now shown as margin notes: founder (Mitra / Satish Basu),
first issue of Jugantar (March / April 1906), Dhaka branch founded (September / October 1906),
when the Dhaka branch reached 500 branches, who the Kennedys were (barrister / "congressman"),
whether Aurobindo was sentenced to death (Barin's page) or acquitted (Alipore page and judgment).
A date conflict that is not in a note: the shooting of Ashutosh Biswas (February 1909 on the
Jugantar page, March 1909 on the Alipore page); the story says "early in 1909".

Left out: the Gandhi quotation on Khudiram's page (no source for it), the detail about
Prafulla Chaki's body, the Cellular Jail escape claim on Barin's page, and all
wording from the earlier drafts. Picture alt text and captions were corrected (the symbol is a
plume, a peacock, a rose and a ribbon, not a torch). The Pramathanath Mitra portrait is too
coarse to show and is not used in any story.
