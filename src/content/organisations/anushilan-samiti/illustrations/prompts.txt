ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Anushilan Samiti
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Do not show clear faces of real people in any of these. Show them from behind or at a distance.

1. The trial that watched all of India  (flagship story)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: the-trial-that-watched-all-of-india
Save it as the-trial-that-watched-all-of-india.png (or .jpg / .webp) in this folder.
Shape: extra wide, 16:6. Generate at 2400 x 900 pixels if your tool lets you choose.
Where it goes: the top of the story "The trial that watched all of India", and its card everywhere on the site

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A large 1900s Indian court building seen from a distance at dawn, a long corridor of wooden columns, a small group of men in white suits and round caps walking slowly down the corridor seen from behind, their faces not shown, morning light coming up the corridor. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, warm saffron and amber light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green. Historically accurate 1900s Indian clothing, architecture and objects. Faces calm and dignified. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no borders. No gore, no blood, no weapons pointed at anyone. Do not include the Indian national flag or the Ashoka Chakra.
