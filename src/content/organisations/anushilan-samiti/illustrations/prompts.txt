ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Anushilan Samiti
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Do not show clear faces of real people in any of these. Show them from behind or at a distance.

A small 1900s Calcutta club building seen from the outside at dusk, a narrow colonnaded veranda, a dark entrance, a few men in loose Indian clothes standing at the far end of the veranda seen from behind, their faces not shown, warm lamplight through the arches. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, warm saffron and amber light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green.
