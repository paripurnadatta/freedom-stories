## Research notes

Founded in Calcutta in 1902 as a fitness club, it operated as an underground society for anti-British revolutionaries. Two founder names are in the record: P. Mitter (Pramathanath Mitra) and Satish Chandra Basu. A group led by Barindra Kumar Ghosh broke away in March 1906 to start the newspaper Jugantar. A branch in Dhaka was led by Pulin Behari Das. The Muzaffarpur bomb (30 April 1908) and the Alipore trial (1908-1910) are the two events that made the group's name.
