On 4 July 1943, in the Cathay Building in Singapore, Subhas Chandra Bose took over the Indian Independence League and the Indian National Army. He had arrived from Germany. The army he took over had come out of a troubled first try. He set out to build it again.

## What it was made of

The second INA began with about 12,000 troops. Another 8,000 to 10,000 former soldiers of the British Indian Army joined it. About 18,000 civilians signed up as well.

The total strength is given differently. One count says about 43,000. Another says about 40,000 in 1945. A third writer puts it as high as 50,000.

## Whose names?

The army's first division had regiments, and each one was called a brigade. Three of the brigades had the names of leaders of the Congress, the party that was leading the struggle at home:

- The **Gandhi Brigade** was the 2nd Guerrilla Regiment. Colonel Inayat Kiani led it.
- The **Azad Brigade** was the 3rd. Colonel Gulzara Singh led it.
- The **Nehru Brigade** was the 4th. Later it was led by Lieutenant-Colonel Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon.

The 1st regiment was the **Subhas Brigade**, named after Bose himself. Colonel Shah Nawaz Khan led it.

<aside class="note"><b>Guerrilla</b>: a fighter who works in small groups, hits quickly, and moves on, instead of fighting in large armies.</aside>

## A motto and a song

The army's motto was *Ittehad, Itmad aur Qurbani*. In English that is "Unity, Faith and Sacrifice". The soldiers marched to a song called *Qadam Qadam Badhaye Ja*, which means "Step by step, keep marching". Their battle cry was "Jai Hind".

<aside class="note"><b>Words</b>: <i>Ittehad</i> means unity. <i>Itmad</i> means faith or trust. <i>Qurbani</i> means sacrifice. Some pages spell them differently, as <i>Ittefaq</i> or <i>Etmad</i>.</aside>

The men came from many backgrounds. The best-known trial of INA officers, at the Red Fort, put a Muslim, a Hindu and a Sikh in the dock together.

## A government as well

On 21 October 1943, in Singapore, Bose announced a Provisional Government of Free India, the Azad Hind government. It declared war on Britain and the United States. Eight countries had links with it, among them Japan, Germany, Italy's wartime government, Thailand and Burma. Bose was the head of state and was called Netaji, which means "leader". The Rani of Jhansi Regiment, an army of women, was also part of this government's forces.

<aside class="note"><b>Two dates</b>: one page gives 21 October 1943 for the government. Another says it declared war on 23 October.</aside>

## Why it matters

People still disagree about Bose. Britain called him a fascist. Many Indians called him a hero. Both views are on record. What is clear is how the INA described itself: an army with a motto about unity, and brigades named for the leaders of the national movement.

<aside class="note"><b>Fascist</b>: someone who believes in one strong ruler and harsh control over everyone else.</aside>
