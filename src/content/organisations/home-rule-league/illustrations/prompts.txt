ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Home Rule League
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Do not show clear faces of real people in any of these. Show them from behind or at a distance.

==============================================================================
1. Two leagues, one word  (flagship story)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: two-leagues-one-word
Save it as two-leagues-one-word.png (or .jpg / .webp) in this folder.
Shape: extra wide, 16:6. Generate at 2400 x 900 pixels if your tool lets you choose.
Where it goes: the top of the story "Two leagues, one word", and its card everywhere on the site

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A wide 1910s Indian landscape, seen from a hill, two small towns far apart, one in the south-west and one on the south-east coast, a small road leading to each one, a few figures in white clothes seen from a distance, their faces not shown, the morning light pale and the sky a thin blue. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, pale morning light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green. Historically accurate 1910s Indian clothing, architecture and objects. Faces calm and dignified. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no borders. No gore, no blood, no weapons. Do not include the Indian national flag or the Ashoka Chakra.
