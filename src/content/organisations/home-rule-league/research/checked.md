## Disagreements found (each shown in a "Two versions" note where a story uses the fact)
- Start of Tilak's league: "April 1916" at Belgaum (Wikipedia) vs "23 April 1916" (INFLIBNET).
- Start of Besant's league: "1 September 1916" (INC page; Wikipedia's S. Subramania Iyer page) vs "September 1916".
- Purchase of the Madras Standard: June 1914 (INC) vs July 1914, renamed New India from 14 July (GKToday).
- Besant as Congress president: "in August 1917" (INC page) vs chosen after release and presided in December 1917
  (Wikipedia, INFLIBNET, GKToday).
- Subramania Iyer gave up his knighthood: 1917 (The Week; Wikipedia's honours section; the Tribune ties it to
  Besant's internment) vs June 1918, days after being scolded by Montagu and Chelmsford (Wikipedia main text;
  Madras Musings, which quotes his own letters to the press).
- Madanapalle college: opened 19 July 1915 by Lord Pentland (INFLIBNET) vs founded 1917 (Wikipedia infobox).
- Margaret Cousins: "composed" the music and was the principal's wife (INFLIBNET) vs wrote down Tagore's notes
  and was vice-principal (Wikipedia, citing James Cousins's autobiography).
- Where Besant opposed non-cooperation in 1920: "Lahore" (INC page) vs the resolution was carried at the special
  session in Calcutta, September 1920 (Gandhi chronology).
- Membership: Tilak's league about 1,400 (April 1916) to about 32,000 (1917) per Wikipedia's Tilak page; Besant's
  league 7,000 (March 1917) to 27,000 (December 1917) per INFLIBNET. Different leagues, so both are used.

## Claims in sources that were NOT used
- GKToday (Commonweal page) says B. P. Wadia "took over management of New India" during the internment, but
  every source, GKToday included, says Wadia was interned with Besant. Left out.
- GKToday (Besant page) says Gandhi joined the Home Rule League in 1917 to protest her internment. The Gandhi
  chronology says he joined on 28 April 1920. Left out.
- GKToday (Besant page) gives her birth as 18 October 1847; INC and Wikipedia give 1 October. Not needed.
- INFLIBNET lists "Common Weal" both as Besant's own paper and among papers that opposed the movement. Its
  second mention looks like a slip; only the first use (Besant's weekly) is kept, matching GKToday and INC.
- Wikipedia's note on the flag says Suraiya Tayyabji sent thirty flag designs in 1916. This looks doubtful
  for 1916 and rests on one source; left out.
- Wikipedia's "Indian Home Rule movement" page is marked "more citations needed"; facts from it were kept only
  where a second source agrees, except the Coimbatore flag ban (cited there to Virmani, Past & Present, 1999).
- Gandhi's 1915 welcome in Madras, presided over by Subramania Iyer, and Iyer's quote about soul-force: true
  but not about the league; kept for a possible later story on Iyer.
