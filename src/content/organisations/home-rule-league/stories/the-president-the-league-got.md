In December 1917, a meeting of the Congress met in the city of Calcutta. And the woman who had been asked to stay in the small house, in the hills, two months before, was elected to the biggest seat in the meeting.

The woman's name was Annie Besant. And the seat was the Congress's presidency, the one that was kept for one year, and then passed to the next person.

<aside class="note"><b>Word</b>: The <i>presidency</i> of the Congress is the biggest seat in the meeting, the one that is kept for one year, and then passed to the next person. Besant was elected to it, in December 1917, in Calcutta.</aside>

Two months before, Besant had come out of the small house, in the hills, after ninety-three days. And the two men who had been with her, in the house, in the summer, had come out of it, too.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: one account says Besant was elected to the Congress's presidency, in December 1917, in Calcutta, for a one-year term. The exact details of how the election was run, and of who else was in the room, at the meeting, are not settled in every record, so this story keeps the fact in the main text and the uncertainty in the note.</aside>

The seat was not the biggest seat in the country. And it was not the seat that gave the Congress the power to make the laws, in the rooms where the laws were made. It was the seat that gave the Congress its name, in the years after, and its quiet work, in the rooms it had chosen to stay in.

<aside class="note"><b>Word</b>: To be <i>elected</i> to a seat means to be the person the meeting chooses, to keep the seat, for the years it is kept, and to speak for the people who were not in the room.</aside>

And that was the seat Besant was given, in December 1917, in Calcutta, two months after she had come out of the small house, in the hills, in the summer.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: one account says the seat Besant was given, in December 1917, was the Congress's presidency, the one that was kept for one year, and then passed to the next person. The exact details of how the seat was kept, in the years after, are not settled in every record, so this story keeps the fact in the main text and the uncertainty in the note.</aside>

It was the quiet thing the two leagues, in the years before, had not been able to do, in the places they had built, in the two different rooms, in the one country. And it was the word Home Rule, in the years after, in the room the Congress had chosen to stay in, in the city of Calcutta, in December 1917.

<aside class="note"><b>Word</b>: The word <i>Home Rule</i>, in the years after 1917, was not just the word of the two leagues, in two places, in the one country. It was the word of the Congress's presidency, in the years after, in the room the Congress had chosen to stay in, in the city of Calcutta.</aside>

The seat, in the years after, was not the place where the laws were made. And it was not the place where the word Home Rule was spoken, in the rooms where the laws were made. But it was the place where the Congress's name was kept, in the years after, and where the quiet work of the two leagues, in the years before, was carried on, in the room the Congress had chosen to stay in.

<aside class="note"><b>Word</b>: To <i>carry on</i> the quiet work of the two leagues means to keep doing it, in the room the Congress had chosen to stay in, in the years after the two leagues were set up, in the same way they had done it, in the years before.</aside>
