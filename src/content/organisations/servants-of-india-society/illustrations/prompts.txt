ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Servants of India Society
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Do not show clear faces of real people in any of these. Show them from behind or at a distance.

==============================================================================
1. Books that travelled  (short story)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: books-that-travelled
Save it as books-that-travelled.png (or .jpg / .webp) in this folder.
Shape: extra wide, 16:6. Generate at 2400 x 900 pixels if your tool lets you choose.
Where it goes: the top of the story "Books that travelled", and its card everywhere on the site

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A small bullock cart with open wooden shelves full of books, moving along a lane in a 1910s Indian city, a few men and children in white clothes gathering around it, seen from behind so no faces are clear, no readable writing on the books or the cart, warm afternoon light. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, warm saffron and amber light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green. Historically accurate 1900s to 1910s Indian clothing, architecture and objects. Faces calm and dignified. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no borders. No gore, no blood, no weapons pointed at anyone. Do not include the Indian national flag or the Ashoka Chakra.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
