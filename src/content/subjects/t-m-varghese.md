## Research notes

Documented association with Travancore State Congress in Kerala.

### Overview

Travancore political leader associated with the 1934 Abstention Movement and later a prominent leader of the Travancore State Congress.

### Chronology

- **1934**: Presided over the joint political agitation associated with the Abstention Movement
- **late 1930s-1940s**: Prominent Travancore State Congress leader in the responsible-government struggle

### Evidence claims

- T. M. Varghese presided over the 1934 joint political agitation at Thiruvalla associated with the beginning of the Abstention Movement in Travancore. (HIGH confidence)
- He became a prominent leader of the Travancore State Congress. (HIGH confidence)
- His opposition to Diwan C. P. Ramaswamy Ayyer carried political costs within the Travancore legislature. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Thiruvalla: 1934 joint political agitation
- Travancore: State Congress politics
