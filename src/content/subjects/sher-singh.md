## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Sher Singh's connection with Ghadar Movement; Lahore Conspiracy.

### Evidence claims

- Sher Singh, son of Lehna Singh from the Gurdaspur region, joined Ghadar activity after emigrating to the United States. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested in February 1915 after returning during the First World War revolutionary mobilization. (HIGH confidence)
- He was convicted on 30 March 1916 in a supplementary Lahore conspiracy prosecution and received imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
