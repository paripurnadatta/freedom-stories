## Research notes

Nationalist thinker and collaborator in Home Rule politics.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Kashi Vidyapith began in the Non-Cooperation era with Bhagwan Das as its first Vice-Chancellor
- **1955-01-26**: Bharat Ratna awarded by presidential notification

### Evidence claims

- Bhagwan Das was born on 12 January 1869 and died on 18 September 1958. (HIGH confidence)
- He left colonial government service and devoted himself to national, educational and intellectual work. (HIGH confidence)
- Bhagwan Das was a founding figure and the first Vice-Chancellor of Kashi Vidyapith, an institution linked to the Non-Cooperation era. (HIGH confidence)
- He was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1955. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Banaras: principal educational/intellectual base
