## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Madras Presidency Congress activist who joined the Vedaranyam Salt Satyagraha and became one of the most prominent women imprisoned during the campaign.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Participated in Vedaranyam Salt Satyagraha and was imprisoned
- **1934**: Elected to the Madras Legislative Council
- **1937**: Entered the Madras Legislative Assembly

### Evidence claims

- Rukmini Lakshmipathi participated in the Vedaranyam Salt Satyagraha of 1930. (HIGH confidence)
- She was imprisoned for a year for her Salt Satyagraha participation. (HIGH confidence)
- She later entered the Madras legislature and became an important woman political leader in the presidency. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Madras / Chennai: Congress political base
- Vedaranyam: Salt Satyagraha
