## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Rani of Jhansi Regiment training and mobilisation.

### Overview

Japan-born teenage freedom fighter who joined the Rani of Jhansi Regiment of the INA in the closing phase of the Second World War.

### Chronology

- **1943**: Met Bose in Japan and became more directly drawn into INA activity
- **1945**: Formally joined the Rani of Jhansi Regiment at age seventeen and went to Bangkok for training

### Evidence claims

- Asha Sahay Choudhury grew up in Japan in a family committed to Indian independence. (HIGH confidence)
- She met Subhas Chandra Bose in Japan in 1943 when she was fifteen. (HIGH confidence)
- At seventeen she joined the Rani of Jhansi Regiment and went to Bangkok for training. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kobe / Japan: Upbringing and nationalist family network
- Bangkok: Rani of Jhansi Regiment training
