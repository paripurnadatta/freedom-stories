## Research notes

Founded the Bombay Youth League, organized Simon Commission protest, and is credited by the source with coining the phrase 'Quit India'.

### Overview

Bombay youth, socialist and labour-oriented nationalist leader central to anti-Simon protest and Quit India-era mobilization.

### Chronology

- **1928**: Organized Bombay youth opposition to the Simon Commission
- **1934**: Participated in the founding of the Congress Socialist Party
- **1942**: Central to Quit India slogan/mobilization and imprisoned during the movement

### Evidence claims

- Yusuf Meherally co-founded the Bombay Provincial Youth League in 1928 and became a major youth leader. (HIGH confidence)
- He was a founding member and later General Secretary of the Congress Socialist Party. (HIGH confidence)
- Ministry sources associate him with the slogans 'Simon Go Back' and 'Quit India' and with underground mobilization after the 1942 arrests. (HIGH confidence)
- He was repeatedly imprisoned and was elected Mayor of Bombay while in jail during the Quit India period. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bombay: Youth, socialist, labour and Quit India organizing
