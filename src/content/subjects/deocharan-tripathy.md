## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Deocharan Tripathy's connection with Congress anti-colonial movement.

### Overview

Congress activist from Cachar who was imprisoned for Non-Cooperation and transferred through colonial jails in Assam.

### Chronology

- **1921-1922**: Participated in Non-Cooperation activity in Cachar and received a one-year rigorous-imprisonment sentence
- **1920s**: Held first in Sylhet Jail and later transferred to Jorhat Jail

### Evidence claims

- Deocharan Tripathy was an active Congress worker in Cachar district, Assam. (HIGH confidence)
- He was sentenced to one year of rigorous imprisonment for participation in the Non-Cooperation Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was initially held in Sylhet Jail and later transferred to Jorhat Jail. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Cachar: Congress and Non-Cooperation activity
- Sylhet Jail / Jorhat Jail: Imprisonment
