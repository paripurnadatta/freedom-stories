## Research notes

Revolutionary associated with Surya Sen's group.

### After the raid

Dutta’s profile shows that the Chittagong movement did not end on 18 April 1930.

### A network under pressure

Her story intersects with Surya Sen and Pritilata Waddedar while preserving her own operational role.

### Capture and sentence

Her prison chronology differs from the martyrdom-centered biographies of some comrades.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Chittagong revolutionary campaign. Joins the network associated with the armoury raid
- **1932**: Post-raid underground planning. Participates in further revolutionary planning
- **1933**: Arrest and sentence. Captured after underground activity

### Evidence claims

- Was a member of Chhatri Sangha and part of Surya Sen’s revolutionary group. (high confidence)
- Participated in the Chittagong revolutionary campaign and later went underground. (high confidence)
- Was arrested in 1933, sentenced to life and later released. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kalpana Dutta with Surya Sen as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kalpana Dutta with Pritilata Waddedar as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kalpana Dutta with Chittagong Armoury Raid as participant in. (high confidence)
