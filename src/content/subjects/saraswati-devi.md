## Research notes

Official profile documents renewed Civil Disobedience participation and imprisonment.

### Overview

Odisha Civil Disobedience activist who trained with the Seva Dal and accepted imprisonment in 1932.

### Chronology

- **1931**: Joined Seva Dal training in Puri
- **1932**: Participated in renewed Civil Disobedience and was imprisoned

### Evidence claims

- Saraswati Devi joined Seva Dal training at Puri in 1931. (HIGH confidence)
- She joined the renewed Civil Disobedience Movement in 1932. (HIGH confidence)
- She was sentenced to four months' rigorous imprisonment and a fine of three hundred rupees. (HIGH confidence)

- Kendrapara: Home region
- Puri: Seva Dal training and Congress context
