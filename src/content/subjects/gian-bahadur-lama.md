## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Gian Bahadur Lama's connection with Indian National Army.

### Overview

Former British-Indian Army sepoy who joined the Indian National Army in Malaya and died fighting on the Burma front in February 1945.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Joined the Indian National Army in Malaya after previous service in the British-Indian Army
- **1942-1945**: Served as a Lance-Naik in INA training/combat organization and fought on the Burma front
- **1945-02-11**: Died in battle in Burma

### Evidence claims

- Gian Bahadur Lama was recorded as a resident of Punjab and had previously served as a sepoy in the British-Indian Army. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Indian National Army in Malaya in 1942 and served as a Lance-Naik. (HIGH confidence)
- He fought on the Burma front and died in battle on 11 February 1945. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Punjab: Recorded residence in ICHR entry
- Malaya: Joined the INA
- Burma front: Combat and death
