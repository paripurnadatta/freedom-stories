## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Pazhassi resistance in Malabar.

### Overview

Kurichiya leader and commander associated with Pazhassi Raja's resistance in Wayanad. Ministry and Census sources identify him as the head of Kurichiya fighters and co-leader with Edachena Kunkan in the successful attack on Panamaram in October 1802. He was captured and publicly executed in November 1805.
