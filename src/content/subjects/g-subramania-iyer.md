## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Madras Mahajana Sabha public politics.

### The press as political infrastructure

Journalism created a public sphere in which nationalist arguments could circulate before mass politics.

### From Madras association to national Congress

His profile bridges the regional Sabha network and the first Congress generation.

### Chronology

- **1878**: The Hindu begins publication. Iyer joins the group that creates an English-language newspaper intended to articulate Indian public opinion
- **1884-1885**: Sabha to Congress. The Madras Mahajana Sabha is founded, followed by Iyer's participation in the inaugural Congress session
- **1903**: Economic critique in print. Iyer publishes a sustained critique of colonial economic policy

### Evidence claims

- G. Subramania Iyer was among the founders of The Hindu in 1878. (high confidence)
- He co-founded the Madras Mahajana Sabha in 1884 with P. Ananda Charlu and M. Veeraraghavachariar. (high confidence)
- At the inaugural Indian National Congress session in 1885, Iyer moved a resolution and publicly argued for India as a national political community. (high confidence)
