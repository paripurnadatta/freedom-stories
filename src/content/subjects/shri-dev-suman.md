## Research notes

Organized civil-rights and nationalist activity in Tehri State, joined Quit India, and died after a prison hunger strike in 1944.

### Documented record

Hailed from the Tehri Princely State and was a writer and editor. Arrested at age fourteen in 1930 for participating in Civil Disobedience. Worked for civil rights in Tehri, used underground publications and propagated Gandhian nonviolence and swadeshi. Participated in Quit India, was arrested 27 December 1943 and died during a prison hunger strike in 1944.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Civil Disobedience arrest
- **1930s-1940s**: Tehri civil-rights organizing
- **1942**: Quit India participation
- **1943-12-27**: Arrest at Champa
- **1944**: Death during hunger strike
