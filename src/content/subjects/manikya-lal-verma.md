## Research notes

A leader of the Bijolia peasant movement who mobilized against forced labour and taxation.

### Overview

Rajasthan peasant leader, constructive worker and later political organizer who became one of the important figures of the Bijolia movement. Inspired by Vijay Singh Pathik, he left employment and mobilized peasants against forced labour and taxation, later extending his work into social uplift and the politics of the princely states.
