## Research notes

Major organizer of peasant movements and Congress leader.

### Organization before charisma

Patel’s mass leadership grew from detailed local organization, communication and collective discipline.

### Kheda to Bardoli

The two Gujarat campaigns show an evolution in his role from organizer to nationally recognized leader.

### The Bardoli information network

The Bardoli Satyagraha Patrika illustrates how print and instructions helped coordinate a dispersed rural movement.

### Chronology

- **1918**: Kheda Satyagraha. Builds organizational experience in agrarian non-cooperation
- **1928**: Bardoli Satyagraha. Leads a highly organized revenue-resistance campaign
- **1930**: Civil Disobedience mobilisation. Continues to connect agrarian politics with national satyagraha

### Evidence claims

- Worked on the ground in the Kheda Satyagraha and emerged as an important peasant leader. (high confidence)
- Organized the Bardoli Satyagraha of 1928 against a 22 percent land-revenue increase. (high confidence)
- Used speeches and disciplined satyagraha to sustain peasant resistance against colonial revenue demands. (high confidence)
