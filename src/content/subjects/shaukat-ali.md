## Research notes

Co-led the Khilafat movement.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Secretary in Central Khilafat Committee constitution. Secretary in Central Khilafat Committee constitution

### Evidence claims

- Shaukat Ali was a leading organizer of the Khilafat movement. (high confidence)
- Under the Central Khilafat Committee constitution accepted in February 1920, Shaukat Ali served as one of its secretaries. (high confidence)
- The archival Khilafat records repeatedly treat Shaukat Ali and his brother Mohamed/Muhammad Ali as prominent joint figures in Muslim political mobilization. (high confidence)
- Contemporary material connects Shaukat Ali with the Khuddam-i-Kaaba organization and its political-religious mobilization. (high confidence)
- Oxford historian B.R. Nanda describes Mohamed and Shaukat Ali as taking over the Khilafat agitation after Abul Kalam Azad. (high confidence)
