## Research notes

Fought for Siraj ud-Daulah at Plassey.

### A profile defined by one decisive day

The surviving package evidence supports a focused account of military service and death at Plassey rather than an invented full-life biography.

### Memory and documentation

The district handbook mixes historical notes with later local remembrance; claims should be kept to the well-supported core.

### Chronology

- **1757-06-23**: Battle of Plassey. Mir Madan fights for Siraj ud-Daulah at Plassey and is killed during the battle

### Evidence claims

- Government district material identifies Mir Madan as a senior military commander in Bengal and links him directly to the Battle of Plassey. (high confidence)
- The same source preserves local memory of Mir Madan's battlefield loyalty and states that he was killed by cannon fire at Plassey. (medium confidence)
