Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Assamese artist-intellectual and left-oriented political activist whose cultural work and class politics were deeply intertwined. Ministry sources portray him as a patriot who used theatre, music and writing to advocate not only independence from British rule but liberation from poverty and class inequality, with close involvement in Assam's IPTA milieu.
