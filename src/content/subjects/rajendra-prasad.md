## Research notes

Major Congress leader from Bihar.

### National strategy, provincial organization

Rajendra Prasad’s importance lies in the practical work of translating Congress campaigns into durable provincial networks.

### Salt Satyagraha in Bihar

His arrest demonstrates that civil disobedience developed through regional leadership beyond the iconic Dandi route.

### The final wartime confrontation

His 1942 speech ties provincial mobilization to the Congress decision for a final anti-colonial struggle.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Bihar Provincial Conference and Non-Cooperation. Helps translate national Non-Cooperation into Bihar's provincial politics
- **1930-07-05**: Arrest during Salt Satyagraha. Arrested while leading the campaign in Bihar
- **1942**: Bihar Congress and Quit India moment. Calls for disciplined nonviolent struggle as the constitutional crisis deepens
