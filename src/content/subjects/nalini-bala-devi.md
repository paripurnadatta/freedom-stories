## Research notes

Participated in Swadeshi and Non-Cooperation while using poetry and khadi work in Assam.

### Overview

Assamese poet and nationalist cultural worker who participated in Non-Cooperation and Swadeshi and helped establish khadi-production centres. Her profile is valuable as a bridge between literary nationalism, women's public participation and constructive economic work.

### Chronology

- **1920s**: Participated in Non-Cooperation/Swadeshi and khadi.... Participated in Non-Cooperation/Swadeshi and khadi work

### Evidence claims

- Nalini Bala Devi participated actively in Non-Cooperation and Swadeshi in Assam. (high confidence)
- She helped establish khadi-production centres to encourage indigenous goods. (high confidence)
- Her literary work included strongly patriotic themes and contributed to Assamese public culture. (high confidence)
