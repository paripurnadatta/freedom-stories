## Research notes

Teenage volunteer killed while leading a flag procession.

### A single day with a long afterlife

Kanaklata Barua’s public memory centers on a highly specific event rather than a long political career. That makes her ideal for testing event-first biography: the person page should deepen the Gohpur action instead of stretching sparse evidence into an artificial life story.

### The Gohpur procession

Government accounts describe a procession seeking to hoist the national flag at Gohpur Police Station during Quit India. Kanaklata was at the front carrying the flag when police fired.

### Martyrdom and the flag

Her death is inseparable from the symbolism of the national flag. A future media/evidence exhibit could connect the event to official commemorative material rather than relying on generic patriotic imagery.

### Quit India in Assam

The profile should redirect users outward from Kanaklata to the wider geography of the 1942 movement in Assam, showing that Quit India took distinct local forms.

### Chronology

- **1942-09-20**: Gohpur flag procession. Joins and leads the procession attempting to hoist the national flag at the police station
- **1942-09-20**: Police firing. Shot during the confrontation as the procession continues toward the station
