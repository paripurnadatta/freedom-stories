## Research notes

Major Congress leader and organizer in South India.

### From Home Rule to Gandhian satyagraha

Rajaji’s career demonstrates continuity between wartime Home Rule networks and later mass civil disobedience.

### Annie Besant’s Madras network

The early Home Rule connection provides a direct bridge to another Batch 003 profile.

### Vedaranyam

The salt campaign should be mapped as a distinct regional march rather than collapsed into Dandi.

### Chronology

- **1916-09**: Salem Home Rule branch. Extends the Home Rule network into the Madras Presidency
- **1919**: Anti-Rowlatt satyagraha. Helps organize nonviolent protest against repressive legislation
- **1930-04**: Vedaranyam Salt March. Leads a regional salt satyagraha to the Tamil coast

### Evidence claims

- Founded a Home Rule League branch in Salem in September 1916. (high confidence)
- Was a major organizer of anti-Rowlatt satyagraha in the Madras Presidency. (high confidence)
- Organized the Vedaranyam Salt March in 1930 as the major Tamil-region parallel to the salt campaign. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects C. Rajagopalachari with Home Rule Movement as participant in. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects C. Rajagopalachari with Annie Besant as worked with. (high confidence)
