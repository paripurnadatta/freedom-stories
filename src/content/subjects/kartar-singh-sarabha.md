## Research notes

Young Ghadar revolutionary executed in 1915.

### A transnational revolutionary at nineteen

Sarabha’s short life compresses migration, diaspora politics, wartime conspiracy and execution.

### Returning for revolt

Ghadar linked overseas publishing and organization to plans for rebellion inside India.

### A model for a later generation

Later revolutionary memory made Sarabha an important reference point for Bhagat Singh’s generation.

### Chronology

- **1912**: California. Moves to the United States and enters diaspora political life
- **1914**: Return to India. Returns after the outbreak of war to organize revolt
- **1915**: Ghadar uprising plan. Works on plans to incite revolt among soldiers
- **1915-11-16**: Execution. Executed after the Lahore Conspiracy Case

### Evidence claims

- Born on 24 May 1896 in Sarabha village, Ludhiana district. (high confidence)
- Became an active member of the Ghadar movement in North America. (high confidence)
- Returned to India during the First World War and worked on plans for military revolt. (high confidence)
- Was executed at Lahore Central Jail on 16 November 1915. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kartar Singh Sarabha with Ghadar Movement as participant in. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kartar Singh Sarabha with Rash Behari Bose as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kartar Singh Sarabha with Bhagat Singh as associated with. (medium confidence)
