## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Kasim Ali's connection with Non-Cooperation Movement.

### Overview

Jorhat-based Non-Cooperation activist who was arrested at a Congress rally and died in colonial custody.

### Chronology

- **1921-1922**: Active in the Non-Cooperation Movement in Jorhat and arrested at a Congress rally
- **1920s**: Died in colonial custody after police ill-treatment, according to the ICHR entry

### Evidence claims

- Kasim Ali was a resident of Jorhat, Assam, and an active participant in the Non-Cooperation Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested at a Congress rally in Jorhat. (HIGH confidence)
- The ICHR entry records that he died in jail after severe police ill-treatment. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Jorhat: Congress rally, arrest and detention context
