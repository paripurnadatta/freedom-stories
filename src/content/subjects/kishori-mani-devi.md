## Research notes

Official profile documents women's mobilization, salt action and picketing.

### Overview

Odisha Congress organizer who mobilized women during the Salt Satyagraha in Ganjam and Huma and joined picketing and salt action.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Organized meetings and women's participation during the Salt Satyagraha in Ganjam/Huma
- **1930**: Participated in picketing and the Huma salt-godown action

### Evidence claims

- Kishori Mani Devi joined the Indian National Congress and took responsibility for political organizing. (HIGH confidence)
- During the Salt Satyagraha she organized meetings in Ganjam/Huma and encouraged women to join the movement. (HIGH confidence)
- She participated in picketing foreign-cloth and liquor shops. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1930 she was involved in an action at the Huma salt godown together with other satyagrahis. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kumarpada / Nimapada / Puri: Home background
- Ganjam / Huma: Salt Satyagraha organizing
