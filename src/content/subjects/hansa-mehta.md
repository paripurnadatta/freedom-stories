## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Women's-rights leader and nationalist who participated in Non-Cooperation and Swadeshi activity, was arrested in the freedom struggle and later carried equality demands into constitution-making.

### Chronology

- **1930-1932**: Active in nationalist campaigning and arrested during the Civil Disobedience-era struggle
- **1937**: Entered the Bombay Legislative Council
- **1946**: Led AIWC and entered the Constituent Assembly

### Evidence claims

- Hansa Mehta participated in Non-Cooperation and Swadeshi activity. (HIGH confidence)
- Her freedom-struggle participation led to arrest in 1932. (HIGH confidence)
- She later became a major All India Women's Conference and Constituent Assembly voice for women's equality. (HIGH confidence)
