## Research notes

Participated in Civil Disobedience and was repeatedly arrested for anti-colonial activity.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Participated in Civil Disobedience and was sentenced to three months' imprisonment
- **1981-02-02**: Died at his residence in Kanpur Nagar

### Evidence claims

- Mewa Lal was born in 1901 in Singhauli village, post-Makanpur, and was the son of Heera Lal. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Civil Disobedience Movement in 1930. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1930 he was sentenced to three months' imprisonment, first in Kanpur Jail and later transferred to Khiri District Jail. (HIGH confidence)
- Mewa Lal died on 2 February 1981 at his residence in Kanpur Nagar. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Singhauli, post-Makanpur: birthplace
- Kanpur Jail: initial imprisonment in 1930
- Khiri district Jail: transferred imprisonment
- Kanpur Nagar: place of death
