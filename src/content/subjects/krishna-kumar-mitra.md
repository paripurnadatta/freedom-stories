### Overview

Bengali political organiser, teacher and journalist whose career links the Indian Association generation to Swadeshi. He joined the Indian Association in 1876, later aligned with Congress politics, and used his journal Sanjibani to mobilize opinion against Bengal partition. Ministry of Culture credits him with an early public boycott call in July 1905; he was deported in 1908. His profile is valuable for explaining how associational and press politics fed into Swadeshi mass mobilisation.
