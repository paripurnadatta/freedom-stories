## Research notes

Led resistance against British rule in the Jaintia Hills.

### Overview

Pnar/Jaintia leader chosen to lead resistance against British taxation and political-cultural interference in the Jaintia Hills. Government sources describe an organized war of attrition beginning around 1860-62, Kiang Nongbah's mobilization of Dalois and local leaders, his capture in December 1862 and public execution at Jowai on 30 December 1862.
