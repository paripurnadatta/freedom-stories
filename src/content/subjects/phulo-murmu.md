## Research notes

Remembered in Santhal resistance traditions alongside Jhano Murmu.

### Women inside the Hul

Phulo's inclusion should correct a male-only representation of the Santhal rebellion without inventing a separate command structure not demonstrated by the evidence.

### Commemoration and evidence

Some later official profiles contain highly specific heroic episodes. Those details should not be promoted to hard fact without corroboration.

### Chronology

- **1855**: Santhal Hul. Phulo Murmu participates in the Santhal uprising and is remembered as part of the leadership and women's mobilisation around the rebellion

### Evidence claims

- Official commemorative sources identify Phulo and Jhano Murmu among the leaders remembered in the Santhal Hul of 1855. (high confidence)
- The Ministry of Culture profile associates Phulo with Bhognadih and with women's mobilisation during the Hul. (medium confidence)
- Government remembrance places Phulo-Jhano alongside Sido-Kanhu and Chand-Bhairav in the anti-exploitation uprising. (high confidence)
