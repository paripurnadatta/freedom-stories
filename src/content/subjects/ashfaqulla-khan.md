## Research notes

Revolutionary executed after the Kakori case.

### Revolution after Non-Cooperation

Khan belonged to a generation that sought armed alternatives after the withdrawal of Non-Cooperation.

### Building the HRA

His relationship with Bismil is best understood through organizational work and shared revolutionary strategy.

### Kakori and execution

The prosecution and executions turned Kakori into a central memory of North Indian revolutionary nationalism.

### Chronology

- **1924**: Hindustan Republican Association. Becomes part of organized armed revolutionary resistance
- **1925-08-09**: Kakori train action. Participates in the HRA operation against government funds
- **1927-12-19**: Execution. Executed after the Kakori case

### Evidence claims

- Helped build the Hindustan Republican Association in the mid-1920s. (high confidence)
- Participated in the Kakori train action and was sentenced to death. (high confidence)
- Was executed at Faizabad Jail on 19 December 1927. (high confidence)
