## Research notes

Continued guerrilla resistance after Kittur's defeat.

### The rebellion after the queen

Rayanna's importance is continuity: the Kittur story did not end with Chennamma's defeat.

### History and folk memory

The folk-song source is valuable evidence of remembrance but should not be the sole basis for precise operational claims.

### Chronology

- **1820s-1830**: Resistance after Kittur's defeat. Rayanna continues armed resistance in the Kittur countryside with loyalist support
- **1831-01-26**: Execution at Nandagad. The colonial state publicly executes Rayanna after his capture

### Evidence claims

- Rayanna is described as a faithful follower and trusted lieutenant of Rani Chennamma of Kittur. (high confidence)
- Rayanna is described as a faithful follower and trusted lieutenant of Rani Chennamma of Kittur. (high confidence)
- He organized armed loyalists and attacked British positions in the Kittur/Belagavi region. (high confidence)
- In 1830 he attacked and plundered offices and the treasury at Nandagad. (high confidence)
- He was captured and publicly hanged at Nandagad on 26 January 1831. (high confidence)
- A collected Kannada folk song documents the durability of his popular memory in the region. (high confidence)
