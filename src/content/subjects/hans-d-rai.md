## Research notes

Documented association with Jaipur Praja Mandal / Quit India in Rajasthan.

### Overview

Veteran Jaipur Praja Mandal activist who worked with Hiralal Shastri in reorganizing the organization and later joined the Azad Morcha during the Quit India period. His profile is best framed around the Jaipur princely-state struggle for representative government.

- **1939**: Imprisoned during Praja Mandal agitation.. Imprisoned during Praja Mandal agitation
- **1942**: Joined Azad Morcha during Quit India.. Joined Azad Morcha during Quit India

### Evidence claims

- Hans D. Rai collaborated with Hiralal Shastri in reorganizing Jaipur Praja Mandal in 1936. (high confidence)
- He was imprisoned during the 1939 Praja Mandal movement. (high confidence)
- In 1942 he joined the Azad Morcha and spread the Quit India 'do or die' message through meetings and demonstrations. (high confidence)
