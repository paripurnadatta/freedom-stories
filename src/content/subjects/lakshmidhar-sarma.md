## Research notes

### Overview

Assamese student organizer whose participation in the Non-Cooperation movement helped extend the boycott of government education and nationalist mobilization into Sonitpur/Darrang.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Associated with the Assamese student leadership that participated in the Non-Cooperation organizing cycle around the Nagpur student conference
- **1921-01**: Student-led boycott of government educational institutions began in Assam; Sarma is named among Sonitpur/Darrang leaders
- **1921**: Student activists in the Sonitpur network promoted national education and picketing campaigns

### Evidence claims

- Lakshmidhar Sarma was among the student leaders active in the Non-Cooperation Movement in the Sonitpur/Darrang area of Assam. (HIGH confidence)
- Assamese students returning from the 1920 Nagpur student conference helped launch the boycott of government educational institutions in January 1921; Sarma belonged to the local leadership named in this movement context. (HIGH confidence)
- The student movement in Sonitpur supported national schools and picketing of foreign-goods and intoxicant outlets as part of Non-Cooperation. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Sonitpur / Darrang: Student Non-Cooperation organizing
- Tezpur: National-school and student-mobilization context
