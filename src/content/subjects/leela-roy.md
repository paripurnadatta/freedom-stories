## Research notes

Pioneer of women's education and revolutionary organizing.

### Overview

Feminist educator, nationalist organizer and revolutionary-associated political activist who built institutions for women's education and public participation. She founded Deepali Sangha, edited Jayashree, worked with Shree Sangha and later joined Subhas Bose's Forward Bloc. Her profile should emphasize women's political agency rather than treating education as separate from nationalism.
