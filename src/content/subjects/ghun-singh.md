## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Ghun Singh's connection with Anglo-Manipur War.

### Overview

Manipuri participant in the 1891 anti-British conflict who was sentenced to transportation for life and died in detention in 1906.

### Chronology

- **1891-03-24**: Associated with the Palace Revolution and subsequent anti-British conflict in Manipur
- **1891**: Convicted by colonial authorities and sentenced to transportation for life
- **1906-12-01**: Died in detention

### Evidence claims

- Ghun Singh was a resident of Sagolband, Manipur, and was involved in the 1891 conflict with British authority. (HIGH confidence)
- He was tried by the colonial authorities and sentenced to transportation for life for waging war against the British. (HIGH confidence)
- He died in detention on 1 December 1906. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Sagolband / Imphal: Home and political context
- Manipur / colonial detention system: Transportation and detention
