## Research notes

Frontline Andhra leader during the Simon Commission protests.

### From barrister to mass politician

The renunciation of legal practice illustrates how Non-Cooperation altered elite professional careers.

### Madras and Andhra in one political life

Prakasam is a useful regional bridge because his public career moved through both Madras Presidency politics and Andhra political identity.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Leaves legal practice for Non-Cooperation. Prakasam abandons the Madras bar for full-time nationalist politics
- **1928**: Simon Commission protest in Madras. His confrontation with armed police becomes the defining public image of his nationalist career
- **1942**: Quit India phase. He remains involved in the Congress's wartime confrontation with British rule
