## Research notes

Documented association with early nationalism in Madras Presidency.

### National politics before mass satyagraha

The early Congress generation built petitions, associations, newspapers and representative demands long before the Gandhian movements.

### Madras as an institutional centre

Charlu's profile connects three co-founders without converting documented organizational work into unsupported personal intimacy.

### Chronology

- **1884**: Madras Mahajana Sabha founded. Charlu joins Subramania Iyer and Veeraraghavachariar in creating one of Madras's major early political associations
- **1891**: Congress presidency at Nagpur. He presides over the seventh session of the Indian National Congress

### Evidence claims

- P. Ananda Charlu co-founded the Madras Mahajana Sabha in May 1884 with G. Subramania Iyer and M. Veeraraghavachariar. (high confidence)
- The Sabha advocated constitutional and administrative reforms including lower taxation, expenditure cuts and simultaneous civil-service examinations. (high confidence)
- Ananda Charlu served as president of the seventh Indian National Congress session at Nagpur in 1891. (high confidence)
