## Research notes

Early mass political organizer and nationalist.

### Nationalism before the mass movement

Banerjea's world was one of meetings, petitions, lectures, newspapers and associations; those methods were foundational rather than merely timid precursors.

### The railway as political infrastructure

His tours show how changing communications enabled an all-India political audience to be organized.

### Chronology

- **1876-07-26**: Indian Association founded. Banerjea and associates establish a political organization intended to coordinate opinion beyond Bengal
- **1877**: Civil-service agitation tour. Banerjea travels across northern India to build a common political campaign around access to the civil service
- **1883**: First National Conference. The Indian Association convenes representatives around constitutional and administrative demands
- **1895 and 1902**: Congress presidencies. Banerjea twice presides over the Indian National Congress

### Evidence claims

- After his dismissal from the Indian Civil Service, Surendranath Banerjea entered public life as a teacher, orator and political organizer. (high confidence)
- He co-founded the Indian Association in Kolkata on 26 July 1876 with Ananda Mohan Bose and other associates. (high confidence)
- He organized all-India tours in 1877 and later years around the civil-service age issue, using the railway network to coordinate political meetings. (high confidence)
- The Indian Association organized National Conferences in 1883 and 1885 that prefigured the all-India political forum later represented by Congress. (high confidence)
- Banerjea later participated regularly in Congress and presided over its 1895 and 1902 sessions. (high confidence)
