## Research notes

Documented association with HSRA / Lahore Conspiracy in Uttar Pradesh.

### Evidence claims

- Jaidev Kapoor was born around Diwali in 1908 in Hardoi. (HIGH confidence)
- As an HSRA member he helped reconnoitre the Central Legislative Assembly and arrange access connected with the 1929 bombing plan. (HIGH confidence)
- He spent many years in prison and participated in hunger strikes, including in the Andamans. (HIGH confidence)
