## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Delhi nationalist organizer who mobilized women for Civil Disobedience, built women's Congress organizations and linked the freedom struggle with labour/socialist politics.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Led women's Civil Disobedience mobilization in Delhi
- **1930s**: Organized Congress Mahila Samaj / Desh Sevika Dal and labour political work
- **1934**: Associated with the founding phase of the Congress Socialist Party

### Evidence claims

- The selected Satyavati Devi is the Delhi freedom activist remembered for organizing large numbers of women in the Civil Disobedience Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- Political-history material identifies her with the Congress Mahila Samaj and Congress Desh Sevika Dal and with efforts to politicize Delhi textile workers. (HIGH confidence)
- She became one of the founder-members of the Congress Socialist Party and endured repeated imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Delhi: Women's, labour and socialist organizing
