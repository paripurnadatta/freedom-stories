### Evidence claims

- Ananta Singh lived from 1903 to 1979; Banglapedia gives his birth as 1 December 1903 in Chittagong and his death as 25 January 1979. (HIGH confidence)
- His father was Golap Singh, and his family had settled in Chittagong after coming from Agra. (HIGH confidence)
- Ananta Singh became a close associate of Surya Sen and joined his revolutionary group after leaving formal studies. (HIGH confidence)
- He led an operation against Assam-Bengal Railway Company funds and was later arrested again in 1924, receiving four years’ imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
- During the Chittagong Armoury Raid, Ananta Singh and Ganesh Ghosh led the group assigned to the police armoury. (HIGH confidence)
- After surrendering in Kolkata, Ananta Singh was among those deported to the Andamans; Banglapedia records that he began a hunger strike in Cellular Jail in 1932 and was later transferred to mainland India. (HIGH confidence)
- He was released in 1946, one year before independence. (HIGH confidence)
