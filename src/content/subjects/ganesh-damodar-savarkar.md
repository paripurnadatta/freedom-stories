## Research notes

Early revolutionary organizer.

### Chronology

- **1909**: Arrest, life sentence and deportation. Arrest, life sentence and deportation
- **1921-1922**: Returned to India and released. Returned to India and released
- **1910-06-01**: Arrival at Port Blair reported in prisoner-list material. Arrival at Port Blair reported in prisoner-list material

### Evidence claims

- He was arrested in 1909, tried for treason, sentenced to life imprisonment and deported to the Andamans. (high confidence)
- An archival prisoner-list reproduction records Ganesh Damodar Savarkar as arriving at Port Blair on 1 June 1910. (medium confidence)
- He was the brother of Vinayak Damodar Savarkar. (high confidence)
- He was also known as Babarao Savarkar. (high confidence)
- He was brought back to India in 1921 and released in 1922 after further imprisonment. (high confidence)
- The Bombay High Court judgment records Ganesh Damodar Savarkar’s conviction under IPC sections 124A and 121 and a sentence of transportation for life under section 121, alongside two years’ rigorous imprisonment under section 124A. (high confidence)
- He was a prominent organizer associated with Abhinav Bharat and the revolutionary network in Maharashtra. (high confidence)
- Ganesh Damodar Savarkar was born on 13 June 1879 and died on 16 March 1945. (high confidence)
