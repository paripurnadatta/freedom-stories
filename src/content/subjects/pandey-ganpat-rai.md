## Research notes

Documented association with 1857 in Jharkhand.

### Overview

Former Dewan in Chota Nagpur who became a military-political leader of the 1857 uprising, fought in the Ranchi-Chatra region and was executed in April 1858.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Entered active rebellion in the Chota Nagpur/Ranchi region
- **1857-10-02**: Rebel forces were defeated at the Battle of Chatra
- **1858-03**: Captured by British authorities
- **1858-04-21**: Executed in Ranchi

### Evidence claims

- Pandey Ganpat Rai had served as Dewan in Chota Nagpur before turning against British interference. (HIGH confidence)
- During the 1857 uprising he assumed command within the rebel forces associated with Thakur Vishwanath Shahdeo. (HIGH confidence)
- He fought in the regional campaign that included the Battle of Chatra on 2 October 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured in March 1858 and hanged in Ranchi on 21 April 1858. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Lohardaga / Chota Nagpur: Home and political base
- Ranchi: Rebel command and execution
- Chatra: Battle context
