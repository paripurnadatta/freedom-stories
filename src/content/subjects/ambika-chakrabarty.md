### Evidence claims

- Ambika Chakrabarty was born in 1892 and died on 6 March 1962. (HIGH confidence)
- He was born in Barma village in Chittagong district; the government profile names his father as Nandkumar Chakraborty. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined Surya Sen’s Indian Republican Army and the Chittagong Yugantar network. (HIGH confidence)
- On 18 April 1930, Chakrabarty led the group tasked with disabling/occupying Chittagong’s telephone and telegraph communications. (HIGH confidence)
- He was seriously injured in the Jalalabad encounter on 22 April 1930 but escaped. (HIGH confidence)
- After arrest he was sentenced to death; the sentence was commuted to life imprisonment and he was transferred to the Cellular Jail at Port Blair. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1952 he was elected to the West Bengal Legislative Assembly. (HIGH confidence)
