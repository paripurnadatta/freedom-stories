## Research notes

Helped bring Gandhi to Champaran.

### The person who brought the national leader to the local struggle

Shukla reverses the usual direction of nationalist storytelling: Gandhi did not discover Champaran on his own; a cultivator insisted that he come.

### Peasant agency before the famous satyagraha

The profile gives the agrarian community a human bridge into the better-known Gandhian narrative.

### Chronology

- **1916**: Shukla presses the Champaran case. He seeks Gandhi out and refuses to let the indigo grievance disappear into the background of national politics
- **1917**: Gandhi reaches Champaran. Shukla's persistence brings Gandhi to Bihar, where an inquiry and satyagraha begin
- **1917-1918**: Indigo system challenged. Official inquiry and political pressure begin dismantling coercive planter practices

### Evidence claims

- Government sources identify Raj Kumar Shukla as a Champaran raiyat/indigo cultivator who sought help against the oppressive indigo system. (high confidence)
- He repeatedly approached Gandhi and persuaded him to visit Champaran in 1917. (high confidence)
- Shukla accompanied Gandhi into the district and helped connect him with the local agrarian grievance that became the Champaran Satyagraha. (high confidence)
- The investigation and agitation contributed to the dismantling of key coercive features of the indigo planter system. (high confidence)
