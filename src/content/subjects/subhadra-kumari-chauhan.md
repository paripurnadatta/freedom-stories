### Overview

Hindi poet and freedom fighter whose political career included Non-Cooperation, the 1923 Jhanda Satyagraha and later imprisonment during anti-colonial protest. Her nationalist poetry—especially on Rani Lakshmibai—became part of the cultural memory of the freedom movement.
