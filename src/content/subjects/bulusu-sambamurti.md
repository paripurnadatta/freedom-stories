## Research notes

Documented association with Civil Disobedience in Andhra Pradesh.

### Overview

Andhra Gandhian and Congress leader who left law for Non-Cooperation, advocated complete independence, joined Salt Satyagraha and later led the Madras legislature.

### Chronology

- **1919-1920**: Moved from Home Rule into Non-Cooperation
- **1923**: Helped organize/participate in the Kakinada Congress session
- **1927**: Associated with Arms Satyagraha and republican/complete-independence politics
- **1930**: Participated in Salt Satyagraha near Kakinada and was arrested

### Evidence claims

- Bulusu Sambamurti joined Home Rule activity and then Non-Cooperation, giving up legal practice for the national movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He took part in the 1923 Kakinada Congress session and became associated with advocacy of complete independence. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Salt Satyagraha near Kakinada in 1930 and was arrested. (HIGH confidence)
- Nehru Archive metadata records his leadership in Hindustani Seva Dal and his 1927 Arms Satyagraha involvement. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kakinada / Godavari: Congress and Salt Satyagraha
- Madras Presidency: Legislative and Congress politics
