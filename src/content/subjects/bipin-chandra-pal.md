## Research notes

Leading Swadeshi-era nationalist.

### Nationalism through speech and print

Pal’s profile should foreground journalism and public oratory as instruments of mobilization.

### The politics of boycott

Swadeshi was political, economic and cultural, and Pal was among its visible advocates.

### Lal-Bal-Pal

The trio is useful as a connection cluster without erasing each leader’s distinct regional history.

### Chronology

- **1886**: Joins Congress. Enters organized nationalist politics
- **1905-1908**: Swadeshi leadership. Advocates boycott and indigenous production
