## Research notes

Organized sanghams and resistance to feudal exactions in the Nalgonda–Warangal region.

### Overview

Andhra Mahasabha and communist peasant leader in Hyderabad State, active against vetti, landlord domination and the Nizam's political order. Telangana's SCERT text places his radicalization in student politics and connects his work to the transformation of the Andhra Mahasabha into a militant rural movement.
