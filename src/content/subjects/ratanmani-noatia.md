## Research notes

Led the Reang uprising against oppressive local authority and the princely-state administration during the wartime crisis.

### Overview

Religious leader of Noatia origin who became the central figure of the 1942-43 Reang uprising in princely Tripura. Tripura tribal-research publications emphasize grievances over taxation, wartime recruitment, famine, exploitation by intermediaries and community autonomy. Crucially, the strongest state research source explicitly says the uprising had no direct organizational link to the Indian freedom movement, even though both opposed coercive structures.
