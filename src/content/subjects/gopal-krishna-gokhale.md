## Research notes

Moderate nationalist and mentor to Gandhi.

### Politics as public service

Gokhale’s nationalism joined constitutional politics to a disciplined ethic of social service.

### Servants of India Society

The society translated political ideals into education, welfare and nation-building work.

### A mentor to Gandhi

The Gokhale–Gandhi relationship is a major bridge between moderate nationalism and the later mass movement.

### Chronology

- **1905-06-12**: Servants of India Society founded. Creates an institution for disciplined national service and social reform
- **1890s-1915**: Moderate nationalist leadership. Works within Congress and public institutions for political reform
- **before 1915**: Mentorship of Gandhi. Becomes a uniquely important political guide for Gandhi
