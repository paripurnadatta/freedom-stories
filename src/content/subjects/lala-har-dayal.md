## Research notes

Intellectual and organizer associated with Ghadar.

### A movement built across oceans

Har Dayal’s career demonstrates how migration and print helped create a transnational revolutionary organization.

### Ghadar

The Ghadar movement connected labour migration, political newspapers and plans for anti-colonial uprising.

### A network that included Kartar Singh Sarabha

The relationship should be represented as shared documented Ghadar organization rather than an invented personal mentorship.

### Chronology

- **1900s**: Expatriate radicalism. Moves from India and Britain into wider revolutionary networks
- **1913**: Ghadar organization and publishing. Helps mobilize Indian migrants through the Ghadar movement
- **1913-1914**: Pacific-coast revolutionary network. The diaspora movement links propaganda abroad to plans for revolt in India

### Evidence claims

- Wrote for radical nationalist publications including The Indian Sociologist before his Ghadar years. (high confidence)
- Became a key participant in the Ghadar Party and helped mobilize Indian migrants abroad for anti-colonial politics. (high confidence)
- Worked within the same Ghadar organizational network as Kartar Singh Sarabha. (high confidence)

- The supplied record connects Lala Har Dayal with Ghadar Party as member of. (high confidence)
