## Research notes

Leader of resistance in Sivaganga; associated with the 1801 proclamation.

### A struggle narrated through two brothers

The canonical record must not silently erase the fact that the strongest official sources describe Periya and Chinna Marudhu together.

### A wider political appeal

The Jambudweep proclamation matters because the official account presents it as a call crossing religious categories in opposition to English rule.

### Chronology

- **1799-1801**: Resistance after Kattabomman's execution. The Sivaganga leadership shelters Oomadurai and the anti-Company war broadens
- **1801-10-24**: Execution at Tiruppathur. The Marudhu brothers are executed after suppression of the rebellion
