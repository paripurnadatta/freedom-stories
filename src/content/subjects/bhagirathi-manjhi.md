## Research notes

Documented association with tribal resistance in Jharkhand.

### Overview

Adivasi leader associated with the Kherwar/Kharwar movement in nineteenth-century Jharkhand. The current strongest official evidence located for this package is Jharkhand government's recognition of Bhagirathi Manjhi as a tribal martyr under the Shaheed Gram Vikas Yojana. More detailed movement chronology in secondary sources places his leadership in the 1870s; staging should therefore remain conservative.

- His historical association is with the Kherwar/Kharwar movement in the nineteenth-century Santhal/Jharkhand region. (medium confidence)
