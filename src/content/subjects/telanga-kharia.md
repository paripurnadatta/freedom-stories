## Research notes

Documented association with tribal resistance in Jharkhand.

### Overview

Kharia leader from Chotanagpur who opposed colonial taxation, land alienation and interference with indigenous self-governance. Government sources recognize his organization of resistance and insistence on traditional self-rule; public-broadcaster material supplies a fuller chronology but should be cross-checked for exact dates and numbers.
