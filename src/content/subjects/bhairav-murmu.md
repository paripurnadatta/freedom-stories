## Research notes

Co-led the Santhal rebellion.

### Overview

One of the Murmu siblings associated with leadership of the Santhal Hul of 1855. Government commemorations consistently name Chand and Bhairav alongside Sidho, Kanhu, Phulo and Jhano as central figures of the uprising against colonial rule and exploitative intermediaries. Bhairav's standalone biography is less richly documented than Sidho or Kanhu, so the profile should emphasize collective Hul leadership.
