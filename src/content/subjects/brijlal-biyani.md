## Research notes

Documented association with Congress / Quit India in Central Provinces.

### Overview

Vidarbha/Berar Congress leader, journalist and mass political organizer active in Civil Disobedience and Quit India. Maharashtra Gazetteer material documents his 1930 public meetings, no-tax advocacy and satyagraha politics, while state histories place him among significant 1942 leaders and in the Matribhoomi press tradition.
