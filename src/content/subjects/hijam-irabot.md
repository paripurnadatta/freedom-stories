## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Radical democratic and socialist leader who dominated much of Manipur's politics from the late 1930s through 1951. He helped transform the Nikhil Manipuri Mahasabha into an overtly political body, focused heavily on peasants and the urban poor, experienced imprisonment, and moved toward communist politics. His profile crosses the late-colonial and post-1947 boundary and must keep those phases distinct.

### Evidence claims

- Hijam Irabot was a founder member of the Nikhil Manipuri Mahasabha and a dominant political figure in Manipur from the late 1930s. (high confidence)
- He emphasized the welfare and political organization of peasants and the urban poor. (high confidence)
- Imprisonment contributed to his turn toward communist ideology. (high confidence)
