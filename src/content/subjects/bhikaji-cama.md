## Research notes

International advocate for Indian independence.

### Exile as political space

Cama shows that the freedom struggle also operated through transnational networks outside British India.

### Stuttgart, 1907

The flag episode should be presented as international advocacy, not as if it were identical to the later national flag.

### Print networks abroad

Her publishing work made Europe a base for revolutionary communication.

### Chronology

- **1900s**: Expatriate nationalist work. Builds political connections among Indian nationalists in Europe
- **1907-08**: Stuttgart flag. Unfurls an independence flag before an international audience
- **1909 onward**: Bande Mataram from Europe. Uses publishing abroad to circulate radical nationalist ideas

### Evidence claims

- Became politically active among Indian nationalist networks in Britain and continental Europe. (high confidence)
- Worked with expatriate nationalists including Dadabhai Naoroji and Shyamji Krishna Varma. (high confidence)
- Unfurled a flag presented as an Indian independence flag at the Stuttgart socialist conference in August 1907. (high confidence)
- Published the Paris edition of Bande Mataram and used print to sustain anti-colonial propaganda abroad. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Bhikaji Cama with Dadabhai Naoroji as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Bhikaji Cama with Shyamji Krishna Varma as worked with. (high confidence)
