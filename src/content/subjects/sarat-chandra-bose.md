### Evidence claims

- Sarat Chandra Bose was born on 6 September 1889 in Cuttack. (HIGH confidence)
- He was a barrister and combined a prominent legal career with nationalist politics. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Non-Cooperation Movement in 1921. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Civil Disobedience Movement of 1930–32 and was arrested in 1932. (HIGH confidence)
- He was detained again from 1941 to 1945. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1946 Sarat Chandra Bose served in the Interim Government as Minister of Works, Mines and Power. (HIGH confidence)
- After leaving the Congress amid political differences, he formed the Socialist Republican Party in 1946 and launched the newspaper Nation in 1948. (HIGH confidence)
