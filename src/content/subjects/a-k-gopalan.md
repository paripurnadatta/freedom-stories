## Research notes

Participated in nationalist campaigns and later socialist politics.

### Overview

Malabar nationalist and worker organizer who participated in Payyannur Salt Satyagraha, Guruvayur Satyagraha and mass labour/peasant mobilization.

### Chronology

- **late 1920s**: Entered nationalist and worker organizing
- **1930**: Participated in Payyannur Salt Satyagraha
- **1930s**: Participated in Guruvayur/Civil Disobedience and labour organizing
- **1936**: Organized a march from Kannur to Madras

### Evidence claims

- A. K. Gopalan joined the freedom struggle through boycott and khadi activity and worked among workers in Kannur and Calicut. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the 1930 Payyannur Salt Satyagraha. (HIGH confidence)
- He took part in Guruvayur Satyagraha and was imprisoned during Civil Disobedience. (HIGH confidence)
- He later organized labour and mass marches, including a 1936 march from Kannur to Madras. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kannur / Calicut: Worker and nationalist organizing
- Payyannur: Salt Satyagraha
- Guruvayur: Temple-entry satyagraha
