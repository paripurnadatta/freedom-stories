## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Awadh resistance in 1857.

### Overview

Zamindar of Chicharee in Palamu who joined the 1857 uprising and became part of the Chero-Bhogta resistance alliance. Ministry of Culture emphasizes the overlap between local zamindar, tribal and peasant responses in Palamu and places Bhaya Kumar Sahee in the same regional resistance field as Nilamber-Pitamber.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Joined the Palamu uprising against East India Comp.... Joined the Palamu uprising against East India Company rule

### Evidence claims

- Bhaya Kumar Sahee was the Zamindar of Chicharee in Palamu and participated in the 1857 uprising. (high confidence)
- The Ministry profile places him within a Chero-Bhogta alliance reflecting overlapping tribal, peasant and landed resistance. (high confidence)
- His regional resistance was connected to the wider Palamu uprising led by figures including Nilamber and Pitamber. (high confidence)
