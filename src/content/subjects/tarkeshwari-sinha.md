## Research notes

### Overview

Bihar student activist who joined Quit India as a teenager and later became a prominent Students' Congress organizer in the final years of colonial rule. Her profile should focus on youth politics, the INA-trial mobilization and women's participation, with post-1952 parliamentary office treated as later legacy.
