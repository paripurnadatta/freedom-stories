## Research notes

Promoted Swadeshi goods and organized women in Bengal and Punjab.

### Overview

Writer, editor and organizer whose Swadeshi and women's political work linked Bengal and Punjab.

### Chronology

- **1905**: Marriage to Ram Bhuj Dutt Choudhary shifted a substantial part of her political work to Lahore/Punjab
- **1905-1919**: Promoted Swadeshi goods, nationalist writing and women's organization across Bengal/Punjab networks
- **1919**: Publicly opposed the Jallianwala Bagh massacre and increasingly followed Gandhian non-violence

### Evidence claims

- Sarla Devi Chaudhurani was born in Kolkata in 1872 and worked as editor of the Bengali journal Bharti. (HIGH confidence)
- She promoted Swadeshi goods through Lakshmi Bhandar and used writing and public activity to encourage nationalist participation. (HIGH confidence)
- After her 1905 marriage and move to Lahore, her political activity expanded in Punjab and included women's organization. (HIGH confidence)
- By 1919 she had aligned with Gandhian non-violence, protested the Jallianwala Bagh massacre and remained active in Congress politics. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kolkata: Journalism and Swadeshi activity
- Lahore / Punjab: Women's organizing and nationalist activity
