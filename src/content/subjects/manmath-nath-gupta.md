## Research notes

HRA revolutionary and later writer/historian.

### Chronology

- **1925**: Kakori action participation. Kakori action participation
- **1972**: Publication of History of the Indian Revolutionary Movement. Publication of History of the Indian Revolutionary Movement

### Evidence claims

- He entered the freedom struggle at about age 13. (high confidence)
- Manmath Nath Gupta was born on 7 February 1908 and died on 26 October 2000. (high confidence)
- At age 17, he actively participated in the 1925 Kakori action. (high confidence)
- He became an active member of the Hindustan Republican Association. (high confidence)
- He later wrote extensively in Hindi, English and Bengali, including a 1972 history of the Indian revolutionary movement. (high confidence)
