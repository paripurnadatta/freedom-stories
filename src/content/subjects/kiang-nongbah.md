## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Jaintia resistance under Kiang Nongbah.

### Overview

Pnar/Jaintia resistance leader who organized the 1862 uprising against British taxation and interference and was executed at Jowai on 30 December 1862.

### Chronology

- **1861**: Jaintia leaders selected Kiang Nangbah to lead resistance against British authority
- **1862**: Led the Jaintia uprising against British taxation and political/cultural interference
- **1862-12-27**: Meghalaya government account dates his capture to 27 December 1862
- **1862-12-30**: Publicly executed at Jowai

### Evidence claims

- Kiang Nongbah, also rendered U Kiang Nangbah, was a Pnar/Jaintia leader of resistance to British rule in the Jaintia Hills. (HIGH confidence)
- He organized the 1862 uprising that followed intensified taxation and colonial interference in Jaintia political and cultural life. (HIGH confidence)
- He coordinated resistance through local leaders including Dolois and Sirdars. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured and publicly hanged at Jowai on 30 December 1862. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Jaintia Hills / West Jaintia Hills: Regional resistance
- Jowai / Iawmusiang: Capture, trial and execution context
