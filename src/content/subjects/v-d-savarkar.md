## Research notes

Revolutionary nationalist; later a major ideological figure.

### History as revolutionary argument

Savarkar’s London activity shows how the interpretation of 1857 could be turned into a political resource.

### Inside the India House network

The profile should distinguish India House as an expatriate organizing space from Abhinav Bharat as a revolutionary organization.

### Rewriting 1857

Use the normalized historical description of his book; do not reproduce transcription errors on the supplied government webpage.

### Chronology

- **1905-1907**: India House revolutionary network. Works within the London centre created by Shyamji Krishna Varma
- **1907**: Fiftieth anniversary of 1857. Uses public commemoration of the uprising for revolutionary mobilization
- **late 1900s**: Writing 1857 as a war of independence. Historical interpretation becomes part of revolutionary political education
