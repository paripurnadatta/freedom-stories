## Research notes

### Evidence claims

- Balwantrai Mehta was born on 19 February 1900. (HIGH confidence)
- He entered nationalist politics during the Non-Cooperation period around 1920. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Bardoli movement in 1928. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned during Quit India, with official media describing a roughly three-year detention. (HIGH confidence)
