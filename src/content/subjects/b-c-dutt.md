## Research notes

Rating whose protest writings helped trigger the naval strike.

### Chronology

- **1941**: Joined the Royal Indian Navy
- **1946-02**: Played a leading role in the Royal Indian Navy mutiny
- **1971**: Published Mutiny of the Innocents

### Evidence claims

- B.C. Dutt was Balrai Chandra Dutt, a Bengali Royal Indian Navy rating who had joined the service in 1941. (HIGH confidence)
- By 1946 Dutt had reached the rank of Leading Telegraphist in the Royal Indian Navy. (HIGH confidence)
- Dutt played a leading role in the 1946 Royal Indian Navy mutiny and was discharged from naval service afterward. (HIGH confidence)
- Dutt later published the memoir Mutiny of the Innocents in 1971. (HIGH confidence)
