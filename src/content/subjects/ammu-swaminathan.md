## Research notes

Congress activist and women's organizer.

### Overview

Women's-rights activist, Congress worker and Constituent Assembly member whose public career connected feminist organization with anti-colonial politics. She helped build the Women's India Association from 1917, joined Congress in 1934, participated in Quit India and was imprisoned.
