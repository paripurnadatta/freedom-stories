### Evidence claims

- Shah Nawaz Khan lived from 1914 to 1983. (HIGH confidence)
- He rose to captain in the British Indian Army and became a prisoner of war after the British defeat at Singapore in 1942. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Indian National Army in 1943 and served both as an INA commander and as a member of Subhas Chandra Bose’s provisional government. (HIGH confidence)
- He was tried with Prem Kumar Sahgal and Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon in the first major INA court-martial at the Red Fort in 1945 on the charge of waging war against the King, with additional charges considered in the proceedings. (HIGH confidence)
- The court convicted the three INA defendants and imposed severe sentences, but the sentences were immediately remitted and they were released. (HIGH confidence)
- After independence, Shah Nawaz Khan became active in the Indian National Congress and later chaired the 1956 committee examining the circumstances of Subhas Chandra Bose’s reported death. (HIGH confidence)
