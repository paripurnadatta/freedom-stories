## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Mangal Kurki's connection with Quit India Movement.

### Overview

Dhekiajuli Congress activist who joined the 20 September 1942 Quit India flag procession and died from injuries sustained during police repression.

### Chronology

- **1942-09-20**: Joined the Dhekiajuli procession connected to the attempt to hoist the national flag at the police station
- **1942-09**: Died from injuries received during the repression of the Dhekiajuli procession

### Evidence claims

- Mangal Kurki was a resident of Dhekiajuli, Assam, and an active Congress member. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the 20 September 1942 procession to Dhekiajuli police station in the Quit India context. (HIGH confidence)
- He died from injuries received during the police firing/lathi-charge and is listed among the Dhekiajuli flag martyrs. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Dhekiajuli / Sonitpur: Quit India flag procession and death
