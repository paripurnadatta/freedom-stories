## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Godavari Parulekar's connection with Individual Satyagraha; Warli Adivasi movement.

### A bridge from nationalism to the left

Parulekar shows how the anti-colonial movement intersected with class politics rather than developing in one ideological line.

### Freedom in the countryside

The Warli struggle focused on everyday labour, debt, landlord power and dignity—making agrarian relations central to the meaning of political freedom.

### Chronology

- **early 1930s**: Civil disobedience and imprisonment. Parulekar moves from legal training into direct nationalist protest
- **1930s**: Institutional and ideological transition. Work in social-service organizations gives way to increasing involvement in Marxist politics
- **1945**: Warli mobilisation. She becomes a leading organizer in the Adivasi agrarian struggle in the Thane region

### Evidence claims

- The government profile identifies Godavari Parulekar as the first woman law graduate in Maharashtra and as an activist who joined the anti-colonial movement. (high confidence)
- She participated in civil-disobedience activity and was convicted for Individual Satyagraha in the early 1930s. (high confidence)
- She became associated with the Servants of India Society before moving toward Marxist and communist politics. (high confidence)
- By the mid-1940s she was deeply involved in organizing Warli Adivasi agricultural labourers and peasants against exploitative landlord and labour relations. (high confidence)
- Her later involvement in the liberation of Dadra and Nagar Haveli belongs to the post-1947 phase and must not be merged into the colonial freedom-struggle chronology. (high confidence)
