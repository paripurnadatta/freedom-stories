## Research notes

Kerala socialist and anti-colonial organizer.

### Overview

Kerala socialist and communist organizer who came through Vaikom and Salt Satyagraha into worker and peasant mobilization.

### Chronology

- **1924**: Participated in Vaikom Satyagraha
- **1930**: Joined the Kozhikode-Payyannur Salt March and was imprisoned
- **1934**: Entered Congress Socialist Party leadership in Kerala
- **1938**: Helped organize major worker action in Alappuzha

### Evidence claims

- P. Krishna Pillai participated in Vaikom Satyagraha as a volunteer. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the 1930 Civil Disobedience/Salt March from Kozhikode to Payyannur and was imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)
- He became secretary of the Congress Socialist Party in Kerala and organized workers, including the 1938 Alappuzha strike. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Vaikom: Anti-caste satyagraha
- Payyannur / Malabar: Salt Satyagraha
- Alappuzha: Worker organizing
