## Research notes

Sikkim-born woman freedom fighter active in Gandhian movements, including Non-Cooperation and Quit India.

### Overview

Lepcha nationalist organizer, also known as Sabitri Devi, who joined charkha/khadi work, Gandhian mobilization and anti-colonial campaigning from the Himalayan region.

### Chronology

- **1917**: Joined charkha and khaddar work in the Himalayan region
- **1918**: Went to Calcutta for nationalist/khadi work
- **1921**: Mobilized people around Kurseong during the Non-Cooperation phase and faced imprisonment
- **1942**: Ministry profile places her in Quit India-era activity

### Evidence claims

- Helen Lepcha, also known as Sabitri Devi, was born in Sangbong in South Sikkim and grew up in the Kurseong area. (HIGH confidence)
- She joined the charkha and khaddar movement in 1917 and went to Calcutta in 1918 to deepen her spinning and nationalist work. (HIGH confidence)
- She mobilized people around Kurseong during Non-Cooperation through door-to-door campaigning against foreign goods and was later imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry profile records her participation in the wider freedom movement through Gandhian networks and later Quit India-era activity. (MEDIUM confidence)

### Places

- Sangbong / South Sikkim: Birthplace
- Kurseong: Non-Cooperation and local mobilization
- Calcutta: Khadi/charkha and nationalist network
