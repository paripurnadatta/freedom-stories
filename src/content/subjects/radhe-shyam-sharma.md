## Research notes

Documented association with Praja Mandal / Azad Morcha in Rajasthan.

### Overview

Jaipur political worker whose activism moved from Arya Samaj circles into Praja Mandal and Azad Morcha activity. Ministry records describe his long service as Praja Mandal secretary, khadi work at Sevagram and militant anti-colonial activity in the 1942 period.

### Chronology

- **1938**: Joined Praja Mandal.. Joined Praja Mandal
- **1942**: Participated in khadi work and Azad Morcha activit.... Participated in khadi work and Azad Morcha activity

### Evidence claims

- Radhe Shyam Sharma joined the freedom struggle young and became an active Praja Mandal member in 1938. (high confidence)
- He served for a long period as Praja Mandal secretary and worked in khadi programmes at Sevagram in 1942. (high confidence)
- He also worked for a period with Azad Morcha during the Quit India phase. (high confidence)
