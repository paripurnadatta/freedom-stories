## Research notes

One of the principal Assamese figures connected to the 1857 uprising.

### Tea, property and political alienation

His turn against Company rule grew through specific experiences of administrative and economic dispossession.

### 1857 beyond North India

The Assam conspiracy expands the geographical meaning of the uprising without pretending it was identical to revolt in Delhi, Kanpur or Awadh.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Assam conspiracy. Maniram attempts to coordinate Ahom restoration and sepoy support during the wider uprising
- **1858-02-26**: Execution at Jorhat. Maniram Dewan and Piyali Barua are publicly hanged after the conspiracy is uncovered
