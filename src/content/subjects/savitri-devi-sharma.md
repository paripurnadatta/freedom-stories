## Research notes

Participated in anti-war protest, Quit India, imprisonment, and the 1946 Faridkot Praja Mandal agitation.

### Overview

Punjab freedom activist who faced imprisonment for anti-war protest, Quit India activity and later participated in the Faridkot Praja Mandal agitation.

### Chronology

- **1941-04-26**: Arrested for anti-war slogans; sentenced to rigorous imprisonment
- **1942-1944**: Imprisoned for Quit India activity; released before full sentence for health reasons
- **1946**: Participated in Faridkot Praja Mandal agitation

### Evidence claims

- Savitri Devi Sharma shifted her nationalist activity to Ludhiana after marriage. (HIGH confidence)
- She was arrested on 26 April 1941 for anti-war slogans and sentenced to two months' rigorous imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
- During Quit India she was arrested in Ludhiana and received a sentence of three years and six months, though she was released in 1944 on health grounds. (HIGH confidence)
- She participated in the Faridkot Praja Mandal agitation in 1946. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Ludhiana: Political activity and Quit India arrest
- Faridkot: Praja Mandal agitation
