## Research notes

Leader of nationalist politics in the United Provinces.

### Overview

Kumaon lawyer, Congress organizer and constitutional nationalist who moved from regional political organization into provincial and all-India leadership. He founded Kumaon Parishad, participated in anti-Simon, Civil Disobedience and Quit India campaigns, endured repeated imprisonment, and later led the United Provinces government before joining the Constituent Assembly.
