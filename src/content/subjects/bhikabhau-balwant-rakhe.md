## Research notes

Organized local resistance, distributed leaflets and worked underground in Marathwada and the Hyderabad State borderlands.

### Overview

Young activist in the Hyderabad State border and anti-Nizam movement whose political formation began during Quit India. Ministry records describe underground leaflet work, Maharashtra Parishad organization, flag actions, resistance to Nizam authorities and later armed-border activity.

### Evidence claims

- Bhikabhau Balwant Rakhe was born in 1925 and became politically active as a student under influence of Hyderabad State nationalist leaders. (high confidence)
