## Research notes

### Overview

Galo political organizer in the North-East Frontier Tracts who helped build late-colonial Congress activity among communities facing British movement, trade and porterage restrictions. Ministry research documents the 1946 Dipa conference, his role as General Secretary of the Dipa Congress Committee, village tours, distribution of Congress material and imprisonment at Pasighat.
