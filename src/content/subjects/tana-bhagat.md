## Research notes

Movement bridged tribal religious reform and anti-colonial noncooperation.

### Overview

Canonical dataset record representing Jatra Tana Bhagat/Jatra Oraon, initiator of the Tana Bhagat movement among Oraons in 1914. District and Census sources describe its agrarian and religious-reform genesis and its later convergence with Gandhian Non-Cooperation. The identity label must be handled carefully because 'Tana Bhagat' also denotes the wider community/movement.
