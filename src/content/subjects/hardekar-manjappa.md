## Research notes

Used Kannada journalism and social work to disseminate nationalist ideas in the Mysore region.

### Chronology

- **1906**: Started the nationalist weekly Dhanurdhari with his brother
- **1919**: Published a Kannada biography of Mahatma Gandhi
- **1924**: Helped organize the Congress session at Belgaum
- **1947-01-03**: Died

### Evidence claims

- Manjappa became active in Swadeshi-era nationalist work and, with his brother, launched the weekly Dhanurdhari in 1906. (HIGH confidence)
- He supported and propagated the Home Rule and Swaraj message in Karnataka. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1919 Manjappa wrote an early Kannada biography of Mahatma Gandhi that helped disseminate Gandhian ideas. (HIGH confidence)
- He played an organizational role in the 1924 Congress session at Belgaum. (HIGH confidence)
- Hardekar Manjappa died on 3 January 1947. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Banavasi: birthplace
- Belgaum: 1924 Congress session work
