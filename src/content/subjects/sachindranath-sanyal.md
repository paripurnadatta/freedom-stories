## Research notes

Revolutionary organizer and mentor to later HRA members.

### A revolutionary across generations

Sanyal links several chapters often treated separately: Anushilan, wartime conspiracy, transportation and HRA.

### From secret society to republican organization

His career shows a shift from regional secret-society structures toward broader North Indian revolutionary organization.

### Repeated transportation

Repeated imprisonment shaped both his political life and writing.

### Chronology

- **1907**: Anushilan Samiti. Begins organized revolutionary work
- **1915**: Military-revolt conspiracy. Participates in plans for an uprising
- **1924**: HRA founding. Helps establish a republican revolutionary organization
- **1920s**: Further imprisonment. Returns to imprisonment after HRA prosecution

### Evidence claims

- Joined Anushilan Samiti and later worked in its Varanasi network. (high confidence)
- Participated in the 1915 conspiracy to incite military revolt and was transported for life. (high confidence)
- Was a founder of the Hindustan Republican Association. (high confidence)
- Was again imprisoned/transported after the Kakori prosecution. (high confidence)
