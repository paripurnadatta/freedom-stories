## Research notes

Senior Congress leader and key participant in mass movements.

### Making independence explicit

Nehru’s role in the 1927 resolution belongs to the longer shift from self-government within empire to complete independence.

### Internationalism and anti-imperialism

His wartime arguments placed India’s freedom within a wider global crisis of empire.

### Ahmednagar Fort

Prison was not only repression but also a setting in which Nehru developed a broad historical interpretation of India.

### Chronology

- **1927-12**: Independence Resolution at Madras. Introduces an explicit demand for complete national independence
- **1940**: Critique of wartime constitutional policy. Rejects imperial proposals that preserve British control
- **1942-1945**: Ahmednagar Fort imprisonment. Long wartime imprisonment becomes a setting for historical and political reflection

- Introduced the Independence Resolution at the 1927 Madras Congress session calling for Poorna Swaraj. (high confidence)
