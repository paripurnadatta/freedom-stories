## Research notes

Official profile identifies him as a freedom fighter and initiator of Vaikom Satyagraha.

### From social reform to national politics

Madhavan's career demonstrates how the freedom movement and the struggle against caste exclusion became institutionally intertwined.

### Vaikom as a bridge

The campaign links SNDP activism, Congress, Gandhi and regional Kerala politics.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Meeting Gandhi. Madhavan takes Kerala's caste-access question directly to Gandhi
- **1923**: Kakinada Congress resolution. He pushes anti-untouchability into the Congress agenda with Nehru's support
- **1924**: Vaikom Satyagraha begins. The struggle over access to public roads around the Vaikom temple becomes a major nonviolent social-reform campaign
