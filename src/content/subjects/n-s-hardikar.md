## Research notes

Documented association with Hindustani Seva Dal in Karnataka.

### Overview

Physician-organizer who linked overseas Indian nationalist work with Karnataka Congress organization and the Hindustani Seva Dal.

### Chronology

- **1915-1920s**: Active in Indian nationalist organizational work in the United States
- **1921-1924**: Entered Karnataka Congress leadership and organized the Seva Dal
- **1920s-1940s**: Participated in Congress movements and volunteer organization

### Evidence claims

- N. S. Hardikar served in nationalist organizations in the United States, including the Indian Home Rule League of America, before returning to India. (HIGH confidence)
- He served as a Karnataka Provincial Congress Committee leader and became central to the Hindustani Seva Dal from the 1920s. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Congress movements and suffered imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Dharwad / Hubli: Congress and Seva Dal work
- United States: Home Rule and diaspora organizing
