## Research notes

Led Khasi resistance to British road-building and expansion.

### Documented record

Was a Khasi chief/Syiem associated with Nongkhlaw. Ordered the British to evacuate Nongkhlaw after disputes over the road project and territorial claims. Led guerrilla resistance during the Anglo-Khasi War for roughly four years. Captured in January 1833 and deported to Dhaka.

### Chronology

- **1829**: Conflict over British expansion
- **1829-1833**: Anglo-Khasi War
- **1833-01**: Capture and deportation
