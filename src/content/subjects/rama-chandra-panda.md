## Research notes

Participated in no-tax campaigns on the Balasore-Midnapore border and was detained in colonial prisons.

### Overview

Odisha-Bengal border activist who joined Civil Disobedience no-tax campaigns and was detained in Alipore and Hijli jails.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Participated in Civil Disobedience-era no-tax campaigns along the Balasore-Midnapore border
- **1930s**: Arrested and detained in Alipore and Hijli jails

### Evidence claims

- Rama Chandra Panda was born in June 1914 in Salikotha near Jaleswar in Balasore district and was raised in the neighboring Midnapore area. (HIGH confidence)
- He took part in no-tax agitation in the Balasore-Midnapore border villages during the Civil Disobedience Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested by Bengal police and detained in Alipore Central Jail and Hijli Central Jail. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Salikotha / Jaleswar / Balasore: Birthplace and local base
- Kunjpur / Midnapore: Upbringing and border agitation
- Alipore / Hijli: Colonial detention
