## Research notes

Led Garhwal Rifles soldiers who refused to fire on unarmed peaceful protesters at Peshawar in 1930.

### Documented record

Served in the British Indian Army and was associated with the Garhwal Rifles. Refused to fire on unarmed civilians during the Peshawar events of 1930. Was imprisoned for eleven years as a result. Was a member of the Arya Samaj and influenced by Gandhian thought.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Peshawar refusal to fire
- **1930s-1940s**: Long imprisonment
