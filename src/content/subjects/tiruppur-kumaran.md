## Research notes

Killed while protecting the national flag during a protest.

### A flag becomes a political act

Kumaran’s memory is rooted in the physical act of carrying the nationalist flag through police repression.

### Desa Bandhu Youth Association

Youth organization provides the local infrastructure behind the iconic final protest.

### Civil disobedience in the Tamil textile region

The profile should connect national Gandhian protest to the local political world of Tiruppur.

### Chronology

- **early 1930s**: Youth organization in Tiruppur. Organizes nationalist youth in a major textile region
- **1932-01**: Nationalist procession. Participates in protest during a period of renewed civil disobedience
- **1932-01**: Death after police beating. Dies after refusing to let the nationalist flag fall during repression

### Evidence claims

- Founded or organized the Desa Bandhu Youth Association for nationalist activity in the Tiruppur region. (high confidence)
- Joined protests associated with the Gandhian civil-disobedience atmosphere after Gandhi's arrest. (high confidence)
- Was fatally beaten by police during a procession in January 1932 while continuing to hold the nationalist flag. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Tiruppur Kumaran with Civil Disobedience Movement as participant in. (medium confidence)
