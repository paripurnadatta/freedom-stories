## Research notes

Led Gond resistance against forest and revenue policies and the Nizam state.

### Freedom from which state?

Komaram Bheem's struggle was directed primarily against the Nizam's Hyderabad State and local feudal/forest authority, not a straightforward British-Indian Congress campaign.

### Land, forest and memory

Resource access and Adivasi autonomy are essential to the profile; later 'Jal, Jangal, Jameen' commemoration should be sourced and phrased carefully.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Adivasi resistance in Hyderabad State. Bheem organizes resistance around land, forests, taxation and local authority in the Gond region
- **1930s-1940**: Jodeghat guerrilla phase. Jodeghat becomes a centre for the armed campaign against Nizam-state forces
- **1940**: Death in armed confrontation. Bheem is killed during the suppression of the resistance

### Evidence claims

- Government sources identify Komaram Bheem as a Gond leader from the Adilabad/Komaram Bheem region who resisted the Nizam's authority in the 1930s. (high confidence)
- The Telangana-textbook-based Ministry profile links the conflict to forest access, grazing/firewood exactions and pressure on Adivasi cultivation. (high confidence)
- Bheem organized armed Gond and Koya supporters and made Jodeghat a centre of guerrilla resistance. (high confidence)
- Government commemorative sources state that he was killed by armed forces in 1940. (high confidence)
- Official commemorative sources credit Bheem with the slogan 'Jal, Jangal, Jameen', which later became widely associated with Adivasi rights movements. (medium confidence)
