## Research notes

Congress leader active in mass campaigns.

### Chronology

- **1920-1921**: Participated in the Non-Cooperation Movement and left legal practice for public work
- **1934**: President of Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha
- **1937-07-31**: Became Speaker of the United Provinces Legislative Assembly
- **1946**: Entered the Constituent Assembly
- **1961**: Awarded the Bharat Ratna

### Evidence claims

- Purushottam Das Tandon was born on 1 August 1882 and died on 1 July 1962. (HIGH confidence)
- Tandon gave up legal practice and became active in the Non-Cooperation Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was active in agrarian organization and served as president of the Bihar Provincial Kisan Sabha in 1934. (HIGH confidence)
- He served as Speaker of the United Provinces/Uttar Pradesh Legislative Assembly from 1937 and again from 1946 to 1950. (HIGH confidence)
- He became a member of the Constituent Assembly in 1946. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Prayag (Allahabad): birthplace and principal political base
