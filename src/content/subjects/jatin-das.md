## Research notes

Died after a prolonged hunger strike in jail.

### The prison as a battlefield

Das’s profile shifts attention from spectacular revolutionary actions to the politics of incarceration and bodily protest.

### Sixty-three days

The hunger strike brought the treatment of political prisoners into national public debate.

### Bhagat Singh, Batukeshwar Dutt and a national funeral

The prison strike connects existing enriched profiles to Subhas Bose through a different kind of political mobilization.

### Chronology

- **1929-06-14**: Arrest in the Lahore conspiracy context. Taken into custody before the political-prisoner protest escalates
- **1929-07-13**: Hunger strike. Joins the strike over prison conditions and political-prisoner status
- **1929-09-13**: Death after 63 days. His death transforms a prison protest into a national political event

### Evidence claims

- Joined the Lahore Jail hunger strike with Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt in July 1929 over the treatment of political prisoners. (high confidence)
- Died on 13 September 1929 after a 63-day hunger strike. (high confidence)
- His funeral became a major nationalist event and was associated with Subhas Chandra Bose's public leadership in Bengal. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Jatin Das with Bhagat Singh as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Jatin Das with Batukeshwar Dutt as worked with. (high confidence)
