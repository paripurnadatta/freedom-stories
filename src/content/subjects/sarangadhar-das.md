Documented association with Praja Mandal in Odisha.

### Historical geography

Dhenkanal State: Princely state in Odisha (Core Praja Mandal theatre)

### Historical geography

Odisha princely states: Garhjat states (Wider States' Peoples' Movement network)

### Overview

Foreign-educated agricultural scientist turned political organiser, Sarangadhar Das became a major figure in the Orissa States' Peoples' Movement and a key advocate for subjects of the princely states. In Dhenkanal he helped give the Praja Mandal movement organisational and socialist depth, linking anti-feudal demands with peasant politics. His profile should emphasize the princely-state question, tenant grievances and democratic rights rather than treating Praja Mandal activity as simply another Congress mass campaign.

### Chronology

- **1938**: Praja Mandal agitation in Dhenkanal intensified; Sarangadhar Das emerged as an important all-Odisha supporter and organiser
- **1940s**: Continued socialist and peasant-oriented political work tied to princely-state democratization and integration

### Evidence claims

- Sarangadhar Das was active in the States' Peoples' Movement and closely associated with Congress Socialist currents. (HIGH confidence)
- He played a leading role in revitalising the All Orissa States' Peoples' Movement and in the Dhenkanal Praja Mandal struggle. (HIGH confidence)
- His politics focused strongly on peasants, tenants and the subjects of Odisha's princely states. (HIGH confidence)
