## Research notes

Pioneer of organized textile labour activism.

### Labour politics inside the freedom-era public sphere

Sarabhai's profile broadens the project beyond Congress campaigns by showing workers building bargaining institutions.

### A woman leading across class lines

Her position within an industrial family did not prevent her from confronting mill owners, including interests close to her own family.

### Chronology

- **1914**: Early textile-worker organizing. Sarabhai becomes a leader in Ahmedabad labour protest
- **1918**: Weavers' wage dispute. She organizes mass worker meetings during a major textile strike in which Gandhi also intervenes
- **1920-02-25**: Textile Labour Association formally established. The labour movement becomes a durable organization rather than a sequence of temporary strikes

### Evidence claims

- Anasuya Sarabhai returned to Ahmedabad after study in England and became involved in improving conditions for women and mill workers. (high confidence)
- She organized workers in the 1914 and 1918 Ahmedabad textile disputes. (high confidence)
- During the 1918 strike, Gandhi undertook a fast while Sarabhai organized daily mass meetings of workers. (high confidence)
- The Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association/Majoor Mahajan Sangh was formally established in 1920 with Sarabhai playing a central founding role. (high confidence)
