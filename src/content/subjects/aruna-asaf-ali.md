## Research notes

Raised the flag at Gowalia Tank and operated underground during Quit India.

### Documented record

Born 16 July 1909 in Kalka. Joined the Indian National Congress and participated in Salt Satyagraha processions, leading to arrest.

### Documented actions

Hoisted the national flag at Gowalia Tank Maidan during Quit India in 1942. Continued underground rather than surrendering after the 1942 action.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Salt Satyagraha participation
- **1942-08**: Gowalia Tank flag-hoisting
- **1942-1946**: Underground political activity
