## Research notes

Documented association with Ho resistance in Jharkhand.

### Overview

Ho Adivasi leader from the Kolhan region associated with the 1836-37 resistance to direct British rule and the Battle of Serengsia. Recent scholarship identifies Poto Ho as a principal organizer from Rajabasa village. Jharkhand's state commemoration of the Serengsia martyrs corroborates the enduring regional memory of the uprising.
