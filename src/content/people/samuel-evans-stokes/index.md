## Research notes

American-born supporter who became an Indian citizen and nationalist worker.

### Chronology

- **1904**: Arrived in India
- **1920-1921**: Joined Congress and supported the Non-Cooperation Movement
- **1921**: Jailed by the British for nationalist activity

### Evidence claims

- Samuel Evans Stokes, born in Philadelphia in 1882, later adopted the name Satyanand Stokes. (HIGH confidence)
- Stokes came to India in 1904 and settled into social-service work in the Simla hills. (HIGH confidence)
- He campaigned against begar, or forced labour, in the Simla hills. (HIGH confidence)
- Stokes joined the Indian National Congress and supported Gandhi's Non-Cooperation Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was jailed by the British in 1921 for his nationalist activity and support of Non-Cooperation. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Simla Hills: social-service, anti-begar and nationalist work
