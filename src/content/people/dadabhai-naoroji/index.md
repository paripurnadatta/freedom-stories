## Research notes

Early nationalist and key Congress leader.

### Counting the cost of empire

Naoroji’s importance lies in turning economic data and public argument into a nationalist language of colonial extraction.

### The drain as political argument

The drain theory connected poverty in India to the structure of imperial rule rather than to isolated administrative failures.

### London and Congress politics

His career linked Indian associations in Britain with the emerging institutional politics of the Indian National Congress.

### Chronology

- **1867**: Drain theory at the East India Association. Frames colonial extraction as a systemic economic problem
- **1886-1906**: Congress leadership. Repeatedly occupies senior leadership in the developing national movement
- **late 19th century**: Economic nationalism. Develops the drain argument into a sustained indictment of colonial rule

### Evidence claims

- Presented an early formulation of the drain theory to the East India Association in London in 1867. (high confidence)
- The East India Association became an important platform for his critique of colonial economic relations. (high confidence)
- Served multiple terms as president of the Indian National Congress and helped make economic critique part of nationalist politics. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Dadabhai Naoroji with Indian National Congress as office holder in. (high confidence)
