How does a boy who loses his father at four or five years old end up a professor and a member of the British Parliament? The first answer is a school that cost nothing.

## Born in Bombay, or Navsari?

Dadabhai Naoroji was born on 4 September 1825. His family were Parsis, followers of the Zoroastrian faith. His father was Naoroji Palanji Dordi. His mother was Manekbai.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: Dinyar Patel's timeline says Dadabhai was born in Khadak, in Bombay. Wikipedia says he was born in Navsari. We cannot tell which is right, so we do not choose.</aside>

His father died while he was still very young. The sources give the age as about four or five. His mother brought him up. His mother put him in a free public school.

## The scholarship

Patel's timeline says that Dadabhai entered Elphinstone College on 1 May 1840. He went on a scholarship. He finished in 1845.

Janata Weekly quotes words that he said about this time. He said: "I realised that I had been educated at the expense of the poor, to whom I myself belong." It adds a second line. He said that he must devote himself to the service of the people.

We do not know when he first said these words. But they tell us how he thought about his own education. It was a loan from people who had little, and he felt he had to pay it back.

## A trip that did not happen

In 1845 an offer came to send him to England. Manu Pillai says that the sponsor blocked the trip. The sponsor feared that he might become a Christian there. So the young graduate stayed in Bombay. He would reach England much later, on his own account.

## "The Promise of India"

Manu Pillai writes that he was known as "the Promise of India". It is a large nickname for a young man.

## Married as a child

It is hard for us to read this part. Patel's family page says that he was married to a girl called Gulbai when he was eleven or twelve years old. She was seven. This was the custom among many families in those days. It does not mean that it was right.

They stayed married for most of his life. Patel's timeline says Gulbai died on 15 May 1909, when Dadabhai was in his eighties.

## What the young man did next

He went on to become a professor. The sources differ on the date. Patel's timeline says 1854. Wikipedia says December 1855.

## Why it matters

Free schools, scholarships and small acts of help are easy to forget. Dadabhai did not forget them. By his own words, he owed a debt to the poor, and he spent a long life working for the people. Look at the other stories about him. You will see a man who thought in that way again and again.
