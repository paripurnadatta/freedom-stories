## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Meerut uprising of 1857.

### Overview

Rao Kadam Singh of Bahsuma/Parikshitgarh, a rural leader of the 1857 uprising in eastern Meerut whose support came from local Gurjar communities.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Led rural anti-Company resistance in eastern Meerut
- **1857**: Associated with Bahsuma/Parikshitgarh leadership during the uprising

### Evidence claims

- Rao Kadam Singh was a local leader from Bahsuma in the Meerut region during the 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
- The peer-reviewed study places him within the rural rebellion of eastern Meerut and the Parikshitgarh/Bahsuma political landscape. (HIGH confidence)
- The study records that British forces eventually captured and executed him. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bahsuma / Mawana: Rural resistance base
- Parikshitgarh: Regional leadership context
