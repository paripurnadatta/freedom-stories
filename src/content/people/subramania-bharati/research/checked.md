# Subramania Bharati: what was checked

A research dossier from another AI is in this folder (report-from-other-ai.pdf). Every story was written fresh from sources opened in this session.

## Sources opened
PIB feature 2016; PIB Vice-President's speech 2021; Wikipedia (Subramania Bharati; V. O. Chidambaram Pillai); Puducherry museum page; TamilNation (biography and the 1918 petition); Tamil Wiki (India magazine); Madras Musings; The Wire; two DT Next articles; Sujata Nahar's chapter (an excerpt only); a storytelling site (used only as a weak source, nothing taken as fact).

## Not opened or blocked
The Telegraph (Venkatachalapathy on Nivedita): blocked. The MIDS archive PDF of the Hindu article (VOC visit): unreadable. Britannica. The 1922 foreword by Chellamma (TamilNation, Tamil text). Commons file pages.

## Held up
Birth 11 Dec 1882, marriage June 1897, Banaras, Swadesamitran Nov 1904, India weekly (cartoons, motto), 1908 move to Pondicherry, 1909 ban, 20 Nov to 14 Dec 1918 jail, petition of 28 Nov 1918, Gandhi 1919, elephant and death in 1921.

## Sources disagree (marked in the stories)
Start of India (May 1906 vs April 1907) and who was editor; length of exile (ten vs 11+ years); years in Banaras; where he met Nivedita (Calcutta 1906 / Benares / "later"); elephant details (fruit vs coconut); funeral numbers (12 vs 14); death date (11 vs 12 September); Central Prison vs District Jail.

## Left out
The red-paper detail is told only as the PIB's claim. The haystack smuggling and escape-by-boat stories come from a weak site and are mentioned as unsupported. VOC and Bharati's friendship (source unreadable). The letter to Tilak (text not seen). The Telegraph's account of three dedications to Nivedita.
