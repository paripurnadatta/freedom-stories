How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Tell Claude, or run:  node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. "The photograph he asked them
to destroy" uses the real Jhansi photograph instead. See ILLUSTRATIONS.md.

About his face: a real photograph of Azad exists (the moustache picture taken
in Jhansi), so his face may be shown. Keep it close to that photograph.
Nehru's face is kept turned away: the story is Azad's.

Under a big leafy jamun tree, two young Indian men sit on the grass talking quietly: one is a strong-built young Indian man with a broad face, a thick upturned moustache, short hair, wearing a plain white dhoti and a simple kurta, aged 24; the other is younger and slimmer, in a kurta.
