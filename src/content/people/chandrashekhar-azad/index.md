## Research notes

Major HRA/HSRA organizer who died in a gunfight in 1931.

### From satyagrahi teenager to underground commander

Azad’s biography reveals the porous boundary between mass nationalist politics and the revolutionary underground.

### Keeping the organization alive

His importance lay partly in preserving networks after arrests and executions weakened the movement.

### Alfred Park

The death should remain anchored in documented underground activity rather than only later mythology.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Non-Cooperation arrest. Participates in the movement as a teenager
- **1920s**: HRA underground. Moves from mass protest into armed revolutionary organization
- **1928-1931**: Revolutionary command. Becomes a major HRA/HSRA organizer
- **1931-02-27**: Death at Alfred Park. Dies during an armed encounter

### Evidence claims

- Born on 23 July 1906 in Alirajpur. (high confidence)
- Participated in the Non-Cooperation Movement as a teenager and adopted the name Azad after arrest. (high confidence)
- Joined the HRA after the withdrawal of Non-Cooperation and became a major underground organizer. (high confidence)
- Died at Alfred Park on 27 February 1931 while avoiding capture. (high confidence)

### Checked in October 2026 (for the stories)

- 1921 court answers (Azad / Swatantrata / Jail) and 15 lashes: Wikipedia (citing Rana 2005), Drishti, The Quint. Some tellings give "Azadi" and 50 lashes; marked in the story.
- Birthplace disputed: Bhabhra (most sources) vs Badarka, Unnao (local claim; Commons photo).
- Kakori: 9 Aug 1925, No. 8 Down, Lahiri pulled the chain, about Rs 8,000, Ahmad Ali killed by accident; arrests 26 Sep 1925; Azad never arrested (Wikipedia, Kakori conspiracy).
- Jhansi: Master Rudra Narayan sheltered him; hut by Hanuman temple on the Satar near Orchha; alias Pandit Harishankar Brahmachari; taught children; learned to drive (Wikipedia, Oneindia, Navbharat Live).
- Photo: taken by Rudra Narayan; Azad sent word via Vaishampayan to destroy it; the family kept it (Navbharat Live only; would like a second source).
- Nehru meeting: Nehru, An Autobiography (1936), pp. 261-262, quoted in DailyO. Only use Nehru's own words; DailyO's other claims are advocacy.
- Alfred Park: Nott-Bower tipped off; Sukhdev Raj escaped; Nott-Bower hit in the wrist, Thakur Vishweshwar Singh in the jaw (Wikipedia); cremated at Rasulabad Ghat; Kamala Nehru at the funeral (Gaur, via DailyO). The Tribune says he killed three policemen: not used, not confirmed elsewhere.
- Pistol: Colt Model 1903 Pocket Hammerless .32 (Wikipedia, citing Hindustan Times 2016); name "Bamtul Bukhara" (Madras Courier). The "taken to England and returned" story: NOT confirmed; flagged in the story.
- Not confirmed: that the British cut down the tree (told as "many accounts say").
