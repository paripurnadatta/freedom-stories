## Research notes

Revolutionary who assassinated Collector Robert Ashe.

### Overview

Tamil revolutionary associated with the Bharatha Matha Sangam who assassinated Collector Robert Ashe at Maniyachi in 1911 and died immediately afterwards.

### Chronology

- **1910-1911**: Active in the Bharatha Matha Sangam revolutionary network
- **1911-06-17**: Assassinated Robert Ashe at Maniyachi and died immediately afterwards

### Evidence claims

- Vanchinathan, also called Vanchinatha Iyer/Sankara Iyer in sources, participated in revolutionary anti-colonial activity in Tirunelveli. (HIGH confidence)
- On 17 June 1911 he shot Tirunelveli Collector Robert Ashe at Maniyachi railway station. (HIGH confidence)
- Vanchinathan died by suicide immediately after the assassination. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry account connects him with the Bharatha Matha Sangam/Association revolutionary network. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Shenkottai / Tirunelveli: Home and revolutionary network
- Maniyachi: Ashe assassination and death
