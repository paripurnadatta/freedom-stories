## Research notes

Active organizer during nationalist campaigns.

### Overview

Congress activist and organizer in Allahabad whose public role expanded sharply during Civil Disobedience. Archival sources record her active national-movement participation, temporary membership in the Congress Working Committee and arrest in January 1931.
