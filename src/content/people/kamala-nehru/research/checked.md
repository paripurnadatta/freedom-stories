# Kamala Nehru: source check (Claude, 22 October 2026)

Five stories were added to the first flagship, all written from sources Claude opened this session: Wikipedia (Kamala Nehru; An Autobiography), The Nehru Archive (person page; letter to his mother 20 Jan 1936; statement of 17 March 1936), The Quint fact-check, The Wire and National Herald (both by Praveen Davar), and the first chapters of Nehru's *An Autobiography* (BITS Pilani repository copy; only the front pages and Chapter VI were readable).

**Held up (two or more sources):** born 1 Aug 1899, Delhi; married 8 Feb 1916 on Vasant Panchami; Indira born 1917; tuberculosis from the mid-1920s; Europe for treatment; arrest 1 Jan 1931; death 28 Feb 1936 in Lausanne; Gandhi's hospital foundation stone, Nov 1939.

**Conflicts shown in the stories:** start of the illness (end 1924 vs c.1925); release from jail in 1931 (end of January vs after two months); Nehru's release in 1935 (4 Sept, Sept, Oct); who took her to Badenweiler (Wikipedia says Bose; Quint says only "May 1935"); who stood beside her at her death (Wikipedia: daughter and mother-in-law; Moraes via Quint: Nehru, Indira and a doctor).

**Single source, labelled:** sale of her jewellery (Wire); ashes by his bedside and Bose at the cremation (Wire); the "almost overlooked her" remark (Wikipedia citing a 1964 newspaper); Swarup Rani's head injury (the two Davar pieces, one author); Bose's letter (quoted by the Wire).

**Could not check:** Frank Moraes's biography (read only through The Quint); Nehru's autobiography passages on the 1930s (the repository text did not reach them); Bose's letter; jail records; any date for Swarup Rani's injury.

**Wikipedia error not used:** the Kamala Nehru page calls Sarojini Naidu "Kamala's mother". That is wrong on its face (Kamala's mother was Rajpati Kaul, per Wikipedia itself, The Wire and the Herald). It is left out of the stories. Worth fixing in the old flagship if it ever repeats it (it does not).
