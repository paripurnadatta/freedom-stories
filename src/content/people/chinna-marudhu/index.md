## Research notes

Co-led resistance against Company authority.

### Overview

The younger of the Marudhu brothers and minister in Sivaganga, Chinna Marudhu became a central leader of resistance to Company power in southern Tamil country. He sheltered Oomadurai after Kattabomman's execution, issued the Jambudweepa proclamation calling diverse communities to resist the English, and fought in the final Sivaganga campaign of 1801 before execution with his brother.
