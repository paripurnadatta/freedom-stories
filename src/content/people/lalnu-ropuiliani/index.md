## Research notes

### Overview

Mizo woman chief remembered for refusing British political supremacy during the colonial conquest of the Lushai Hills. Ministry sources describe her leadership at Denlung, arrest and imprisonment in 1893, transfer from Lunglei to Rangamati, and death in detention in January 1894.

### Chronology

- **1893**: Arrested and imprisoned by British authorities.. Arrested and imprisoned by British authorities
- **1894-01**: Died in detention at Rangamati.. Died in detention at Rangamati

### Evidence claims

- After the death of her husband Vandula, Ropuiliani became a woman chief in the area now known as Mizoram. (high confidence)
- She refused to recognize British colonial rule and was imprisoned in 1893. (high confidence)
- She died in detention at Rangamati in January 1894. (high confidence)
