## Research notes

Commander of the Rani of Jhansi Regiment.

### A freedom struggle fought through medicine, politics and military organization

Lakshmi Sahgal’s wartime career joined professional medical work, diaspora politics, the Indian Independence League, the Indian National Army and the Azad Hind government.

### The Rani of Jhansi Regiment

Her most distinctive role was the command of the INA’s all-women Rani of Jhansi Regiment, a military and political experiment intended to make women visible as active participants in armed anti-colonial struggle.

### Inside the Azad Hind state project

Sahgal’s appointment as Minister for Women’s Affairs placed her not only within the INA’s military structure but also within the Provisional Government of Free India.

### The archival afterlife of Captain Lakshmi

Her surviving papers provide an unusual bridge between the INA era and post-independence political and social activism, making her a strong candidate for a future archival-document exhibit.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Medical work in wartime Singapore. Provides medical aid during the Japanese occupation of Singapore
- **1943**: Joins Bose’s reorganized independence movement. Meets Subhas Chandra Bose in Singapore and enters the INA/IIL political-military structure
- **1943**: Rani of Jhansi Regiment. Takes command of the all-women regiment of the INA
- **1945**: Arrest in Burma. Captured during the INA retreat and detained before repatriation to India
- **2012-07-23**: Death. Dies in 2012 after a long post-independence life of medical and political work
