## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Achraj Singh's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Subedar in the Ramgarh Battalion who joined the 1857 uprising and was killed during fighting at Chatra.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Joined the uprising with the Ramgarh Battalion
- **1857**: Killed during fighting at Chatra

### Evidence claims

- Achraj Singh served as a Subedar in the Ramgarh Battalion. (HIGH confidence)
- He took part in resistance against the East India Company during the 1857 uprising at Chatra. (HIGH confidence)
- The ICHR entry records that he was killed during the fighting at Chatra. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Chatra: 1857 battle and death
