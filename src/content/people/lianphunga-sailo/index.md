## Research notes

Remembered by the Government of Mizoram as an early freedom fighter who fought British invasion of the Lushai Hills and sacrificed his life.

### Overview

Mizo chief's son and resistance figure remembered for opposing British intrusion into the Lushai Hills during the early colonial conquest period.

### Chronology

- **1890-1891**: Active in Lushai resistance against expanding British control
- **1891-01-13**: Historical journal records Lianphunga being sent under escort with other chiefs for detention/deportation
- **1891**: Died while in confinement; the exact circumstances should remain conservative

### Evidence claims

- Lianphunga Sailo, son of Sailo chief Suakpuilala, is officially commemorated in Mizoram as an early freedom fighter who resisted British invasion of the Lushai Hills. (HIGH confidence)
- Historical research places Lianphunga among chiefs active in resistance during the British consolidation of the Lushai Hills around 1890-91. (HIGH confidence)
- Lianphunga was deported with other resistance leaders in January 1891 and later died in confinement. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Lushai Hills: Resistance to British intrusion
- Tezpur / Assam detention route: Detention/deportation context
