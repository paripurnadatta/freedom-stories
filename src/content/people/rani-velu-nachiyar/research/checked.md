- Married the Sivaganga ruler (Wikipedia: 1746, at 16): Wikipedia, News Minute.
- June 1772 attack, 21 June occupation, 25 June death of Muthu Vaduganatha: district history and Wikipedia (district).
- Flight to Virupakshi with Vellachi and Tandavaraya Pillai: district history.

- Memorial of July 2014: News Minute and The Hindu. Stamp of 31 December 2008: PIB.

## Corrections to the report

- The report suggests that Hyder Ali's government allowed the return. The district history says it was "the Nawab" (not named) who ordered the return, "frustrated by the joining of forces against him". Hyder Ali is named only as her protector at Virupakshi. The story follows the district history.
- The report says the textbook names "Commandant Syed" of Dindigul. The textbook passage we read did not contain this. Left out.

## Disagreements, shown in the stories as "Two versions" notes

- **Who protected her**: Hyder Ali (district history, PIB) or Gopala Nayakkar (district "eminent people" page). PIB gives both.
- **The letter**: five thousand horsemen (Wikipedia) or 5,000 infantry and 5,000 cavalry (textbook). The letter itself was not seen.
- **How Muthu Vaduganatha died**: in a battle against Benjour's force (district history), when the palace was stormed (textbook), or at Kalaiyar Kovil with a garbled second place (Wikipedia). PIB adds that a second wife was killed.
- **The return**: by battle (PIB, News Minute, textbook, Wikipedia) or by an agreement with the Nawab with a kist (district history).
- **When Sivaganga was retaken**: 1780 (district pages, Wikipedia on Velu Nachiyar) or 1789 (Wikipedia's page on the Marudhu brothers).
- **Death date**: 25 December 1796 (district "eminent people" page, Wikipedia, News Minute) or about 1790, date not known (district history). Ruled for a decade (PIB, Wikipedia) or more than ten years (News Minute).
- **Mother's name**: Sakandhimuthal, Sakandhimuthathal, Muthathal Nachiyar.
- **Marudu brothers**: Kongulu Street, Ramnad (district) or Mukkulam near Narikudi, 1748 and 1753 (Wikipedia).
- **Udaiyaal**: a shepherd girl killed for not giving information (textbook), the queen's adopted daughter (Wikipedia, district), another adopted daughter who died fighting (News Minute).
- **"First queen"**: PIB, News Minute and Wikipedia say so. "First Indian ruler" cannot be right, because Hyder Ali fought the Company in 1767-69 (Wikipedia).
