## Research notes

Local leader connected with peasant grievances in Champaran.

### Before Gandhi came to Champaran

The profile should make clear that local peasant resistance had its own leaders and history before 1917.

### A deliberately restrained biography

The package securely supports Gulab's role in the agrarian movement, but not a richly documented full-life narrative.

### Chronology

- **1907**: Champaran indigo resistance. Sheikh Gulab and other local leaders organize opposition to coercive indigo cultivation before Gandhi's arrival in the district
- **1917**: Champaran enters national politics. Gandhi's arrival transforms an already-existing agrarian conflict into a nationally visible satyagraha

### Evidence claims

- Press Information Bureau material records that Sheikh Gulab and Sital Rai raised their voices against indigo cultivation in Champaran in 1907. (high confidence)
- Educational material identifies Sheikh Gulab among local agitators who resisted the panchkathiya system imposed on indigo cultivators. (high confidence)
- The pre-1917 agitation forms an important local precursor to the Champaran movement later associated with Gandhi. (high confidence)
