## Research notes

### Overview

Assamese Gandhian, social worker and political organizer associated with Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience and Quit India. Assam government and Ministry sources identify him as a leading regional activist and place him, with Pushpalata Das, in organizing women and satyagrahi formations in Darrang during 1942.

### Evidence claims

- Omeo Kumar Das was an Assamese Gandhian freedom fighter, educationist and social worker. (high confidence)
- He belonged to the generation of young Assamese leaders who joined Non-Cooperation by boycotting government institutions. (high confidence)
- During Quit India, he and Pushpalata Das helped organize women and satyagrahi formations in Darrang. (high confidence)
