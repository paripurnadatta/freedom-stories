## Research notes

### Overview

Ruler of Amjhera who joined the 1857 uprising in Central India, seized Dhar fort, gathered regional supporters and later moved toward alliance with Feroze Shah's forces. Ministry sources record his capture through betrayal and execution in February 1858.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Led Amjhera resistance and seizure of Dhar fort.. Led Amjhera resistance and seizure of Dhar fort
- **1858-02-10**: Executed by British authorities.. Executed by British authorities

### Evidence claims

- Bakhtawar Singh was ruler of Amjhera and took an active part in the 1857 uprising. (high confidence)
- His forces captured Dhar fort and resisted a British siege in October 1857. (high confidence)
- After leaving Dhar, rebel forces moved toward Mandsaur to join Feroze Shah. (high confidence)
- Bakhtawar Singh was captured after betrayal and executed on 10 February 1858. (high confidence)
