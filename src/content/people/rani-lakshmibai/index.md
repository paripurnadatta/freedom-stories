## Research notes

Led forces in the 1857 uprising and died in combat.

### A succession dispute becomes a war of sovereignty

The profile should begin with Jhansi’s political structure and annexation rather than later legend.

### Defending Jhansi

The defense involved formal military resistance and broader mobilization.

### From queen to symbol

Her later symbolic afterlife should remain distinct from the narrower documentary record of the campaign.

### Chronology

- **1853-1854**: Doctrine of Lapse crisis. British annexation policy turns succession into a sovereignty conflict
- **1857**: Jhansi enters the rebellion. Lakshmibai organizes resistance against Company power
- **1858**: Campaign beyond Jhansi. The struggle expands into Central India alongside allied rebel commanders

### Evidence claims

- Was born Manikarnika Tambe and became queen of Jhansi through marriage to Gangadhar Rao. (high confidence)
- Jhansi became a target of British annexation under the Doctrine of Lapse after a succession dispute. (high confidence)
- Mobilized resistance in defense of Jhansi during the uprising of 1857. (high confidence)
- Worked in military alliance with Tantia Tope during the wider Central India campaign. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Rani Lakshmibai with Revolt of 1857 as participant in. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Rani Lakshmibai with Tantia Tope as worked with. (high confidence)
