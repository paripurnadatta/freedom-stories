## Research notes

Documented association with Birsa movement in Jharkhand.

### Evidence claims

- Maki Munda, wife of Gaya Munda, participated in the Munda Ulgulan and the confrontation with colonial police at Etkedih in January 1900. (HIGH confidence)
- The academic account states that she received two years’ rigorous imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
