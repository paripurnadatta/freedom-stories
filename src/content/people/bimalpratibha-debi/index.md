## Research notes

Official profile documents Salt Satyagraha, processions, revolutionary support and long detention.

### Overview

Bengal-based nationalist organizer who moved between Congress, women's organizations and revolutionary-support networks and endured repeated imprisonment.

### Chronology

- **1918**: Began sustained political activity and joined Nari Karmamandir
- **1927**: Became president of the Nikhil Bharat Naojoan Sabha
- **1930-06**: Imprisoned for six months after leading a prohibited procession in Calcutta
- **1941-1945**: Imprisoned in Presidency Jail following another arrest

### Evidence claims

- Bimalpratibha Debi entered political activity from 1918 and joined the non-violent movement through Nari Karmamandir. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1927 she became president of the Nikhil Bharat Naojoan Sabha and later participated in the Salt Satyagraha as a Congress worker. (HIGH confidence)
- In June 1930 she was imprisoned for six months for leading a prohibited procession in Calcutta. (HIGH confidence)
- After further revolutionary-support activity she spent years in detention, and was again imprisoned from 1941 until 1945. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Cuttack: Birthplace
- Calcutta: Political organizing, Salt Satyagraha and imprisonment
