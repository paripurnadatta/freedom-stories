## Research notes

### Overview

Ruler of Hindoria in Damoh district who led a regional rising during the 1857 rebellion. Ministry records state that his forces attacked and temporarily captured Damoh in July 1857 before British counter-operations forced him into the forests.

### Chronology

- **1857-07-10**: Attacked and captured Damoh during the regional up.... Attacked and captured Damoh during the regional uprising
- **1857-07-25**: British counterattack forced withdrawal into fores.... British counterattack forced withdrawal into forested terrain

### Evidence claims

- Raja Kishor Singh of Hindoria joined the 1857 uprising in the Damoh region. (high confidence)
- His forces attacked the British at Damoh and temporarily captured the town on 10 July 1857. (high confidence)
- After British counterattack he evaded capture by retreating into forested terrain and later died from injuries. (medium_high confidence)
