## Research notes

Documented association with Jaipur Praja Mandal in Rajasthan.

### Overview

Rural reconstruction worker, Jaipur Praja Mandal leader and later first Chief Minister of Rajasthan. Archival summaries of his papers document his transition from Jaipur State service to constructive work, leadership of Jaipur Rajya Praja Mandal, people's satyagraha and All India States' Peoples' Conference activity.
