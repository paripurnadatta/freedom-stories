## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Devichand Sagarmal's connection with Praja Mandal; anti-Cripps protest.

### Overview

Sirohi Praja Mandal organizer, journalist and social worker whose anti-colonial career crossed Rajasthan, Bombay and Madras. He worked with Jai Narayan Vyas on Agniban, opposed the Cripps Mission through a banned publication and endured repeated imprisonment.

### Evidence claims

- Devichand Sagarmal served as a minister in the Sirohi State Praja Mandal and worked on the newspaper Agniban with Jai Narayan Vyas. (high confidence)
- He published a magazine protesting the Cripps Mission; it was banned and he was jailed. (high confidence)
- His work combined political organization, village development and social causes including women. (high confidence)
