## Research notes

Assertive nationalist and Home Rule leader.

### Making Swaraj a popular demand

Tilak pushed self-government from constitutional aspiration toward a more openly mobilizing demand.

### Home Rule and responsible government

His Home Rule politics should be distinguished from earlier moderate constitutionalism and later Gandhian non-cooperation.

### Before Gandhi

The profile should show continuities and differences between Tilak’s politics and later Gandhian mass mobilization.

### Chronology

- **1905-1908**: Swadeshi-era assertive nationalism. Becomes a leading advocate of more assertive nationalist politics
- **1916-1918**: Home Rule. Places self-government at the center of political mobilization
- **1917**: The National Demand. Articulates a forceful demand for responsible government
