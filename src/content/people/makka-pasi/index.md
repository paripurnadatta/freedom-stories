## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Makka Pasi's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Dalit soldier associated with Awadh's 1857 resistance who fought in the Battle of Chinhat and is remembered alongside his wife, Uda Devi.

### Chronology

- **1857-06-30**: Fought in the Battle of Chinhat
- **1857**: Died during the 1857 resistance according to the Ministry profile

### Evidence claims

- Makka Pasi is described by the Ministry as a Dalit soldier associated with the Awadh resistance of 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- He fought in the Battle of Chinhat on 30 June 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry profile identifies Uda Devi as his wife and fellow participant in the 1857 resistance. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Lucknow / Awadh: Military resistance
- Chinhat: Battle of Chinhat
