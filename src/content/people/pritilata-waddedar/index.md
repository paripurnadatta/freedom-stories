## Research notes

Participated in the Pahartali European Club attack and died by suicide to avoid capture.

### Documented record

Born 5 May 1911 in the Chittagong region. Associated with Deepali Sangha during her student years.

### Documented actions

Worked under Surya Sen in the Chittagong revolutionary network. Led the Pahartali European Club attack on 24 September 1932 and died after taking cyanide when capture appeared imminent.

### Chronology

- **1930-1932**: Revolutionary organization
- **1932-09-24**: Pahartali European Club attack
- **1932-09-24**: Death
