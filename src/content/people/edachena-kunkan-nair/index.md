## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Pazhassi resistance in Malabar.

### Overview

Commander in Kerala Varma Pazhassi Raja's forces and an important organizer of anti-Company resistance in Wayanad. Government census and local-government histories place Edachena Kunkan alongside Thalakkal Chandu in the attack on the Panamaram British outpost in October 1802 and in the wider guerrilla struggle in Wayanad.
