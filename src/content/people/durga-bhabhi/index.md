## Research notes

Revolutionary associate who helped Bhagat Singh evade arrest.

### The infrastructure of revolution

Durga Bhabhi’s history shows that revolutionary action depended on mobility, disguise, finance and support networks as much as on spectacular attacks.

### The Lahore escape

Her role exploited colonial gender expectations to move a wanted revolutionary through public space.

### A revolutionary in her own right

Her organizational membership should remain visible rather than reducing her to a supporting role defined by male revolutionaries.

### Chronology

- **1928**: Escape from Lahore. Helps move Bhagat Singh out of Lahore after the Saunders action
- **1929-1931**: Support for imprisoned revolutionaries. Contributes to the support network surrounding jailed revolutionaries

### Evidence claims

- Was an active member of Naujawan Bharat Sabha. (high confidence)
- Helped Bhagat Singh escape Lahore after the Saunders action by posing as his companion while Rajguru travelled with them. (high confidence)
- Supported imprisoned revolutionaries and campaigns around their cases. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Durga Bhabhi with Naujawan Bharat Sabha as member of. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Durga Bhabhi with Bhagat Singh as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Durga Bhabhi with Shivaram Rajguru as worked with. (high confidence)
