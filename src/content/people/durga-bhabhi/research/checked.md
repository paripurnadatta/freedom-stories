## Two versions and unresolved claims

- **School date:** two Times of India articles say 1940; Maclean says she set it up after independence. Neither original 1940 register nor foundation deed was inspected. The story distinguishes the versions.
- **Saunders date:** Maclean gives 18 Dec 1928; NBT and government summaries say 17 Dec 1928. Both accounts of the train escape agree on the principal participants. No eyewitness/railway ticket independently verified.
- **Lamington Road:** Maclean's research dates the attack to 8 Oct **1930**; NBT's glossary says 1 Oct **1931** and identifies a different framing. This did **not** become a story.
- **UNVERIFIED historical speech:** “Lamba Chacha!” and recalled statements attributed to Bhagwati Charan Vohra in Maclean [source](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles). No original contemporary recording inspected; none used in stories.
