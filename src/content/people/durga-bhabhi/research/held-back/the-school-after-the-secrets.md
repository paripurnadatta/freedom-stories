What happened to Durga Bhabhi after the years of secret journeys and police searches?

Part of her answer was a school. It stood in Lucknow, far from the railway journey that made her famous.

Durga Devi Vohra had helped Bhagat Singh leave Lahore in 1928. She later worked in public life too.

In the years that followed, she put her effort into teaching children.

## A very different kind of work

Historian Kama Maclean traced Durga Devi's life beyond the escape story.

She describes her involvement with Congress activities in the late 1930s. She also writes about her later work with a Montessori school in Lucknow.

<aside class="note"><b>Montessori</b>: a way of teaching that gives children activities to explore and practise with care.</aside>

A classroom was different from a secret meeting. The aim was not to hide people from the police.

It was to help children learn. A school also needed teachers, lessons, rooms and support from families.

Durga Devi was linked to both kinds of work during her long life.

## When did the school begin?

A Times of India report published in 2019 names the Lucknow Montessori Inter College.

It says Durga Devi began the school in 1940. Another report from the same newspaper gives the same year.

That was seven years before India became independent.

Maclean's article says that she established the school after independence. This is not the same date.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: Times of India reports put the school’s start in 1940. Kama Maclean writes that Durga Devi set up a Montessori school after independence. Both accounts describe her educational work, but their wording on the starting time differs.</aside>

We cannot settle the difference by making up an answer. It might involve an earlier school and later changes. But we have not seen the original school register.

## A school remembered

In 2015, the newspaper reported problems at the school. It also reported memories of its past.

In 2019, it described a plan for new classrooms and teaching equipment.

That tells us another story. Buildings and lessons must be cared for by each new generation.

A school does not stay useful only because a famous person started it. People still have to make it work.

The newspaper reports came years after Durga Devi's death. They record the school's later history, not her private words.

## Why it matters

Durga Devi's life cannot fit into one train journey. The revolutionary years were one part of it.

The school shows another part: working to build a place where children could learn.

Both should be remembered. The real work of independence was also about what people did for others afterwards.
