# Durga Devi Vohra (Durga Bhabhi) — research report
**Date:** 22 October 2026. **Status:** ChatGPT research, not checked by Claude. **Scope:** Person; research only, not story text.

## 1. Subject and sources
- **Name:** Durga Devi Vohra, also Durga Bhabhi. Scholar Kama Maclean writes “Durga Devi Vohra (1907–1999)” and identifies the name “Durga Bhabhi” [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles). Delhi University researchers also date her life 1907–1999 [D](https://dup.du.ac.in/index.php/SAMIKHIYA/article/view/911). Precise dates of birth and death are not independently confirmed from civil certificates.
- **Family:** Husband Bhagwati Charan Vohra and son Sachi appear in scholar's reconstructed accounts [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles), also in a separate National Book Trust retelling [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf). Never present first-person recalled dialogue as a contemporary transcript.
- **Organisation:** Associated with HSRA and Naujawan Bharat Sabha after September 1928. [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) traces networks, [D](https://dup.du.ac.in/index.php/SAMIKHIYA/article/view/911) describes her part in the revolutionary movement. **HRA** is the right term before the group's 1928 reorganisation.
- **Chronology:** 1928 aided the escape from Lahore [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles), [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf); 1930 Lamington Road shooting according to contemporary sources analysed in Maclean [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles), while [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) gives an inconsistent 1931 date; late 1930s Congress organising and later schoolwork are in [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles); Lucknow school founded 1940 per [T](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/smart-chapter-for-durga-bhabhis-school/articleshow/68769661.cms), [U](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/like-durga-bhabhi-her-school-fades-into-oblivion/articleshow/48407951.cms).
- **Connected people / places:** Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev, Bhagwati Charan Vohra, Sachi, Chandra Shekhar Azad; Lahore, Lucknow, Calcutta and Bombay [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles), [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf).

## 2. Ten angles (evidence size; do not pad)

1. **The family on the train — SHORT.** Hook: A mother and child helped disguise an escape. Maclean names the servant role and train [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles); NBT identifies Durga and Rajguru [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf). Key disagreement: Maclean dates shooting 18 Dec while most accounts say 17 Dec. No original tickets or eyewitness transcripts.
2. **The school after the struggle — SHORT.** Hook: What did she build when she was no longer organising an underground escape? Maclean reports Lucknow Montessori school after independence [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles); *Times of India* independently dates the school's founding to 1940 [T](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/smart-chapter-for-durga-bhabhis-school/articleshow/68769661.cms), [U](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/like-durga-bhabhi-her-school-fades-into-oblivion/articleshow/48407951.cms). **Disagreement:** date—school founded in 1940, well before independence; scholar's wording “After independence ... set up” may refer to later dedication or be a chronological error. Tell readers both, do not assert she established it after 1947.
3. **The home as a meeting place — THIN.** Hook: How can a home keep a network together? Scholar records meetings and organisational work [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles); [U](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/like-durga-bhabhi-her-school-fades-into-oblivion/articleshow/48407951.cms) calls her home a meeting point. Later newspaper account, no household register or meeting minutes; not enough Rajguru-independent detail for 350 words.
4. **The money saved for an emergency — THIN.** Hook: How did funds travel with people? Maclean describes 4,000 or 5,000 rupees and cites Durga's later recollection [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles); no verified independent financial record in [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf). **LEGEND / LATER MEMORY** from Durga's recollections; exact amount unconfirmed.
5. **Her husband and the Ravi accident — THIN.** Hook: How did one death change the underground circle? [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) and [O](https://amp.scroll.in/article/805597/freedom-files-the-secret-life-of-durga-devi-vohra) describe the 1930 bomb-test accident; same historian authors both, so they are not independent confirmation. No original incident record inspected.
6. **The Lamington Road case — THIN / HOLD.** Hook: How can sources differ by an entire year? [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) dates shots at a European couple to **8 Oct 1930**, in Bombay; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) describes Sergeant Taylor and wife on **1 Oct 1931**; a government memorial summary cited in search results was **not opened** and is not evidence here. The event and victims require original trial/press checks. No standalone children's story until resolved.
7. **The people accused with her — THIN.** Hook: Why do people remember the case differently? [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) distinguishes Durga's recollection from prosecutors' names of passengers in a car. Original court papers not read; source lists disagree on defendants.
8. **When women were underestimated — THIN.** Hook: Did police ideas about women change who could travel unnoticed? [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) provides gender analysis; [D](https://dup.du.ac.in/index.php/SAMIKHIYA/article/view/911) addresses theme but abstract may repeat Maclean. This is an interpretive topic, not a unique action with adequate independent material.
9. **An organiser beyond 1931 — THIN.** Hook: What followed the failed underground years? [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) describes Congress work in 1937–38. [U](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/lucknow/like-durga-bhabhi-her-school-fades-into-oblivion/articleshow/48407951.cms) confirms later public work but not the specific Congress post. Do not assert office dates without second source.
10. **The women behind the photographs — THIN.** Hook: What do archives show that popular posters hide? [M](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) identifies an archival family photograph and Durga with son; however copyright is reserved to the archival image holder and there is no verified Commons relicense. Do not import those images without permission.
