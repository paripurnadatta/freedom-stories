## Research notes

Assassinated Curzon Wyllie in London and was executed.

### Violence at the centre of empire

Dhingra’s action made expatriate revolutionary politics impossible for British authorities to treat as a distant colonial problem.

### India House and London

His history belongs to a transnational political geography connecting Punjab, Indian students in Britain and imperial institutions.

### Trial and execution

The profile should keep the chronology tightly tied to the documented 1909 action and execution.

### Chronology

- **1906**: Arrival in England. Studies in Britain and moves into the expatriate nationalist network
- **1909-07-01**: Assassination of Curzon Wyllie. Carries out an assassination in imperial London
- **1909-08-17**: Execution. Executed in London after conviction

### Evidence claims

- Went to England in 1906 and entered the circle of Indian revolutionary nationalists there. (high confidence)
- Came into contact with V.D. Savarkar in the London revolutionary milieu. (high confidence)
- Shot Curzon Wyllie in London on 1 July 1909 and was executed on 17 August 1909. (high confidence)
