## Research notes

### Overview

Rural political organizer from the Dewas region who joined Quit India-era Prajamandal politics and agrarian agitation. Ministry sources connect him with the Dewas Prajamandal, Kisan Andolan and Charnoi agitation and record his arrest for anti-colonial activity.

### Chronology

- **1943-1944**: Active as a Dewas Prajamandal worker in the Quit I.... Active as a Dewas Prajamandal worker in the Quit India aftermath

### Evidence claims

- Thakur Bhanwar Singh Chawda came from Bangar village near Dewas and joined the freedom movement during the Quit India period. (high confidence)
- He worked with the Dewas Prajamandal and participated in kisan and charnoi agitations. (high confidence)
- He was arrested for his political activity but continued Prajamandal work. (high confidence)
