## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Dhoni's connection with Peasant anti-colonial resistance.

### Overview

Participant in the 1894 Mangaldoi peasant rising in Darrang who was killed in police firing.

### Chronology

- **1894-01**: Joined the Mangaldoi peasant rising in Darrang
- **1894-01**: Killed during police firing in the course of the rising

### Evidence claims

- Dhoni was a resident of Khotara in the Dipila/Mangaldoi area of Darrang, Assam. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the January 1894 peasant rising against British authority in Mangaldoi subdivision. (HIGH confidence)
- He was in the forefront of the rising and was killed in police firing. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Khotara / Dipila / Mangaldoi: Peasant resistance
