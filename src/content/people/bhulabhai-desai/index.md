## Research notes

Prominent nationalist lawyer; defended INA officers at the Red Fort trials.

### Chronology

- **1928**: Represented peasants in the Bardoli inquiry
- **1930**: Joined the Indian National Congress
- **1945**: Led the legal defence of INA prisoners at the Red Fort trial

### Evidence claims

- Bhulabhai Desai entered politics through Annie Besant's Home Rule League. (HIGH confidence)
- He represented Gujarat peasants before the British inquiry connected with the Bardoli Satyagraha of 1928. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Indian National Congress in 1930 and was imprisoned during the Civil Disobedience period. (HIGH confidence)
- Desai served in the Indian Legislative Assembly from 1935 to 1946. (HIGH confidence)
- He was a leading defence lawyer for the INA prisoners tried at the Red Fort in 1945. (HIGH confidence)
- Desai conducted political negotiations with Liaquat Ali Khan over a possible power-sharing arrangement during the final years of British rule. (MEDIUM confidence)

### Places

- Bardoli: agrarian inquiry/political work
- Red Fort, Delhi: INA trial defence
