After the Kakori train robbery in August 1925, the British police hunted for the young men who did it.

In September 1925, they arrested many of them in one sweep. In October, they caught the leader, Ram Prasad Bismil, in Shahjahanpur.

But one man from Shahjahanpur slipped through their fingers. It was Bismil's closest friend, Ashfaqulla Khan.

## The sugarcane field

At first, Ashfaq did not go far. He hid in a sugarcane field, about half a mile from his home.

Sugarcane grows taller than a man. A person can hide in a thick field and not be seen from the road.

The police were all over the town. So his friends could not visit him in daylight. They sent him food only at night.

## A new name, a new job

Ashfaq knew he could not stay hidden in a field for ever. He had to get far away.

He travelled east, to a town called Daltonganj, in what is now Jharkhand. There he got a job as a clerk in an engineering company.

He did not use his real name. He told people he came from a farming family in Mathura. Nobody guessed that this quiet clerk was one of the most wanted men in India.

<aside class="note"><b>Clerk</b>: someone who works in an office, writing letters, keeping accounts and filing papers.</aside>

## The boss who loved poetry

Here a surprising thing happened. The owner of the company loved Urdu poetry, especially the short verses called shers.

Ashfaq, of course, was a poet. When his boss heard his verses, he was delighted. One biography says he even raised Ashfaq's salary.

For a while, Ashfaq lived a strange double life. By day he was a clerk from Mathura. In his heart, he was still a revolutionary.

## A friend from home

After some months, Ashfaq went to Delhi. He hoped to find a way to leave India and go abroad.

In Delhi, he met a man he knew from Shahjahanpur. They were both Pathans. They had been at school together. Ashfaq trusted him, and asked for his help.

That was a mistake. The friend went to the police and told them where Ashfaq was.

The police came, and Ashfaq was arrested.

<aside class="note"><b>When was he caught?</b> Most sources say Ashfaq was arrested in Delhi on 7 December 1926. One account says 17 July 1926. We cannot be sure which is right.</aside>

## The one they could not break

After his arrest, the police tried hard to get Ashfaq to give evidence against his friends. That would have saved his life.

He refused.

He was tried, together with another member of the group, Sachindra Nath Bakshi. The judge sentenced Bakshi to prison for life. He sentenced Ashfaq to death.

The man who had escaped for more than a year could not escape this.
