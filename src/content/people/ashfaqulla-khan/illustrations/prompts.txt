ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Ashfaqulla Khan
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs

Only stories that need a painting are listed. "The last morning in Faizabad"
uses a real photograph. Photographs of Ashfaqulla Khan and Ram Prasad Bismil
exist, so their faces may be shown: keep them close to those photographs.

Evening in an old courtyard in Shahjahanpur, early 1920s, oil lamps glowing. Two young friends sit together on a cotton durrie: a lean, handsome young Indian Muslim man in his early twenties, Pathan features, neatly combed dark hair, a thin moustache, wearing a white kurta and a dark waistcoat, reading aloud from a notebook of Urdu poems, and a slightly older young Indian man with a broad face, short hair and a thick moustache, in a white kurta and dhoti, leaning in and smiling, pointing at a line as if suggesting a better word. Between them on the floor is one shared brass thali with rotis and a bowl of dal. A small group of friends listens in the warm shadows behind. Warm, joyful, brotherly mood. Wide composition, 16:9.
