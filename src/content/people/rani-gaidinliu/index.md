## Research notes

Led Naga resistance and spent years imprisoned by the British.

### Documented record

Born 26 January 1915 in present-day Tamenglong district. Joined Haipou Jadonang and the Heraka movement in 1927 at about age thirteen. Led armed anti-British resistance in the region after Jadonang. Arrested in 1932 and imprisoned until 1947.

### Chronology

- **1927**: Joins Heraka movement
- **1931-1932**: Anti-British resistance
- **1932**: Arrest and life sentence
- **1947**: Release
