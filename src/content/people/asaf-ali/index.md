## Research notes

Congress leader and nationalist lawyer; husband of Aruna Asaf Ali.

### Law as a site of anti-colonial politics

Asaf Ali's courtroom work makes legal defence part of the freedom-struggle graph rather than a separate professional biography.

### A bridge across ideological camps

His connections link Congress constitutional politics to revolutionaries and the INA without implying ideological identity among them.

### Chronology

- **1929**: Assembly bomb case defence. Asaf Ali's legal career intersects directly with the HSRA revolutionary network through the defence of Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt
- **1945**: INA Defence Committee. He helps organize the Congress legal response to the treason trials of INA officers

### Evidence claims

- Asaf Ali returned to India after legal studies in London and became involved in nationalist politics. (high confidence)
- He became a respected lawyer and defended Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt in connection with the Central Legislative Assembly bomb case. (high confidence)
- In 1945 he served as convener of the INA Defence team organized by the Indian National Congress. (high confidence)
