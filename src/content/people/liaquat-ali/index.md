## Research notes

Associated with rebel administration in Allahabad.

### A city briefly contested

The profile should emphasize the struggle for authority in Allahabad rather than construct a detailed biography from thin evidence.

### Proclamation as evidence

The existence of rebel proclamations is firmer ground than many later anecdotes about exact duration, titles or administrative detail.

### Chronology

- **1857-06**: Allahabad uprising. Liaquat Ali emerges as a local leader as rebellion spreads through Allahabad/Prayagraj

### Evidence claims

- Archival historical material records proclamations issued by Liaquat Ali of Allahabad during the uprising of 1857. (high confidence)
- Regional-archive reporting identifies Liaquat Ali as a leader associated with the June 1857 uprising of the 6th Bengal Native Infantry and rebel authority in Allahabad. (medium confidence)
