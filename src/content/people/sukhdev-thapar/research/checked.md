# Sukhdev Thapar report: what was checked (21 October 2026)

Report: report-2026-10-21.pdf (another AI).

## Held up (used in stories)
- Born 15 May 1907, Naughara Mohalla, Ludhiana; name from nine Thapar houses (Tribune, Jain; Wikipedia).
- Father Ram Lal died 1910 (Tribune, Jain). Mother died early (Tribune, Ramjilal quoting N. N. Vohra).
- Raised in Lyallpur by Lala Achint/Chint Ram and cousin Gauran Devi (Tribune, Jain). Paternal vs
  maternal uncle: the two Tribune writers disagree; shown as a note.
- Refused to salute a visiting officer (Tribune, Ramjilal).
- Matric 1922, National College (Tribune, Jain). NewsClick says he joined National College in 1921: note added.
- Aliases Grameen, Kisan, Gwar, Swami, Dyal (Tribune, Ramjilal); "Swami alias peasant" in FIR (Wikipedia).
- Naujawan Bharat Sabha, Feroz Shah Kotla 8-9 Sept 1928, Punjab secretary (Tribune, Ramjilal).
- Clothes for "friends" (Gauran Devi), Shiv Verma, fellow prisoner memories (Tribune, Jain).
- FIR: Sukhdev 1st, Bhagat Singh 12th, Rajguru 20th; "Crown versus Sukhdev and others" (Tribune, Wikipedia).
- "Brain of the conspiracy" / "right arm" (Tribune, Jain).
- Room 69, Kashmir Building, rented in Durga Devi's name; Kishori Lal and empty shells (The Nation, Nijjar quote).
- Raid 15 April 1929, arrests of Kishori Lal, Sukhdev, Jai Gopal (Wikipedia, HSRA).
- Durga Devi and the "new friend" disguise (Wikipedia, Durgawati Devi).
- Execution advanced 11 hours, 7.30 pm 23 March 1931; rear wall; Sutlej; Kuldip Nayar "I will go first"
  (labelled) (Tribune, Ramjilal).
- House: Tribune 2007 report, H. C. Arora PIL, full protection Nov 2014 (Tribune, Jain); hunger strike
  over the access road, August 2026 (Tribune).
- Letter to Gandhi: delivered via Chint Ram mid-March 1931, printed in Young India, cautious reply
  (Tribune, Jain "Unfinished argument of 1931"); "temporary truce" (NewsClick); Gandhi's meetings with
  Irwin (Janata Weekly). Both views given.
