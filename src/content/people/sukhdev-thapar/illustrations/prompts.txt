ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Sukhdev Thapar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Sukhdev's face may be shown: real photographs of him exist (thin face, thin moustache,
white cap). Never show the faces of Gauran Devi or Lala Achint Ram: show them from
behind or at a distance. Gandhi's face may be shown.

Where it goes: the top of the story "Shirts for his friends", and its card everywhere on the site

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Late 1920s, the courtyard of a modest brick house in a Punjab town. A thin young Indian man of about 21, thin moustache, wearing a white cap and a worn, patched white kurta, smiles as he collects a neat pile of folded shirts and kurtas into a cloth bundle. An Indian woman in a salwar kameez and dupatta, seen from behind, hands him one more folded shirt. Warm afternoon light, gentle and affectionate mood. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle.
