## Research notes

HSRA organizer executed in 1931.

### The organizer behind the iconic trio

Sukhdev’s profile should foreground organizational labour rather than treating him only as one of three executed revolutionaries.

### Naujawan Bharat Sabha and HSRA

The open youth organization and underground revolutionary organization should remain distinct.

### A Lahore revolutionary network

His ties to Bhagat Singh and Rajguru form one of the archive’s most important bidirectional relationship clusters.

### Chronology

- **1926**: Naujawan Bharat Sabha. Works in a public youth organization linked to Lahore revolutionary politics
- **late 1920s**: HSRA organization. Helps sustain cells and underground revolutionary activity
- **1931-03-23**: Execution. Executed with Bhagat Singh and Rajguru after the Lahore conspiracy prosecution

### Evidence claims

- Was active in the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association and helped organize revolutionary cells. (high confidence)
- Was associated with Naujawan Bharat Sabha, founded in Lahore in 1926 as an open youth organization. (high confidence)
- Was executed on 23 March 1931 with Bhagat Singh and Shivaram Rajguru. (high confidence)
