## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Patna resistance in 1857.

### An urban history of 1857

Pir Ali’s story brings the uprising into Patna’s bookselling, communication and urban political networks.

### Patna, not Delhi

The profile should remain rooted in Bihar’s local history rather than serving only as a footnote to the better-known northern centres of rebellion.

### An uncertain birthplace

The archive should surface the disagreement instead of silently choosing one birthplace.

### Chronology

- **before 1857**: Bookseller in Patna. Lives and works in the city before the uprising
- **1857**: Patna uprising. Emerges as a local anti-British leader during the rebellion
- **1857**: Capture and execution. Colonial authorities suppress the Patna network and impose capital punishment

### Evidence claims

- Was a bookseller and long-time resident of Patna according to the supplied government profile. (high confidence)
- Played a leading role in anti-British activity in Patna during the uprising of 1857. (high confidence)
- Was captured and sentenced to capital punishment by the colonial authorities. (high confidence)
- His birthplace is not securely established; the supplied profile notes competing traditions and a colonial report describing him as a native of Lucknow long resident in Patna. (disputed confidence)
- The supplied record connects Pir Ali Khan with Revolt of 1857 as participant in. (high confidence)
