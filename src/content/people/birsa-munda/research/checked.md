## What held up

- Birth place Ulihatu, parents Sugana Munda and Karmi Hatu: Wikipedia. (Bara does not name the parents.)
- Mission schooling at Chaibasa 1886-1890; teacher Jaipal Nag; baptised Daud: Wikipedia, Rycroft, Bara.
- The legend of the lightning: Roy tells it as fact; Bara calls it "legend". We label it as a legend.
- First arrest attempt 9 August 1895 failed (Rycroft, citing K. S. Singh); arrest in August 1895 (Rycroft, Roy, Wikipedia).
- Ulgulan from Christmas Eve 1899 (Roy, Rycroft, Wikipedia). 9 January 1900 at Dumari / Sail Rakab hill (Roy, Rycroft, Wikipedia, Bara).
- Died 9 June 1900 in Ranchi jail (Rycroft, Wikipedia). Official cause dysentery and cholera; mission suggested poisoning (Rycroft).
- 1903 Act and 1908 CNT Act, with Hoffmann's role (Bara, Roy, Wikipedia).
- Photograph: Roy's 1912 caption puts it after the 1900 arrest; Rycroft argues for August 1895. The missing-handcuffs point is in Rycroft.
- Songs: Bara and Rycroft print the lines we quote.

## Disagreements, all shown in the stories as "Two versions" notes

- **Birth date**: 15 Nov 1875 (Wikipedia); 22 July 1872 with baptism 11 Aug 1872 (Bara, from Areeparampil and Bharmi Munda); Roy implies about 1874 (age 21 in 1895).
- **Date of the 1895 arrest**: 23 August (Rycroft) or 24 August (Wikipedia). Who made it: a sub-inspector from Khunti (Rycroft, via Singh) or the District Superintendent on an elephant (Roy).
- **Sentence in 1895**: two years (Rycroft, Wikipedia) or two and a half (Roy).
- **Date of the Chutia temple episode**: last week of January 1897 (Roy) or January 1898 (Bara, Wikipedia).
- **Deaths at Dumari Hill**: four men plus three women and a boy (official report as quoted by Roy); more than twenty (Rycroft); unknown (Wikipedia). Spelling of the place: Dumari, Dumbari, Dombari; "Sail Rakab" in Rycroft and Wikipedia.
- **Place of the 1900 arrest**: Jamkopai forest (Wikipedia); near Santara village (Commons caption).
- **Number tried in 1900**: 460 accused, 63 convicted (Wikipedia); 87 sent to Sessions (Roy); 300 (Rycroft).
- **Cause of death**: illness (jail superintendent) or poison (mission). No proof either way in any source opened.
- **Translation of Dharti Aba**: Roy says "Father of the World".

## Points from the report that could NOT be checked, and so were left out

- The Indian Express claim that the date of the final arrest was 3 March 1900 (the report says 3 February is on the Commons caption only). We give 3 February from Wikipedia and Commons and say the day is not in Roy or Rycroft.
- The 2026 Indian Express article's claim that Dumari was in "January 1899", and that "hundreds" died (community memory). Not opened. Roy, Rycroft and Wikipedia all give 1900. We say plainly there is no agreed number.
- Budhram Munda's family memories of Birsa's childhood: Indian Express only. Not used.
- Whether Birsa married or had children: no source opened says. Left out.
- The report's note on "diku": we used Bara's wording that the target was the exploiter, not every outsider.
- The report's point about 28 January 1898 at Chutia comes from Wikipedia and Bara, and we kept it.

## Left out on purpose

- Roy's mocking details (the string between two trees, the fire-and-brimstone day, the demand for clothes) except the fire day, which is shown with Bara's warning about hostile writers.
- Missionary insults ("young monkey") are mentioned once, as an example of how enemies wrote.
- Details of the Christmas Eve attacks beyond a gentle summary.
- Modern political quarrels about Birsa and religion (Bara mentions a quarrel over a bust of Hoffmann, sourced to a Hindustan Times article we cannot open).
