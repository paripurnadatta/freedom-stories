Most people think of Sardar Patel as a lawyer in a courtroom, or as a leader in a crowd. This story is about a different job. He worked for a city's health.

In 1917 he "became active in public life after getting elected as a member of Ahmedabad Municipality." A municipality is the council that looks after a city's roads, water and cleaning.

## Drains and electricity

Patel served as the municipality's Sanitation Commissioner. He "extended electricity, drainage and sanitation facility to all parts of the city." He also pushed for school reform, and for the recognition and pay of teachers.

<aside class="note"><b>Sanitation</b>: keeping a place clean and healthy, with clean water, drains and waste removal.</aside>

## Sweeping the streets

Patel was president of the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation from 1924 to 1928. He led a clean-up. He and volunteers "cleaned the streets of Ahmedabad with brooms and dustcart, beginning with Harijan Basti."

<aside class="note"><b>Harijan Basti</b>: the old name for a neighbourhood where people from Dalit communities lived. Those parts of cities were usually the last to be cleaned. Today most people use the word Dalit, not Harijan.</aside>

In a plague, Patel "worked almost round the clock with his volunteers to help the victims and their families." He risked catching the disease, and the strain hurt his health.

<aside class="note"><b>Plague</b>: a deadly disease that spread through cities in India in the early 1900s.</aside>

the dates do not agree. The Sardar Patel Trust and the Constitution website say he was elected to the municipality in 1917. Wikipedia says he was elected municipal president in 1922, 1924 and 1927. The Press Information Bureau says he was president from 1924 to 1928. The dates are given here as each source gives them.

## The great rain of 1927

In July 1927, Gujarat was hit by floods. "Gujarat was ravaged by floods after the torrential rains of July 23, 1927."

Patel "arranged and managed flood relief work exemplarily in Gujarat." He "initiated a fund-raising drive and mobilised an army of volunteers to move supplies to the affected areas." He led a large rescue and rehabilitation effort. The Bombay government recommended him for an award, and he declined it.

## A change of job

Patel was working for a city council when the Bardoli farmers asked him for help in 1928. He "resigned from the Ahmedabad Municipality to lead the Bardoli 'no tax' campaign." That is a story for another day.

## Why it matters

This part of Patel's life has few exciting pictures. There is no march, no jail and no speech. There are drains, brooms, and relief for flood victims. But the Press Information Bureau says this is what built his name as a leader of ordinary people.
