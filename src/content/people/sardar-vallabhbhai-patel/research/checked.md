# Patel: what was checked (22 October 2026)

Report filed: report-from-other-ai.pdf (Research Dossier, 22 October 2026). Tested against sources opened in this session:
Sardar Patel Trust (His Journey, Life Story), Indian National Congress biography page, PIB "Organiser par excellence",
Constitution of India member page, Nehru Archive (statement of 8 March 1930, Karachi resolution, 1942 prison diary),
the 1950 White Paper on Indian States (Wikisource: States Department, 5 July statement, 25 July meeting, conclusion),
Mahadev Desai's *The Story of Bardoli* (1929, archive.org), Dr Hari Desai (Rashtra Vimarsh), Wikipedia (Vallabhbhai Patel,
Bardoli Satyagraha, Kheda Satyagraha), mkgandhi.org (Kheda struggle), Azad's *India Wins Freedom*.

## Held up
- Adopted birthday 31 October 1875 (Trust, Congress page); childhood at Karamsad; London 1910, return 1913; Ahmedabad municipality 1917.
- Arrest at Ras on 7 March 1930; Nehru statement of 8 March 1930 (Nehru Archive).
- Karachi 1931, Resolution on Fundamental Rights of 31 March 1931 (Nehru Archive).
- 1942 arrest; Nehru's list of "Twelve of us"; bricked windows, no news; Patel and the garden (Nehru diary).
- States Department: 13 June meeting, 27 June communique, Patel in charge with Nishtar, Menon secretary; 5 July statement; 25 July meeting; accession by 15 August barring Hyderabad, Kashmir and Junagadh (White Paper, official).
- Kheda: four-anna rule, pledge, Patel gave up practice, Gandhi's unhappy ending (mkgandhi.org).

## Disagreements found (told in the stories as "Two versions")
- Who first said "Sardar": women of Bardoli (Wikipedia, no source); Bhikhiben of Akoti, April 1928 (Hari Desai, citing a 1990 thesis); farmer Kuverji Durlabh Patel (PIB); Mahadev Desai, 1929: "I do not know who gave" it. The report's Hindustan Times claim could not be opened; not used.
- Birthplace Nadiad (Congress page, Wikipedia) against Karamsad (Trust).
- Bardoli tax: 30% (Settlement Officer), 29%, 22% (Government), per Desai; Wikipedia mixes them. The Wikipedia 6.03% inquiry figure could not be confirmed; left out.
- Municipal offices and years (Trust 1917; Wikipedia 1922, 1924, 1927; PIB president 1924-28).
- Number of states: 565 (Mountbatten), "nearly 560" (Trust), 550/565/600+ (Wikipedia).
- London course: two years (Congress page) against 30 months (Wikipedia).
- Ten against twelve leaders at Ahmednagar (Azad against Nehru).
- Ras arrest: "as Gandhi began" (Wikipedia) against 7 March (Nehru Archive).

## Could not be checked, left out
- Indian Express "Role in the Dandi march" article and Hindustan Times piece: blocked from here. Patel "chose Dandi" claim left out.
- Embassy of India page on Karamsad and National Unity Day: not opened.
- Ghanshyam Shah's Bardoli article (ResearchGate): not opened; Patrika circulation figures left out.
- Telegram during the murder trial: the Congress page tells it with no telegram; we repeat only the cross-examination, labelled as later biography. Father and the Mutiny: labelled hearsay.
- Nagpur flag satyagraha 1923: the Constitution page says "Nagpur Satyagraha" without a flag; Wikipedia says flag. Left out of stories.
- Statue of Unity, Hyderabad and Junagadh in detail, partition and RSS-ban questions: not researched here. Left out.

## Weak points
- Municipal and plague story rests on PIB, a Government of India feature article. No council records.
- Photo licences: the Commons pages say public domain in India; the dossier flagged that US status is not established for some Photo Division images. We used pre-1947 photos of unknown authorship and a Kulwant Roy photo.
