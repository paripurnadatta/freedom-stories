4. Disputes, legends and doubts
  ● Details of Early Literary Beginnings: While popular accounts frequently highlight her
     writing a long Persian play (Maher Muneer) at a very young age (around 12 or 13) which
     caught the attention of the Nizam, academic biographers note that exact corroboration of
     these early manuscripts can sometimes lean on family recollection and traditional
     hagiography rather than strict archival documentation. However, the overarching fact of
     her precocious academic brilliance at Madras University remains fully verified by
     university records.
