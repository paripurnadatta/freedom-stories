# Sarojini Naidu: what was checked (22 October 2026)

Private note, never published. The stories were written fresh from sources opened in this session. The ChatGPT report for her was thin (mostly Wikipedia), so nearly all of its claims were re-checked or left out.

## What held up (two or more sources I opened)

- Born 13 February 1879, Hyderabad (Wikipedia, Britannica, INC, Encyclopedia.com, DVPP, Symons).
- Father Aghorenath Chattopadhyay, mother a Bengali poet, eldest of eight (Wikipedia, Encyclopedia.com, Symons).
- Matriculation 1891, aged twelve, first in the Madras presidency (Wikipedia, Encyclopedia.com, Banglapedia).
- To England 1895 on the Nizam's scholarship; King's College then Girton; back in 1898 (Wikipedia, Symons, Banglapedia, EBSCO).
- Married Dr Govindarajulu Naidu in 1898 (Wikipedia, DVPP, Banglapedia, Encyclopedia.com).
- The Golden Threshold (1905), The Bird of Time (1912), The Broken Wing (1917) (Wikipedia, EBSCO, Encyclopedia.com, Banglapedia).
- Congress president at Kanpur, 1925 (Wikipedia, INC x2, Wire, Banglapedia).
- Died 2 March 1949 at Government House, Lucknow (Wikipedia, INC, ETV Bharat, Britannica).
- Governor of the United Provinces from 15 August 1947 (Wikipedia, NWE).
- Dharasana raid 21 May 1930 and Webb Miller's dispatch (Wikipedia x2, GKToday, Satyagraha Foundation print of the dispatch).

## Where sources disagree (flagged in the stories)

- Who led the 21 May raid: Naidu and Azad (Wikipedia Dharasana); Naidu with Manilal Gandhi and Imam Sahib (GKToday); Naidu after Tyabji and Kasturba were arrested (Wikipedia Salt March); Miller names no leader. When Naidu was arrested is not given by any source; INC and Banglapedia say "with Gandhi", which conflicts with Wikipedia's account of Gandhi's night arrest.
- Casualties at Dharasana: 170 (official, via Miller), 200+ (Miller at noon), 320 (British papers, Wikipedia) and two dead.
- Her age at school end: 12 (Wikipedia, Encyclopedia.com), 11 (EBSCO).
- Father: principal (Wikipedia) or founder (Symons, Encyclopedia.com) of Nizam College.
- Reaction to her marriage: both families approved (Wikipedia) versus alienated (EBSCO). Children: five, or four.
- First woman Congress president: Wikipedia/Britannica say first Indian woman; INC list says second after Besant (1917). Besant's year: 1917 (INC list) or 1916 (INC tribute, The Wire).
- 1942 jail: 21 months (Wikipedia, INC, NWE, Banglapedia) versus freed 21 March 1943 (mkgandhi.org). Banglapedia: arrested 2 October 1942.
- Death: 2 March 1949 Lucknow (most), Allahabad (Banglapedia), 1 February 1949 (DVPP).
- The nickname: Gandhi (Britannica), "Bharat Kokila" (INC), "Bulbul-e-Hind" from the 1905 book (Banglapedia). The Sceptred Flute: 1928 (Wikipedia) or 1943 (Banglapedia).
- WIA founders: Wikipedia (Naidu page) says Naidu co-founded; the WIA page does not mention her. AIWC likewise.
- The 1919 trip to London: 1917 (Brewminate) versus August 1919 (Wikipedia suffrage page).

## Single source only (said so in the stories)

- Symons's story of the poem over the algebra problem, the novel at 14 to 16, the Nizam's scholarship "against her will".
- Father dismissed and banished (NWE).
- The 1896 magazine poem (DVPP).

## Could not check

- The ChatGPT report's claim of a Persian play "Maher Muneer" (and the Nizam's reaction): no source I opened mentions it. Left out.
- Her meeting with Gandhi in 1914 and with Gokhale: no source gives a date or place I could verify. Left out.
- The return of the Kaisar-i-Hind medal after Jallianwala Bagh: Wikipedia marks it "citation needed"; Banglapedia says it is "widely reported" but does not state it. Left out.
- Webb Miller's full dispatch beyond what the Satyagraha Foundation page printed; no page I read mentions Naidu by name in that dispatch.
- Kanpur 1925 session dates and her full speech.
- The Round Table Conference (1931) and the 1928 US tour: only one-line mentions. Left out.
- Her work as governor: no source describes it.

## Left out on purpose

- Poems: not quoted.
- The graphic details of the Dharasana injuries.
- No picture was added; the existing portrait.webp stays.
