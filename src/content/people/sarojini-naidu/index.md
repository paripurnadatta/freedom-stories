## Research notes

Major nationalist leader and Congress president.

### Voice, organization and political authority

Naidu’s political significance lay in combining public oratory and cultural authority with formal nationalist leadership.

### Women in mass civil resistance

Her Salt Satyagraha role shows women moving from symbolic participation into operational leadership.

### Aga Khan Palace

Her detention connects her directly to the wartime suppression of the Congress leadership.

### Chronology

- **1920s**: Congress leadership. Emerges as a major woman leader in national politics
- **1930**: Salt Satyagraha leadership. Takes a leading role in the civil-disobedience campaign
- **1942-1943**: Quit India detention. Held at Aga Khan Palace during wartime repression
