## Research notes

Early revolutionary nationalist and editor of Bande Mataram.

### Complete independence before it was Congress orthodoxy

Aurobindo’s journalism helped push nationalist argument beyond limited constitutional reform.

### Bande Mataram as political weapon

The newspaper made the press a central arena of confrontation with the colonial state.

### Two prosecutions, not one

The 1907 sedition case and the later Alipore Bomb Case must remain clearly separated in chronology and evidence.

### Chronology

- **1907**: Bande Mataram sedition prosecution. Colonial authorities prosecute Aurobindo over the nationalist newspaper; he is acquitted
- **1908**: Arrest in the Alipore Bomb Case. A distinct conspiracy prosecution follows the Muzaffarpur bomb incident
- **1908-1909**: Alipore trial. The case becomes one of the best-known revolutionary prosecutions of the Swadeshi era

### Evidence claims

- Served as editor and a leading intellectual force behind Bande Mataram, which argued for complete independence. (high confidence)
- Was prosecuted for sedition in 1907 over Bande Mataram and was acquitted. (high confidence)
- Was separately an accused in the 1908–1909 Alipore Bomb Case. (high confidence)
