## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Hasan Ali Khan's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Police Jamadar in Tirhut who joined local rebels during the 1857 uprising and was executed by colonial authorities.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Joined Tirhut-area rebels, was captured and executed

### Evidence claims

- Hasan Ali Khan was a Police Jamadar posted at Tirhut in Bihar. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined local rebels during the 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured, sentenced to death and executed by hanging in 1857. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Tirhut: Police posting, rebellion and execution context
