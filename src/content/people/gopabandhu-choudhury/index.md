## Research notes

Left government service and joined the freedom movement.

### Resigning authority to join mass politics

His shift from magistrate to activist illustrates the moral and professional rupture demanded by Non-Cooperation.

### The ashram after the march

Political mobilisation and rural reconstruction belonged to the same Gandhian strategy in Odisha.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Leaves government service. Choudhury resigns his magistracy and commits himself to Gandhian nationalist work
- **1922**: Alakashrama established. He creates a rural centre for Congress workers, education and constructive programmes
- **1930**: Odisha Salt Satyagraha. Choudhury leads the provincial Civil Disobedience effort before arrest disrupts the march
- **1934**: Bari constructive-work phase. Following Gandhi's advice, Choudhury builds a new base for rural work

### Evidence claims

- Public-broadcaster history records that Gopabandhu Choudhury resigned as a Deputy Magistrate after several years in colonial service in order to work for the people and join Gandhian programmes. (high confidence)
- He became associated with the Odisha Congress and established Alakashrama in Jagatsinghpur in 1922 as a centre for rural reconstruction and education. (high confidence)
- Ministry of Culture history identifies him as the head of the Civil Disobedience/Salt Satyagraha effort in Odisha in 1930. (high confidence)
- He was arrested during the Salt Satyagraha phase and later continued constructive work from Bari. (high confidence)
