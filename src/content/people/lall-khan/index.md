## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Lall Khan's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Former Bengal Army sepoy who joined the 1857 rebels, was transported for life to the Andaman Islands and died in detention in January 1859.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Left Bengal Army service and joined the uprising
- **1858-04-06**: Deported to the Andaman Islands under a transportation-for-life sentence
- **1859-01-04**: Died in detention in the Andamans

### Evidence claims

- The selected Lall Khan was a Bengal Army sepoy who left Company service to join the 1857 rebels. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured and sentenced to transportation for life. (HIGH confidence)
- He was deported to the Andaman Islands on 6 April 1858 and died there in detention on 4 January 1859. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Residence unknown: Pre-capture residence not established
- Andaman Islands: Transportation and death in detention
