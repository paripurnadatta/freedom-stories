## Research notes

Co-led the Santhal rebellion.

### A mass Indigenous uprising before 1857

The Santhal Hul must stand in the archive on its own terms rather than serving merely as a prelude to 1857.

### Land, debt and colonial authority

The rebellion emerged from a specific political economy of revenue, intermediaries and moneylending.

### Collective leadership

The profile should avoid turning the Hul into a one-person biography; the brothers were part of a broader community leadership structure.

### Chronology

- **1855-06-30**: Mass mobilization. Santhal leaders publicly declare resistance to colonial authority
- **1855-1856**: Santhal Hul. The uprising spreads before violent colonial suppression

### Evidence claims

- Kanhu Murmu and Sidhu Murmu were principal leaders of the Santhal Hul of 1855–56. (high confidence)
- The uprising emerged from grievances involving colonial rule, zamindars, moneylenders and land dispossession. (high confidence)
- The movement mobilized thousands of Santals in open rebellion against colonial authority. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kanhu Murmu with Santhal Hul as participant in. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kanhu Murmu with Sidhu Murmu as family relation. (high confidence)
