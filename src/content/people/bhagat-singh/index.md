## Research notes

Revolutionary executed in 1931.

### Revolution as action and argument

Bhagat Singh’s historical significance is not exhausted by armed action. His career joined underground organization, dramatic public acts, courtroom politics, prison protest and sustained ideological writing.

### From Saunders to the Assembly

The killing of Saunders and the later Assembly bombing belonged to different political moments but were both shaped by the HSRA’s attempt to make revolutionary action communicate a political message beyond the immediate act itself.

### The prison as a political stage

The 1929 hunger strike shifted attention toward the treatment of political prisoners. Contemporary statements show that the strike attracted national concern even from leaders who did not share the revolutionaries’ methods.

### A date disagreement worth preserving

The pilot should deliberately expose uncertainty rather than silently harmonize it: an official modern government profile gives 28 September 1907, while the ICHR Dictionary entry says the exact birth date is not known.

### Chronology

- **1926**: Naujawan Bharat Sabha. Helps build a radical youth organization in Lahore
- **1928-12-17**: Saunders shooting. Participates in the operation intended as retaliation for the death of Lala Lajpat Rai
- **1929-04-08**: Central Legislative Assembly bombing. Bhagat Singh and Batukeshwar Dutt throw bombs in the Assembly and surrender to make the action politically visible
- **1929-07**: Political-prisoner hunger strike. The hunger strike becomes a national political issue and draws public statements from figures including Jawaharlal Nehru
- **1931-03-23**: Execution at Lahore. Executed with Rajguru and Sukhdev after the Lahore Conspiracy Case

## Angle research (October 2026)

Notes behind each planned story. Check these again while writing.

- **The last day in Lahore jail.** Eyewitness account by the journalist-prisoner Virendra: barber Barkat brought word around 2 pm; Bhagat Singh's black fountain pen and a comb carved with his name were passed to fellow prisoners; warder Chattar Singh came at 4 pm; hanged at 7:33 pm; the family's meeting was refused when only parents were allowed. The "finishing a book on Lenin" story is told by Virendra, but The India Forum calls the Lenin-book request possibly apocryphal, so treat it as told, not proven. [Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/1079415/end-of-a-glorious-chapter-a-journalists-first-person-account-of-bhagat-singhs-execution), [The India Forum](https://www.theindiaforum.in/article/when-past-melts)
- **The wedding he ran away from / The man called Balwant.** Left home for Kanpur when his marriage was planned; worked at the Pratap Press; returned in 1925 once promised he would not be forced to marry; later hid in Delhi as Balvant Singh, working at Vir Arjun. Pen names: Balwant, Ranjit, Vidrohi. [Encyclopaedia of Sikhism](https://eos.learnpunjabi.org/BHAGAT%20SINGH%20(1907-1931).html), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagat_Singh)
- **Sixty thousand rupees.** Arrested May 1927 over an October 1926 Lahore bombing; released on a surety of Rs 60,000 about five weeks later. Find the primary source before writing. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagat_Singh)
- **The disguise on the train.** Escaped to Calcutta disguised as a wealthy man (Encyclopaedia of Sikhism); Durga Bhabhi as wife, Rajguru as servant.
- **The hat in the photograph.** Taken by Ram Nath at Kashmiri Gate, Delhi, a few days before the Assembly bombing; Dutt went too and has no hat in his photos; Outlook says the felt hat was the studio's. [Outlook](https://outlookindia.com/art-entertainment/what-does-the-iconic-photo-of-bhagat-singh-actually-mean)
- **116 days without food.** Strike ended 5 October 1929 after 116 days; Jatin Das died 13 September 1929 after 63 days. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagat_Singh)
- **Three hundred books in 716 days.** Reading counts from M. M. Juneja (Times of India, 2011); notebook opens with epigrams from Shakespeare and Ghalib; he gave it to his younger brother Kulbir Singh before the execution. [The India Forum](https://www.theindiaforum.in/article/when-past-melts), [Frontline](https://www.pressreader.com/india/frontline/20190104/283111365097463)
- **The village India swapped for twelve.** Returned to India on 17 January 1961 in exchange for 12 villages near the Sulemanki Headworks, Fazilka. B. K. Dutt and his mother Vidyavati were also cremated there. Memorial area was overrun in the 1971 war and rebuilt. [Wikipedia: Hussainiwala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hussainiwala)
- **The square in Lahore.** Bhagat Singh Memorial Foundation of Pakistan petitioned the Lahore High Court on 6 September 2015 to rename Shadman Chowk. Check the current status before writing. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhagat_Singh)
- **Birth date.** Most sources give 27 September 1907, some 28 September. Keep writing "September 1907".
