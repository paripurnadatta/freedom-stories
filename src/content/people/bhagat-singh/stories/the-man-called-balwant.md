In 1925, a new young man started work at a Delhi newspaper called Vir Arjun. It was a daily paper printed in Hindi. The newcomer was quiet and he wrote well. He said his name was Balwant Singh.

It was not his real name.

He was Bhagat Singh, and the police were looking for him.

## Why was he hiding?

That year, groups of Sikh protesters were marching to a town called Jaito in Punjab. The British tried hard to stop them. When one group passed by, Bhagat Singh helped to welcome them.

<aside class="note"><b>Jaito</b>: a town in Punjab where Sikh protesters held a long protest against the British, from 1923 to 1925.</aside>

For that, the police issued a warrant for his arrest. So he slipped away to Delhi and became Balwant. He hid there for about five months.

<aside class="note"><b>Warrant</b>: an official order that lets the police arrest someone.</aside>

## A writer with many names

Balwant was not a new name for him. He had used it before, at a fiery Hindi newspaper in Kanpur called Pratap. He had worked there after running away from home to escape an arranged marriage. Over the next few years he wrote under at least two more.

One was Ranjit. The other was Vidrohi. In Hindi, vidrohi means "rebel".

Why use other names? A writer who attacked the British in print could be arrested. A made-up name made it harder for the police to know who had written what.

<aside class="note"><b>Pen name</b>: a made-up name a writer uses instead of their real one.</aside>

## What did Vidrohi write?

Vidrohi wrote for a magazine called Kirti, made for farmers and workers. Bhagat Singh later helped to edit it.

In 1927, Vidrohi wrote about the young men of the Kakori case. They had robbed a train carrying British government money, and some of them were sentenced to death. Vidrohi praised their courage.

In February 1928, Vidrohi wrote for a Hindi magazine called Chand. It had brought out a special issue all about phansi, which means hanging. It was about the freedom fighters the British had hanged.

The police found out who Vidrohi really was. That article was one of the reasons they arrested Bhagat Singh in 1927.

## The names together

So Balwant, Ranjit and Vidrohi were all the same person. Each name hid a teenager, and then a young man, who was learning to fight with words.

Later he would become the most famous of all of them, under his own name.
