## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Bakul Koch's connection with Peasant anti-colonial resistance.

### Overview

Peasant participant from Sarabari, Sipajhar in Darrang district who was killed during the January 1894 anti-revenue uprising in Mangaldoi subdivision. The ICHR Dictionary of Martyrs grounds his entry in Assam archival files, making this a compact but unusually secure martyr profile.

- Bakul Koch belonged to Sarabari, Sipajhar, Darrang district and joined the January 1894 peasant rising against British authorities in Mangaldoi subdivision. (high confidence)
