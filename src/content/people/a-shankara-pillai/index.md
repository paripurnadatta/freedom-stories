## Research notes

Documented association with Travancore State Congress / Quit India in Kerala.

### Overview

South Travancore Congress leader who organized responsible-government and Quit India agitation and endured repeated imprisonment.

### Chronology

- **1937**: His home had become a Congress activity center in South Travancore by the time Gandhi visited
- **1939-09**: Arrested after organizing a procession in Nagarkovil
- **1942-09**: Arrested again for Quit India participation

### Evidence claims

- A. Shankara Pillai became president of the Nagarkovil District Congress Committee after entering Congress agitation. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned in 1939 for organizing a procession and was later arrested again for Congress leadership activity. (HIGH confidence)
- He actively participated in Quit India and was arrested again in September 1942. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Nagarkovil / Nagercoil: District Congress and protest organizing
- South Travancore: Responsible-government and Quit India activity
