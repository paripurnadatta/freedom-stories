## Research notes

### Overview

Young participant in the Rudri/Navagaon jungle satyagraha of 1930 in Dhamtari district. Ministry sources describe his nonviolent preparation, participation in symbolic forest-law violation, police firing and death from wounds four days later.

### Chronology

- **1930-09-21**: Participated in Rudri/Navagaon jungle satyagraha a.... Participated in Rudri/Navagaon jungle satyagraha and was shot
- **1930-09-25**: Died from injuries.. Died from injuries

### Evidence claims

- Meendhu Kumhar was born in 1912 in Lamkeni village and joined the 1930 jungle satyagraha at age eighteen. (high confidence)
- On 21 September 1930 he participated in deliberate forest-law violation at Rudri/Navagaon. (high confidence)
- He was shot during police firing and died on 25 September 1930. (high confidence)
