Where do the heroes of the past come from? Often from small villages that few people have heard of.

Roshan Singh was born in Nawada, in the Shahjahanpur district of Uttar Pradesh. Satyarth Prakash Nyas, a Hindi website, and Garima Times, a Hindi news site, both name the village. This story is about what the village looked like long after he was gone.

## A river in the way

The news site Garima Times says that Nawada lies near the Khannaut river. It says that for decades after independence, there was no bridge over it.

When the rains came, the river rose. The site says the area was cut off from the main roads for four or five months at a time. It says sick people could not reach hospitals. Children could not get to school. Farmers could not take their crops to the market on time.

It says three generations of villagers lived like this.

## The bridge

Garima Times says villagers met Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath and asked for a bridge. First they asked for a pontoon bridge, which floats on boats or drums. Then, on 25 February 2018, in Shahjahanpur, the Chief Minister approved a permanent bridge over the Khannaut. The site says he also approved roads and a degree college.

The site says he visited Nawada on 30 December 2018, after the bridge was built. It says he paid respects at a statue of Roshan Singh at the family home, and honoured elders of the family. It says many villagers were moved to tears.

It also says that ambulances now reach the village in time. It says students can travel to school and college, and farmers can reach the market.

## The house and the college

The same report says the house where he lived is being made into a memorial. It says a degree college has been approved and is being built in the village.

## How to read this

Garima Times wrote a piece that praises the government and the Chief Minister. It does not cite a government order or any outside report. We did not find a second source for the dates or the details of the bridge.

So we tell it as the site tells it. The dates and the claims about the bridge come from that one source. We have not seen the bridge, the statue or the college.

## Why it matters

A hero's memory does not live only in speeches. It also lives in a road, a school, or a bridge that lets a sick child reach a doctor.

Roshan Singh gave his life in 1927. If the report is right, then it took until 2018 for his village to get a permanent bridge over the river near it. The gap between those two dates is worth thinking about.
