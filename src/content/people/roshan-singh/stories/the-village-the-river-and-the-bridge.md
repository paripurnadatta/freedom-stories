Where do the heroes of the past come from? Often from small villages that few people have heard of.

Roshan Singh was born in Nawada, in the Shahjahanpur district of Uttar Pradesh. Satyarth Prakash Nyas, a Hindi website, and Garima Times, a Hindi news site, both name the village. This story is about what the village looked like long after he was gone. It is told as one news site tells it.

## A river in the way

Nawada lies near the Khannaut river. For decades after independence, there was no bridge over it, according to Garima Times.

When the rains came, the river rose. The area was cut off from the main roads for four or five months at a time. Sick people could not reach hospitals. Children could not get to school. Farmers could not take their crops to the market on time.

Three generations of villagers lived like this, the site says.

## The bridge

Villagers met Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath and asked for a bridge. First they asked for a pontoon bridge, which floats on boats or drums. Then, on 25 February 2018, in Shahjahanpur, the Chief Minister approved a permanent bridge over the Khannaut. He also approved roads and a degree college.

On 30 December 2018, after the bridge was built, he visited Nawada. He paid respects at a statue of Roshan Singh at the family home, and honoured elders of the family. Many villagers were moved to tears, the site says.

Now, it adds, ambulances reach the village in time. Students can travel to school and college, and farmers can reach the market.

## The house and the college

The house where Roshan Singh lived is being made into a memorial. A degree college has been approved and is being built in the village.

We found no second source for the dates or the details of the bridge. We have not seen the bridge, the statue or the college.</aside>

## Why it matters

A hero's memory does not live only in speeches. It also lives in a road, a school, or a bridge that lets a sick child reach a doctor.

Roshan Singh gave his life in 1927. If the report is right, it took until 2018 for his village to get a permanent bridge over the river near it. The gap between those two dates is worth thinking about.
