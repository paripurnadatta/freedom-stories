## 1. Sources, basic facts, chronology and uncertainties

**Names:** Thakur Roshan Singh, also Raushan/Roshan Singh. [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf), a retrospective institutional court history, spells his name "Raushan Singh"; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf), National Book Trust's *Hanged for Patriotism* printed pp.44–46, uses "Roshan Singh". [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/) identifies Thakur Roshan Singh among the four executed after the Kakori proceedings.

**Birth conflict:** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) says he was born in **1894**, Shahjahanpur district; [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh) (Wikipedia, poorly sourced) says **22 January 1892**, village Nabada; a published Ministry of Culture dictionary's search preview also says 1894, **but the PDF itself failed to open** and is NOT counted as checked. Do not declare a settled birth year or age.

**Family:** no independently inspected original records for father, spouse, child or siblings. [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) gives education through middle school and primary-teacher experience. The memoir dialogue and prison visitors are uncorroborated.

**Non-Cooperation era:** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) reports activity in Shahjahanpur and Bareilly during Gandhian Non-Cooperation, imprisonment for about two years following a Bareilly firing incident. [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh) repeats a similar sequence, though poorly cited. Exact court docket and years remain unchecked.

**Organisation:** Later joined the **Hindustan Republican Association (HRA)** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh); [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) mentions the seized HRA constitution. The name HSRA is **wrong** for acts before September 1928.

**Kakori question:** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf), printed p.45, says Roshan **“was not even present”** at the main scene of the Kakori train robbery, though tried in the wider **Kakori conspiracy** case. [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh) states he did not participate in that action; [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) says charges involved **four** robberies, including Bamrauli and Bichpuri, but does not isolate individual perpetrators. A Ministry of Culture *Dictionary of Martyrs* summary visible only as **search result** describes him as involved in the train robbery; the official PDF itself returned 502, so that wording remains **unverified as an opened document** and cannot serve as a source for publishing the contrary view. Report the uncertainty; do not assert his train presence.

**Court:** The case heard in Lucknow ended with Judge Hamilton's **6 April 1927** verdict [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf), [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf). In NBT, the death sentence is linked specifically to **Bamrauli robbery** and evidence described by the judge, not a finding of Roshan's physical presence on the Kakori train. [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) confirms major offences and murder prosecutions in same wider case.

**Execution:** **19 December 1927** at Allahabad/Naini jail [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf), [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/), [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh). Another three condemned: Bismil at Gorakhpur, Ashfaqulla at Faizabad; Rajendra Lahiri at Gonda **17 Dec** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf), [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/). No authenticated last words.

**Connections:** Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Rajendra Lahiri, HRA, Non-Cooperation and Kakori conspiracy; Shahjahanpur, Bareilly, Bamrauli, Bichpuri, Lucknow and Allahabad/Naini jail [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/).

**Sources opened:** H—Allahabad High Court history PDF page index 6 https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf; N—NBT PDF screenshots of chapter printed pp.44–46 https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf; I—Indian Express 2023 long explainer https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/; W—Wikipedia Roshan Singh https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh (use only for explicit conflicting version, not alone as established evidence); M—scanned historical book on Kakori prosecution from a politically affiliated digital library https://library.bjp.org/jspui/bitstream/123456789/117/13/Chapter6_368-429p.pdf PDF p.385, screenshot inspected; C—Wikimedia Commons file page https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roshan_Singh.jpg. ## 2. Ten independently evaluated story angles

1. **His name in a case about a train — SHORT.** Hook: can a person be tried in the Kakori case without proof that he rode the train? [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) distinguishes four prosecuted robberies; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) printed p.45 expressly says Roshan was not present at the Kakori scene. [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh) supports the latter, but is weakly sourced. **Dispute** with government dictionary search snippet inaccessible at original: physical participation **not conclusively authenticated**, so phrase carefully. The short explains case breadth and the death penalty without declaring exoneration.
2. **From a protest to a secret group — SHORT, cautious.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) pp.44–45 describes his Non-Cooperation participation and Bareilly imprisonment; [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh) mentions the same, though likely repeats shared traditional materials; [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/) provides independent broad HRA/nonviolence context, not independent confirmation of Roshan's exact criminal docket. Explain uncertainty, avoid invented protest scene.
3. **What the judge meant by two sentences — THIN.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) attributes to Judge Hamilton five years for conspiracy and death for Bamrauli; [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) independently gives conspiracy and robbery charges and the death judgment, but not full Roshan-specific reasoning. No original judicial decree authenticated.
4. **The Bamrauli village case — THIN.** [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) dates Bamrauli to 25 Dec 1924 and notes a death there; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) identifies Roshan as associated. Witness details only secondary. No independent original court evidence to write a standalone scene.
5. **Why Gandhi stopped Non-Cooperation — THIN personal angle.** [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/) provides independent 1922 Chauri Chaura context and political differences; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) links Roshan's changed path, [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshan_Singh) similarly. Actual private motives not known; do not state his thoughts.
6. **A trial involving many prisoners — THIN individual angle.** [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) says 24 persons prosecuted and 18 convicted in main case; [M](https://library.bjp.org/jspui/bitstream/123456789/117/13/Chapter6_368-429p.pdf) retells 25 initial accused and other counts. Different stages of proceedings may explain discrepancy. Need original docket before a numbers-focused story.
7. **Learning Bengali in prison — THIN.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) printed p.46 offers remembered conversation, no independent contemporaneous proof. The quoted talk is **UNVERIFIED** and excluded.
8. **Letters from the last week — THIN.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) gives a supposed 13 Dec 1927 letter; no original manuscript, signature or reliable first publication trace opened. **UNVERIFIED**; cannot use quoted words in story.
9. **Four places on a single week — SHORT as context, not standalone.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) and [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-robbery-action-9074349/) give distinct jail cities and the Dec 17/19 dates; can close Angle 01 or 02 without adding a near-duplicate story.
10. **A photograph passed from book to Commons — THIN.** [C](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roshan_Singh.jpg) is a public-domain image scanned from Bengali memoir (*Jele Trish Bachar...*, Dhaka 2004 p.243), unknown original author and disputed dating.
