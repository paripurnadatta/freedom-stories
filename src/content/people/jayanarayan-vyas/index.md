## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Marwar political organizer, journalist and later Chief Minister of Rajasthan whose pre-independence importance lies in building political organizations demanding civil liberties and responsible government in Jodhpur State. He became closely identified with the Marwar Lok Parishad and nationalist press.

### Chronology

- **1930s-1940s**: Led/participated in Marwar political organizations.... Led/participated in Marwar political organizations seeking civil liberties and responsible government

### Evidence claims

- Jai Narayan Vyas was a major political figure from Jodhpur/Marwar and is institutionally remembered as a social reformer and freedom-era leader. (high confidence)
- Public records identify him as an active member of the Marwar Lok Parishad and editor of Tarun Rajasthan. (high confidence)
- His politics focused on representative government and civil rights within princely Marwar. (medium_high confidence)
