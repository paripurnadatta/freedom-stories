### Evidence claims

- Ganesh Ghosh was born on 22 June 1900 and died on 16 October 1994. (HIGH confidence)
- After release from earlier detention, Ghosh associated with Surya Sen, joined Jugantar and became active in revolutionary politics. (HIGH confidence)
- On 18 April 1930 Ganesh Ghosh participated in the Chittagong Armoury Raid; Banglapedia identifies him with Ananta Singh as a leader of the police-armoury group. (HIGH confidence)
- After arrest, Ghosh was sentenced to life imprisonment in 1932 and was confined in the Cellular Jail until his release in 1946. (HIGH confidence)
- After independence, Ghosh was elected to the West Bengal Legislative Assembly in 1952, 1957 and 1962 and to the Lok Sabha in 1967. (HIGH confidence)
