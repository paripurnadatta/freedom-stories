## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Parbati Giri's connection with Khadi movement; Quit India Movement.

### Overview

Western Odisha Congress activist who entered nationalist organizational work at a very young age and later participated in Quit India. Ministry profiles remember her both for anti-colonial mobilization and for extensive post-independence social work.

### Evidence claims

- Parbati Giri was born in 1926 in Samlaipadar and was drawn into Congress political activity through the nationalist environment of her village. (high confidence)
- She left school young and worked with local Congress organizers. (high confidence)
- She participated in Quit India and later became known for major social-service work in western Odisha. (high confidence)
