## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Ghoolam Ali's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Participant in the Patna uprising of 3 July 1857 who was captured and ordered hanged four days later.

### Chronology

- **1857-07-03**: Participated in the Patna uprising
- **1857-07-07**: Ordered to be hanged after capture

### Evidence claims

- Ghoolam Ali took part in the Patna uprising against the British on 3 July 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured by Company troops. (HIGH confidence)
- He was ordered to be hanged on 7 July 1857. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Patna: 1857 uprising, capture and execution context
