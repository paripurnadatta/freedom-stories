## Research notes

Led the Khudai Khidmatgar movement.

### Nonviolence on the Frontier

Ghaffar Khan shows that disciplined nonviolent resistance developed in a distinct Pashtun political context.

### Khudai Khidmatgar

The organization must remain a first-class historical institution, not merely a Congress auxiliary.

### The North-West Frontier Province

Historical geography is essential because the movement unfolded in a region now largely within Pakistan.

### Chronology

- **1919**: Rowlatt protest and imprisonment. Faces imprisonment during early anti-colonial mobilization in the Frontier
- **1929**: Khudai Khidmatgar founded. Builds a disciplined Pashtun movement committed to nonviolent resistance
- **1930s**: Congress alliance in the Frontier. Khudai Khidmatgar becomes deeply linked with wider Congress anti-colonial politics
