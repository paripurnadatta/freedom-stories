## 1. Identity, dates, connected people and source register
- **Rajendra Nath Lahiri**, also Rajendranath Lahiri / Lahiri (often transliterated Lahiri or Lahiri). The [Allahabad High Court's history of Avadh courts](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf), PDF page 7, explicitly names “Rajendra Nath Lahiri” among people sentenced in the Kakori case.
- **Birth dispute:** *The Wire* [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) says 1901, Pabna district (then Bengal, now Bangladesh); R. K. Tandon's *Hanged for Patriotism* [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf), printed p.40 / PDF index 39, says 1892, Mohanpur in Pabna. Neither claim is checked against an original birth record. Keep the date as a YEAR DISPUTE. Wikipedia and the site's index give 1901 but these are not independent primary birth documentation.
- **Execution:** [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) dates death **17 December 1927**; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) independently has 17 December at Gonda. High Court's published case summary [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) records the 6 April 1927 sentence; distinguishes other condemned prisoners and sentences.
- **Education / ideas:** [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) describes Varanasi studies, Sachindranath Sanyal, university literary work and disagreements about religious practice; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) gives some Kashi activities but has chronological/name inaccuracies. School registers, original articles and archived 1958 reminiscences not opened.
- **HRA:** Hindustan Republican Association **before September 1928** [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf); do NOT call his pre-1928 circle HSRA, anachronistically as [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) does.
- **Kakori action:** **9 August 1925**, not 1927. [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) records government treasury robbery, passenger Ahmad Ali's death, and legal charges. [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-action-full-story-british-raj-10825020/lite/) records Lahiri pulling the train cord and the 1925 date. [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) repeats the cord detail but mistakenly types **9 August 1927** in one paragraph.
- **Connected people / places:** Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla Khan, Roshan Singh, Sachindranath Sanyal, Chandra Shekhar Azad, Manmath Nath Gupta; Pabna, Varanasi, Kakori/Lucknow, Dakshineswar and Gonda prison [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp).

## 2. Ten possible story angles (no padding)
1. **The cord on the Kakori train — SHORT.** The train was stopped on **9 Aug 1925** to take government funds [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf), [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-action-full-story-british-raj-10825020/lite/); Lahiri reportedly pulled the cord [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-action-full-story-british-raj-10825020/lite/), [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp). The train held ordinary passengers and Ahmad Ali died [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf), [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-action-full-story-british-raj-10825020/lite/). Disagreement: Wire erroneously writes **1927** in its Kakori paragraph; use multiple 1925 sources. Exact carriage or private speech not checked.
2. **Four places, four sentences — SHORT.** Kakori case death sentences, different prisons, Lahiri executed at Gonda on **17 Dec** ahead of others [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf), [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf), [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp). Never narrate undocumented final dialogue. Reason for earlier execution **UNSURE**: NBT confidently attributes a rescue leak, without documentary citation; other historical literature says uncorroborated.
3. **The student who debated religion — THIN.** [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) relies on Manmath Nath Gupta / Jogesh Chandra Chatterji's retrospective accounts of Lahiri and caste customs. [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) refers to his studies, but no independently opened text substantiates specific sacred-thread incident. Hold standalone story.
4. **A young organiser in Banaras — THIN.** [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) gives local HRA leadership and studies; [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) identifies Varanasi education but differs on birth and timeline. No primary association membership or university document. Hold.
5. **The letters nobody has checked — THIN.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) reproduces supposed October and December 1927 letters. A scan or family archive is not provided, so their verbatim wording is **UNVERIFIED**. Exclude historical direct speech.
6. **The Dalshineswar/Dakshineswar case — THIN.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) mentions associated bombing activities, [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) describes Bengal travels. Cannot confidently connect exact act, date and charges without case files. Hold.
7. **The HRA manifesto and its republic — SHORT as general context, THIN for Rajendra's direct authorship.** [I](https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-history/kakori-train-action-full-story-british-raj-10825020/lite/) quotes the HRA manifesto and [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) describes a manifesto seized by police; [W](https://m.thewire.in/article/history/rajendranath-lahiri-indian-revolutionary/amp) relates Lahiri to the movement, not authorship. Could be told as context, but do not attribute writing to Lahiri.
8. **Justice at the Ring Theatre — THIN.** [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) says trial heard at a theatre in Lucknow; [H](https://www.allahabadhighcourt.in/event/HistoryOfTheCourtInAvadhFrom1856HKGhose.pdf) verifies the case and its judicial proceedings but does not confirm stage details. No drawing or dated theatre photo opened; exclude scene.
9. **A prisoner in two stories — THIN.** Different books say rescue threats explain the earlier execution [N](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf); a second revolutionary historian questions corroboration (linked PDF could not be opened). Avoid choosing a reason and dramatizing a secret rescue.
10. **A portrait and a memorial plaque — THIN standalone, useful media.** Commons file [C](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Revolutionary_Rajendra_Lahiri.jpg) names Punjab State Archives and PD India; [P](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rajendra_Prasad_Lahiri_Inscription.jpg) is a 2012 CC BY-SA 3.0 modern memorial-inscription photograph. Neither proves the historical claims written on the plaque; useful with caption and credit.
