**Independent support and limits:** The High Court history [H] and Indian Express [I] agree Kakori action year 1925. The Wire [W] contains the obvious typo **“August 9, 1927”** in a paragraph; do not use it for that year. The reason Lahiri was hanged two days earlier is not authenticated by original prison orders; NBT's account of a foiled rescue is a later claim, not a verified conclusion. Reproduced farewell letters in NBT were not checked against manuscript and cannot be quoted as if their origin is proven. The 1901/1892 birth conflict remains unresolved.
