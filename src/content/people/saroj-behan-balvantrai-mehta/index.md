## Research notes

Documented association with Rajkot Satyagraha / Quit India / women in Gujarat.

### Overview

Bhavnagar nationalist organizer who moved from student activism into princely-state political work, Rajkot Satyagraha and Quit India.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Student/local nationalist organizing in Bhavnagar
- **1938**: Participated in Rajkot Satyagraha
- **1942**: Detained during Quit India in Bhavnagar

### Evidence claims

- Saroj Behan Balvantrai Mehta participated in prabhat pheris, padyatras and local village mobilization from her student days. (HIGH confidence)
- She joined the Bhavnagar Rajkiya Parishad and became an active member. (HIGH confidence)
- She participated in the Rajkot Satyagraha in 1938 and was detained in Bhavnagar during Quit India in 1942. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bhavnagar: Rajkiya Parishad and Quit India activity
- Rajkot: Rajkot Satyagraha
