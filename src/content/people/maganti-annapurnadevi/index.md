## Research notes

Documented association with Non-Cooperation / women in Andhra Pradesh.

### Overview

Andhra nationalist and khadi organizer who responded to Gandhi's movement through material sacrifice, public campaigning and institution-building before her early death.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Responded to Gandhi's call, donated jewellery and intensified nationalist work
- **1921-1924**: Promoted khadi/Swaraj and built the Mohandas Khaddar Parisramalayam
- **1927**: Died after prolonged ill health

### Evidence claims

- Maganti Annapurna Devi gave jewellery to Gandhi's nationalist fundraising drive during his Andhra tour in 1921. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)
- She adopted khadi and toured in Andhra to raise funds and support Swaraj-oriented work. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)
- She founded the Mohandas Khaddar Parisramalayam at Ellore/Eluru as a constructive nationalist institution. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry of Culture index includes Maganti Annapurna Devi among West Godavari freedom fighters. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Eluru / West Godavari: Khadi and nationalist institution-building
- Andhra Desa: Public campaigning and fundraising
