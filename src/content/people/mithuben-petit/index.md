## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Parsi Gandhian activist who mobilized women in Bardoli and participated in Salt Satyagraha and constructive work.

### Chronology

- **1928**: Led women's mobilization in the Bardoli Satyagraha
- **1930-04**: Participated in Salt Satyagraha, including the Bhimrad phase

### Evidence claims

- Mithuben Petit was a prominent woman leader in the 1928 Bardoli Satyagraha, addressing meetings and organizing women. (HIGH confidence)
- Her Bardoli work included khadi promotion, constructive work and women's mobilization. (HIGH confidence)
- She also participated in the Salt March/Salt Satyagraha context and was present with Gandhi at Bhimrad in April 1930. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bardoli / Surat: Peasant satyagraha and women's organizing
- Bhimrad / Gujarat coast: Salt Satyagraha
