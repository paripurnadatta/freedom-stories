Documented in the cited source in connection with Paika resistance in Khordha.

### Historical geography

Khurda region: Company-ruled Odisha (Paika resistance context)

### Overview

A participant in the Paika resistance associated with Bakshi Jagabandhu. The accessible strong-source base is notably thinner than for Jagabandhu: Odisha government historical writing places a Krushna Chandra alongside Bakshi in the later surrender/settlement phase and indicates that his property was targeted by colonial repression. This profile should remain narrow and evidence-conscious until archival identification is expanded.
