## Research notes

Early southern resistance to Company expansion.

### Before the better-known Polygar wars

Puli Thevar belongs to an earlier phase in which local sovereignty, tribute and Company-backed revenue collection collided in the Tamil south.

### A confederacy, not an isolated duel

His importance lies in the effort to organize western Palayakkarars into a broader resistance rather than in a single battle.

### Chronology

- **1755**: Company expedition and tribute conflict. The Nawab and Company move militarily into Tirunelveli as Puli Thevar refuses submission and becomes a centre of western Palayakkarar resistance
- **1767**: End of the long resistance phase. Nerkattumseval is eventually captured after years of conflict; official educational material treats the campaign as an early anti-British resistance
