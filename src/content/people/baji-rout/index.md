## Research notes

Youngest known martyr of the movement in Odisha; killed while resisting police.

### Chronology

- **1938-10-11/1938-10-12**: Killed during the Praja Mandal confrontation. ['Sources commonly distinguish the night of 11 October from the commemorated date 12 October; do not over-normalize without archival confirmation.']

### Evidence claims

- Baji Rout was born on 5 October 1926 at Nilakanthapur in Dhenkanal. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Dhenkanal Praja Mandal movement through its Banar Sena youth organization. (HIGH confidence)
- He was shot after refusing to ferry armed police across the Brahmani river during the Praja Mandal agitation in October 1938. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Nilakanthapur, Dhenkanal: birthplace and movement context
