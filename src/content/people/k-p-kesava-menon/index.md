## Research notes

Nationalist journalist and organizer.

### Overview

Malabar Congress leader, journalist and satyagrahi whose work ranged from Non-Cooperation and Vaikom to the Indian Independence League in Southeast Asia.

### Chronology

- **1920-1921**: Joined Non-Cooperation and Malabar Congress leadership
- **1923**: Helped establish Mathrubhumi
- **1924**: Participated in Vaikom Satyagraha and was imprisoned
- **1940s**: Joined Indian Independence League in Singapore and was arrested in 1944

### Evidence claims

- K. P. Kesava Menon gave up law practice to join Non-Cooperation and became a prominent Congress leader in Malabar. (HIGH confidence)
- He founded Mathrubhumi in 1923 as a nationalist newspaper. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Vaikom Satyagraha and was imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)
- He later joined the Indian Independence League in Singapore and was arrested in 1944. (HIGH confidence)

- Calicut / Kozhikode: Congress and journalism
- Vaikom: Vaikom Satyagraha
