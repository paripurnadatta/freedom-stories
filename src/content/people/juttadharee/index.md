## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Juttadharee's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Native Infantry sapper who joined the 1857 uprising, was captured during the British recapture of Arrah and was condemned to death.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Joined the uprising
- **1857-1858**: Captured in the Arrah campaign and sentenced to death

### Evidence claims

- Juttadharee belonged to a Sappers Regiment of the Native Infantry in East India Company service. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the 1857 uprising and was arrested during fighting connected with the British recapture of Arrah. (HIGH confidence)
- He was convicted under colonial law and ordered hanged, with execution arrangements sent to Dinapore/Danapur. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Arrah: Capture during 1857 campaign
- Dinapore / Danapur: Execution-transfer context
