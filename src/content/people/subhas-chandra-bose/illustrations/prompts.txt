ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Subhas Chandra Bose
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Netaji's face may be shown: many real photographs of him exist. Keep it close
to them (round face, round glasses).

Calcutta, the middle of a cold January night in 1941. A large old colonial-era house with a gate and a dim lamp. A black 1930s German sedan car with its headlights low rolls quietly out of the gate onto an empty street. At the wheel, a young Indian man in a shirt; in the back seat, half in shadow, a stocky Indian man in his forties with a round face, round wire-rimmed glasses and a high forehead now wearing a full dark beard, a long brown coat and a small black fez cap, looking out of the window. Across the street, two plain-clothes watchers in shawls are dozing under a lamp post. Deep blue night, mist, a single warm glow from the car's interior. Wide composition, 16:9.
