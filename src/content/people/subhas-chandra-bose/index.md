## Research notes

Congress leader who later led the INA and Provisional Government of Free India.

### From Congress president to wartime commander

Bose’s career should show the strategic rupture between mass-party politics and the armed international campaign he later pursued.

### Forward Bloc

The Forward Bloc emerged from a concrete political dispute inside the Congress rather than as a separate starting point.

### Azad Hind and the INA

The government-in-exile, army and civilian mobilisation formed a linked political-military project.

### Rani of Jhansi Regiment

The women’s regiment connects Bose’s military strategy directly to the existing enriched profile of Lakshmi Sahgal.

### Chronology

- **1939**: Forward Bloc formed. Breaks with the dominant Congress strategy and creates a new political organization
- **1943-10-21**: Provisional Government of Free India. Proclaims Azad Hind government in Singapore
- **1943-1944**: INA mobilisation. Expands military and administrative mobilisation across Southeast Asia

### Evidence claims

- Formed the Forward Bloc in 1939 after resigning from the Congress presidency amid strategic conflict. (high confidence)
- Proclaimed the Provisional Government of Free India in Singapore on 21 October 1943. (high confidence)
- Called for total mobilisation of Indians in East Asia in support of the Indian National Army in 1944. (high confidence)
- Announced the formation of the Rani of Jhansi Regiment within the INA in 1943. (high confidence)
