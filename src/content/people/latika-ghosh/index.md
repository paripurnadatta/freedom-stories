## Research notes

Documented association with women / nationalist organizing in Bengal.

### Overview

Bengal women's political organizer associated with Anti-Simon mobilization, the 1928 Congress women's volunteers and the Mahila Rashtriya Sangha.

### Chronology

- **1928**: Organized women in Anti-Simon and Congress volunteer activity in Calcutta
- **1928**: Helped establish and lead the Mahila Rashtriya Sangha for women's political work

### Evidence claims

- Latika Ghosh was among prominent women associated with the Women's Unit/Bengal Volunteers during the 1928 Congress session and Anti-Simon agitation. (HIGH confidence)
- Academic research identifies her as a founder/organizer of the Mahila Rashtriya Sangha in 1928 for women's political mobilization. (HIGH confidence)
- She recruited and organized women volunteers in connection with the 1928 Calcutta Congress session. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Calcutta: Anti-Simon protest, Congress session and women's organizing
