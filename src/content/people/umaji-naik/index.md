## Research notes

Led armed Ramoshi resistance against British rule in the Pune region.

### Overview

Ramoshi leader in western India who waged armed resistance against British authority after the collapse of Maratha power. Ministry of Culture sources describe guerrilla attacks, proclamations, an expanding following and eventual arrest and execution in Pune in 1832.
