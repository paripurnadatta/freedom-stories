## Research notes

Important leader associated with fakir resistance against Company rule.

### Overview

Madariya Sufi fakir and the most prominent leader of the Fakir-Sannyasi resistance in late eighteenth-century Bengal and adjoining Bihar. Scholarly work grounded in Company correspondence traces mobile armed resistance, attacks on Company/revenue establishments, support among villagers and Majnu Shah's attempts to defend traditional fakir mobility and collection practices against colonial restrictions.
