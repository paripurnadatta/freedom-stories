### Overview

Bombay lawyer, municipal reformer and major early Congress constitutionalist. He helped found the Bombay Presidency Association, played a formative role in the Indian National Congress, and presided over its 1890 Calcutta session. His politics stressed constitutional reform, public institutions and liberal methods, while his municipal work and later journalism made him a central figure in the civic infrastructure of early nationalism.
