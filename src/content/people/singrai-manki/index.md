## Research notes

Documented association with Kol uprising in Jharkhand.

### Overview

Manki leader associated with the Kol uprising of 1831-33 in Chotanagpur. Government-linked reference material explicitly lists the Kol Rebellion as led by Singhrai Manki and Bindrai Manki. More detailed narrative sources connect the revolt to dispossession, outsider revenue intermediaries and erosion of traditional authority.

### Chronology

- **1831-1833**: Led or co-led sections of the Kol uprising in Chot.... Led or co-led sections of the Kol uprising in Chotanagpur

### Evidence claims

- Singrai Manki is listed as a leader of the Kol Rebellion of 1831-33. (high confidence)
- The Kol uprising arose in the Chotanagpur region amid colonial administrative and land-tenure disruption. (high confidence)
- His leadership belongs to the Sonpur/Singhbhum-Chotanagpur resistance network rather than to the later Santhal Hul. (high confidence)
