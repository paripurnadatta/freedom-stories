## Research notes

Built Sevadal networks in Garhwal and Kumaon, led local peasant resistance and participated in a 1942 attempt to disrupt British control at Lansdowne.

### Overview

Garhwal peasant and anti-colonial organizer who linked forest-rights agitation, civil resistance and Quit India-era mobilization across the hill districts.

### Chronology

- **1920-1921**: First encountered colonial policing as a school-age protester
- **late 1920s-1930s**: Led Gujadu/forest-rights and peasant agitation
- **1932-09-06**: Participated in an anti-governor protest in Pauri
- **1942-1945**: Active in Quit India-era Garhwal resistance and imprisoned until July 1945

### Evidence claims

- Ram Prasad Nautiyal was born on 1 August 1905 in Kanda, Bironkhal, Pauri Garhwal. (HIGH confidence)
- He became a major organizer of the Gujadu peasant movement and forest-rights agitation in Garhwal. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in a 1932 protest against Governor Malcolm Hailey and experienced repeated imprisonment under colonial rule. (HIGH confidence)
- During Quit India he led anti-colonial mobilization in the Lansdowne region and remained imprisoned until 14 July 1945. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kanda / Bironkhal / Pauri Garhwal: Home and peasant organizing
- Lansdowne: Quit India-era resistance
- Garhwal / Kumaon: Forest-rights and Seva Dal organizing
