## Research notes

Documented association with 1857 / Sambalpur resistance in Odisha.

### Historical geography

Ghess: Sambalpur region (Madho Singh's zamindari base)

### Historical geography

Singoda Ghati: Strategic pass near Sambalpur (Defensive position held by Madho Singh and followers)

### Overview

Madho Singh, identified in Odisha historical writing as Madhab Singh Bariha, was the elderly Zamindar of Ghess who supported Veer Surendra Sai during the Sambalpur phase of the 1857 uprising. He and his sons defended the strategic Singoda Ghati route, helping sustain rebel communications and resistance. The evidence base is narrower than for major national leaders, so the profile should stay closely tied to documented Sambalpur resistance rather than broad biographical claims.

### Chronology

### Evidence claims

- Madho Singh is identified as Madhab Singh Bariha, the Zamindar of Ghess, and was already elderly during the 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
