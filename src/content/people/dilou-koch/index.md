## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Dilou Koch's connection with Peasant anti-colonial resistance.

### Overview

Peasant rebel from Mangaldoi in Darrang district killed in the January 1894 anti-revenue rising. The ICHR Dictionary of Martyrs identifies him as being at the forefront when police firing killed him, with underlying citations to Assam archive files.
