## Research notes

Led tribal and peasant mobilization in Mewar.

### Overview

Leader of the Eki movement among Bhil and Garasiya communities in southern Rajasthan and adjoining Gujarat. The movement protested begar, high revenue and princely/jagirdari exploitation. Tejawat's politics drew inspiration from Bijolia and Gandhian idioms but developed within a distinct tribal-peasant movement in princely western India.
