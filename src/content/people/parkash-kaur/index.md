## Research notes

Official profile documents student procession activity and nine-month Quit India imprisonment.

### Overview

Punjab freedom activist who assisted revolutionary networks, participated in student protest and later faced imprisonment during Quit India.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Participated in student/nationalist activity and supported revolutionary messaging
- **1942**: Joined Quit India activity, was arrested in Lyallpur and imprisoned for nine months

### Evidence claims

- Parkash Kaur was influenced by Bhagat Singh and helped revolutionary activists by passing messages. (HIGH confidence)
- She participated in student protest during the Civil Disobedience period. (HIGH confidence)
- During Quit India she left her hospital job, was arrested in Lyallpur and was sentenced to nine months' imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Ferozepur / Amritsar: Education and nationalist activity
- Lyallpur: Quit India arrest
