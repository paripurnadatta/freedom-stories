## Research notes

Long-running resistance against British takeover in Sambalpur.

### Overview

Sambalpur royal claimant and long-running leader of anti-British resistance whose career spanned pre-1857 conflict and the 1857-62 guerrilla phase. Government sources emphasize disputes over succession and annexation, his imprisonment at Hazaribagh, release during the 1857 uprising, reorganization of regional and tribal supporters, and surrender in 1862 followed by later detention.

### Evidence claims

- Surendra Sai was born in 1809 near Sambalpur and became a major leader of resistance to British rule. (high confidence)
- The Sambalpur resistance developed in two major phases, including a renewed 1857-62 guerrilla campaign after his release from Hazaribagh jail. (high confidence)
- He surrendered on 16 May 1862 after years of guerrilla resistance. (high confidence)
