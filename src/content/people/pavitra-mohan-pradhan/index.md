## Research notes

Documented association with Talcher Praja Mandal in Odisha.

### Historical geography

Talcher: Princely state, Odisha (Primary political base)

### Historical geography

Angul: British Odisha adjacent to Talcher (Refuge/Hizarat camp context)

### Overview

President of the Talcher State Prajamandal and one of the strongest organisers of anti-autocratic politics in the Odisha princely states. He helped lead the 1938 Talcher/Hizarat agitation against forced labour, forest restrictions and princely repression, endured imprisonment, and remained active when Quit India reached Talcher in 1942. Official accounts connect his disappearance/rumoured death to the collapse of state authority and the short-lived parallel-government episode.

### Historical significance

### Chronology

- **1938-11**: Talcher/Hizarat agitation expanded under Pradhan's leadership
- **1940**: Pradhan was imprisoned in connection with the Praja agitation
- **1942-08-30**: Escaped from Talcher jail during the Quit India phase, according to Odisha Review
- **1942-08-31**: Talcher's administration collapsed amid popular upsurge and a parallel-government experiment

### Evidence claims

- Pavitra Mohan Pradhan was a principal leader in the Talcher Praja Mandal movement and is identified as President of the Talcher State Prajamandal. (HIGH confidence)
- He led or strongly shaped the 1938 Talcher/Hizarat agitation and was later imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)
- He escaped from Talcher jail during the Quit India phase in 1942 and continued underground activity. (HIGH confidence)
- A rumour that he had been killed helped trigger the 1942 popular upsurge in Talcher, where a parallel governmental structure briefly emerged. (HIGH confidence)
