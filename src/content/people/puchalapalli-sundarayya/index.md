## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Formation of the Indian National Congress.

### Overview

Andhra communist and peasant organizer whose anti-colonial activity began in student protest and Salt Satyagraha and later moved into agricultural-worker and anti-zamindari mobilization.

### Chronology

- **1928**: Participated in the Simon Commission boycott
- **1930**: Participated in Salt Satyagraha in West Godavari
- **1930s**: Built agricultural-worker and anti-zamindari organization in Andhra
- **1940s**: Became a leading figure in the Telangana peasant/communist struggle

### Evidence claims

- Puchalapalli Sundarayya participated in the 1928 Simon Commission boycott. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Salt Satyagraha in West Godavari. (HIGH confidence)
- He helped build agricultural-worker and anti-zamindari organizing in Andhra in the 1930s. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Nellore / Andhra region: Peasant and communist organizing
- West Godavari: Salt Satyagraha
- Telangana: Late-colonial peasant struggle
