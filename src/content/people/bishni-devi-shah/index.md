## Research notes

Hoisted the national flag at the Almora municipal office in 1930 and was jailed; later remembered as a pioneering woman freedom fighter from Uttarakhand.

### Overview

Kumaoni freedom activist who raised the national flag at Almora in 1930, was arrested and became an important symbol of women's participation in Uttarakhand's freedom movement.

### Chronology

- **1930-05-25**: Participated in the Almora flag-hoisting action and was arrested
- **1930s**: Continued nationalist/Swadeshi-oriented activity after imprisonment

### Evidence claims

- Bishni Devi Shah participated in the 1930 national-flag action at Almora municipality. (HIGH confidence)
- She was arrested for this activity and is identified in Uttarakhand official educational material as the first woman from the region arrested in the freedom struggle. (HIGH confidence)
- The President of India has publicly recalled her contribution among Uttarakhand's freedom fighters. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Almora / Kumaon: Flag-hoisting and nationalist activity
