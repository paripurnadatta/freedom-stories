## Research notes

Led the Rampa rebellion against colonial forest and administrative policies.

### Documented record

Led the Rampa Rebellion of 1922–24. The rebellion developed amid colonial forest restrictions that disrupted traditional podu cultivation and movement. Remembered locally as Manyam Veerudu.

### Chronology

- **1922**: Rampa Rebellion begins
- **1922-1924**: Forest-region campaign
- **1924**: Rebellion suppressed
