## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Palnadu peasant leader associated with the Pullari no-tax/forest-grazing agitation during the Non-Cooperation era. Ministry sources describe the movement as resistance to colonial taxes imposed on grazing and collection of forest produce. Hanumanthu became its most remembered local leader and was shot during the confrontation at Minchalapadu in February 1922.
