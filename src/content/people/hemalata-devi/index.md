## Research notes

Official profile documents 1935 arrest and imprisonment for anti-government speech.

### Overview

Ganjam freedom activist who worked in Congress-linked ashram networks and was imprisoned after anti-government political speech in 1935.

### Chronology

- **1934-1935**: Entered deeper Congress/ashram freedom work in Odisha
- **1935**: Arrested after political speech and detained at Berhampur/Vellore

### Evidence claims

- Hemalata Devi lived and worked with the Rama Devi/Malati Devi ashram network after marriage and assisted freedom-struggle and social-work activity. (HIGH confidence)
- She trained at Puri's Azad Sevashram. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1935 she and Gouri Shankar Samant were arrested after anti-government speech activity and were imprisoned at Berhampur and later Vellore. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Ganjam / Khallikote: Political and social-work base
- Berhampur / Vellore: Colonial detention
