## Research notes

Participated in the Assembly bombing and served a long prison term.

### Documented record

Born 18 November 1910 and was a member of the HSRA. With Bhagat Singh, threw bombs in the Central Legislative Assembly on 8 April 1929 and surrendered.

### Documented actions

Bhagat Singh and Dutt issued a joint court statement on 6 June 1929 explaining the political intent of the action. Participated in the political-prisoner hunger strike demanding improved treatment.

### Chronology

- **1929-04-08**: Central Legislative Assembly bombing
- **1929-06-06**: Joint court statement
- **1929**: Political-prisoner hunger strike
