### Overview

Lawyer and constitutional politician from Bengal who became active in the Congress in the 1890s and presided over its 1915 Bombay session. His career is historically distinctive because he sought political advance through legal and governmental institutions, becoming the first Indian Advocate-General of Bengal and later the first Indian member of the Viceroy's Executive Council. His profile belongs in the constitutionalist strand, while noting the tensions between office-holding and nationalist politics.
