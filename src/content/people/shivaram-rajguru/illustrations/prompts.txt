ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Shivaram Hari Rajguru
Only stories without a suitable free archival picture are listed here.
These prompts describe symbolic scenes. They are not claims that these exact photographed scenes exist.

==============================================================================
1. Nine paise and a long walk (short)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: nine-paise-and-a-long-walk
Save as nine-paise-and-a-long-walk.png in this folder.
Shape: extra wide 16:6, 2400 x 900.
Where it goes: story top and card for "Nine paise and a long walk"

PROMPT:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A young teenage boy seen from far behind, walking alone on a long dusty country road towards a distant river town in the 1920s. He wears a simple dhoti and a plain shirt, with nothing in his hands. Dry fields on both sides, a few trees, a faint line of temple spires and stone riverside steps on the horizon. No face shown, no invented portrait. Small and alone in a wide landscape, hopeful mood. Wide 16:6 composition.
Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, warm saffron and amber light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green. Historically accurate 1900s–1940s Indian clothing, architecture and objects. Faces calm and dignified. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no borders. No gore, no blood, no weapons pointed at anyone. Do not include the Indian national flag or the Ashoka Chakra.

2. A court with fewer doors (short)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: a-court-with-fewer-doors
Save as a-court-with-fewer-doors.png in this folder.
Shape: extra wide 16:6, 2400 x 900.
Where it goes: story top and card for "A court with fewer doors"

PROMPT:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An interpretive, non-literal scene evoking a 1930s colonial Indian courthouse: empty timber benches, a high judge's desk, tall windows, neat piles of papers whose surfaces show only unreadable marks, and a tall closed doorway in shadow. No judges or prisoners and no claim to reproduce the specific Lahore tribunal room. Warm lamp light crossing the benches, a thoughtful quiet mood, no text, insignia, weapons, violence, or invented period documents. Extra-wide composition.
