## Conflicts retained

- **Shooting date:** [NBT pp. 15–16](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) and [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/articles/legal-proceedings-martyrdom-bhagat-singh-sukhdev-rajguru-526902) give 17 December 1928; [Maclean](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271936364_What_Durga_Bhabhi_did_next_or_was_there_a_gendered_agenda_in_revolutionary_circles) gives 18 December. Stories about the incident visibly state the disagreement.
- **Officer accused of baton charge:** [Home Affairs](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=26383) names Scott; [NBT p.15](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) names Saunders. Avoid settling that detail without police records.
- **Birthplace:** [Information and Broadcasting ministry](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=3&relid=41773) names Khed; [NBT p.17](https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf) names Varanasi. The five chosen stories do not depend on a birth certificate, and the preservation story says the *identified* birth room is in Rajgurunagar.
- **Arrest date:** NBT prints September **1928** after describing December 1928 events, plainly inconsistent; a modern compilation gives September **1929**. Exact arrest date excluded.
- **Appeal:** ordinary appeal withheld, but legal historian describes a special application to the Privy Council. Stories describe both rather than saying nobody could apply anywhere.

## Quotes not to use as speech

- **UNVERIFIED direct speech attributed to Rajguru:** “Our time to die has come.” Printed in NBT on page 18: https://www.nbtindia.gov.in/writereaddata/freebooks/pdf/Hanged%20for%20Petriotism.pdf . No direct witness / primary transcript inspected. **Not used in stories.**
- Reported personal words of Durga Devi, Bhagwati Charan Vohra, or her child appear in retrospective sources. No verified original recording. **Not used as dialogue.**

## Claude's own check, 22 October 2026 (two new flagships)
Opened this session: English Wikipedia (Rajguru; John P. Saunders), shivaramrajguru.com, PIB Home Affairs page, PIB Information and Broadcasting page (Khed, "now named as Rajguru Nagar"), LiveLaw, NBT book, Kakori Wikipedia, Allahabad High Court history.
"The shot meant for someone else":
- Shooting date: every opened source gives 17 Dec 1928; the HSRA notice "Beware, Ye Bureaucracy" is dated 18 Dec (Wikipedia). The 18 Dec date from Maclean's paper was NOT opened by Claude, so it is not in the story.
- Rajguru fired first, then Bhagat Singh (Wikipedia, shivaramrajguru.com; NBT: "Jaigopal signalled and Rajguru fired"). Who shot Chanan Singh: no source says.
- Saunders: born 24 July 1907, joined Indian Police 8 April 1927, on probation, killed aged 21 (Wikipedia).
- Case figures (607 witnesses, 457 examined by 26 Aug 1930, five approvers): LiveLaw. "No direct evidence" is LiveLaw's view, labelled so.
- Left out: gun models, route details, any thoughts of Rajguru, the Durga Bhabhi escape (belongs in her stories).
"Seven puzzles about Rajguru":
- Birthplace: Khed (Wikipedia, shivaramrajguru.com, PIB I&B) vs Varanasi (NBT). Parents: NBT says both died when he was 6; Wikipedia and the website say his father did.
- Kakori: Wikipedia says he took part (no citation); the website calls it disputed; he is not in the participant list on Wikipedia's Kakori page or in the High Court history. Said "probably wrong".
- Arrest: NBT prints 30 Sept 1928 (impossible, after the Dec 1928 shooting); the website gives 30 Sept 1929 with pistol and 14 cartridges. Betrayal by Sharat Keskar: NBT only.
- Baton charge officer: PIB Home Affairs and Wikipedia say Scott; NBT says Saunders.
- Bodies: PIB says secretly cremated at Hussainiwala, ashes reportedly into the Sutlej, yet remains collected next day; the website says near Ganda Singh Wala. Shown as unclear.
