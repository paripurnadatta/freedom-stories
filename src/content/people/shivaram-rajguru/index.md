## Research notes

HSRA revolutionary executed in 1931.

### A revolutionary in a collective network

Rajguru’s history is most useful when connected to the HSRA network rather than presented as an isolated martyr biography.

### Lahore, 1928

The profile should distinguish documented participation from later simplified retellings of individual roles.

### The three executions

The shared execution became a powerful political image, documented even in material catalogued as proscribed by the colonial state.

### Chronology

- **late 1920s**: HSRA underground. Joins the revolutionary organization operating across North India
- **1928**: Lahore revolutionary action. Participates in the HSRA campaign associated with the Saunders killing
- **1931-03-23**: Execution. Executed with Bhagat Singh and Sukhdev

### Evidence claims

- Was a member of the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association. (high confidence)
- Was part of the revolutionary group associated with the Saunders action in Lahore. (medium confidence)
- Was executed in 1931 alongside Bhagat Singh and Sukhdev; colonial-era proscribed material depicted the three together in prison. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Shivaram Rajguru with Hindustan Socialist Republican Association as member of. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Shivaram Rajguru with Bhagat Singh as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Shivaram Rajguru with Sukhdev Thapar as worked with. (high confidence)
