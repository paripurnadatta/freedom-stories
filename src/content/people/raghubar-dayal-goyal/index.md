## Research notes

Documented association with Bikaner Praja Mandal in Rajasthan.

### Overview

Bikaner lawyer and political organizer who became a central figure in the Praja Parishad movement for responsible government. Rajasthan's district gazetteer records the state's repeated repression of political organizations and Goyal's externment; later movement histories place him at the center of the 1942 Praja Parishad.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Praja Parishad activity in Bikaner was suppressed .... Praja Parishad activity in Bikaner was suppressed and Goyal was externed from the state

### Evidence claims

- Raghubar Dayal Goyal was a Bikaner political worker involved in the princely-state movement for responsible government. (high confidence)
- The Bikaner State externed him during the suppression of Praja Parishad political activity. (high confidence)
- His role belongs to the longer Bikaner struggle against restrictions on political organization, press and public representation. (high confidence)
