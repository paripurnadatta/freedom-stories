## Research notes

Founded India House in London and supported revolutionary nationalism.

### Building a revolutionary infrastructure abroad

Krishna Varma's importance lies in creating institutions where expatriate nationalism could organize, publish and recruit.

### India House

The hostel became a political centre for radical Indian students and revolutionaries in Britain.

### The Indian Sociologist

The journal connected political theory, expatriate networks and criticism of colonial rule over many years.

### Chronology

- **1905**: India House and The Indian Sociologist. Creates a London hub for expatriate revolutionary nationalism
- **1905-1914**: The Indian Sociologist. Publishes nationalist argument across London and Paris phases
- **1907**: Move to Paris. Political work shifts to continental Europe under pressure from British authorities

### Evidence claims

- Founded India House and The Indian Sociologist in London in 1905. (high confidence)
- Founded the Indian Home Rule Society and used it to criticize British rule. (high confidence)
- Moved to Paris in 1907 while The Indian Sociologist continued as an expatriate nationalist publication. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Shyamji Krishna Varma with India House as founder of. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Shyamji Krishna Varma with V.D. Savarkar as associated with. (high confidence)
