## Research notes

Odisha nationalist and Gandhian worker.

### Historical geography

Sakhigopal / Satyabadi: Puri district, Odisha (Educational and social-service base)

### Historical geography

Inchudi: Balasore district, Odisha (Salt Satyagraha site)

### Overview

Teacher, social reformer and Gandhian activist associated with Satyabadi's nationalist educational tradition. His public role combined education, social service and anti-colonial mobilisation. In the 1930 Civil Disobedience campaign he led salt-law defiance at Inchudi and was arrested; later accounts also place him in Quit India-era imprisonment. His life is best framed as a bridge between nationalist education, constructive work and mass satyagraha rather than as a conventional office-holding politician.
