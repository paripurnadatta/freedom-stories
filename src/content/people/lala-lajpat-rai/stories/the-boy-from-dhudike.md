Lajpat Rai was born on 28 January 1865 in a village called Dhudike, in Punjab. Britannica, Wikipedia, the Congress and the AllAboutSikhs website all give the same day and village.

## His family

His father was Munshi Radha Krishan. He taught Urdu and Persian in a government school, say the Congress paper and AllAboutSikhs. His mother was Gulab Devi. Wikipedia says Lajpat was the eldest of six children. It says he spent much of his youth in Jagraon, where his house is now a library and a museum.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: Wikipedia says the family was Agrawal Jain. The Congress paper says only "a Punjabi Hindu family." Wikipedia places Dhudike in Moga district today. AllAboutSikhs places it in Ferozepur district.</aside>

## School and the Arya Samaj

He studied at Rewari. From 1880 he studied law at Government College in Lahore. Wikipedia says he met there two future leaders, Lala Hans Raj and Pandit Gurudutta.

In Lahore he met the ideas of Swami Dayanand Saraswati, the founder of the Arya Samaj. Wikipedia says he joined the Arya Samaj in Lahore and founded a paper called the *Arya Gazette*.

<aside class="note"><b>Arya Samaj</b>: a reform movement founded by Swami Dayanand Saraswati. It wanted Hindus to return to the Vedas and give up customs it saw as wrong.</aside>

## Hisar

In 1886 he went to Hisar and began to work as a lawyer. The Congress paper says that he helped to set up the Hisar Bar Council with Babu Churamani. Wikipedia says he helped to start the local branches of the Congress and the Arya Samaj. It says he went to the Congress sessions at Allahabad in 1888 and 1889.

## Lahore again

In 1892, says Wikipedia, he moved to Lahore to work before the High Court there. He also wrote for newspapers, including *The Tribune*.

The Congress paper says that in 1886 he helped Mahatma Hansraj to set up the Dayananda Anglo-Vedic school in Lahore, the DAV. AllAboutSikhs says that he helped to found the DAV college. The two accounts word his part differently.

## A change in 1914

In 1914 he gave up the law to work only for the freedom of India. That year he left for England and then for America. The rest of that story is told in "Five years in America."

## Why it matters

Lajpat Rai did not begin as a famous leader. He was a village boy who studied law, worked in small towns, and helped to build schools and groups before he led a national protest. The pieces of his early life come from different sources, and we have tried to show where they agree and where they do not.
