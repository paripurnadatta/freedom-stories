In Lahore in the early 1890s, a group of Indians wanted a bank of their own.

## The idea

The Punjab National Bank's own website tells the story. It says a man named Rai Mool Raj, a member of the Arya Samaj, first suggested an Indian-owned national bank. Lala Lajpat Rai then sent a circular, a printed letter, to selected friends. He called for an Indian bank owned by shareholders.

<aside class="note"><b>Joint stock bank</b>: a bank owned by many people who each buy a share. The profit is divided among them.</aside>

Lala Harkrishan Lal, who had come back from England with ideas about trade and industry, was eager to start it.

## The dates

Wikipedia says the bank was registered on 19 May 1894 under the Indian Companies Act. The first meeting of the board was on 23 May 1894. The bank opened for business on 12 April 1895. The Punjab National Bank's page gives the same registration date, and says the opening was the day before the festival of Baisakhi.

The office was in a rented house in Anarkali Bazar in Lahore.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: The bank's own page says the rented house was "opposite the Arya Samaj Mandir" in one place, and "opposite the post office" in another. We cannot say which is correct.</aside>

## The board

The bank's page lists the first board. It says there were seven directors, but then it names eight men. They included Sardar Dayal Singh Majithia, who was the first chairman, and Lala Harkishan Lal, who was the board's first secretary. Other names include Kali Prosanna Roy, E. C. Jessawala and Lala Lalchand. Bulaki Ram Shastri was the first manager.

The board came from different parts of India, says Wikipedia, and from different faiths. The founders were leaders of the Swadeshi movement. Wikipedia quotes their aim: "a truly national bank that would further the economic interest of the country."

## Where was Lajpat Rai?

Was Lajpat Rai a founder of the bank? The sources give different answers.

The bank's page says he was the first person to open an account, and that he joined the board soon after. Wikipedia's article on the bank does not list him among the founders. It says only that he took an active part in managing it in the early years. Wikipedia's article on Rai says that he was "linked" with the Punjab National Bank and with the Lakshmi Insurance Company.

So the sources agree that he was part of it, but not on how to describe his part. The bank names him as the man who called for it by circular. A careful writer says "he helped start the bank" and does not say "founder" without saying who counts as one.

## Why it matters

The founders are described by Wikipedia as leaders of the Swadeshi movement. A bank owned by Indians, set up in Lahore in 1894, was one way of working for that aim. It shows that the freedom struggle was not only marches and speeches. People also set up banks, schools and companies.
