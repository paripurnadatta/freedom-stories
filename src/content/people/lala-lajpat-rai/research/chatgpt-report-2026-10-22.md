4. Disputes, legends and doubts
  ● Exact Year of Birth: While standard mainstream sources and Wikipedia list his birth
     year as 1865, some regional archives and academic profiles (such as local college
     records) cite January 28, 1866.
  ●
  ● Direct Medical Cause of Death: Indian nationalist accounts and contemporary leaders
     maintained that his death on November 17, 1928, was a direct result of the physical
     blows dealt by police superintendent James A. Scott during the Simon Commission
     protest. Conversely, when the incident was raised in the British Parliament, government
     officials and Earl Winterton denied any official link or evidence showing his death was
     caused by police blows, classifying it without medical proof of direct homicide.
