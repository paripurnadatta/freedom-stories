# Lajpat Rai: what was checked (22 October 2026)

Private note, never published. Stories were written fresh from sources opened in this session. Nothing was copied from the ChatGPT report.

## What held up (two or more sources I opened)

- Born 28 January 1865 at Dhudike (Britannica, Wikipedia, INC, AllAboutSikhs). Father Munshi Radha Krishan, mother Gulab Devi.
- Died 17 November 1928 (Britannica, Wikipedia, INC, AllAboutSikhs, Tribune).
- Lahore protest 30 October 1928; Scott ordered the charge (Wikipedia x3, INC, Swarajya, Dawn).
- Hansard 26 November 1928: the Under-Secretary's answers, quoted from the page itself.
- Mandalay 1907: Regulation III of 1818, six months (Tribune, AllAboutSikhs, Wikipedia). Released 11 November 1907 (Tribune only for the exact day).
- US years: Puri's chapter (scholarly) agrees with Wikipedia on the League (October 1917), Young India (1918) and the 1919 return.
- Servants of the People Society, November 1921, Lahore (Wikipedia). Gulab Devi hospital 1934 (Wikipedia, Dawn).
- PNB: registered 19 May 1894, opened 12 April 1895 (PNB, Wikipedia).

## Where sources disagree (flagged in the stories)

- Who struck him: Scott (Wikipedia, INC), Saunders on Scott's order (Tribune), both (Swarajya).
- Cause of death: doctors believed the blows hastened it (Wikipedia, Dawn-85); heart attack (Wikipedia Simon Commission page); government said no evidence (Hansard). A second Dawn article puts his death "at the Lahore Railway Station", which is wrong by the other sources; used only as an example of drift.
- Quote on the evening of 30 October: three different wordings.
- Ajit Singh's arrest: 2 June (Tribune) vs "May" (Wikipedia).
- Length of stay abroad: Puri 1914 to Dec 1919 (and "1920" in another line); Wikipedia 1917 to 1920.
- 1921 prison: 3 December 1921 for a year and a half vs 1921 to 1923 (both inside Wikipedia).
- Founder of PNB: PNB's own page vs Wikipedia's bank page. Also PNB's two descriptions of the first house.
- Family: Agrawal Jain (Wikipedia) vs Punjabi Hindu (INC). District: Moga vs Ferozepur.
- DAV: "helped Hansraj set up the school, 1886" (INC) vs "helped found the college" (AllAboutSikhs).

## Single source only (said so in the stories)

- Hardikar's "banned his re-entry" claim, Tilak's $5,000 (Rathore), and "in panic" (Puri's own view).
- Detail that Rai said the government detective lived in his house (his farewell speech, via Puri).
- Swarajya's "head and center" telegram and the letter to his father (Swarajya is an admirer's article).

## Could not check

- The ChatGPT report's claims on the 1928 funeral, Hansard debate wording beyond the page I read (page ran to 138,000 characters; I read the first 100,000), and any medical report. No source I opened gives a post-mortem.
- Dates of Lakshmi Insurance Company (Wikipedia only says "linked"). Left out of the stories except in the bank story as a one-line "linked with" claim.
- The Wire article on the 1907 Colonisation Bill: only metadata loaded.
- The dossier's note that a college blog gives 1866 as birth year: not opened. Four sources I opened give 1865.
- Portrait: the existing portrait.webp (statue, CC BY-SA 4.0) stays. No new pictures were added.

## Left out on purpose

- The Hindu Mahasabha and communal politics of the 1920s: sources opened were too thin to be fair to every side. Needs a proper source (e.g. Grewal and Banga volume).
- Lajpat Rai's quarrel with Gandhi after 1920 and the book Unhappy India: only passing mentions in what I opened.
- The Saunders shooting itself: already told in the Bhagat Singh stories.
