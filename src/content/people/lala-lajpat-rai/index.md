## Research notes

Major Punjabi nationalist; died after injuries from a protest.

### Punjab nationalism on a national stage

Lajpat Rai moved between regional agrarian agitation and national political leadership.

### Lal-Bal-Pal and assertive nationalism

The trio provides a relationship cluster, but Rai’s Punjab-specific history must remain visible.

### The Simon Commission protest

His death became a major catalyst in revolutionary memory and later HSRA action.

### Chronology

- **1905-1907**: Swadeshi and Punjab unrest. Emerges as a leading assertive nationalist
- **1907**: Deportation. Deported during the Punjab agrarian unrest
- **1928**: Simon Commission protest. Injured during anti-Simon protest and dies later that year

### Evidence claims

- Was a leading figure of the Lal-Bal-Pal nationalist current. (high confidence)
- Was prominent in the Punjab agrarian agitation and was deported with Ajit Singh in 1907. (high confidence)
- Led opposition to the Simon Commission in Lahore and was injured during police action. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Lala Lajpat Rai with Bal Gangadhar Tilak as worked with. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Lala Lajpat Rai with Bipin Chandra Pal as worked with. (high confidence)
