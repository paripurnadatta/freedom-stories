## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Awadh resistance in 1857.

### Overview

The Maulvi of Faizabad, a prominent 1857 resistance leader in Awadh whose campaigning extended through Faizabad, Lucknow and Shahjahanpur.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Led major anti-Company resistance in Faizabad and wider Awadh
- **1857-1858**: Continued resistance through Lucknow/Shahjahanpur and surrounding areas
- **1858-06-05**: Died during the final phase of resistance

### Evidence claims

- Maulavi Ahmadullah Shah became a central rebel leader in Faizabad during the 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
- He led resistance across parts of Awadh, including operations connected with Ismailganj, Lucknow and Shahjahanpur. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry account records his death on 5 June 1858. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Faizabad / Ayodhya region: Principal 1857 headquarters and political base
- Lucknow / Shahjahanpur: Continued resistance
