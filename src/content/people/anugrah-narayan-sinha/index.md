### Overview

Bihar nationalist and Gandhian organiser who joined the Champaran Satyagraha and later became one of the key provincial Congress leaders. His career linked mass mobilisation, institutional politics and wartime imprisonment, followed by major responsibilities in Bihar's government. For the freedom-struggle timeline, the focus should remain Champaran, Civil Disobedience, individual satyagraha/Quit India, and his partnership with Bihar's wider Gandhian leadership.

### Chronology

- **1917**: Joined the Champaran Satyagraha

### Evidence claims

- Anugrah Narayan Sinha joined Gandhi's Champaran Satyagraha and is listed among the principal Bihar colleagues who assisted the campaign. (HIGH confidence)
- He later became a major Congress organiser in Bihar and participated in Civil Disobedience. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned during the Quit India period and later served as Bihar's first Deputy Chief Minister/Finance Minister after independence. (HIGH confidence)
