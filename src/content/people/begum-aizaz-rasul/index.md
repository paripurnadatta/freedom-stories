## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Begum Aizaz Rasul's connection with Anti-colonial constitutional politics.

### Overview

Late-colonial Muslim woman politician from the United Provinces whose pre-independence significance lay in elected politics, anti-feudal positions and opposition to partition/separate communal representation.

### Chronology

- **1937**: Entered the U.P. Legislative Assembly
- **1937-1946**: Worked in late-colonial provincial politics and opposed feudal/communal arrangements
- **1946**: Entered the Constituent Assembly

### Evidence claims

- Begum Aizaz Rasul entered electoral politics in 1937 and was elected to the U.P. Legislative Assembly from a non-reserved seat. (HIGH confidence)
- She opposed partition and feudal practices such as zamindari. (HIGH confidence)
- She later joined the Constituent Assembly and argued against separate communal political arrangements. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- United Provinces / Sandila-Lucknow political sphere: Late-colonial electoral politics
