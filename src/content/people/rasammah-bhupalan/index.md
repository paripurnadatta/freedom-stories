## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Rani of Jhansi Regiment training and mobilisation.

### Overview

Malayan INA veteran who joined the women's wing of the Indian National Army as a teenager and served in Burma before returning to Malaya in 1945.

### Chronology

- **1943-1945**: Served in the women's wing of the INA during the Second World War
- **1940s**: Stationed in Burma during INA service
- **1945**: Returned to Malaya

### Evidence claims

- The Indian High Commission in Malaysia identified Rasammah Bhupalan as an INA veteran and member of the Jhansi Regiment who fought for India's independence. (HIGH confidence)
- At age sixteen she enlisted in the women's wing of the INA and was stationed in Burma. (HIGH confidence)
- She returned to Malaya in 1945 after contracting malaria. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Ipoh / Malaya: Early life and INA recruitment
- Burma: INA service
