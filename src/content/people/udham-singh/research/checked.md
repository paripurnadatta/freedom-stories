# Udham Singh: what was checked

A research dossier from another AI is in this folder (report-from-other-ai.pdf). Every story was written fresh from sources opened in this session.

## Sources opened
South Asian Britain (University of Bristol); Vinay Lal (UCLA); Counsel magazine (Paramjit Ahluwalia); Wikipedia (Udham Singh; Jallianwala Bagh massacre; Michael O'Dwyer); NBT book "Hanged for Patriotism" (summary only); The Tribune (myths article; seven letters article); Scroll.in (Kim Wagner extract); Penguin (V. N. Datta excerpt); Open magazine; British Library catalogue record.

## Not opened or blocked
The Indian Express (both articles) and The Times of India: blocked. The Papers Past report of 9 July 1940: blocked. The Tribune fingerprint article (the link gave an unrelated page). Commons File pages for the pictures.

## Held up
Names; 1927 arrest (30 Aug 1927, released 23 Oct 1931); Caxton Hall on 13 March 1940; trial 4 to 5 June 1940; the tattooed name; the withheld statement; hanging on 31 July 1940; the seven letters.

## Sources disagree (marked in the stories)
Birthday (26 Dec 1899 vs 23 Aug 1901); presence at Jallianwala Bagh (yes / doubted / abroad); brother and sister killed in 1919 vs brother died 1917; route to America; London 1933 vs 1934; name (Mohamed vs Ram Mohammad Singh Azad); hunger strike (36 vs 42 days); execution date (31 July vs NBT 12 June); remains (1974 vs 1975).

## Left out
The marriage and children claims (Wikipedia only, no reliable source). "Acting on Bhagat Singh's orders" (Wikipedia only). The 1936 passport story and the 1940 Home Department inquiry (Times of India, blocked). The Indian Express account of the revolver. The fingerprint card. Death imagery in the 30 March letter (not copied, on purpose). The hollow-book scene, the "wrong man" theory and the chauffeur story are told only as myths.
