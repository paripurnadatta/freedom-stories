## Research notes

### Overview

Assamese nationalist organizer remembered for leading youth participation in Non-Cooperation and for involvement in the early Assam Congress organization. The strongest current sources are Assam educational and institutional histories rather than a dedicated archival biography, so the profile should remain movement-centered.

### Chronology

### Evidence claims

- Chandranath Sarma was among the Assamese leaders who mobilized youth to boycott government educational institutions during Non-Cooperation. (high confidence)
- He was part of the early Assam Congress organizational network and was later elected to AICC representation from Assam. (high confidence)
