## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Oossa Maratteang's connection with Jaintia anti-British resistance.

### Overview

Jaintia/Khasi resistance participant from Rumbai (Lal Rymbai) who was captured with his brother during the January 1863 conflict and sentenced to death.

### Chronology

- **1863-01**: Participated in Khasi-Jaintia resistance to British expeditions
- **1863-01-16**: Sentenced to death after capture

### Evidence claims

- Oossa Maratteang was a resident of Rumbai (Lal Rymbai), Meghalaya, and son of Ooriang. (HIGH confidence)
- He played a major role in Khasi and Jaintia armed resistance to British expeditions in January 1863. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured with his brother Soowar and sentenced on 16 January 1863 to be hanged. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Rumbai / Lal Rymbai: Home and resistance base
