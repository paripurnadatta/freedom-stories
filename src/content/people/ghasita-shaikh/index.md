## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Ghasita Shaikh's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Patna-area participant in the 1857 uprising who was captured and executed by colonial authorities.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Participated in Patna-area resistance, was captured and executed

### Evidence claims

- Ghasita Shaikh was a resident of Patna and participated in resistance during the 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured during an encounter and sentenced to death for participation in the rebellion. (HIGH confidence)
- He was executed in 1857. (HIGH confidence)

- Patna: 1857 uprising and execution context
