Documented association with Jaipur Praja Mandal / Azad Morcha in Rajasthan.

### Overview

Jaipur Praja Mandal activist and organizer of the Azad Morcha during the Quit India period. Ministry records emphasize mass-awareness work through pamphlets, strikes and political organization and connect him closely to Hiralal Shastri's Jaipur movement.
