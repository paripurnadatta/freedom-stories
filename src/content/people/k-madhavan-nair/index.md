### Evidence claims

- K. Madhavan Nair lived from 1882 to 1932. (HIGH confidence)
- He became the first president of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee in 1920. (HIGH confidence)
- He was among the early Kerala leaders imprisoned during Non-Cooperation. (HIGH confidence)
- He helped found Mathrubhumi in 1923. (HIGH confidence)
