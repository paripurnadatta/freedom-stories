## Research notes

Documented association with women / Seva Dal / Quit India in Karnataka.

### Overview

Karnataka women’s organizer who built the women’s wing of the Hindustani Seva Dal, helped organize the 1924 Belgaum Congress and sheltered underground activists.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Moved into active Congress work and adopted khadi
- **1923-1924**: Led women’s Seva Dal organizing and helped organize the Belgaum Congress session
- **1932-1934**: Imprisoned for freedom-movement activity
- **1942**: Provided assistance to underground Quit India workers

### Evidence claims

- Umabai Kundapur began Congress volunteering after the political impact of Tilak's 1920 funeral procession. (HIGH confidence)
- She became leader of the women’s wing of the Hindustani Seva Dal and worked closely with N. S. Hardikar. (HIGH confidence)
- She helped organize the 1924 Belgaum Congress session and recruited large numbers of women volunteers. (HIGH confidence)
- She was imprisoned in the 1930s and later sheltered underground freedom fighters in her home. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Mangalore / Dakshina Kannada: Early life and nationalist work
- Hubli: Seva Dal and underground-support activity
- Belgaum: 1924 Congress session
