## Research notes

Led the Chittagong uprising.

### Documented record

Born 22 March 1894 in the Chittagong region. Participated in Non-Cooperation and was imprisoned in 1926–28 for revolutionary activity.

### Documented actions

Led the Chittagong Armoury Raid on 18 April 1930. Captured in 1933 and executed on 12 January 1934.

- **1926-1928**: Imprisonment
