On 14 August 1933, a special tribunal in Chittagong gave its decision. Surya Sen and his friend Tarakeswar Dastidar were sentenced to death. Kalpana Dutt, who had been tried with them, was sentenced to life in prison.

Reuters reported the news the same day. It said the charge was conspiracy against the King-Emperor. It also said the court called Surya Sen the "master mind" of the plot.

## Waiting

Kalpana Dutt writes that during the trial, Surya Sen took it for granted that he would hang. She says he was busy handing over his work. He explained to Tarakeswar what must be done, even while sitting in the dock.

After the sentence, she says he tried to give courage to the others. Appeals could be made, he told them, to the High Court and even the Privy Council. He promised her he would see her before he was hanged.

<aside class="note"><b>Privy Council</b>: a body in London that was the highest court of appeal for the British Empire at that time.</aside>

## The letter

On 11 January 1934, the Daily Star and The Better India say, he wrote a last letter to his friends. Here are some lines they print.

"Death is knocking at my door. My mind is flying away towards eternity."

"Only one thing, that is my dream, a golden dream-the dream of Free India."

"Never forget the 18th of April, 1930, the day of the eastern Rebellion in Chittagong."

And he asked them to write "in red letters in the core of your hearts" the names of the patriots who had given their lives. The Daily Star adds that he told his comrades to "never retrace your step."

<aside class="note"><b>Where the letter comes from</b>: The Daily Star and The Better India both quote the letter. Neither one tells us where the original is kept. Wikipedia also mentions the letter. We have not seen the original, so the words may differ a little in other books.</aside>

## The hanging

Surya Sen and Tarakeswar Dastidar were hanged in Chittagong Central Jail, Wikipedia says. He was 39 years old.

Kalpana Dutt was in another jail. She learned later, from Chittagong prisoners, that the hanging was done at midnight. She writes that the Government had kept the whole town and the jail under military control. His family was not given his body.

<aside class="note"><b>Two versions</b>: Most sources say the hanging was on 12 January 1934. Kalpana Dutt's book gives 12 January in one place and 13 January in another. If it was done at midnight, that might explain the difference. Banglapedia mixes up the dates as well. We follow Wikipedia and the Daily Star, which say 12 January. So does a memorial stone at his birthplace, shown below.</aside>

## What we cannot be sure about

Several writers tell more. Wikipedia says that he and Tarakeswar Dastidar were tortured by police before the hanging. The Better India says the same, and gives painful details. But it gives no document, name or witness for them. We could not confirm them, so we do not repeat them.

Other writers say his body was thrown into the sea. The Better India says it was placed in a cage. An article from the Sri Aurobindo Society and one in Manushi magazine give another account. They say the bodies were carried on a ship and thrown into the Bay of Bengal. They say the British feared a backlash. Kalpana Dutt only says the family was not given the body. We could not check these accounts, and we do not know what was done with it.

## A hawker's words

Kalpana Dutt was let out of prison in May 1939. Soon afterward, she says, a hawker said something to her. "The day they hanged Surjya Sen, the sun did not dawn."

She says she just could not believe that Masterda was gone. She made a promise: "We shall carry forward your heritage!"

## Why it matters

Today the old gallows of Chittagong Central Jail is protected by the Government of Bangladesh as a historical monument, says Wikipedia.

When writers do not agree about the last night of a man's life, the right thing is to say so. What we know for sure is smaller than the legends. But it is enough: he was sentenced on 14 August 1933, and he was hanged in January 1934, and he asked his friends to remember the names of those who died.
