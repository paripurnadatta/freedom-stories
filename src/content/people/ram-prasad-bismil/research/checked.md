# Ram Prasad Bismil report: what was checked (21 October 2026)

Report: report-2026-10-21.pdf (another AI). Every claim used was checked against a page opened
in this session.

## Held up (used in stories)
- Born 11 June 1897, Shahjahanpur; parents Muralidhar and Moolmati (Wikipedia, NBT).
- Pen names Ram, Agyat, Bismil (Wikipedia).
- Hindi from father, Urdu from a maulvi, English school (Wikipedia, NBT, Sankalp).
- Arya Samaj, Swami Somdev; Bhai Parmanand's death sentence; poem Mera Janm (Wikipedia, Sankalp, NBT).
- Somdev's warning "It is easy to take a vow but hard to keep it" (NBT, Tandon).
- America Ki Swatantrata Ka Itihas, fake author "Babu Harivans Sahai, B.A.", banned in UP (Sankalp, Wikipedia).
- Matrivedi, Genda Lal Dixit (teacher, Auraiya), Shivaji Samiti; leaflet 28 Jan 1918; absconders
  1 Nov 1919; Yamuna escape (labelled as later account) (Sankalp, Wikipedia).
- HRA named October 1924 at Cawnpore; Bismil head of arms (Wikipedia).
- Kakori from the court history: 8 Down, communication cord, safe in brake van, Ahmad Ali,
  24 prosecuted, 18 convicted, judgment 6 April 1927, Bennett's supplementary trial,
  Chief Court appeal 11 August 1927 (Allahabad HC history).
- Autobiography, last pages 17 Dec 1927, mother's visit 18 Dec (labelled as later retelling) (NBT).
- Sarfaroshi ki Tamanna is by Bismil Azimabadi (Yojana 2022, Rekhta, Wikipedia); PIB 2021 says
  otherwise: both shown in the story.

## New things found while checking
- Court history gives the Kakori loot as Rs 4,553-3-6 (FIR: 4,601-15-6; Wikipedia: about 8,000).
  Added to both Kakori stories.
- The old site portrait of Bismil was the "Accused of Cacori Conspiracy" group photo. Its Commons
  description lists eight people and Bismil is NOT one of them. Replaced with
  "Pandit Ram Prasad Bismil.jpg" (UP State Archives, public domain).
- Autobiography: Wikipedia says printed 1928 by Ganesh Shankar Vidyarthi; NBT says 1929 and banned.
  Shown as "Two versions".

## Could not be checked (blocked)
- Both Indian Express articles (2019, 2023) are blocked here. So left out: "Barrack 11 of Lucknow
  jail" for the autobiography, and the "Jai Hind" last words.
