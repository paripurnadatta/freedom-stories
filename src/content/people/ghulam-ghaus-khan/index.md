## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Jhansi resistance in 1857.

### Overview

Chief gunner in Jhansi's 1857-58 resistance under Rani Lakshmibai who remained in the city's defence until his death in April 1858.

### Chronology

- **1857-06-07**: Appointed chief gunner in Jhansi's army
- **1857-1858**: Served in the military defence of Jhansi
- **1858-04-03**: Died during fighting at Jhansi

### Evidence claims

- Ghulam Ghaus Khan served as a gunner in Jhansi and was appointed chief gunner by Rani Lakshmibai on 7 June 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- He became an important military figure in Jhansi's resistance to British forces. (HIGH confidence)
- He died in battle on 3 April 1858 according to the Ministry profile. (HIGH confidence)
