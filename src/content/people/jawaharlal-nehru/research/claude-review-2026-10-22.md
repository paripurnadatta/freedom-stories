# Claude's review of ChatGPT's Nehru research (22 October 2026)

I opened the main Selected Works pages (nehruarchive.in), the PMO biography, the JNMF chronology, the 1928 letter to Indira and the Discovery of India preface myself. Every quote in the published stories comes from those pages. Nothing was copied from ChatGPT's sentences.

## What held up
Dates and quotes for: Tryst with Destiny, 15 June 1947 partition speech, Lahore address and flag speech, 6 January 1930 statement, 11 January 1928 letter, 27 December 1928 amendment, Quit India speeches, Bose statements, Discovery preface, JNMF chronology.

## Errors found in ChatGPT's report
- "On the British statement of 3 June 1947" is NOT a 3 June speech. It is a Congress resolution of 13 June 1947 (with a draft). Used as such.
- The 1939 election: the 26 January 1939 statement shows Nehru wanted Maulana Azad to stand and opposed Bose standing. ChatGPT did not say this. Neither page names Bose's rival, so the story does not.
- Gandhi's Calcutta 1928 resolution: the page does not mention "two years" or Dominion Status wording; the only deadline in the footnote is 31 December 1929.
- The "I have been proclaimed a republican" style wording is not in the Lahore address; the real line is "I must frankly confess that I am a socialist and a republican."
- 26 January 1930: the press statement has a programme (flag at 8 am, processions, meetings at 5 pm) but no pledge text.
- "Discovery of India" was written April to September 1944 (five months). First publication year (1946) rests on a Penguin page I did not open, so no story gives it.
- Selected Works footnote dates Gandhi's Young India article 12 January 1928, a day AFTER Nehru's letter dated 11 January 1928. Unexplained; said so in the story.
- PMO says "Madras Congress 1926"; Nehru's own papers say Madras 1927. Margin note in "A pledge in Lahore".
- Two Bose statements are quoted in footnotes with different dates (21 and 24 January 1939). Said so.
