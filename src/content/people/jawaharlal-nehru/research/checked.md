## Facts needing caution or source reconciliation
1. **Two Allahabad family houses:** [Prayagraj district](https://prayagraj.nic.in/tourist-place/anand-bhawan/) calls the later Anand Bhawan a 1930s building and gives an imprecise 1920s gift of the old house; [Uttar Pradesh government](https://www.nri.up.gov.in/article/en/swaraj-bhawan) gives donation in 1930. Accounts differ. **Do not assign one settled year** or confuse old Swaraj Bhawan with new Anand Bhawan.
2. **Madras session:** [PMO biography](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/former_pm/shri-jawaharlal-nehru/) says “Madras Congress 1926,” whereas [Nehru's January 1928 dated statement](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/statement-on-the-independence-resolution-27-january-1928-e92wy) concerns **1927**. Tell both versions and check Congress proceedings; do not conflate the Madras debate with Lahore 1929.
3. **Letters location:** [Nehru's 1928 letter](https://collections.paperlanterns.ink/nehru-father-daughter-letters/nehru-001-the-book-of-nature/) specifically says HE was Allahabad and Indira in Mussoorie. An editorial description of [Selected Works, First Series vol. 3](https://nehruarchive.in/selectedworks/volume-3-series-1) appears to reverse location. Prefer the explicit first-person correspondence pending original edition checking.
4. **Gandhi:** Nehru's [letter](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/to-mahatma-gandhi-11-january-1928-pg2zy) reliably shows Nehru's disagreement, but does not independently verify what Gandhi privately thought. Read Gandhi's own replies before writing speech attributed to him.
5. **Bose:** Nehru's [22 Feb 1939 statement](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/the-re-election-of-subhas-bose-22-february-1939-2vopje) gives Nehru's account; Bose's opposing arguments are also quoted in editorial material around [the 26 Jan 1939 Congress election](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/the-congress-presidential-election-26-january-1939-znkpvy). Claude should independently inspect Bose's original contemporary statement for a two-sided story.
6. **Partition:** [3 June 1947 response](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/on-the-british-statement-of-3-june-1947-3-june-1947-7ye2v5) and [15 June Congress speech](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/the-unavoidability-of-partition-15-june-1947-pgdewj) are Nehru's perspective. [Selected Works editorial account](https://nehruarchive.in/selectedworks/volume-3-series-2) places Congress, League, Patel and Mountbatten negotiations in context. **Need separate Jinnah/League, Gandhi and Patel accounts for a fully balanced explanation.** Do not turn Nehru's explanation into a definitive account of every party's intention.
7. **Prison histories:** 1934–35 autobiography distinct from 1944 writing of `The Discovery of India`. Avoid saying he wrote uninterrupted for four years or entirely alone; the Discovery author's preface thanks fellow prisoners.

## Exact-word policy and unverified quotations
The dossier contains 15 short checked quotation snippets with exact URLs and primary/secondary labels. Each is **VERIFIED AS WORDING DISPLAYED** in its linked opened page; “primary” identifies a Nehru-authored letter, diary or speech **in transcription**, not possession of a handwritten original. **UNVERIFIED and not for any story:** invented childhood dialogue, supposed private talks with Gandhi or Motilal beyond actual letters, the exact last words of Kamala, invented Indira replies, words assigned to Bose without Bose's own source, unnamed prison officers' conversations, or dramatized dialogue during flag hoisting. The phrase “Long years ago...” is in a published independence speech transcript but should not be rewritten from memory.
