## Picture choice by angle
- **1 (childhood):** No securely dated childhood-room photograph cleared; painting suggested.
- **2 (Harrow/Cambridge):** use period Harrow photograph, no painting needed.
- **3 (two family houses):** genuine modern Anand Bhawan photo, clearly labelled *modern*; no painting needed.
- **4 (Pratapgarh peasants):** No original 1920 farmers' meeting picture securely identified; a restrained painting is preferable to mislabelling a later Gandhi/Nehru photograph.
- **5 (Kamala):** contemporary wedding or 1931 family photo, no painting needed.
- **6 (daughter's letters):** family photograph may illustrate relationship, but a painting of generic objects can illustrate the historical act of writing without forging a document.
- **7 (debating independence):** period portrait exists, but no independently identified exact 1927–28 debate photograph; painting of an unoccupied meeting desk is suitable.
- **8 (Lahore 1929):** historic period Nehru press portrait, but no verified photo of the **precise flag-hoisting scene** in this list. A symbolic non-flag painting is optional.
- **9 (European journey):** 1930 portrait is too late for the exact Brussels meeting; use as a clearly dated portrait only, or a date card. Do NOT caption it as Brussels 1927.
- **10 (autobiography):** a Wardha photo illustrates the person but not the prison-writing scene. Painting of a generic cell or notebook may suit it.
- **11 (Bose):** real period-ish photograph of both men, not demonstrably the election scene.
- **12 (Quit India):** genuine 1942 Gandhi–Nehru image, not a substitute for historical arguments.
- **13 (Discovery in Ahmednagar):** modern fort photo as context, plus a restrained prison-writing illustration.
- **14 (midnight speech):** use an actual 1947 Constituent Assembly photograph; no painting necessary.
- **15 (post-1947 coda):** authentic Nehru photograph from period or date card; not necessary to invent a portrait.

## Exclusions
- Many Commons files are **post-independence photographs**, miscaptioned as events from 1928 or 1931 by automated metadata; do not use metadata `year` alone.
- Stamps or Government of India works under GODL-India are permitted by the owner's 22 October rule, **but none is listed without opening its individual file licence page**.
- Getty / agency photography with unclear freedom-of-use claims, unknown-location montage art and AI-generated facsimiles are excluded. The wedding image is listed solely under the exact Commons public-domain claim, not because the commercial original supplier automatically grants permission.
- Archive letters reproduced on websites are not automatically freely reusable as **images** simply because their text can be cited. Never invent a photographed letter.
