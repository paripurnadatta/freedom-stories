## Evidence rule and notation
Only sources opened in this research session are listed as established evidence. All important details have at least one exact URL on the **same bullet**, usually two. Where a contemporary first-person document and a later biography disagree, both versions are identified. A Nehru speech or letter is primary evidence of **what Nehru said**, not automatically proof that every assertion within it was true. No biography can supply fabricated private dialogue. Quotes are short and verbatim. The Nehru Portal was indexed by search but its pages timed out when opened; its snippets are NOT evidence in this report. “FLAGSHIP” is a source-based suggestion, not an instruction to pad.

## Basic facts and firm anchors
- **Full name and birth:** Jawaharlal Nehru, **14 November 1889**, Allahabad, United Provinces (present-day Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh); parents **Motilal** and **Swarup Rani**. The PM biography says “born in Allabahad on November 14, 1889”; the Memorial Fund says “1889 Born 14 November in Allahabad”. [P](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/former_pm/shri-jawaharlal-nehru/) [J](https://www.jnmf.in/chrono.html). His spelling varies (Allabahad/Allahabad); town identity does not.
- **Death:** **27 May 1964**, New Delhi. The US National Park Service gives “Date of Death: May 27, 1964”, and the PMO records the end of his term the same day. [N](https://home.nps.gov/people/prime-minister-nehru.htm) [P](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/former_pm/shri-jawaharlal-nehru/).
- **Education:** home tutors; Harrow (1905–07), Trinity College, Cambridge (1907–10), Inner Temple London (1910–12); returned 1912 to work in law. [P](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/former_pm/shri-jawaharlal-nehru/) [J](https://www.jnmf.in/chrono.html).
- **Marriage and child:** married Kamala Kaul **8 February 1916**; Indira born **19 November 1917**. [J](https://www.jnmf.in/chrono.html) [I](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=44825). Kamala died **28 February 1936** in Lausanne; no unsourced deathbed dialogue. [J](https://www.jnmf.in/chrono.html).
- **Political group:** Indian National Congress; participated in the Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience and Quit India campaigns. Nehru was **not** a founding or combatant member of the HRA/HSRA merely because he knew revolutionaries. [P](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/former_pm/shri-jawaharlal-nehru/) [J](https://www.jnmf.in/chrono.html).
- **Jails:** multiple arrests from 1921 onward, with a long 1942–45 term. Memorial Fund has individual dates; do **not** invent an exact lifetime total from a page that timed out. [J](https://www.jnmf.in/chrono.html) [P](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/former_pm/shri-jawaharlal-nehru/).
- **HRA/HSRA naming safeguard:** refer to Hindustan Republican Association before September 1928 and Hindustan Socialist Republican Association after; Nehru's movement was Congress and not identical to either group.
