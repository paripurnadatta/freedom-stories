## Research notes

Bihar nationalist and Congress leader.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Joined Non-Cooperation; Khilafat/Congress organizational work. Joined Non-Cooperation; Khilafat/Congress organizational work
- **1937-1939**: Minister in Bihar. Minister in Bihar

### Evidence claims

- He served as General Secretary of the AICC/Indian National Congress. (high confidence)
- He served as General Secretary of the Central Khilafat Committee. (high confidence)
- He was a Cambridge contemporary of Jawaharlal Nehru and practised as a barrister at the Patna High Court. (high confidence)
- He joined the Non-Cooperation Movement in 1921. (high confidence)
- He later served in ministerial office in Bihar, including 1937–39. (high confidence)
- Syed Mahmud lived from 1889 to 1971. (high confidence)
- PMML describes Syed Mahmud as born at Bhitre in Saidpur Tahsil, Ghazipur district, educated at Etawah, Aligarh and Cambridge, and later practising at Patna High Court. (high confidence)
