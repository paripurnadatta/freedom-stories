What do you say on the day before a country adopts its most important law? You might say thank you. You might say well done.

Dr Ambedkar did something else. He gave a warning.

## The committee

India's Constituent Assembly was the body that wrote the country's Constitution. On 29 August 1947, it set up a Drafting Committee. Dr Ambedkar was made its chairman.

The other members were K. M. Munshi, Muhammed Sadulla, Alladi Krishnaswamy Iyer, N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Devi Prasad Khaitan and B. L. Mitter.

<aside class="note"><b>Draft</b>: a first version of a text, written to be discussed and changed.</aside>

The first draft was not written by the committee. Wikipedia says the Assembly's constitutional adviser, B. N. Rau, prepared it. It was ready in February 1948. It had 243 articles and 13 schedules.

After debate and changes, the final Constitution had 395 articles and 8 schedules.

<aside class="note"><b>Article and schedule</b>: an article is one numbered rule. A schedule is a list added at the end of the Constitution.</aside>

## How long it took

Counting the time is harder than it sounds. Wikipedia gives three figures. It says the work took "almost three years". It also says 165 days of sittings. And it says 2 years, 11 months and 18 days.

These measure different things. The page also says that Ambedkar told the Assembly the Drafting Committee itself sat for 141 days.

## The speech

On 25 November 1949, Ambedkar spoke to the Assembly. A part of it is printed by the Supreme Court Observer.

He said: "we are going to enter into a life of contradictions."

He explained: "In politics we will have equality and in social and economic life we will have inequality."

In simple words: the law would treat everyone as equal, but life in society and in the economy would not.

He warned that if this was denied for long, it would put democracy in danger. He said that those who suffer from inequality will "blow up the structure of political democracy."

He also said: "Without fraternity, equality and liberty will be no deeper than coats of paint."

<aside class="note"><b>Fraternity</b>: a feeling of brotherhood, that people of all kinds belong together.</aside>

## Two dates

The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949. It came into force on 26 January 1950. Some parts began earlier, on 26 November 1949.

One more date is in doubt. Wikipedia's timeline says the Constitution was signed on 24 January 1950. Another part of the same page says 284 members signed on 26 November 1949. The page does not explain this.

## Why it matters

In this speech, Ambedkar named a weak spot instead of only praising the work.

His question is still asked: laws can promise equality, but what makes people feel they belong together?
