ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: B. R. Ambedkar
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. Real photographs of Ambedkar exist, but these scenes show no faces.
Any paper or book must show only unreadable squiggles, never real letters.

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evening in 1931, a sunlit verandah of a large Bombay house on a hill, with a view of palm trees and the sea far below. Two plain wooden chairs face each other across a low table, with a spinning wheel (charkha) beside one chair and a stack of law books beside the other. Nobody is seated: two sets of footprints in the dust of the doorway suggest they have just left. A quiet, serious mood. No people, no faces. Wide cinematic composition.
