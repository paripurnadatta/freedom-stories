## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Ahom resistance after the Treaty of Yandabo.

### Overview

Ahom prince and early anti-British leader in Assam after the Treaty of Yandabo. Assam government records state that Gomdhar Konwar declared himself king in 1828 and led an early resistance to British authority before being suppressed and sentenced to a long term of imprisonment.

- **1828**: Revolt suppressed; sentenced to imprisonment.. Revolt suppressed; sentenced to imprisonment

### Evidence claims

- Gomdhar Konwar was an Ahom prince remembered by the Assam government as a leader of the state's earliest resistance to British rule. (high confidence)
- In 1828 he declared himself king and the revolt was suppressed by British authorities. (high confidence)
- The Jorhat district history states that he was sentenced to fourteen years' imprisonment. (high confidence)
