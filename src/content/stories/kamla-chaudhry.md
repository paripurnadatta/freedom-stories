## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Kamla Chaudhry's connection with Civil Disobedience Movement.

### Overview

Hindi writer and nationalist who joined Congress during Civil Disobedience, faced repeated imprisonment and later carried social-reform concerns into constitution-making.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Joined Civil Disobedience and Congress political work
- **1930s-1940s**: Continued nationalist activity and faced imprisonment
- **1946-1950**: Served in constitution-making/public life

### Evidence claims

- Kamla Chaudhry actively participated in the Civil Disobedience Movement of 1930. (HIGH confidence)
- She joined the Indian National Congress and underwent imprisonment during the freedom struggle. (HIGH confidence)
- Her later public work connected nationalism with rural development and women's education. (HIGH confidence)
