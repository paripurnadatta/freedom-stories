## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Formation of the Indian National Congress.

### Building the machinery of public opinion

His work belongs to the institutional history of nationalism: press ownership, associations and meetings.

### A three-person Madras cluster

Veeraraghavachariar, Subramania Iyer and Ananda Charlu form a tightly evidenced network based on co-founding activity.

### Chronology

- **1878**: The Hindu founded. Veeraraghavachariar joins a group of young Madras intellectuals seeking an Indian-controlled newspaper
- **1884-1885**: Mahajana Sabha and the first Congress. The new regional association becomes one institutional channel carrying Madras opinion into the national Congress
