## Research notes

### Overview

Senior Indian officer in Company service who emerged as a leading rebel in Sagar during the 1857 uprising. Ministry records describe soldiers proclaiming him 'General', the seizure of weapons, reorganization of a guard headquarters and fighting with Company forces in July 1857.

### Evidence claims

- Sheikh Ramzan was a senior Indian officer in East India Company service who joined and led the Sagar uprising in 1857. (high confidence)
- Rebel sepoys proclaimed him their General and used the Quarter Guard as a headquarters. (high confidence)
- His forces fought Company troops in July 1857 before being compelled to leave Sagar. (high confidence)
