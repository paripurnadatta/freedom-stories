## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Chuar uprisings.

### Overview

Dispossessed zamindar of Raipur in the Jungle Mahals who led a major phase of the Chuar uprising in 1798-99. Ministry of Culture links the rebellion to exploitative revenue policies, resumption of land and displacement of hereditary paiks, and records Durjan Singh as the leader of the Raipur-centered rising.

### Chronology

- **1798-1799**: Led the Raipur-centered phase of the Chuar uprisin.... Led the Raipur-centered phase of the Chuar uprising

### Evidence claims

- Durjan Singh led the Chuar Rebellion in the Raipur/Bankura region in 1798-99. (high confidence)
- The uprising was driven by colonial land-revenue policy, land resumption and displacement of hereditary paiks and cultivators. (high confidence)
- Under Durjan Singh, rebels attacked colonial revenue/police institutions and the rising spread into neighbouring parganas. (high confidence)
