## Research notes

Major Kerala Gandhian and satyagrahi.

### Freedom and social equality

Kelappan's politics cross the archive's artificial boundary between anti-colonial campaigns and struggles against caste exclusion.

### A Kerala network

His profile creates sourced connections to Vaikom, Gandhi, Guruvayur and contemporaries such as Malaviya.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Salt Satyagraha in Kerala. Kelappan leads salt-law defiance at Payyannur and Calicut
- **1931-1932**: Guruvayur Satyagraha. Temple-entry agitation culminates in Kelappan's fast, ended at Gandhi's request
- **1940**: Individual Satyagraha. Kelappan is chosen as Kerala's first individual satyagrahi
