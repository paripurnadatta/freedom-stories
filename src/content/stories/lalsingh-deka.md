## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Lalsingh Deka's connection with Quit India Movement.

### Overview

Young Assamese Quit India participant killed during a military raid on Mikirgaon in August 1942.

### Chronology

- **1942-08-29**: Killed during the military raid on Mikirgaon/Barapujia in the Quit India context

### Evidence claims

- On 29 August 1942, a military party raided Mikirgaon of Barapujia, Assam, to arrest Quit India activists. (HIGH confidence)
- Lalsingh Deka, recorded as a 25-year-old youth, was killed by military gunfire during the raid. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Mikirgaon / Barapujia: Quit India raid and death
