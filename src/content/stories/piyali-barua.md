## Research notes

Associated with Maniram Dewan's resistance.

### The organizer beside Maniram Dewan

Enriching Piyali turns an existing one-person story into a documented Assam network rather than leaving Maniram as an isolated node.

### 1857 as restoration and rebellion

The Assam plan combined anti-colonial opposition with a specific political objective: restoring Ahom authority.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Assam conspiracy takes shape. Piyali organizes locally while Maniram Dewan works from Calcutta to coordinate an Ahom-restoration strategy
- **1857**: Letters are intercepted. Colonial authorities uncover the network through intercepted communications
- **1858-02-26**: Execution at Jorhat. Piyali Barua and Maniram Dewan are hanged after conviction for the conspiracy

### Evidence claims

- Ministry of Culture material identifies Piyali Barua, also known as Mahesh Chandra Gabharumelia, as a principal associate of Maniram Dewan in the Assam conspiracy of 1857. (high confidence)
- While Maniram worked from Calcutta, Piyali acted as a central organizer in Assam and helped carry messages and plans to local supporters. (high confidence)
- The conspiracy sought to restore Ahom rule under Kandarpeswar Singha while drawing support from sepoys and regional elites. (high confidence)
- Intercepted correspondence exposed the plan; Maniram Dewan and Piyali Barua were publicly executed at Jorhat on 26 February 1858. (high confidence)
