## Research notes

Documented association with Sambalpur resistance in Odisha.

### Evidence claims

- Kunjal Singh, son of Madho Singh, continued armed resistance in Sambalpur after 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- The government profile records that he refused the 1861 amnesty. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured in 1865 and executed at Sambalpur jail. (HIGH confidence)
