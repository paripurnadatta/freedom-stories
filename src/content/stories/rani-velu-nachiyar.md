## Research notes

Organized armed resistance against the Company and reclaimed Sivaganga.

### Anti-Company resistance before 1800

Velu Nachiyar broadens the archive beyond the familiar nineteenth-century framework of resistance.

### Alliance as strategy

Her campaign depended on regional alliance-building rather than isolated resistance.

### Sivaganga, not generic South India

The profile must preserve Sivaganga’s historical political geography.

### Chronology

- **1770s**: Exile after loss of Sivaganga. Rebuilds political and military support
- **1780**: Campaign to recover Sivaganga. Launches allied military action against Company forces

### Evidence claims

- Was born in Ramanathapuram in 1730 and became queen of Sivaganga. (high confidence)
- Entered conflict with the East India Company after the death of her husband and loss of Sivaganga. (high confidence)
- Built alliances including with Hyder Ali and regional allies before a campaign to recover Sivaganga. (high confidence)
- The supplied government profile credits her with organizing women soldiers during the campaign. (medium confidence)
- The supplied record connects Rani Velu Nachiyar with Sivaganga resistance and Velu Nachiyar's campaign as leader of event. (high confidence)
