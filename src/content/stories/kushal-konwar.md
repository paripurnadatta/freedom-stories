## Research notes

Only Assamese freedom fighter executed by the British in connection with Quit India.

### A Gandhian in a sabotage prosecution

The profile should preserve the tension between Konwar’s Gandhian identity and the violent act for which colonial authorities prosecuted him.

### Colonial prosecution during Quit India

Do not convert a conviction automatically into a confident claim that he personally carried out the sabotage.

### Jorhat Jail, 1943

His execution connects Assam’s regional history directly to wartime repression.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Quit India in Assam. The wartime movement creates a context of mass protest and sabotage prosecutions
- **1942-1943**: Train-sabotage prosecution. Konwar is prosecuted and sentenced in connection with the case
- **1943-06-15**: Execution at Jorhat Jail. Executed during the wartime repression of the movement

### Evidence claims

- Was associated with Gandhian nationalist politics in Assam. (high confidence)
- Was convicted in connection with a train-sabotage case during the Quit India period. (high confidence)
- Was executed at Jorhat Jail on 15 June 1943. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kushal Konwar with Quit India Movement as participant in. (high confidence)
