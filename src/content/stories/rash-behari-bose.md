## Research notes

Long-running revolutionary organizer who later worked in East Asia. | Pioneered the political groundwork for the INA before Bose's leadership.

### Documented record

Born 25 May 1886 and died 21 January 1945. Was among planners of the proposed February 1915 uprising linked to the Ghadar movement.

### Documented actions

Later played a central role in the Azad Hind Fauj/INA project abroad. Transferred INA leadership to Subhas Chandra Bose in Singapore in July 1943.

### Chronology

- **1914-1915**: Ghadar-linked uprising planning
- **1942-1943**: INA/Azad Hind organization abroad
- **1943-07**: Leadership transition to Subhas Chandra Bose
- **1945-01-21**: Death
