## Research notes

Led resistance in Lucknow on behalf of her son.

### Documented record

Assumed leadership of the uprising in Awadh on behalf of her minor son Birjis Qadr. Was present on the battlefield during major clashes according to British records summarized by the source. Refused to surrender after Lucknow was recaptured and moved toward Nepal with forces and allied leaders.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Leadership in Awadh uprising
- **1858-03**: British recapture of Lucknow
- **1858**: Retreat toward Nepal

### Evidence claims

- Assumed leadership of the uprising in Awadh on behalf of her minor son Birjis Qadr. (high confidence)
- Was present on the battlefield during major clashes according to British records summarized by the source. (high confidence)
- Refused to surrender after Lucknow was recaptured and moved toward Nepal with forces and allied leaders. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Begum Hazrat Mahal with Revolt of 1857 as participant in. (high confidence)
