## Research notes

Founded a major Home Rule League and became Congress president.

### Home Rule through organization and print

Besant’s political strength came from combining a national demand with repeatable local forms of organization.

### Newspapers as movement infrastructure

New India and Commonweal made political argument part of daily mobilization.

### Internment becomes propaganda

Her detention showed how colonial repression could widen rather than contain a political campaign.

### Chronology

- **1916-09**: Home Rule League in Madras. Builds an organization for self-government through local branches and propaganda
- **1917-06**: Internment. Colonial repression intensifies the public campaign around Home Rule
- **1917**: Home Rule at national scale. The League's network becomes a major wartime nationalist platform
