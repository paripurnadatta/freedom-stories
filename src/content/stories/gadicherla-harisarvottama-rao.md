## Research notes

Documented association with Swadeshi / press in Andhra Pradesh.

### Overview

Andhra nationalist editor, Home Rule organizer and student activist remembered for the 1907 Vande Mataram protest and later political/journalistic work.

### Chronology

- **1907-04**: Led Vande Mataram student protest and was expelled
- **1910s**: Worked as nationalist journalist and Home Rule organizer
- **1920s onward**: Continued public, educational and political work in Andhra

### Evidence claims

- Gadicherla Harisarvottama Rao led students in a 1907 Vande Mataram badge protest and strike and was expelled from college. (HIGH confidence)
- He later became a nationalist editor and served as secretary of the Andhra Division of the Home Rule League. (HIGH confidence)
- His nationalist work combined political journalism, education and organizational activity. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Rajahmundry / Andhra region: Student-nationalist protest
- Andhra / Madras Presidency: Journalism and Home Rule organizing
