## Research notes

Underground Quit India activist from Narsinghpur associated with clandestine transport and support work.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Worked underground during the Quit India Movement and joined the Lal Kursi Dal

### Evidence claims

- Phoolchand Gupta was born on 19 September 1924 in Tendukheda village, Narsinghpur district. (HIGH confidence)
- During the Quit India Movement in 1942, Gupta was ordered to avoid arrest and work underground. (HIGH confidence)
- He travelled via Bina, Betul and Chicholi to Nagpur and joined the Lal Kursi Dal. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Tendukheda: birthplace and local political base
- Nagpur: underground organizational contact
