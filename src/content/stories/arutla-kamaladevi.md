## Research notes

Organized women through Andhra Mahasabha-linked work and participated in peasant land struggles against landlords and Nizam forces.

### Historical geography

Nalgonda / Aler / Kolanupaka: Hyderabad State (Social and political organizing base)

### Overview

Women organiser and communist/peasant activist in the Telangana movement, closely associated with Andhra Mahasabha and the anti-feudal struggle in Hyderabad State. A detailed memorial essay by veteran trade-union leader Raj Bahadur Gour presents her as a women's organiser, participant in peasant land struggles and an activist in the armed phase against Nizam forces. Because the strongest accessible source is memorial rather than archival, the profile should be rich but carefully caveated.
