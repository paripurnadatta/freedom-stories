## Research notes

Senior Manipuri leader executed after the 1891 war.

### Documented record

Born in 1858 and was a son of Maharaja Chandrakirti Singh. Served as a leading military figure in resistance to British intervention in Manipur. British forces occupied Manipur in April 1891. Arrested and hanged on 13 August 1891.

### Chronology

- **1891**: Anglo-Manipur confrontation
- **1891-04-27**: British occupation of Manipur
- **1891-08-13**: Execution
