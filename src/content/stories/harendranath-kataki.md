## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Harendranath Kataki's connection with Non-Cooperation Movement.

### Overview

School student from Sibsagar who joined Non-Cooperation, was imprisoned at Gauhati Jail and died in detention.

### Chronology

- **1921-1922**: Participated in the Non-Cooperation Movement as a school student in Assam
- **1920s**: Imprisoned for three months at Gauhati Jail and died in detention

### Evidence claims

- Harendranath Kataki was a school student from Sibsagar, Assam, who participated in the Non-Cooperation Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested and sentenced to three months' imprisonment in Gauhati Jail. (HIGH confidence)
- The ICHR entry records that he died in jail after ill-treatment and gives his age as twelve. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Sibsagar: Home and student activism
- Gauhati Jail: Imprisonment and death
