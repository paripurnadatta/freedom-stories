### Overview

Bombay lawyer, municipal leader and early Congress statesman who was among the small number of Muslim delegates at the first Congress session in 1885. He later served in the Bombay and Imperial legislative councils and presided over the 1896 Congress session. His presidential address combined economic criticism of colonial rule with a sustained argument for Muslim participation in a shared Indian political nationality.
