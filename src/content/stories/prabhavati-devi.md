## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Prabhavati Devi's connection with Civil Disobedience; Individual Satyagraha; Quit India.

### A political life in her own right

The profile must resist reducing Prabhavati to Jayaprakash Narayan's wife.

### Gandhian conviction and political difference

The official source notes that her Gandhian commitments could differ from her husband's political choices.

### Chronology

- **late 1920s**: Sabarmati Ashram political formation. Prabhavati's sustained life in the ashram gives her a Gandhian identity distinct from merely being a political spouse
- **1930s**: Civil Disobedience and imprisonment. She moves from ashram life into direct anti-colonial protest
- **1942-1944**: Quit India imprisonment. She is arrested during the wartime crackdown and remains incarcerated until 1944
