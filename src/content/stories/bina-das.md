## Research notes

Attempted to assassinate Bengal Governor Stanley Jackson.

### From student networks to direct action

Das’s career shows how women’s student organizations in Bengal could feed into revolutionary politics.

### The convocation as political theatre

The 1932 action turned a ceremonial colonial university setting into a public confrontation.

### Revolutionary and Congress activist

Her later Quit India role complicates a simple division between revolutionary and Congress histories.

### Chronology

- **1932-02-06**: Calcutta University convocation action. Attempts to shoot Governor Stanley Jackson and is arrested
- **1932-1939**: Imprisonment. Serves a long sentence before release
- **1942-1945**: Quit India imprisonment. Participates in the 1942 movement and is imprisoned again

### Evidence claims

- Was a member of Chhatri Sangha. (high confidence)
- Attempted to shoot Bengal Governor Stanley Jackson at the University of Calcutta convocation on 6 February 1932. (high confidence)
- Was sentenced to nine years rigorous imprisonment. (high confidence)
- After release joined Congress and later participated in Quit India, again facing imprisonment. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Bina Das with Quit India Movement as participant in. (high confidence)
