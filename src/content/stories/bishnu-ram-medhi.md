## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Bishnu Ram Medhi's connection with Non-Cooperation Movement.

### Overview

Assamese Congress leader who entered Non-Cooperation in 1921 and later held sustained leadership roles in the provincial Congress.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Joined the Non-Cooperation Movement and participated in the developing Assam Congress organization
- **1926**: Served as Joint Secretary of the reception committee for the Pandu Congress session
- **1930-1938**: Served as President of the Congress in Assam

### Evidence claims

- Bishnu Ram Medhi joined the Non-Cooperation Movement in 1921. (HIGH confidence)
- He was among the leaders associated with the formation of the Assam Provincial Congress Committee in 1921. (HIGH confidence)
- He served as Joint Secretary of the reception committee for the Congress session at Pandu in 1926. (HIGH confidence)
- He became President of the Congress in Assam in 1930 and continued in office until 1938. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Hajo / Kamrup: Early life and political base
- Guwahati / Pandu: Congress organization and 1926 session
