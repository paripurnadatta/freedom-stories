## Research notes

HRA leader executed after the Kakori case.

### Organization, writing and action

Bismil’s importance comes from combining revolutionary argument with organization and operations.

### The HRA as a political project

The HRA’s publications articulated a republican program beyond armed fund-raising.

### Kakori

Kakori should be presented as an operation embedded in organizational strategy rather than as an isolated train robbery.

### Chronology

- **1918**: Mainpuri conspiracy. Combines organization, writing and armed fund-raising
- **1924**: HRA formation. Helps establish the republican revolutionary organization
- **1925-08-09**: Kakori train action. Leads a major HRA operation
- **1927-12-19**: Execution. Executed after the Kakori conspiracy trial

### Evidence claims

- Participated in the Mainpuri conspiracy and revolutionary publishing before Kakori. (high confidence)
- Was among the principal founders/leaders of the Hindustan Republican Association. (high confidence)
- Helped execute the Kakori train action on 9 August 1925. (high confidence)
- Was executed at Gorakhpur Jail on 19 December 1927. (high confidence)
