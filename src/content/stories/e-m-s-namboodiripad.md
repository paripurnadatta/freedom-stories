## Research notes

Participated in anti-colonial and socialist politics.

### Overview

Kerala nationalist-socialist who left college for the freedom struggle, was jailed in satyagraha and helped connect anti-imperialist politics with peasant and worker organization.

### Chronology

- **1931**: Left college for the freedom struggle and was imprisoned
- **1934**: Became an all-India Congress Socialist Party joint secretary
- **late 1930s-1940s**: Worked in Kerala's left-led peasant and worker political networks

### Evidence claims

- E. M. S. Namboodiripad left college in 1931 to join the freedom struggle and was jailed in satyagraha. (HIGH confidence)
- He became a leading Congress Socialist in Kerala and was elected an all-India joint secretary of the Congress Socialist Party in 1934. (HIGH confidence)
- His 1930s-40s politics increasingly linked national freedom with worker, peasant and anti-feudal struggles. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Malabar / Kerala region: Congress, socialist and peasant politics
- Kannur / Vellore detention contexts: Freedom-struggle imprisonment context
