## Research notes

Documented association with tribal / forest resistance in Madhya Pradesh.

### Overview

Tribal leader from Chhindwara in Central India associated with mass protest and forest-law resistance. A Government of India museum note records a major demonstration at the Collector's bungalow in 1923, arrest, renewed arrest for breaking forest laws in August 1930, and death in prison in 1940.

### Chronology

- **1923**: Led mass demonstration at the Collector's bungalow.... Led mass demonstration at the Collector's bungalow in Chhindwara
- **1930-08**: Arrested at Ramakona for violating forest laws.. Arrested at Ramakona for violating forest laws
- **1940**: Died in prison; official note says he was reported.... Died in prison; official note says he was reportedly poisoned

### Evidence claims

- Badal Bhoi was born in 1845 in Chhindwara district and became a tribal resistance leader. (high confidence)
- In 1923 thousands of tribal protesters demonstrated at the Collector's bungalow under his leadership and he was arrested after a lathi charge. (high confidence)
- He was arrested again in August 1930 at Ramakona for breaking forest laws and died in prison in 1940. (high confidence)
