### Overview

Socialist thinker and anti-colonial organiser whose freedom-struggle career connected Civil Disobedience, the Congress Socialist Party and the underground phase of Quit India. Educated partly in Berlin, Lohia returned to India with a developed socialist orientation and joined the nationalist struggle. Parliament and Ministry of Culture sources place him among the central figures of India's socialist current. His post-1947 political thought should be included only as legacy context.
