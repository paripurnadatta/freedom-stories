## Research notes

HSRA revolutionary and political prisoner.

### Chronology

- **1928-09-08/1928-09-09**: HSRA reorganization meeting
- **1929-05-13**: Arrest at Saharanpur Bomb Factory
- **1946-02**: Release

### Evidence claims

- Official sources use both “Shiv Verma” and “Shiv Varma” for the same revolutionary. (HIGH confidence)
- He was a member of the Hindustan Socialist Republican Association and participated in the 1928 Feroz Shah Kotla reorganization meeting. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Non-Cooperation Movement at age 17. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested on 13 May 1929 at the Saharanpur Bomb Factory with Gaya Prasad and Jaidev Kapoor. (HIGH confidence)
- He was sentenced to life imprisonment in the Lahore Conspiracy Case and deported to the Cellular Jail in the Andamans. (HIGH confidence)
- He was released in February 1946. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Khateli, Hardoi district: birthplace
- Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi: HSRA meeting
- Saharanpur: arrest/bomb factory
- Cellular Jail, Andaman: imprisonment/hunger strike
