## Research notes

Revolutionary organizer who later became a writer.

### Chronology

- **1929-12-23**: Bombing of the Viceroy’s train
- **1932-01-22**: Arrest

### Evidence claims

- Yashpal was born on 3 December 1903 in Bhumpal, Hamirpur. (HIGH confidence)
- After an early Gandhian/Non-Cooperation phase, Yashpal joined the revolutionary movement associated with the HSRA. (HIGH confidence)
- He was linked to the 23 December 1929 bombing of the train carrying Viceroy Irwin. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested on 22 January 1932 after becoming a senior HSRA leader. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bhumpal, Hamirpur: reported birthplace
