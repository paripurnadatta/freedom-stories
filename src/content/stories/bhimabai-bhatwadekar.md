## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Bhimabai Bhatwadekar's connection with Civil Disobedience Movement.

### Overview

Bombay Civil Disobedience satyagrahi arrested and convicted in December 1930.

### Chronology

- **1930-12-04**: Arrested, convicted and sentenced for Civil Disobedience activity in Bombay

### Evidence claims

- Bhimabai Bhatwadekar participated in the Civil Disobedience Movement in Bombay. (HIGH confidence)
- She was arrested and convicted on 4 December 1930. (HIGH confidence)
- The sentence was three months' simple imprisonment and a fine of Rs 100, with an additional default term if the fine was unpaid. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bombay: Civil Disobedience and arrest
