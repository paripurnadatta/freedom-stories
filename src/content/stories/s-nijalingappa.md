## Research notes

Documented association with Mysore Congress in Karnataka.

### Overview

Mysore State Congress organizer who campaigned for representative government, participated in Forest Satyagraha and was imprisoned again during Quit India.

### Chronology

- **1938**: Helped establish Mysore State Congress
- **1939**: Participated in Forest Satyagraha at Thuruvanuru and was imprisoned
- **1942**: Joined Quit India and was arrested again

### Evidence claims

- S. Nijalingappa joined Congress as a young man and became a major organizer in Mysore State. (HIGH confidence)
- He helped establish the Mysore State Congress in 1938 to demand representative and accountable government. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Forest Satyagraha and received an eighteen-month prison sentence. (HIGH confidence)
- After release he joined Quit India and was arrested again. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Mysore State / Chitradurga region: Representative-government agitation
- Thuruvanuru: Forest Satyagraha
