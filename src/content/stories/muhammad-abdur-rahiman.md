## Research notes

Nationalist journalist and leader in Malabar.

### Nationalism in a divided aftermath

His relief work after 1921 should be presented carefully, without flattening the complex and violent history of the Malabar Rebellion.

### Al-Ameen as political infrastructure

The newspaper enabled a specifically Muslim public sphere for nationalism in Malabar.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Relief work and imprisonment after the Malabar Rebellion. Abdur Rahiman becomes deeply involved in the political aftermath of the uprising
- **1924**: Al-Ameen established. The newspaper becomes a vehicle for nationalism and reform among Malabar Muslims
- **1930**: Salt Satyagraha. He joins salt-law defiance and is injured in police action
- **1940-1945**: Long wartime imprisonment. Colonial authorities confine him for most of the war years
