## Research notes

### Overview

Korku leader from Betul who became prominent in the 1930 forest satyagraha during Civil Disobedience. Ministry of Culture records that he led hundreds of Gond and Korku protesters into reserved forest on 22 August 1930, evaded immediate arrest, and was later captured and sentenced to five years' rigorous imprisonment.

### Evidence claims

- Ganjan Singh Korku was a prominent leader of forest satyagraha in Betul district during Civil Disobedience. (high confidence)
- On 22 August 1930 he led hundreds of Gond and Korku protesters into reserved forest in defiance of colonial forest law. (high confidence)
