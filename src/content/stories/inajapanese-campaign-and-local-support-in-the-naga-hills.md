## Research notes

During the 1944 Japanese–INA advance through the Naga Hills, local residents performed different documented roles. At Ruzazho, Poswuyi Swuro was appointed area administrator and Vesuyi Swuro interpreter in Netaji's local wartime administration; at Phesama, Rokosieo served Japanese forces as interpreter, mediator and food collector.
