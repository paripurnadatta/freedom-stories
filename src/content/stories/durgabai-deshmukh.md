## Research notes

Joined Salt Satyagraha and later organized women and relief work.

### Politics before adulthood

Her profile shows how nationalist organization could draw adolescents into disciplined public roles long before they entered formal politics.

### Women's mobilisation as movement infrastructure

Durgabai should not be presented simply as an individual heroine; her importance includes organizing other women into satyagraha.

### Chronology

- **1923**: Kakinada Congress volunteer. The young Durgabai is given responsibility at the Khadi Exhibition during the Congress session
- **1930-1933**: Civil Disobedience and repeated imprisonment. She organizes women satyagrahis and is imprisoned multiple times

- Durgabai Deshmukh was involved in nationalist politics from an unusually young age and established a Hindi school for girls. (high confidence)
