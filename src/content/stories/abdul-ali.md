## Research notes

Civilian participant killed during the 1946 Bombay unrest.

### Chronology

- **1946-02-23**: Participated in a Bombay demonstration supporting the RIN ratings and was fatally shot by British police

### Evidence claims

- Abdul Ali was a Bombay resident born in 1929 who participated in public support for the 1946 Royal Indian Navy ratings' revolt. (HIGH confidence)
- He was severely wounded by British police firing during a demonstration on 23 February 1946 and died the same day. (HIGH confidence)
- The source identifies Abdul Ali as a civilian demonstrator/supporter of the naval ratings rather than as a Royal Indian Navy rating. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bombay: residence and fatal demonstration
