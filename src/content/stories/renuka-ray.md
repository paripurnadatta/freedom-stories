## Research notes

Nationalist and women's rights organizer.

### Overview

Gandhian activist and women's-rights advocate whose pre-independence work linked nationalist protest, the All India Women's Conference and campaigns for women's legal and labour rights.

### Chronology

- **1920s**: Entered Gandhian nationalist and women's political work
- **1930s-1940s**: Worked through the All India Women's Conference on legal and social reform
- **1946**: Entered the Constituent Assembly from West Bengal

### Evidence claims

- Renuka Ray was drawn into Gandhian anti-colonial politics at a young age and spent time in Gandhi's ashram while participating in protests. (HIGH confidence)
- She served as legal secretary of the All India Women's Conference and worked on women's legal disabilities, trafficking and labour conditions. (HIGH confidence)
- Her later Constituent Assembly work extended a pre-existing women's-rights political agenda rather than beginning only after independence. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Calcutta / Bengal: Nationalist and women's-rights activity
- India-wide AIWC network: Women's legal and labour advocacy
