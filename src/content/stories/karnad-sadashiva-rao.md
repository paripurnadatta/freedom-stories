## Research notes

Documented association with Non-Cooperation / Salt Satyagraha in Karnataka.

### Overview

Mangalore Gandhian organizer who led Non-Cooperation in South Karnataka, built national institutions and participated in Salt Satyagraha.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Participated in Congress/Non-Cooperation organizing in South Karnataka
- **1930**: Joined Ankola Salt Satyagraha and was imprisoned
- **1934**: Arrested again during Gandhian protest activity

### Evidence claims

- Karnad Sadashiva Rao became a foremost Non-Cooperation leader in South Karnataka and promoted national schools and handloom work. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Ankola Salt Satyagraha in 1930 and was imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)
- He established constructive-nationalist institutions including Sadhakashrama and Tilak Vidyalaya in Mangalore. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Mangalore / Dakshina Kannada: Non-Cooperation and constructive work
- Ankola: Salt Satyagraha
