## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Snehlata Varma's connection with Mewar freedom movement; women's mobilization.

### Overview

Woman activist in Mewar associated with the later phase of the Bijolia peasant movement and with education/uplift of women. Ministry and Rajasthan cultural sources identify her alongside Manikyalal Verma and other women organizers in the 1927-41 phase of agrarian mobilisation.

### Chronology

- **1927-1941**: Active in the third phase of the Bijolia movement .... Active in the third phase of the Bijolia movement and women's mobilization
