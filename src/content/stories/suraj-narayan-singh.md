Nepal border region: Cross-border underground zone (Training and refuge)

### Overview

Revolutionary and socialist freedom fighter from Bihar whose best-documented contribution comes during the Quit India underground. After escaping Hazaribagh Central Jail with Jayaprakash Narayan, Yogendra Shukla and others in November 1942, he helped paralyse colonial administration in north Bihar, participated in building the Azad Dasta network and later helped raid Hanuman Nagar jail to free detained leaders. His profile is an important direct bridge to Jayaprakash Narayan.

### Chronology

- **1943-05**: Participated in Hanuman Nagar jail raid

### Evidence claims

- Suraj Narayan Singh became involved in anti-colonial politics from youth and was influenced by the execution of Bhagat Singh. (HIGH confidence)
- On 9 November 1942 he escaped Hazaribagh Central Jail with Jayaprakash Narayan, Yogendra Shukla and other leaders. (HIGH confidence)
- He helped establish and lead Azad Dasta structures in north Bihar/Nepal during the Quit India underground. (HIGH confidence)
- In May 1943 he participated in the Hanuman Nagar jail raid that freed Jayaprakash Narayan and other leaders. (HIGH confidence)
