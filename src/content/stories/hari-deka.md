## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Hari Deka's connection with Peasant anti-colonial resistance.

### Overview

Peasant participant from Mangaldoi subdivision in Darrang district killed in the January 1894 anti-revenue rising. His ICHR Dictionary of Martyrs entry is archival and concise, recording that he was hit by police fire and died on the spot.

### Chronology

- **1894-01**: Participated in the Mangaldoi-area peasant uprisin.... Participated in the Mangaldoi-area peasant uprising and was killed in police firing

### Evidence claims

- Hari Deka was a resident of Mangaldoi in Darrang district and participated in the January 1894 peasant rising against British authorities. (high confidence)
- He was hit by police fire and died on the spot. (high confidence)
- The wider movement protested enhanced land-revenue assessments in Darrang and neighbouring Assam districts. (high confidence)
