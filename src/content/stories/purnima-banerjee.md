## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Purnima Banerjee's connection with Congress organization; Quit India-era nationalist movement.

### Overview

Allahabad Congress Socialist organizer who participated in Individual Satyagraha and Quit India while building links to peasants and trade unions.

### Chronology

- **1934**: Joined Congress Socialist Party and deepened organizational work
- **1941**: Arrested in Individual Satyagraha
- **1942**: Participated in Quit India and faced further imprisonment
- **1946**: Entered the Constituent Assembly

### Evidence claims

- Purnima Banerjee joined the Congress Socialist Party in 1934 and served as secretary of the Allahabad City Congress Committee. (HIGH confidence)
- She participated in Individual Satyagraha and was arrested in 1941. (HIGH confidence)
- She later participated in Quit India and endured multiple imprisonments. (HIGH confidence)
- Her Congress work included meetings with kisans and trade unions to broaden rural and working-class engagement. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Allahabad / Prayagraj: Congress Socialist, kisan and trade-union organizing
