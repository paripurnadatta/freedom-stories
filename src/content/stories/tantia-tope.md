## Research notes

Major military commander in the 1857 uprising.

### The rebellion after the cities fell

Tope is crucial for understanding how the uprising continued as a mobile military struggle.

### Mobility as strategy

His campaign should eventually be visualized as a route rather than a single pin.

### An execution with a disputed afterlife

Because an official retrospective profile itself notes controversy, the enriched record must preserve that uncertainty.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Kanpur campaign. Commands rebel forces in the Kanpur theatre
- **1858**: Central India campaign. Continues fighting through mobile operations
- **1859-04-18**: Reported execution at Sipri. Conventional execution date; a supplied official profile notes later controversy about identity

### Evidence claims

- Was also known as Ram Chandra Pandurang and was closely associated with Nana Sahib. (high confidence)
- Played a major military role in the 1857 fighting around Kanpur and later Central India. (high confidence)
- Used highly mobile and guerrilla-style warfare while pursued by British forces. (high confidence)
- One supplied Government of India profile notes controversy over whether the historical Tantia Tope was the person executed at Sipri on 18 April 1859. (disputed confidence)
- The supplied record connects Tantia Tope with Revolt of 1857 as participant in. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Tantia Tope with Rani Lakshmibai as worked with. (high confidence)
