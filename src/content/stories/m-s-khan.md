## Research notes

Chairman of the Naval Central Strike Committee during the 1946 uprising.

### Chronology

- **1946-02**: Served in the leadership of the Naval Central Strike Committee during the Royal Indian Navy revolt

### Evidence claims

- M.S. Khan was a leading rating in the 1946 Royal Indian Navy revolt and served as president of the Naval Central Strike Committee. (HIGH confidence)
- The Naval Central Strike Committee coordinated demands and communications among the striking naval ratings during the February 1946 revolt. (HIGH confidence)
