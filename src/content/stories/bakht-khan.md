## Research notes

Led rebel forces in Delhi.

### A soldier becomes a rebel administrator

Bakht Khan’s importance lies in combining military command with efforts to create governing structure in rebel Delhi.

### Delhi command

The rebel capital involved contested attempts to organize both command and administration.

### Resistance after Delhi

His later movement toward Awadh links Delhi’s rebellion to continued campaigns around Begum Hazrat Mahal.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Command in Delhi. Takes high command under Bahadur Shah Zafar
- **1857-1858**: From Delhi to Awadh. Continues resistance after the collapse of rebel Delhi

### Evidence claims

- Had extensive prior military experience before joining the 1857 rebellion. (high confidence)
- Arrived in Delhi with rebel troops and was appointed commander-in-chief under Bahadur Shah Zafar. (high confidence)
- Attempted to rationalize military and governing arrangements in rebel Delhi. (high confidence)
- After Delhi became untenable he moved toward Awadh and fought alongside Begum Hazrat Mahal. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Bakht Khan with Revolt of 1857 as participant in. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Bakht Khan with Bahadur Shah Zafar as served under. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Bakht Khan with Begum Hazrat Mahal as worked with. (high confidence)
