## Research notes

Senior commander in Siraj ud-Daulah's forces.

### Chronology

- **1756**: Elevated to senior office under Sirajuddaula
- **1757-06-23**: Battle of Plassey
- **1757-07**: Reported imprisonment at Purnia after Sirajuddaula’s death

### Evidence claims

- Under Sirajuddaula, Mohanlal was elevated to peshkar of the Diwan Khana and became an influential figure in the administration. (HIGH confidence)
- Mohanlal remained an active commander on Sirajuddaula’s side during the Battle of Plassey and continued to press the fight after Mir Madan was struck. (HIGH confidence)
- The Riyazu-s-Salatin records Mohanlal protesting the order to withdraw/reconcentrate troops during the Plassey fighting. (HIGH confidence)
- A later official district history states that after Sirajuddaula’s death, Mohan Lal was imprisoned at Purnia when Nazir Ali Khan seized control of the town and treasury. (MEDIUM confidence)

### Places

- Murshidabad: court/administrative centre
- Plassey: battlefield
- Purnia: post-Plassey imprisonment context
