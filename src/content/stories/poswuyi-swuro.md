Appointed Dobashi/area administrator by Netaji at Ruzazho and assisted INA-Japanese forces with local communication, supplies and route guidance.

### Evidence claims

- Poswuyi Swuro helped guide/support wartime forces associated with Netaji’s advance into the Naga Hills and served as a local intermediary. (HIGH confidence)
