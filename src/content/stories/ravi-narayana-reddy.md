## Research notes

Led peasant-rights agitation in Nalgonda and helped radicalize the Andhra Mahasabha's program.

### Historical geography

Nalgonda / Telangana: Hyderabad State (Peasant movement base)

### Overview

Telangana peasant leader and freedom fighter associated with the Andhra Mahasabha and the anti-feudal/armed peasant struggle in Hyderabad State. Telangana government commemoration emphasizes his role in struggles over land, livelihood and the welfare of marginalized people. His profile should explain that this was simultaneously anti-feudal, anti-Nizam and, in a broader sense, anti-colonial-era politics rather than simply a Congress campaign.
