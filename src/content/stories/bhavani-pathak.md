### Overview

A prominent sannyasi leader associated with the Fakir-Sannyasi resistance in late eighteenth-century Bengal. Academic sources place Bhavani Pathak among the best-known leaders alongside Majnu Shah and describe links between sannyasi and fakir bands. Later literary representations—especially the Devi Chaudhurani tradition—must be kept separate from recoverable historical evidence.
