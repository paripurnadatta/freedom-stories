### Evidence claims

- Kashi Prasad Jayaswal lived from 1881 to 1937 and was born in Mirzapur. (HIGH confidence)
- Jayaswal was a barrister, lawyer and nationalist historian whose scholarship focused heavily on ancient Indian political institutions. (HIGH confidence)
- His proposed Calcutta University appointment was blocked by the colonial government because of his political associations/views. (HIGH confidence)
- He practiced law in Calcutta and later moved to Patna in 1914, continuing legal and scholarly work there. (HIGH confidence)
- He played an important role in establishing the Bihar and Orissa Research Society in 1914. (HIGH confidence)
- His writings on “Hindu polity” argued for sophisticated political institutions in ancient India and became a landmark in nationalist historiography. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry profile identifies History of India, 150 A.D. to 350 A.D. (1933) as one of his major works. (HIGH confidence)
