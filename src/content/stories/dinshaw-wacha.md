### Historical geography

Bombay: Bombay Presidency (Political and municipal base)

### Overview

Parsi publicist, economic critic and founder-member of the Indian National Congress. Wacha served for years as Congress secretary, worked closely with Dadabhai Naoroji and Pherozeshah Mehta, and became Congress president in 1901. He specialized in public finance, exposing the fiscal and military burdens of colonial government. His profile helps explain the economic critique that underpinned early constitutional nationalism.
