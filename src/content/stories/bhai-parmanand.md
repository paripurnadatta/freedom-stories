## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Bhai Parmanand's connection with Ghadar Movement; nationalist organizing.

### Evidence claims

- Bhai Parmanand was born on 4 November 1876 at Kariala in Jhelum district. (HIGH confidence)
- He was associated with Ghadar-era revolutionary organization and nationalist writing. (HIGH confidence)
- He received a death sentence in the 1915 Lahore Conspiracy Case, later commuted to transportation for life. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned in the Andamans until release under amnesty in 1920. (HIGH confidence)
