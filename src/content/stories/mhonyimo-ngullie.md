## Research notes

Speared and mortally wounded British political officer Captain John Butler during the 1875 Pangti resistance.

### Overview

Lotha Naga figure from Pangti village remembered in local oral tradition for resistance to British expansion in the Naga Hills. The Ministry of Culture profile explicitly acknowledges that birth/death details and much documentary information are unknown and that the surviving account depends heavily on oral history. This profile should therefore remain concise and transparently oral-history based.

### Evidence claims

- Mhonyimo Ngullie hailed from Pangti village in present-day Wokha district and is remembered as an opponent of British control. (high confidence)
- The Ministry of Culture account explicitly states that much of his story survives through oral tradition and that birth/death details are unknown. (high confidence)
- His remembered resistance belongs to the period of expanding British expeditions into the Naga Hills. (high confidence)
