## Research notes

Led underground activity and a parallel government in Satara during the Quit India period.

### Overview

Satara underground nationalist and organizer of the Quit India-era parallel government known as the Prati/Patri Sarkar.

### Chronology

- **1929-1932**: Underground anti-colonial activity
- **1932-1942**: Repeated imprisonment and political organizing
- **1942-1946**: Quit India underground activity and Satara parallel-government leadership

### Evidence claims

- Nana Patil spent periods underground and was repeatedly imprisoned for anti-colonial activity before Quit India. (HIGH confidence)
- During Quit India he remained underground for an extended period and became a leading figure in the Satara underground movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was instrumental in organizing the Satara parallel government, commonly known as the Prati/Patri Sarkar, which functioned from 1943 into 1946. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Satara / Sangli: Underground movement and parallel government
