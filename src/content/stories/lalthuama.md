## Research notes

Son and ally of Ropuiliani, captured with her in 1893 and deported to Rangamati in 1894.

### Overview

Son of Lalnu Ropuiliani and a participant in the southern Lushai resistance who was captured and imprisoned alongside the anti-colonial chief.

### Chronology

- **1893**: Lalthuama was captured/imprisoned in the colonial campaign against southern Lushai resistance
- **1890s**: Remained a prisoner during Lalnu Ropuiliani's detention; later carried her remains home

### Evidence claims

- Lalthuama was the son of Lalnu Ropuiliani and was himself a prisoner during the colonial suppression of resistance in the Lushai Hills. (HIGH confidence)
- Historical accounts place Lalthuama among resistant chiefs captured during British operations in the South Lushai Hills. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry account records that Lalthuama carried his mother's remains home after her death in prison. (HIGH confidence)
