## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Hari Ram's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Ramgarh Battalion sepoy who joined the 1857 uprising, fought Company forces at Chatra and received a death sentence in October 1857.

### Chronology

- **1857-09**: Charged by colonial authorities after joining the uprising
- **1857-10-02**: Fought at Chatra
- **1857-10-13**: Received a death sentence

### Evidence claims

- The selected Hari Ram was a sepoy of the Ramgarh Battalion. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the 1857 rebels and fought Company troops at Chatra on 2 October 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- He received a death sentence on 13 October 1857. (HIGH confidence)
