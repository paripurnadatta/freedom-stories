## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Khaikhojam Kuki's connection with Kuki Rebellion.

### Overview

Kuki resistance participant from Bongbal Khollen connected to the Kuki Rebellion of 1917-18. The ICHR/Ministry of Culture Dictionary of Martyrs states that he was arrested, held in connection with the rebellion, and tortured to death in police custody on 17 September 1918, citing Manipur State Archives material.
