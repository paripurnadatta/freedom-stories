## Research notes

Poligar who resisted East India Company demands.

### Who had the right to collect tribute?

The confrontation was not simply personal defiance: it grew from a transfer of fiscal authority that threatened Palayakkarar autonomy.

### From Kattabomman to the Marudhu resistance

His execution did not end southern opposition; Sivaganga's Marudhu leadership became part of the next phase.

### Chronology

- **1798-1799**: Revenue dispute becomes political confrontation. Company claims to tribute collection sharpen the conflict with Panchalankurichi
- **1799**: Defeat, capture and execution. After armed resistance, Kattabomman is captured and executed at Kayatharu
