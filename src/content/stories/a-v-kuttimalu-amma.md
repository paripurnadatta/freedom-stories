## Research notes

Prominent woman participant in Kerala's nationalist movement.

### Women as organizers, not just participants

The Malabar women's movement had its own committees, processions and boycott campaigns.

### Repeated incarceration

Kuttimalu Amma's chronology makes the long duration of women's participation visible across multiple Gandhian phases.

### Chronology

- **1930-1931**: Women enter direct nationalist protest in Malabar. Kuttimalu helps organize public campaigning and foreign-cloth picketing
- **1932**: Civil Disobedience imprisonment. She leads a banned procession and is imprisoned
- **1940-1942**: Individual Satyagraha and Quit India. Repeated arrest fails to end her political activity
