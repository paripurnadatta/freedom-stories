Source-specific record documenting Guneswari Devi's connection with Non-Cooperation; Civil Disobedience; Quit India.

### Overview

Assamese woman organizer from Kaliabor whose nationalist career crossed Swadeshi/khadi work, Civil Disobedience and Quit India. Ministry sources describe her organizing local women, imprisonment in 1930 and a major Quit India meeting in Nagaon in 1942.

### Evidence claims

- Guneswari Devi organized women in Kaliabor around Swadeshi, khadi and anti-colonial politics. (high confidence)
- She was arrested and imprisoned during Civil Disobedience in 1930. (high confidence)
- During Quit India she organized a large public meeting in Nagaon, defended the national flag and was imprisoned again. (high confidence)
