The police had a description. They were looking for a young Sikh man from Punjab, with long hair tied under a turban and a beard on his chin. Two days earlier, a police officer had been shot dead outside the police office in Lahore. Now every road and every railway platform in the city was being watched.

The young man they wanted was Bhagat Singh. And he had a plan.

## A new face

Sikh men do not cut their hair. It is part of their faith. So the police were sure their man would still have his long hair and beard.

Bhagat Singh cut his hair short. He shaved off his beard. He put on a smart Western suit and a felt hat, the kind a rich young gentleman might wear.

<aside class="note"><b>Turban</b>: a long cloth wrapped around the head. Many Sikh men wear one over their uncut hair.</aside>

When he looked in the mirror, a stranger looked back.

## A borrowed family

A rich gentleman would not travel alone. He would have a wife, perhaps a baby, and a servant to carry the bags.

So Durga Devi, the wife of one of his comrades, agreed to pretend to be his wife. She brought her own little son along, and held him on her lap. People called her Durga Bhabhi, and she knew exactly how dangerous this was. If they were caught, she would be arrested too.

His friend Rajguru dressed as their servant and carried the luggage.

## Walking past the police

At the station, the little family walked along the platform. There were policemen everywhere, staring at every face.

They saw a gentleman in a hat. They saw his wife and their small child. They saw a servant with the bags. They did not see a Sikh revolutionary at all.

The family got onto the train, and the train pulled out of Lahore. Nobody stopped them. Days later, Bhagat Singh was safe in Calcutta, hundreds of kilometres away.

## Why it matters

A few months later, Bhagat Singh would walk into the Assembly in Delhi and throw his bombs. He would not try to escape that time.

But on this day, he showed that he could vanish whenever he wanted to. When he later chose to stay and be arrested, it was not because he was trapped. It was because he had decided to.
