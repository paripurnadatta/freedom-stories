## Research notes

Documented association with Civil Disobedience / Quit India in Odisha.

### Historical geography

Anakhia / Jagatsinghpur area: Coastal Odisha (Constructive rural work with Malati Choudhury)

### Historical geography

Dhenkanal and other Garhjat states: Odisha princely states (Praja Mandal political work)

### Overview

Odia nationalist, socialist and constructive worker who later became Chief Minister of Odisha. His pre-independence importance lies in peasant politics, Congress Socialist activity and the Praja Mandal struggles in the princely states. Together with Malati Choudhury he worked among rural communities and became a prominent organiser in Dhenkanal and related movements. Post-independence office should appear as legacy context, not dominate the freedom-struggle profile.

### Historical significance

This profile was selected for Batch 008 because it repairs one or more post-175 gaps while adding a historically distinct strand to the archive. The final implementation should privilege the verified evidence claims and chronology above over generic fame-based biography.

### Chronology

- **1927**: Married Malati Choudhury; they developed a shared programme of rural constructive work
- **1938**: Played an active role in Praja Mandal mobilisation in the Odisha princely states
- **1950-1956**: Served as Chief Minister of Odisha

### Evidence claims

- Nabakrushna Choudhury and Malati Choudhury married in 1927 and jointly pursued community and nationalist work. (HIGH confidence)
- The couple became important supporters and organisers of Praja Mandal mobilisation in Odisha's princely states, including Dhenkanal. (HIGH confidence)
- Nabakrushna Choudhury was associated with socialist currents within the nationalist movement. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)
- He later served as Chief Minister of Odisha from 1950 to 1956. (HIGH confidence)
