## Research notes

Operated the underground Congress Radio.

### Documented record

At age twenty-two, while a Wilson College student, helped establish Congress Radio during Quit India. Congress Radio broadcast from clandestine locations in Bombay to circulate uncensored movement news.

### Documented actions

Nanik Motwane and other collaborators helped provide equipment and technical support.

### Chronology

- **1942-08**: Congress Radio begins
- **1942**: Underground broadcasts
- **1942**: Colonial suppression of the station
