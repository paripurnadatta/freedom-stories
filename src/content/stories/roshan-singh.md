## Research notes

Revolutionary executed in connection with the Kakori case.

### Chronology

- **1925-08-09**: Kakori action/case context
- **1927-04-06**: Death sentence
- **1927-12-19**: Execution at Naini Jail

### Evidence claims

- Roshan Singh “Thakur” was born in 1894 in Nevada/Javada village, Shahjahanpur district. (HIGH confidence)
- He took part in the Non-Cooperation Movement before joining the revolutionary party in the United Provinces. (HIGH confidence)
- He became a close associate/admirer of Ram Prasad Bismil and took part in revolutionary raids including Bichpuri, Bamrauli and Shergunj. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested and tried in the Kakori Conspiracy Case. (HIGH confidence)
- Judge Hamilton sentenced Roshan Singh to death on 6 April 1927 along with Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqullah Khan and Rajendra Nath Lahiri. (HIGH confidence)
- He was hanged at Naini Jail, Allahabad, on 19 December 1927 at age 33. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Shahjahanpur district: birth/early activity
- Naini Jail, Allahabad: imprisonment/execution
