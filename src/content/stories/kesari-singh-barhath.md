## Research notes

Poet and nationalist organizer.

### Overview

Rajasthani revolutionary, writer and political organizer whose 1903 Chetawani ra Chungatia used bardic political language to dissuade Maharana Fateh Singh from attending Curzon's Delhi Durbar. He also helped build revolutionary and princely-state political networks in Rajputana.
