## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Avadh peasant and women's organizer who mobilized Kisanin Sabha participants against landlord exactions and linked agrarian struggle with nationalist campaigns.

### Chronology

- **1920s-1930s**: Organized women in Avadh peasant/Kisanin Sabha struggles
- **1940-1942**: Peasant-nationalist mobilization converged with individual satyagraha and Quit India

### Evidence claims

- Jaggi Devi played a leading role in the Avadh peasant movement against landlord exactions and colonial-backed agrarian oppression. (HIGH confidence)
- She organized women through Kisanin Sabhas and led them into satyagraha. (HIGH confidence)
- The source places Avadh peasant mobilization in later convergence with anti-war individual satyagraha and Quit India activity. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Pratapgarh / Avadh: Peasant and women's organizing
