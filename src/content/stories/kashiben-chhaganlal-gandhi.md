## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Kashiben Chhaganlal Gandhi's connection with Individual Satyagraha.

### Overview

Gandhian satyagrahi whose anti-colonial work spanned South Africa, early ashram life in India and Individual Satyagraha.

### Chronology

- **1913**: Participated in South African satyagraha and faced imprisonment
- **1915 onward**: Worked in Gandhian ashram/constructive institutions in India
- **1941**: Participated in Individual Satyagraha and was arrested

### Evidence claims

- Kashiben Chhaganlal Gandhi participated in Gandhi's South African satyagraha in 1913 and was arrested multiple times. (HIGH confidence)
- She later became part of the Kocharab/Sabarmati/Sevagram ashram constructive-work network in India. (HIGH confidence)
- During Individual Satyagraha in 1941 she travelled encouraging women's participation and was arrested in the United Provinces. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Phoenix / Natal: Early satyagraha
- Kocharab / Sabarmati / Sevagram: Ashram and constructive work
- United Provinces: Individual Satyagraha arrest
