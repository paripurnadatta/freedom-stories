## Research notes

Major Bengal nationalist and founder of the Swaraj Party.

### Overview

Bengal nationalist lawyer and mass political leader who moved from landmark political defence work into Non-Cooperation, Swaraj Party organization and municipal leadership.

### Chronology

- **1908**: Defended Aurobindo Ghose in the Alipore Bomb Case
- **1917**: Entered the forefront of Bengal nationalist politics
- **1920-1922**: Participated in Non-Cooperation and gave up a lucrative legal practice for nationalist politics
- **1923**: Co-founded the Swaraj Party with Motilal Nehru
- **1924**: Served as mayor of Calcutta and publicly defended Subhas Chandra Bose

### Evidence claims

- Chittaranjan Das defended Aurobindo Ghose in the Alipore Bomb Case of 1908 and gained national prominence. (HIGH confidence)
- He rose to the forefront of nationalist politics from 1917 and became an important leader during Non-Cooperation. (HIGH confidence)
- He co-founded the Swaraj Party with Motilal Nehru and became a major advocate of council-entry politics after the Non-Cooperation phase. (HIGH confidence)
- As Calcutta mayor he publicly defended Subhas Chandra Bose against colonial repression. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Calcutta / Kolkata: Legal, Congress and municipal nationalist politics
- Gaya: Congress session / Swaraj strategy context
