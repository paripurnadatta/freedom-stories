## Research notes

Congress organizer and freedom movement worker.

### The infrastructure behind women's satyagraha

The profile should focus on committees, volunteer training and picketing, not only individual courage.

### From boycott to peace work

Her career connects anti-colonial mobilization with the difficult politics of communal violence and rehabilitation.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Foreign-cloth boycott organization. Sarabhai helps coordinate women volunteers in Ahmedabad's boycott movement
- **1940s**: Peace work amid communal violence. Her activism turns toward direct intervention in riot-affected areas and organized peace work

### Evidence claims

- Mridula Sarabhai worked in the Ahmedabad women's boycott network and served as secretary of the Videsh Kapada Bahishkar Samiti. (high confidence)
- Women volunteers organized processions, meetings and picketing of foreign-cloth and liquor shops. (high confidence)
- Gandhi recognized her courage during communal crises, and she worked with the Shanti Sevak Sangh. (high confidence)
- Gandhi nominated her as a trustee of the Kasturba Gandhi National Memorial Trust. (high confidence)
