## Research notes

Major Haryana nationalist organizer who mobilized Non-Cooperation, supported Salt Satyagraha and was repeatedly imprisoned through 1942.

### Documented record

Two official government profiles disagree on whether he was born on 4 September or 7 September 1887 in Kelanga, Rohtak district. His early nationalist awakening was shaped by the exile of Lala Lajpat Rai and punishment of Bal Gangadhar Tilak. By 1917 he was publicly active and associated with Congress politics. Was closely associated with the Home Rule Movement connected with Tilak.

### Chronology

- **1887-09**: Birth — official-source disagreement
- **1907-1908**: Early nationalist awakening
- **1917**: Public nationalist activity
