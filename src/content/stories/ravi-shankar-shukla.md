### Overview

Central Provinces Congress leader whose career spanned local-body mobilization, Civil Disobedience, provincial premiership and constitutional politics. Ministry records show him using the Raipur District Council to spread satyagraha, boycott and nationalist education; constitutional sources document his later provincial and Constituent Assembly leadership.
