## Research notes

Mizo chief associated with armed resistance to British expeditions; later captured and imprisoned, dying in custody in 1896.

### Overview

Zahau Mizo chief who took part in armed resistance to British expeditionary forces in the Lushai Hills. The Ministry of Culture/ICHR Dictionary of Martyrs records his birth around 1839, participation in armed resistance in 1871, capture, imprisonment first in Rangoon and later Calcutta, and death in detention in 1896.

- Nikuala Zahau was born about 1839 and was a chief in the Aizawl/Lushai Hills region. (high confidence)
