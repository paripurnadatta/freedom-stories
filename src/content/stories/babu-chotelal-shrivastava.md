## Research notes

### Overview

Dhamtari-area nationalist organizer and peasant leader most strongly associated with the Kandel Nahar Satyagraha. He also built local institutions—a library and political council—and helped make his home a node of anti-colonial organization.

### Evidence claims

- Babu Chotelal Shrivastava was born in 1889 in Kandel and became a freedom fighter in Dhamtari. (high confidence)
- He established a library in 1915 and helped organize the Dhamtari Tehsil Political Council in 1918. (high confidence)
- He became a principal organizer of the Kandel Canal Satyagraha against punitive irrigation taxation. (high confidence)
