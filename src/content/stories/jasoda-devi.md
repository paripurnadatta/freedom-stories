## Research notes

Official profile documents repeated Civil Disobedience arrests.

### Overview

Odisha Civil Disobedience activist who trained with the Hindustan Seva Dal and repeatedly organized processions despite imprisonment in 1932.

### Chronology

- **1931**: Joined Hindustan Seva Dal training in Puri
- **1932**: Arrested in a Balasore Civil Disobedience procession and sentenced to rigorous imprisonment
- **1932**: Rearrested after renewed procession organizing and imprisoned again
- **1932**: Arrested in Bhadrak after another procession; received a further prison sentence and refused to pay the imposed fine

### Evidence claims

- Jasoda Devi entered the freedom movement in 1931 through training at the Hindustan Seva Dal centre in Puri. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1932 she joined a Civil Disobedience procession in Balasore, was arrested and sentenced to one month of rigorous imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
- After release she again organized a procession, was rearrested and received another prison term. (HIGH confidence)
- She later organized a procession in Bhadrak, was arrested after police seized the national flag she carried, and served a further sentence after refusing to pay a fine. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Puri: Hindustan Seva Dal training
- Balasore: Civil Disobedience processions and imprisonment
- Bhadrak: Procession organizing and arrest
