## Research notes

Participated in satyagraha campaigns and died in British detention.

### Not merely 'Gandhi's wife'

The profile should make her participation, arrests and political labor visible in their own right.

### A political partnership with unequal archival visibility

Her relationship to Gandhi matters historically, but it should not replace her own chronology.

### Chronology

- **early 1900s-1910s**: Satyagraha and constructive work. Kasturba participates in the evolving Gandhian repertoire of resistance and community work
- **1930s**: National satyagraha campaigns. Her public role extends into repeated national civil-disobedience phases
- **1942-1944**: Quit India imprisonment. Kasturba is detained at the Aga Khan Palace after the August 1942 crackdown and dies there in February 1944

### Evidence claims

- Gandhi Smriti's commemorative history explicitly describes Kasturba's role in early satyagraha and in three national satyagraha campaigns. (high confidence)
- During Quit India she was arrested and imprisoned with Gandhi and other associates at the Aga Khan Palace. (high confidence)
- Kasturba died while still under detention at the Aga Khan Palace on 22 February 1944. (high confidence)
