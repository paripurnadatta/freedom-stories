### Evidence claims

- Prem Kumar Sahgal lived from 1917 to 1992. (HIGH confidence)
- Sahgal served as a captain in the British Indian Army in Malaya and was taken prisoner during the Japanese campaign. (HIGH confidence)
- After becoming a prisoner of war, he joined the INA and later served as a commander in the Burma campaign. (HIGH confidence)
- Sahgal was tried jointly with Shah Nawaz Khan and Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon at the Red Fort in 1945 on the principal charge of waging war against the King. (HIGH confidence)
- The court convicted the three INA defendants; their sentences were immediately remitted and they were released. (HIGH confidence)
