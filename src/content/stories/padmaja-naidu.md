## Research notes

Recognized by the Ministry of Culture as a woman freedom fighter and arrested in Hyderabad during Quit India after a flag action at the Residency.

### Overview

Hyderabad nationalist and daughter of Sarojini Naidu who participated in anti-Nizam and Quit India-era politics and was arrested in 1942.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Arrested in Hyderabad after placing the Congress flag on the Residency building during Quit India-era agitation

### Evidence claims

- Padmaja Naidu is officially recognized by the Government of India as a freedom fighter in her own right. (HIGH confidence)
- During the Quit India period in Hyderabad she was arrested after placing the Congress flag on the Residency building. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Hyderabad: Congress and Quit India-era political activity
