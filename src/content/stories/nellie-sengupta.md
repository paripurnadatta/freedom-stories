## Research notes

Official profile documents Non-Cooperation participation and imprisonment.

### Overview

Congress leader and freedom activist who joined Non-Cooperation, supported labour struggles, endured imprisonment and became Congress president in 1933.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Participated in Non-Cooperation and khadi campaigning
- **1931**: Civil-Disobedience-era political work culminated in arrest and four months' imprisonment in Delhi
- **1933**: Elected President of the Indian National Congress

### Evidence claims

- Nellie Sengupta joined the Non-Cooperation Movement alongside her husband Jatindra Mohan Sengupta and sold khadi door to door. (HIGH confidence)
- She supported labour and worker-rights struggles connected to the Bengal-Assam Railway and steamer-service movements. (HIGH confidence)
- She was arrested after speaking at a banned meeting in Delhi and imprisoned for four months. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1933 she was elected President of the Indian National Congress. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Chittagong: Political and family base
- Calcutta: Congress and public activity
- Delhi: Banned meeting, arrest and imprisonment
