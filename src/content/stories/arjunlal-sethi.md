## Research notes

Nationalist teacher and organizer.

### Overview

Jaipur revolutionary, educator and organizer who helped link early revolutionary networks with the broader political awakening of Rajputana. Ministry records name him among founders of the Rajputana-Madhya Bharat Sabha and other Rajasthan freedom narratives repeatedly identify his influence on younger political workers.

### Chronology

- **1919**: Helped found Rajputana-Madhya Bharat Sabha.. Helped found Rajputana-Madhya Bharat Sabha

### Evidence claims

- Arjun Lal Sethi was among the founders of the Rajputana-Madhya Bharat Sabha in 1919. (high confidence)
- The Sabha aimed to awaken people of the princely states and support political/agrarian struggles against oppressive laws, rulers and jagirdars. (high confidence)
- Sethi's influence on younger Rajasthan activists is documented in Ministry profiles of later freedom fighters. (high confidence)
