### Evidence claims

- Gurbaksh Singh Dhillon lived from 1914 to 2006. (HIGH confidence)
- Dhillon joined the British Indian Army in 1933 and was sent to Singapore in 1941, where he later became a prisoner of war. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the INA and served as a commander in Burma. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry of Culture profile states that Dhillon was responsible for training and welfare of the 5th Guerrilla Regiment and was later promoted to command the Nehru Brigade. (HIGH confidence)
- Dhillon was one of the three INA officers tried jointly at the Red Fort in 1945 with Shah Nawaz Khan and Prem Kumar Sahgal. (HIGH confidence)
- The court convicted the three defendants; their sentences were immediately remitted and they were released. (HIGH confidence)
