### Evidence claims

- Achyut Patwardhan was born in 1905. (HIGH confidence)
- He was a leader of the Congress Socialist Party. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Civil Disobedience Movement in 1932 and was imprisoned several times. (HIGH confidence)
- The Nehru Archive records him as a member of the Congress Working Committee in 1936. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Quit India Movement and remained underground from 1942 to 1946. (HIGH confidence)
- During the Quit India period, Patwardhan wrote some Congress Radio talks and contributed to the logistics of setting up the clandestine station. (HIGH confidence)
- The Nehru Archive states that he retired from politics in 1950. (HIGH confidence)
