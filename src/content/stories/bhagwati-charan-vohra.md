## Research notes

HSRA organizer and theorist; died during bomb testing.

### Chronology

- **1928-09-08/1928-09-09**: HSRA reorganization meeting
- **1929-12-23**: Viceregal Train bombing plan
- **1930-05-28**: Fatal bomb test on Ravi bank

### Evidence claims

- He was a founder and secretary of the Naujawan Bharat Sabha. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the 8-9 September 1928 Feroz Shah Kotla meeting that reorganized the HRA into the HSRA. (HIGH confidence)
- The official profile says the 23 December 1929 Viceregal Train bombing plan was made by Vohra together with Yashpal. (HIGH confidence)
- Vohra wrote “Philosophy of the Bomb,” the HSRA response to Gandhi’s criticism of revolutionary violence. (HIGH confidence)
- He died on 28 May 1930 after a bomb exploded while being tested on the banks of the Ravi. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Lahore: birth and revolutionary activity
- Feroz Shah Kotla, Delhi: HSRA reorganization meeting
- Ravi river bank: bomb testing/death
