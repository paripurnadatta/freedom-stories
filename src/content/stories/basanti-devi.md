## Research notes

Prominent woman nationalist and spouse of C.R. Das.

### Overview

Bengal nationalist organizer whose political role became especially visible during Non-Cooperation. She was arrested in 1921, served as Bengal Congress president, sustained nationalist journalism after C.R. Das's death and became a public symbol of women's participation in mass politics.

- Basanti Devi joined the freedom struggle and was arrested during Non-Cooperation in 1921. (high confidence)
