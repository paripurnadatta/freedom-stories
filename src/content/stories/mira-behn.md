## Research notes

British-born Gandhian follower who participated in the movement.

### Overview

British-born disciple of Gandhi who came to India in 1925, joined constructive nationalist work, accompanied Gandhi to the Round Table Conference and was imprisoned during Quit India.

### Chronology

- **1925**: Came to India to join Gandhi and his ashram work
- **1931-1932**: Accompanied Gandhi to the Round Table Conference
- **1942-1944**: Confined at Aga Khan Palace after arrest during Quit India

### Evidence claims

- Madeleine Slade, known as Mira Behn, came to India in 1925 to join Gandhi. (HIGH confidence)
- She accompanied Gandhi to the Round Table Conference in 1931-32 and later lived/worked in Sevagram. (HIGH confidence)
- She was arrested during the Quit India Movement and confined at the Aga Khan Palace from 1942 to 1944. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Sabarmati / Dehradun: Ashram and constructive work
- London: Round Table Conference
- Pune / Aga Khan Palace: Quit India detention
