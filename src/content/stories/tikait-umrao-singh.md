## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Awadh resistance in 1857.

### Overview

Khatanga estate leader in Chotanagpur who organized opposition to Company rule in 1857 and worked with other Ranchi-region resistance leaders.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Organized anti-Company resistance in the Ranchi/Chotanagpur region
- **1857-1858**: Captured by British authorities and sentenced to death

### Evidence claims

- Tikait Umrao Singh was associated with Khatanga Estate in the Ranchi region and became an important 1857 resistance leader. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry account says he encouraged soldiers to join the uprising and helped organize a regional rebel force. (HIGH confidence)
- The source directly places Pandey Ganpat Rai among the leaders of the same organized rebel force. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Khatanga / Ranchi: Regional resistance and estate base
