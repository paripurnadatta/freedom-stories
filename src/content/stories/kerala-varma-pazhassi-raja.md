## Research notes

Led prolonged resistance in Malabar.

### A long war in Malabar

The resistance should be presented as a sustained regional war, not a single battle or an isolated royal rebellion.

### Terrain as strategy

Wayanad’s geography shaped the movement, logistics and survivability of the resistance.

### A coalition wider than a royal court

The participation of Kurichiya, Kurumba, Nair and Mappila fighters makes social composition central to the story.

### Chronology

- **1793-1805**: Cotiote/Pazhassi War. A long conflict develops over Company power, land, revenue and Wayanad
- **late 1790s-1800s**: Guerrilla resistance in Wayanad. Mobile warfare and local alliances prolong the conflict
- **1805-11-30**: Death in action. Pazhassi Raja is killed in the closing phase of the war

### Evidence claims

- Led a prolonged anti-Company resistance in Malabar and Wayanad during the period commonly described as the Cotiote/Pazhassi War. (high confidence)
- Used mobile and guerrilla-style warfare suited to Wayanad's terrain. (high confidence)
- His forces included local Nair, Mappila, Kurichiya and Kurumba participants in varying phases of the resistance. (high confidence)
- Was killed in action on 30 November 1805 during the final phase of the conflict. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Kerala Varma Pazhassi Raja with Cotiote War as leader of event. (high confidence)
