## Research notes

Tripura freedom fighter linked to Surya Sen's revolutionary group, the Chittagong Armoury Raid and later Tripura political organization.

### Overview

Tripura anti-colonial activist whose career linked youth organizations in princely Tripura with Bengal revolutionary networks and later the Tripura people's movement. Yojana's Publications Division profile places him in the Bhatri Sangha, the Surya Sen network surrounding the Chittagong Armoury Raid, several detention regimes and later Tripura State Congress/Gana Parishad politics.

### Evidence claims

- Sachindra Lal Singh entered political activity as a school student in Agartala through Bhatri Sangha, which had links with Bengal's Jugantar movement. (high confidence)
- He became connected to Surya Sen's revolutionary network around the 1930 Chittagong Armoury Raid and was detained afterward. (high confidence)
- He later joined Congress-oriented political organization in princely Tripura and worked with Tripura State Congress/Gana Parishad politics. (high confidence)
- The Government of Tripura officially lists Sachindra Lal Singha among freedom fighters. (high confidence)
