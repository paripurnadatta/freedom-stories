### From speech to district mobilisation

The supported story is the translation of national strategy into district political organization at the opening of Non-Cooperation.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Swaraj speech at Viluppuram. Satyamurti addresses Congress activists as local Home Rule, boycott and Non-Cooperation currents converge
- **1921**: District Non-Cooperation intensifies. Anti-liquor picketing and boycott tactics expand the movement into everyday economic life
