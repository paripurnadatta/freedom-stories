## Research notes

Official profile explicitly describes him as nationalist, revolutionary, freedom fighter and journalist.

### Evidence claims

- Bhagwati Charan Panigrahi was born 1 February 1908 in Viswanathpur village, Puri district. (? confidence)
- He became active in nationalist, left and peasant political organizing in Odisha. (? confidence)
- He was associated with Prajamandal agitation and peasant mobilization. (? confidence)
- He edited Krushak, a publication focused on communist ideology and peasant issues. (? confidence)
- During the Second World War period he was arrested, convicted in a conspiracy/treason case, sentenced to about two and a half years, and sent to Angul jail. (? confidence)
- He was released in 1942 and died on 23 October 1943 after illness while engaged in relief/peasant work. (? confidence)
