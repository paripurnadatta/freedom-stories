## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Heirang Khogja's connection with Anglo-Manipur War.

### Overview

Manipuri soldier who died at the Khongjom battlefield during the 1891 Anglo-Manipur War.

### Chronology

- **1891-05-25**: Died fighting at the Khongjom battlefield during the Anglo-Manipur War

### Evidence claims

- Heirang Khogja was from Heirang in Manipur and served as a Manipuri soldier. (HIGH confidence)
- He died at the Khongjom battlefield on 25 May 1891 during the Anglo-Manipur War. (HIGH confidence)
