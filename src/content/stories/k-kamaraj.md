## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Tamil Congress organizer who entered politics young, participated in flag and Salt Satyagrahas and endured repeated imprisonment through Quit India.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Became a full-time Congress worker
- **1923-1925**: Participated in Nagpur Flag Satyagraha
- **1930**: Imprisoned for Salt Satyagraha
- **1942-08**: Arrested during Quit India

### Evidence claims

- K. Kamaraj became a full-time Congress political worker in 1920. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Nagpur Flag Satyagraha and later Salt Satyagraha, for which he received a two-year jail term in 1930. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested again in August 1942 in connection with Quit India. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Virudhunagar: Congress political base
- Madras / Tamil region: Satyagraha and Congress organizing
