## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Raja Ram Shastri's connection with Non-Cooperation; revolutionary and labour movement.

### Overview

Non-Cooperation participant, Lahore librarian connected to revolutionary reading networks, and later Kanpur labour organizer.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Participated in Non-Cooperation
- **1926-1931**: Worked at Dwarkadas Library in Lahore and supported revolutionary reading networks
- **1931 onward**: Became active in Kanpur labour movement

### Evidence claims

- Raja Ram Shastri participated in Gandhi's Non-Cooperation Movement in 1921. (HIGH confidence)
- From 1926 to 1931 he worked as librarian at Dwarkadas Library in Lahore, founded by Lala Lajpat Rai. (HIGH confidence)
- He became close to Bhagat Singh and other revolutionaries and supplied books to them, including during imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
- From 1931 he became active in the labour movement in Kanpur. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Lahore: Dwarkadas Library and revolutionary intellectual network
- Kanpur: Labour movement
