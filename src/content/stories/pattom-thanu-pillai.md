## Research notes

Documented association with Travancore State Congress in Kerala.

### Overview

Travancore State Congress leader who became a central figure in the agitation for responsible government and was imprisoned after the organization was proscribed.

### Chronology

- **1920s**: Entered nationalist politics through Non-Cooperation-era activity
- **1938**: Elected Travancore State Congress president and led responsible-government agitation
- **1938-08**: Arrested after the Travancore State Congress was proscribed
- **1945**: Arrested again in Bombay in connection with Congress activity

### Evidence claims

- Pattom Thanu Pillai joined the freedom struggle through Non-Cooperation and later became a major Travancore political leader. (HIGH confidence)
- He was elected president of the Travancore State Congress in 1938. (HIGH confidence)
- After the Travancore State Congress was proscribed in August 1938, he was arrested and imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Thiruvananthapuram: Political and legal base
- Bombay: 1945 Congress arrest context
