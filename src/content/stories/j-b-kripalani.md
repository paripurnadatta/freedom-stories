## Research notes

Congress organizer and president in the transition to independence.

### Chronology

- **1947**: Congress president during transfer of power. Congress president during transfer of power

### Evidence claims

- He was president of the Indian National Congress during the transfer of power in 1947. (high confidence)
- J.B. Kripalani’s full name was Jivatram Bhagwandas Kripalani; he was widely known as Acharya Kripalani. (high confidence)
- Kripalani played a major role in the Indian freedom struggle and worked closely within the Gandhian Congress movement. (high confidence)
- The official profile gives 11 November 1888–19 March 1982. (high confidence)
- The official profile associates him with major Gandhian phases including Civil Disobedience, Quit India and work around Noakhali. (high confidence)
- PMML records Kripalani’s participation in the Champaran Satyagraha in 1917. (high confidence)
- PMML records J.B. Kripalani as General Secretary of the Indian National Congress from 1934 to 1946 and its President in 1946–47. (high confidence)
