## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Chaodonpa's connection with Anglo-Manipur War.

### Overview

Manipuri resistance participant who fought in the 1891 war against British forces and died from wounds received in battle.

### Chronology

- **1891**: Participated in the Anglo-Manipur conflict against British forces
- **1891**: Received fatal wounds at Kheba Hill in the Khumbong/Thoubal area

### Evidence claims

- Chaodonpa hailed from Kiyamgei in Imphal, Manipur. (HIGH confidence)
- He took part in Manipur's 1891 war against British rule. (HIGH confidence)
- He was badly wounded by enemy fire at Kheba Hill near Khumbong/Thoubal and died from those injuries. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kiyamgei / Imphal: Home region
- Kheba Hill / Khumbong / Thoubal: Battle and fatal wounds
