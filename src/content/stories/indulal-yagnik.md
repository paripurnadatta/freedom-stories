## Research notes

Gujarat activist and later Mahagujarat leader.

### A bridge between Gandhian and agrarian politics

Yagnik's career shows how people could move from early Gandhian campaigns toward more class-focused peasant organization.

### Print, province and peasantry

His profile ties together Gujarati political journalism and national agrarian networks.

### Chronology

- **1917-1918**: Home Rule and Kheda phase. Yagnik works in Gujarat's Home Rule and peasant-satyagraha political environment
- **1920s**: Congress and nationalist journalism. He works through Gujarat political organization and print, including the Navjivan milieu
- **1936-1942**: Kisan Sabha politics. Yagnik becomes an important all-India peasant leader and later presides over an AIKS session

### Evidence claims

- Government cultural history lists Indulal Yagnik among prominent leaders of the All India Kisan Sabha formed in 1936. (high confidence)
- Archival reference material describes his earlier involvement in the Home Rule movement in Gujarat, the Kheda Satyagraha, Gujarat Congress organization and nationalist journalism. (high confidence)
- A Government of India speech recalls that Navjivan passed into Gandhi's editorship from Indulal Yagnik, reflecting his role in Gujarati nationalist journalism. (medium confidence)
- By the late 1930s and early 1940s his politics were deeply engaged with peasant organization and Kisan Sabha leadership. (high confidence)
