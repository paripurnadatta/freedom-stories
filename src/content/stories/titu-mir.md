## Research notes

Led resistance in Bengal against oppressive revenue and zamindari structures backed by colonial rule.

### Chronology

- **1831**: Led the final phase of the Barasat-region rising and was killed in confrontation with British forces

### Evidence claims

- Titu Mir was Syed Mir Nisar Ali, a Bengali peasant and religious leader who lived from 1782 to 1831. (HIGH confidence)
- He led resistance against local zamindars and European indigo planters and ultimately confronted British authority. (HIGH confidence)
- Titu Mir was associated with the Tariqah-i-Muhammadiya reform current in Bengal. (HIGH confidence)
- His movement culminated in armed confrontation with British forces in 1831. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Chandpur: reported birthplace
- Barasat region: principal area of revolt
