## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Hem Baruah's connection with Quit India Movement.

### Overview

Assamese poet and political activist who left his college teaching post to join Quit India and was imprisoned in 1943.

### Chronology

- **1941**: Began serving as an English lecturer at J.B. College, Jorhat
- **1942**: Left the college post and joined the Quit India Movement
- **1943**: Imprisoned for participation in the movement

### Evidence claims

- Hem Baruah served as an English lecturer at J.B. College in Jorhat in 1941. (HIGH confidence)
- He left his job in 1942 to join the Quit India Movement. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned in 1943 for his participation in the movement. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Jorhat: Teaching and entry into Quit India
- Assam: Political activity
