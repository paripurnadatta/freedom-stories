## Research notes

### Overview

Chhattisgarh nationalist organizer active in student/youth politics, forest satyagrahas and the second Civil Disobedience phase.

### Chronology

- **1925**: Entered visible public activism through a student conference
- **1928**: Active in the Raipur Youth League
- **1930**: Participated in forest satyagraha organizing
- **1932-03-29**: Arrested during the second Civil Disobedience phase
- **1942**: Died according to the Ministry profile

### Evidence claims

- Shankar Rao Ganoudwale was born in 1887 near Raipur in Ganoud. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in a 1925 student conference and became active in a Youth League led by Thakur Pyarelal Singh and Rauf Khan in 1928. (HIGH confidence)
- He helped mobilize support during forest satyagrahas including Tamora and Rudri. (HIGH confidence)
- During the second Civil Disobedience phase he was arrested on 29 March 1932 and sentenced to four months' imprisonment and a fine. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Ganoud / Raipur: Home and youth politics
- Tamora / Mahasamund: Forest Satyagraha
- Rudri / Dhamtari: Jungle Satyagraha
