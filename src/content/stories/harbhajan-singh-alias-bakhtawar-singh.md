## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Harbhajan Singh alias Bakhtawar Singh's connection with Ghadar Movement; Naujawan Bharat Sabha.

### Evidence claims

- Harbhajan Singh was also known as Bakhtawar Singh/Bakhtawar Singh Sathi in Ghadar records. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Ghadar revolutionary planning connected with Bhai Randhir Singh and Ferozepur. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested on 9 May 1915. (HIGH confidence)
- He was convicted on 3 March 1916 in the Second Lahore Conspiracy Case and sentenced to transportation for life, later reduced. (HIGH confidence)
