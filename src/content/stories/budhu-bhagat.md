## Research notes

Led Oraon resistance against colonial and landlord oppression.

### Before 1857, a major Chotanagpur rebellion

His profile helps establish that the region's anti-colonial history did not begin with the sepoy uprising.

### Colonialism through local intermediaries

The conflict shows how resistance could be directed simultaneously at Company authority and the revenue, credit and landlord networks through which it operated.

### Chronology

- **1831**: The Chotanagpur uprising expands. Budhu Bhagat becomes a prominent organizer as attacks spread against colonial-linked institutions and intermediaries
- **1831-1832**: Mobile resistance. Local forces use knowledge of the terrain to sustain armed opposition
- **1832-02**: Death during suppression. Budhu Bhagat is killed as colonial forces crush the uprising

### Evidence claims

- Government publications identify Budhu Bhagat as an Oraon leader from Silagai who became a central figure in the 1831–32 Larka/Kol uprising. (high confidence)
- His resistance drew on anger against exploitative revenue structures, moneylenders and outside intermediaries operating under colonial rule. (high confidence)
- The government profile credits him with organizing large numbers of local fighters and using mobile or guerrilla tactics. (high confidence)
- Budhu Bhagat was killed in February 1832 during the suppression of the uprising. (high confidence)
