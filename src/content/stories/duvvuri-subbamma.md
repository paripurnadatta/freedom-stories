## Research notes

Documented association with Non-Cooperation / women in Andhra Pradesh.

### Overview

Andhra women’s nationalist organizer associated with Congress, Civil Disobedience, Salt Satyagraha, khadi and anti-untouchability work.

### Chronology

- **1923**: Participated in the Kakinada Congress session
- **1930s**: Participated in Civil Disobedience/Salt Satyagraha and was imprisoned

### Evidence claims

- Duvvuri Subbamma was an Andhra freedom activist associated with Civil Disobedience and Salt Satyagraha and was imprisoned for political activity. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)
- She participated in the 1923 Kakinada Congress session alongside other Andhra nationalist leaders. (HIGH confidence)
- Her public work also included khadi promotion and anti-untouchability activity. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)

### Places

- East Godavari / Rajahmundry: Congress and women's nationalist activity
- Kakinada: 1923 Congress session
