## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Awadh resistance in 1857.

### Overview

Ruler of Ballabhgarh who joined the 1857 uprising and allied with Bahadur Shah Zafar in the defense of Delhi's approaches. Ministry/NGMA material records his arrest, trial and execution in Delhi on 9 January 1858.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Participated in the Delhi-region uprising as ruler.... Participated in the Delhi-region uprising as ruler of Ballabhgarh
- **1858-01-09**: Executed in Delhi.. Executed in Delhi

### Evidence claims

- Raja Nahar Singh was the ruler of Ballabhgarh and joined the 1857 uprising. (high confidence)
- He joined hands with Bahadur Shah Zafar and helped guard Delhi's borders against British forces. (high confidence)
- He was arrested, tried and publicly executed on 9 January 1858. (high confidence)
