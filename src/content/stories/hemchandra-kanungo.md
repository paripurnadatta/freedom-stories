## Research notes

Revolutionary who trained abroad in bomb-making.

### Chronology

- **1871**: Birth year reported
- **1902**: Met Aurobindo Ghosh and joined Barindra Kumar’s group
- **1900s**: Paris explosives training before return to Bengal

### Evidence claims

- He was the son of Kshetramohan Kanungo of Radhanagar village in Midnapore district. (HIGH confidence)
- He met Aurobindo Ghosh in 1902 and subsequently joined Barindra Kumar Ghosh’s revolutionary group. (HIGH confidence)
- Hemchandra Kanungo obtained training in the manufacture of bombs and explosives in Paris. (HIGH confidence)
- After his return, the Anushilan Samiti applied bomb-making knowledge in Bengal, including at the Muraripukur garden house in Maniktala. (HIGH confidence)
- The Calcutta Dictionary of National Biography states that Hem Chandra was convicted in the Alipore conspiracy case, sentenced to transportation for life, and released in 1921. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Radhanagar, Midnapore: home/origin
- Paris: training
- Maniktala, Calcutta: bomb-making/revolutionary activity
