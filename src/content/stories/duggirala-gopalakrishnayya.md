## Research notes

Documented association with Non-Cooperation in Andhra Pradesh.

### When non-cooperation meant leaving the town

Chirala-Perala stands out because protest was spatial: residents attempted to defeat municipal taxation by moving beyond the municipality.

### Local grievance, national repertoire

The campaign was locally specific but consciously connected to Gandhi and the wider Non-Cooperation moment.

### Chronology

- **1919-1921**: Chirala-Perala tax conflict. Municipal restructuring and a tenfold tax increase produce an organized no-tax campaign
- **1921**: Rama Dandu and mass evacuation. After consulting Gandhi, Duggirala organizes a disciplined volunteer corps and relocation to Ramnagar
- **1923**: Kakinada Congress network. He remains part of the wider Andhra Congress political sphere

### Evidence claims

- Duggirala Gopalakrishnayya led the no-tax campaign against conversion of Chirala and Perala into a municipality with sharply higher taxation. (high confidence)
- In 1921 he met Gandhi in Vijayawada to discuss the campaign's strategy. (high confidence)
- He created the Rama Dandu volunteer corps and organized a mass relocation of roughly 13,000 residents to a camp outside the villages. (high confidence)
- His imprisonment contributed to the campaign's decline. (high confidence)
- He later appears among prominent Andhra leaders associated with the 1923 Kakinada Congress session. (high confidence)
