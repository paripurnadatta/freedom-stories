## Research notes

Documented association with tribal resistance / 1857 in Maharashtra / Central India.

### A forest-region resistance leader

The profile adds Gond and eastern Maharashtra/Central India history to a graph otherwise dominated by better-documented urban and princely centres.

### Evidence depth matters

Modern commemoration is strong, but this package deliberately avoids turning commemorative memory into unsupported operational detail.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Armed resistance in Chanda. Shedmake becomes associated with a Gond-led challenge to British authority in the Chanda forest region
- **1858**: Suppression, capture and execution. The regional rising is suppressed and Shedmake is captured and executed

### Evidence claims

- The supplied synthesis identifies Bapurao (Baburao) Shedmake as a Gond leader active in the Chanda region during the 1857–58 resistance. (medium confidence)
- The same synthesis places his armed followers in a force remembered as the Jangom Dal and associates them with attacks on British positions in the forested Chanda-Gadchiroli region. (medium confidence)
- Modern Maharashtra and tribal-affairs institutions commemorate Shedmake as a regional anti-colonial figure. (medium confidence)
- The supplied synthesis records his capture in 1858 and execution at Chanda. (medium confidence)
