## Research notes

Documented association with Travancore State Congress in Kerala.

### Overview

Travancore lawyer and nationalist who became a leading woman in the Travancore State Congress and repeatedly faced imprisonment during the responsible-government struggle.

### Chronology

- **1938 onward**: Active in the Travancore State Congress responsible-government agitation
- **late 1930s**: Arrested during Civil Disobedience activity at Chengannur
- **1940s**: Continued anti-colonial and responsible-government political work until the end of British rule

### Evidence claims

- Annie Mascarene joined the Travancore State Congress agitation for responsible government. (HIGH confidence)
- She participated in Civil Disobedience activity and was arrested at Chengannur. (HIGH confidence)
- As a Travancore State Congress Working Committee member she was prosecuted for treason-related political activity and imprisoned for about one and a half years. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Thiruvananthapuram: Law practice and political base
- Chengannur: Civil Disobedience arrest
