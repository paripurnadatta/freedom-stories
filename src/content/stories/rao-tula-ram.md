## Research notes

Led resistance in Rewari and sought external support against British rule.

### Government in rebellion

Rao Tula Ram's importance is not just military: the Rewari rising temporarily created taxation, administration and military production outside Company control.

### 1857 beyond the battlefield

His later journeys show that resistance did not necessarily end with local military defeat.

### Chronology

- **1857-05-17**: Rebel government at Rewari. Tula Ram removes Company officials and establishes local authority from Rampura
- **1857-11-16**: Rewari rebellion defeated. British forces retake the region after a major confrontation
- **1858-1863**: Search for external support. After the rebellion's defeat, Tula Ram seeks assistance outside India and dies in Kabul

### Evidence claims

- Rao Tula Ram took control of Rewari tehsil on 17 May 1857 and established a local government with Rampura as headquarters. (high confidence)
- His government recognized Bahadur Shah Zafar and supplied money and material to the wider rebellion. (high confidence)
- He raised a force of roughly five thousand and established local manufacture of guns and ammunition. (high confidence)
- After defeat in November 1857 he left the region and sought external support in Iran and Afghanistan; he died in Kabul in 1863. (high confidence)
