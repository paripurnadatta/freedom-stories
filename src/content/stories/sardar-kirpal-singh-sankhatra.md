## Research notes

Official profile documents Congress offices, Individual Satyagraha and Quit India imprisonment.

### Evidence claims

- Sardar Kirpal Singh Sankhatra was born on 17 January 1917 in Sialkot district. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Indian National Congress in 1936 and served as a Punjab delegate by 1938. (HIGH confidence)
- He received imprisonment for Individual Satyagraha in 1941. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned again during Quit India. (HIGH confidence)
