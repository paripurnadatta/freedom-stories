## Research notes

Editor and publicist associated with the Malayali Memorial and political advocacy.

### Overview

Travancore journalist and political reformer who used the press and petition politics to challenge state discrimination and helped connect Travancore issues with wider Indian and overseas campaigns.

### Chronology

- **1891**: Associated with the Malayali Memorial campaign in Travancore
- **1890s-1900s**: Used journalism and political advocacy to publicize Travancore and wider Indian grievances

### Evidence claims

- G. Parameswaran Pillai, also known as G. P. Pillai, used the Madras Standard to criticize Travancore administration and advocate political reform. (HIGH confidence)
- He was one of the architects of the Malayali Memorial of 1891. (HIGH confidence)
- He carried campaigns concerning social disabilities to London and also supported Gandhi's South African political work through journalism and advice. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Travancore / Thiruvananthapuram: Political reform and journalism
- Madras: Madras Standard journalism
- London: Political advocacy
