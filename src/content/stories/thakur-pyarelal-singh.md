Documented in the cited source in connection with Early nationalist press activity.

### Overview

Lawyer, labour organizer and anti-colonial activist in Rajnandgaon and wider Chhattisgarh. His historical distinctiveness lies in organizing mill workers and linking labour rights with Non-Cooperation and nationalist education. Ministry sources credit him with leading long strikes in 1920, 1924 and 1937 and with giving durable organizational form to Chhattisgarh's labour movement.
