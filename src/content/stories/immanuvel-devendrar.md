## Research notes

Official profile states he took part in Quit India at age 18 and was imprisoned.

### Evidence claims

- Immanuvel Devendrar was born on 9 October 1924 at Sellur in Ramanathapuram district. (HIGH confidence)
- At about eighteen he participated in Quit India and was imprisoned for roughly three months. (HIGH confidence)
- The profile states that he enlisted in the British Indian Army in 1945 after his earlier nationalist imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
