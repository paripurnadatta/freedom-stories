## Research notes

Participated in Non-Cooperation and Vaikom; later pursued a distinct political path.

### Nationalism was not the only political question

Periyar's career makes caste hierarchy and social equality central rather than secondary to the story of political freedom.

### From alliance to critique

The profile should show both his participation in Congress-era struggles and his later ideological divergence without forcing him into a single nationalist category.

### Chronology

- **1919-1920s**: Congress political phase. Periyar participates in Congress politics while increasingly confronting caste discrimination within public life
- **1924-1925**: Vaikom Satyagraha. He actively participates in the Kerala struggle over access to public roads around the Vaikom temple
- **mid-1920s onward**: Self-Respect Movement. His politics develop into a distinct anti-caste and rationalist movement increasingly critical of Congress nationalism

### Evidence claims

- University histories place E.V. Ramasamy in the Congress from around 1919 before his later break with the organization. (high confidence)
- Kerala government history records Periyar as an active participant in the Vaikom Satyagraha against caste restrictions on public roads. (high confidence)
- After his break with Congress, Periyar developed the Self-Respect Movement around anti-caste, rationalist and social-equality principles. (high confidence)
- The Self-Respect programme challenged caste hierarchy and promoted gender equality, inter-caste marriage, widow remarriage and rationalist critique. (high confidence)
