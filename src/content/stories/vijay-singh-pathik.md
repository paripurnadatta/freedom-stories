## Research notes

Major organizer of peasant and princely-state resistance.

### A bridge between revolutionary and peasant politics

Pathik is especially valuable to the graph because his career crosses two political repertoires usually presented separately.

### Building a movement through organization and print

The Bijolia story was not only a confrontation with local authority; it also depended on institutions capable of sustaining protest and attracting wider attention.

### Chronology

- **1910s**: From revolutionary underground to Rajasthan. Pathik's political trajectory shifts toward regional organization and peasant mobilisation
- **1917 onward**: Bijolia peasant struggle. He helps create a more disciplined organization around resistance to taxes and feudal burdens
- **1920**: Rajasthan Seva Sangh. Pathik broadens the political infrastructure connecting peasant grievances, journalism and nationalism

### Evidence claims

- Government profiles identify Vijay Singh Pathik as born Bhoop Singh and connect his early revolutionary activity with figures including Sachindranath Sanyal and Rash Behari Bose. (high confidence)
- He reached the Bijolia movement and became a leading organizer of peasant resistance against taxes and feudal exactions. (high confidence)
- Pathik used schools, organizations and newspapers to build political capacity around the peasant movement. (high confidence)
- He helped establish the Rajasthan Seva Sangh and used publications such as Rajasthan Kesari and Naveen Rajasthan to connect regional grievances with wider nationalist politics. (high confidence)
- Government material records contact with Gandhi and wider Congress circles without making Pathik simply a subordinate Gandhian organizer. (high confidence)
