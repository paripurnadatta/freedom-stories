## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Ghusita Khulefa's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Patna resident who participated in the 1857 uprising and was condemned to death by colonial authorities in July 1857.

### Chronology

- **1857-07**: Participated in the Patna uprising and was arrested
- **1857-07-09**: Ordered to be executed by colonial authorities

### Evidence claims

- Ghusita Khulefa was a resident of Patna and son of Sheik Jan Mohamud. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in the Patna uprising of 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- Colonial authorities arrested him and ordered his execution on 9 July 1857. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Patna: 1857 uprising and colonial prosecution
