## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Ghulam Yahya's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

Bihar participant in the 1857 uprising who was captured by Company forces and executed in 1858.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Joined the Bihar uprising and fought Company forces
- **1858**: Captured and executed

### Evidence claims

- Ghulam Yahya was an inhabitant of Bihar who joined the 1857 rebels. (HIGH confidence)
- He fought East India Company forces at several places. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured and executed by hanging in 1858. (HIGH confidence)
