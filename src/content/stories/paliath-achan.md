## Research notes

Participated in anti-Company resistance with Velu Thampi.

### A state-level rebellion

The episode was led from the ministerial centres of two princely states and grew from conflicts over political interference and sovereignty.

### Alliance and collapse

Show Paliath Achan as Velu Thampi's ally while avoiding unsupported embellishment of every military episode.

### Chronology

- **1808**: Travancore-Cochin alliance against Company dominance. Paliath Achan joins Velu Thampi in armed opposition to British interference
- **1809**: Suppression and political settlement. The rebellion is defeated and British political control over Cochin is tightened

### Evidence claims

- Kerala government history identifies Paliath Achan as the Prime Minister of Cochin who raised a rebellion against the East India Company alongside Velu Thampi of Travancore. (high confidence)
- Cochin civic history records suppression of Paliath Achan's rebellion and a subsequent British political settlement in 1809. (high confidence)
- The uprising belongs to the 1808–09 phase of resistance in Travancore and Cochin against expanding British control. (high confidence)
