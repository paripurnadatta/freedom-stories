## Research notes

Major Bihar nationalist associated with Champaran.

### Historical geography

Champaran: Bihar Province (Satyagraha)

### Historical geography

Patna: Bihar Province (Bihar Vidyapith)

### Overview

Bihar lawyer and Gandhian nationalist closely associated with the Champaran Satyagraha and nationalist education. Government of Bihar lists him among the key colleagues who joined Gandhi in Champaran. He later became first Vice-Chancellor of Bihar Vidyapith in 1921, tying peasant satyagraha to the building of nationalist institutions.
