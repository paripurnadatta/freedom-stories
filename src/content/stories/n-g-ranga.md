## Research notes

Major peasant leader active in nationalist politics.

### Overview

Andhra Gandhian and peasant leader who joined mass civil disobedience and helped make agrarian politics a major current within the national movement.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Participated in Salt Satyagraha
- **1930s**: Expanded peasant political organization and agitation
- **1942**: Participated in Quit India

### Evidence claims

- N. G. Ranga became a prominent freedom fighter and champion of peasant politics. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Salt Satyagraha and was imprisoned during the freedom struggle. (HIGH confidence)
- He also participated in Quit India and played a major role in peasant mobilization. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Guntur / Nidubrolu: Peasant organizing
- Madras: Teaching and political work
