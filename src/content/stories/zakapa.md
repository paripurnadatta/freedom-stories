## Research notes

Resisted a British political officer at Khawhri in 1891, leading to an armed clash, his capture and imprisonment.

### Overview

Fanai chief remembered in Mizoram's anti-colonial history for resistance during the consolidation of British authority in the southern Lushai Hills.

### Chronology

- **1891-1892**: Associated with the southern Lushai resistance during the early consolidation of British administration
- **1890s**: Captured during colonial suppression of resistant chiefs in the South Lushai Hills

### Evidence claims

- Zakapa is officially commemorated by the Government of Mizoram among the region's freedom fighters who resisted colonial rule. (HIGH confidence)
- Academic historical work treats Zakapa as a Fanai chief whose confrontation with colonial authority formed part of the southern Lushai resistance of the early 1890s. (HIGH confidence)
- Colonial-era resistance records place Zakapa among chiefs captured during British efforts to suppress resistance in the South Lushai Hills. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- South Lushai Hills: Chiefly resistance to colonial authority
