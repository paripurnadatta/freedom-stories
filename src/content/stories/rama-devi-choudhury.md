## Research notes

Major woman Gandhian organizer in Odisha.

### Women were organizers, not an auxiliary crowd

Rama Devi's profile should show women planning meetings, recruiting volunteers and leading civil disobedience.

### A political partnership without erasure

Her marriage to Gopabandhu Choudhury is historically relevant, but her own record is substantial and independently documented.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Non-Cooperation and khadi. Rama Devi enters organized nationalist politics and Gandhian constructive work
- **1930**: Women enter Odisha's Salt Satyagraha. She mobilizes women around Inchudi and surrounding villages and participates in salt-law defiance
- **1930s-1940s**: Civil Disobedience and constructive work. Rama Devi continues through arrest, organization and institution-building beyond a single campaign

### Evidence claims

- The Ministry of Culture profile states that Rama Devi joined the Non-Cooperation movement in 1921 alongside her husband Gopabandhu Choudhury and worked in the Khadi movement. (high confidence)
- She became closely associated with Gandhi during his visits to Odisha. (high confidence)
- During the 1930 Salt Satyagraha she mobilized women, addressed volunteers and participated in breaking the salt law. (high confidence)
- Odisha government historical writing records her continued leadership during Civil Disobedience and later Gandhian constructive work. (high confidence)
