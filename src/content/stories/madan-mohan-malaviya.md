## Research notes

Major nationalist leader and founder of Banaras Hindu University.

### Education as nationalist strategy

Malaviya's importance in the freedom era cannot be reduced to protest campaigns; institution-building was central to his political vision.

### A constitutional-nationalist bridge

His profile strengthens the archive's early Congress and education networks rather than duplicating revolutionary clusters.

### Chronology

- **1900s-1910s**: Congress leadership and national education. Malaviya's political work increasingly converges with plans for a major Indian university
- **1915-1916**: Banaras Hindu University established. The BHU Act and university founding turn Malaviya's educational nationalism into a durable institution
