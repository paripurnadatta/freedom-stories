## Research notes

Documented association with Kol uprising in Jharkhand.

### Overview

Manki leader associated with the Kol uprising of 1831-33 in Chotanagpur. Government-linked reference material names Bindrai together with Singhrai Manki as leaders of the uprising. His profile should be anchored in collective defense of indigenous land and political authority, without overclaiming undocumented personal biography.

### Chronology

- **1831-1833**: Led or co-led sections of the Kol uprising in Chot.... Led or co-led sections of the Kol uprising in Chotanagpur

### Evidence claims

- Bindrai Manki is listed as a leader of the Kol Rebellion of 1831-33. (high confidence)
- The uprising was rooted in Chotanagpur's colonial land, revenue and political disruptions. (high confidence)
- Bindrai's role belongs to the Kol uprising leadership network in the Chotanagpur region. (high confidence)
