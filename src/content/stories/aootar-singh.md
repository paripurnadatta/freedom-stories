## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Aootar Singh's connection with Revolt of 1857.

### Overview

1857 rebel who was captured, sentenced to transportation for life and died in colonial custody in the Andaman Islands in 1859.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Joined the uprising and was captured
- **1857-12**: Sentenced to transportation for life
- **1859-03**: Transported to the Andaman Islands
- **1859-12**: Died in colonial custody

### Evidence claims

- Aootar Singh joined rebel forces during the 1857 uprising and fought the British. (HIGH confidence)
- He was captured and sentenced to transportation for life in December 1857. (HIGH confidence)
- He was sent to the Andaman Islands in March 1859 and died there in custody in December 1859. (HIGH confidence)

- Andaman Islands: Transportation and death in custody
