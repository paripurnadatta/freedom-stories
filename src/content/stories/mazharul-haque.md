## Research notes

Important Bihar nationalist and close associate of Gandhi.

### Historical geography

Patna / Bankipur: Bihar Province (Home Rule, Rowlatt protest, Sadaqat Ashram and political journalism)

### Historical geography

Champaran: Bihar Province (Gandhian indigo-peasant campaign)

### Overview

Lawyer, educator and nationalist leader from Bihar who combined constitutional politics, Home Rule, Gandhian mobilisation, journalism and an explicit programme of Hindu-Muslim unity. He led Bihar Home Rule activity in 1916, took part in Champaran, presided over anti-Rowlatt mobilisation in Patna, helped establish Sadaqat Ashram and used The Motherland to support Non-Cooperation and Khilafat politics. His profile is a major bridge from pre-Gandhian Bihar nationalism into mass politics.

### Historical significance

This profile was selected for Batch 008 because it repairs one or more post-175 gaps while adding a historically distinct strand to the archive. The final implementation should privilege the verified evidence claims and chronology above over generic fame-based biography.

### Chronology

- **1912**: Prominent local leader at the Bankipur Congress session context
- **1916**: Led Bihar Home Rule activity at Bankipur
- **1917**: Participated in the Champaran movement and was imprisoned according to New India Samachar
- **1919**: Presided over planning for Patna's Rowlatt Act protest programme
- **1921**: Became first Chancellor of Bihar Vidyapith and used The Motherland in support of Non-Cooperation/Khilafat politics

### Evidence claims

- Mazharul Haque was a lawyer, educator and anti-colonial leader who strongly promoted Hindu-Muslim unity. (HIGH confidence)
- He led Bihar Home Rule activity in 1916 and chaired the Bankipur meeting associated with creating a Home Rule League. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Champaran and later in Non-Cooperation/Khilafat politics. (HIGH confidence)
- He helped establish Sadaqat Ashram and used the weekly The Motherland for anti-colonial political writing, leading to prosecution. (HIGH confidence)
- In 1921 he became the first Chancellor of Bihar Vidyapith. (HIGH confidence)
