## Research notes

Sikkim freedom fighter remembered as Gandhi Pokhrel; the official profile says his documented participation in the freedom struggle began during Quit India.

### Evidence claims

- Trilochan Pokhrel participated in Gandhian nationalist activity associated with Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience and Quit India and promoted khadi. (HIGH confidence)
- Government sources present him as a pioneering/first prominent freedom fighter from Sikkim. (HIGH confidence)
