## Research notes

Official profile documents Inchudi Salt Satyagraha participation and repeated imprisonment.

### Evidence claims

- Chintamani Senapati was born on 12 March 1908 at Kaharakula in Balasore district. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Salt Satyagraha at Inchudi in 1930 and was imprisoned. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in anti-liquor and foreign-cloth boycott activity and underwent further imprisonment. (HIGH confidence)
