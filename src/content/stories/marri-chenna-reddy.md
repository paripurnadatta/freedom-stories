## Research notes

General Secretary of Andhra Mahasabha in 1942 and later a Hyderabad State Congress standing-committee member and Hyderabad City Congress general secretary.

### Evidence claims

- Marri Chenna Reddy was born on 13 January 1919 at Peddamangalaram/Padma Mangalaram in the Hyderabad region. (HIGH confidence)
- He entered organized political activity through Andhra Mahasabha in the early 1940s. (HIGH confidence)
- By 1946 he held organizational roles in the Hyderabad State Congress/city Congress. (HIGH confidence)
