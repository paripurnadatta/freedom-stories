## Research notes

HSRA revolutionary convicted in the Lahore case.

### Chronology

- **1928-09-08/1928-09-09**: Bejoy Kumar Sinha at HSRA reorganization meeting

### Evidence claims

- Bejoy Kumar Sinha participated in the 8-9 September 1928 Feroz Shah Kotla meeting that reorganized HRA into HSRA. (HIGH confidence)
- Bejoy Kumar Sinha authored the autobiographical prison work “In Andamans, the Indian Bastille,” first published in 1939. (HIGH confidence)
- Nehru’s prison-diary notes record Bejoy Kumar Sinha as one of the Lahore Conspiracy Case prisoners sentenced to transportation for life on 7 October 1930. (HIGH confidence)
