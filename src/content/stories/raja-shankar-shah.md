## Research notes

Raja Shankar Shah of Garha/Jabalpur, associated with the uprising of 1857.

### Historical geography

Jabalpur: Central Provinces / Central India (Core setting of the 1857 resistance and execution)

### Overview

Gond royal figure from the Jabalpur region executed by the British during the 1857 uprising together with his son Raghunath Shah. Government of India sources describe both as descendants of the Gond royal line who opposed British rule and were executed on 18 September 1858. The existing dataset relationship to Raghunath Shah is historically well-founded and should be preserved as a direct father-son edge.

### Historical significance

This profile was selected for Batch 008 because it repairs one or more post-175 gaps while adding a historically distinct strand to the archive. The final implementation should privilege the verified evidence claims and chronology above over generic fame-based biography.

### Chronology

- **1857-1858**: Opposed British rule during the uprising in the Jabalpur/Central India region
- **1858-09-18**: Shankar Shah and Raghunath Shah were executed by the British

### Evidence claims

- Raja Shankar Shah and Kunwar Raghunath Shah were descendants of the Gond royal line in the Jabalpur region. (HIGH confidence)
- They opposed British rule in the context of the 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
- Raghunath Shah was Shankar Shah's son. (HIGH confidence)
- Both were captured and executed by the British on 18 September 1858. (HIGH confidence)
