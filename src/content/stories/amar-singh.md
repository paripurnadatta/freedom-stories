## Research notes

Continued the Jagdishpur resistance after Kunwar Singh.

### The rebellion after Kunwar Singh

Amar Singh is valuable to the graph because he shows how resistance survived beyond the death of its best-known regional commander.

### A deliberately bounded profile

The supplied package supports the relationship and continuation role strongly, but it does not justify an over-detailed reconstruction of every campaign.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Jagdishpur enters rebellion. Amar Singh joins Kunwar Singh's armed resistance in Shahabad
- **1858**: Leadership after Kunwar Singh. After his brother's death, Amar Singh continues the Bihar resistance rather than allowing the Jagdishpur front to disappear immediately

### Evidence claims

- The supplied government profile identifies Amar Singh as the brother and close associate of Kunwar Singh in the Bihar theatre of the 1857 uprising. (high confidence)
- He fought within the Jagdishpur-Shahabad resistance and continued the struggle after Kunwar Singh's death. (high confidence)
- His role belongs to the prolonged Bihar phase of the rebellion rather than only the initial 1857 outbreak. (medium confidence)
