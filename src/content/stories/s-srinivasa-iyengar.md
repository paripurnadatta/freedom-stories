## Research notes

Prominent Madras Presidency nationalist.

### Overview

Madras lawyer and Congress leader who resigned high colonial legal office after Jallianwala Bagh, joined Non-Cooperation and became a major Swarajya and anti-Simon political strategist.

### Chronology

- **1920**: Resigned colonial offices/honour and joined Congress Non-Cooperation
- **1923-1926**: Led Swarajya Party politics in Madras
- **1926**: Presided over the Gauhati Congress session
- **late 1920s**: Organized anti-Simon Commission politics

### Evidence claims

- S. Srinivasa Iyengar resigned as Advocate-General and from the Governor's Executive Council and returned his C.I.E. in 1920 in protest after Jallianwala Bagh. (HIGH confidence)
- He joined the Indian National Congress and participated in Non-Cooperation. (HIGH confidence)
- He later became a leader of the Swarajya Party in Madras and presided over the 1926 Gauhati Congress session. (HIGH confidence)
- He organized opposition to the Simon Commission. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Madras / Chennai: Legal and Congress/Swarajya politics
- Gauhati: 1926 Congress presidency
