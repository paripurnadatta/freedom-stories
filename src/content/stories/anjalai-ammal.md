## Research notes

Official profile records participation across three major Gandhian movements.

### A Cuddalore woman in repeated mass movements

Her profile should emphasize continuity across Non-Cooperation, Salt and Quit India.

### Identity safety matters

A separate public commemorative profile describes another Anjalai Ammal who served in the INA; the two must not be merged.

### Chronology

- **1921**: Non-Cooperation entry. Anjalai Ammal begins her documented political activism
- **1932**: Vellore imprisonment. Her incarceration during pregnancy and return to prison after childbirth records the costs borne by women activists
- **1942**: Quit India participation. She takes part in the wartime mass movement

### Evidence claims

- The Cuddalore Anjalai Ammal began political activism with the Non-Cooperation Movement in 1921. (high confidence)
- She later participated in the Salt Satyagraha and Quit India Movement. (high confidence)
- In 1932 she was imprisoned at Vellore while pregnant, released temporarily for childbirth and then returned to prison. (high confidence)
- The official profile records an encounter with Gandhi despite colonial restrictions on his visiting her. (high confidence)
