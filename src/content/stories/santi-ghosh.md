## Research notes

Teenage revolutionary involved in the assassination of a British magistrate.

### Overview

Young Bengal revolutionary associated with Jugantar who worked directly with Suniti Chaudhury in the December 1931 Comilla attack on District Magistrate C.G.B. Stevens and was imprisoned afterward. As with Suniti, the profile should remain neutral and evidence-centered.
