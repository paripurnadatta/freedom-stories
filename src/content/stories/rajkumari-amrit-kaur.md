## Research notes

Gandhian organizer and member of the Congress Working Committee.

### From royal household to Gandhian politics

Her biography disrupts simple class assumptions about who entered mass nationalism.

### Women's rights inside the national movement

Amrit Kaur's gender-equality work belongs inside, not beside, her freedom-struggle politics.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Salt and Gandhian campaigning. Amrit Kaur moves from organizational work into direct civil-disobedience confrontation
- **1942**: Quit India mobilisation. She leads processions and is imprisoned during the wartime crackdown
