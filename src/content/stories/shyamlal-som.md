## Research notes

### Overview

Leader of Adivasi forest-rights satyagraha in the Sihawa-Nagri region of Dhamtari. Ministry sources place him at the head of an early 1922 forest satyagraha during Non-Cooperation and again in the 1930 Rudri/Navagaon phase of Civil Disobedience.

### Chronology

- **1922-01**: Led Sihawa-Nagri forest satyagraha during Non-Coop.... Led Sihawa-Nagri forest satyagraha during Non-Cooperation
- **1930-08-24**: Arrested during Navagaon/Rudri Jungle Satyagraha.. Arrested during Navagaon/Rudri Jungle Satyagraha

### Evidence claims

- Shyamlal Som led Adivasi forest-rights satyagraha in the Sihawa-Nagri region in 1922. (high confidence)
- The movement challenged colonial restrictions on customary access to reserved forests. (high confidence)
- He was arrested again on 24 August 1930 during the Navagaon/Rudri Jungle Satyagraha. (high confidence)
