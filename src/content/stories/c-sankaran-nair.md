### Nationalism from inside constitutional institutions

Nair's profile complicates a simple movement-versus-government divide.

### Disagreement within nationalism

His criticism of Gandhi represents a real strategic disagreement, not proof that he stood outside nationalist politics.

### Chronology

- **1897**: Congress presidency. Nair presides over the Congress at Amraoti
- **1915**: Viceroy's Executive Council. He enters one of the highest imperial governing bodies open to an Indian
- **1919**: Resignation after Jallianwala Bagh. The Punjab massacre produces a decisive break with his position inside the executive
