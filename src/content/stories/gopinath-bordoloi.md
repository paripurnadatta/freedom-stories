## Research notes

Principal Congress leader in Assam.

### Freedom struggle from Assam

Bordoloi’s profile makes provincial politics central rather than treating Assam as a peripheral extension of national Congress history.

### Congress and provincial government

The late 1930s altered the relationship between nationalist organization and administrative responsibility.

### Assam in the 1940s

His importance lies partly in the constitutional geography of a frontier province at the end of empire.

### Chronology

- **1930s**: Congress leadership in Assam. Emerges as a central provincial nationalist leader
- **late 1930s**: Provincial government. Congress politics moves from opposition into government in Assam
- **1940s**: Assam and the constitutional transition. Works to secure Assam's political position as British rule ends
