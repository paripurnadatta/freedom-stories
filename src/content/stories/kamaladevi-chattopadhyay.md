## Research notes

Active in Civil Disobedience and women's political mobilization.

### Freedom as political and social reconstruction

Kamaladevi’s freedom-struggle role connects civil disobedience to the demand that women participate as political actors.

### Women in Salt Satyagraha

Her intervention makes Salt Satyagraha a case for showing debate within nationalist leadership as well as conflict with colonial rule.

### Training volunteers

Her organizational work demonstrates the disciplined preparation behind public protest.

### Chronology

- **1920s**: Entry into nationalist politics. Joins Congress and women’s political organizing
- **1926**: Legislative contest. Becomes a pioneering woman candidate in Madras politics
- **1930**: Salt Satyagraha. Participates in civil disobedience and presses for women’s equal role
- **1930s**: Volunteer training. Helps train women for nationalist action
