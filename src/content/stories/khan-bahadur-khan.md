## Research notes

Prominent rebel leader in Rohilkhand.

### Bareilly as a rebel capital

His profile should show 1857 as a struggle over political authority, not only a succession of battles.

### Religious language and coalition politics

Archival proclamations and later commemorative accounts should be presented without oversimplifying the motives of a socially diverse rebellion.

### Chronology

- **1857-05-31**: Rebel authority declared in Bareilly. Khan Bahadur Khan establishes a local government in Rohilkhand during the uprising
- **1858**: British reconquest and flight. The rebel government collapses under military pressure and Khan Bahadur Khan moves toward Nepal

### Evidence claims

- Khan Bahadur Khan formed a rebel government in Bareilly during the 1857 uprising. (high confidence)
- The Ministry of Culture profile dates his declaration of independence at Bareilly to 31 May 1857. (high confidence)
- The same source emphasizes measures intended to preserve Hindu-Muslim cooperation under his authority. (medium confidence)
- Archival historical material preserves proclamations associated with Khan Bahadur Khan during the uprising. (high confidence)
- After British reconquest he fled toward Nepal, was captured and returned to British custody. (medium confidence)
