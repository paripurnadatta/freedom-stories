## Research notes

Documented association with Quit India in Karnataka.

### Overview

Karnataka Gandhian/revolutionary activist from Motebennur who is remembered for Dandi March participation and resistance that ended in his death in 1943.

### Chronology

- **1930**: Associated with the Dandi March/Salt Satyagraha phase
- **1942-1943**: Active in intensified anti-colonial resistance in Karnataka
- **1943**: Killed during anti-colonial action in the Haveri region

### Evidence claims

- Government of Karnataka's Haveri district history recognizes Mailara Mahadevappa of Motebennur as a freedom fighter who resisted British rule. (HIGH confidence)
- The India Post-derived commemorative-stamp account records that he accompanied Gandhi in the Dandi March. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)
- He died in 1943 during anti-colonial resistance in the Haveri region. (MEDIUM_HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Motebennur / Haveri: Home and nationalist base
- Hosaritti / Haveri: Final resistance context
