## Research notes

### Overview

Quit India martyr from Assam associated with the Gohpur police-station flag procession of 20 September 1942. Ministry sources place him alongside Kanaklata Barua and record that he was shot while attempting to carry forward the flag after she fell.

### Chronology

- **1942-09-20**: Killed during the Gohpur police-station flag proce.... Killed during the Gohpur police-station flag procession

### Evidence claims

- Mukunda Kakati participated in the Quit India procession to Gohpur police station on 20 September 1942. (high confidence)
- After Kanaklata Barua was shot, Mukunda Kakati carried forward the flag and was also killed by police firing. (high confidence)
