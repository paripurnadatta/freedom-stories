## Research notes

INA officer and close associate of Subhas Chandra Bose.

### Evidence claims

- Abid Hasan Safrani served in the Indian National Army and worked closely with Subhas Chandra Bose. (HIGH confidence)
- He accompanied Bose on the wartime submarine journey from Europe toward Asia. (HIGH confidence)
- The Orient BlackSwan biography credits Abid Hasan with the salutation “Jai Hind”; this attribution should be presented as source-attributed rather than as an uncontested sole-authorship claim. (MEDIUM confidence)
