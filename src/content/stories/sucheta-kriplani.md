## Research notes

Congress organizer who worked underground during Quit India.

### Organization beyond symbolic representation

Kriplani’s freedom-struggle role should emphasize the organizational structures through which women entered nationalist politics.

### Quit India and wartime politics

Her 1942 role belongs to the dispersed underground phase that followed mass arrests.

### From movement to constitution-making

Her later public role creates a bridge from anti-colonial struggle to the new republic without requiring a full post-1947 biography.

### Chronology

- **1940**: Women’s organization in Congress. Helps strengthen formal women’s organization within Congress politics
- **1942**: Quit India activity. Takes part in the wartime movement and underground phase
- **1946-1947**: Constituent Assembly. Participates in constitution-making at the end of colonial rule

- Born on 25 June 1908 in Ambala. (high confidence)
- Joined the freedom struggle and became closely associated with Gandhi. (high confidence)
