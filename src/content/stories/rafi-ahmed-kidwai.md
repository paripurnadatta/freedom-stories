## Research notes

Congress organizer and nationalist.

### Chronology

- **1920-1922**: Active in Khilafat and Non-Cooperation politics and imprisoned
- **1926-1929**: Chief whip for the Swaraj Party in the Central Legislative Assembly
- **1946**: Elected to the Constituent Assembly

### Evidence claims

- Rafi Ahmed Kidwai was born on 18 February 1894 and died in 1954. (HIGH confidence)
- Kidwai participated in the Non-Cooperation Movement and was imprisoned for his role. (HIGH confidence)
- He was active in the Khilafat movement in the early 1920s. (HIGH confidence)
- Kidwai served as chief whip for the Swaraj Party in the Central Legislative Assembly from 1926 to 1929. (HIGH confidence)
- He was elected to the Constituent Assembly from the United Provinces and served on the Union Constitution and Rules of Procedure Committees. (HIGH confidence)
