## Research notes

Documented association with Quit India in Karnataka.

### Overview

Bengaluru freedom fighter who joined Quit India in 1942, was imprisoned for almost a year and remained active in anti-colonial work after release.

### Chronology

- **1942**: Joined Quit India-era anti-colonial activity in Bengaluru
- **1942-12-18**: Imprisoned in Bengaluru Central Jail
- **1943-12-08**: Released from Bengaluru Central Jail and returned to independence activity

### Evidence claims

- H. S. Doreswamy joined the freedom struggle in 1942 during the Quit India movement. (HIGH confidence)
- A Bengaluru Central Jail document reported by The Indian Express records his imprisonment from 18 December 1942 to 8 December 1943. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry of Home Affairs later listed Doreswamy as a Karnataka freedom fighter under the Swatantrata Sainik Samman framework. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Bengaluru: Quit India organizing and imprisonment
