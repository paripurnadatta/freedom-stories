## Research notes

Source-specific record documenting Kat Daloi's connection with Jaintia anti-British resistance.

### What a limited record should look like

Kat Daloi is deliberately included in the pilot because the surviving accessible biographical record is short. The product should not compensate by inventing a childhood, personality or precise chronology. A strong limited-record profile can still be useful if it is transparent about what is known.

### The Jaintia political world

The title Daloi refers to a local office connected to an Elaka. The broader resistance involved local authorities and communities reacting to taxation and British interference in political and religious life.

### Armed resistance, 1860–62

The ICHR entry identifies Kat Daloi as a leading participant in the armed resistance and records his death in the battlefield in 1862.

### Absence is also historical information

Birth date, family history and many biographical details are not established by the supplied evidence. The interface should visibly mark these gaps instead of presenting a thin record as if it were complete.

### Chronology

- **1860**: Resistance to colonial taxation. The Jaintia resistance intensifies after the imposition of new taxation and administrative interference
- **1861-1862**: Armed resistance. Kat Daloi is recorded as one of the leading participants in the Jaintia struggle
- **1862**: Death in battle. Dies while fighting British rule in the Jaintia Hills

### Evidence claims

- Hailed from Nartiang in the Jaintia Hills and held the office of Daloi, head of an Elaka. (high confidence)
- Played a leading role in armed resistance to British taxation and political-religious interference in the Jaintia Hills. (high confidence)
- Died in battle in 1862. (high confidence)
- The broader Jaintia resistance involved Dalois, Pators and local leaders opposing colonial intervention and repression. (high confidence)
