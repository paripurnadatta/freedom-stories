## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Jhansi resistance in 1857.

### Overview

Ruler of Banpur in Bundelkhand and ally of the Rani of Jhansi during the 1857 uprising. Ministry records describe his seizure of local transport/strategic points, declaration of independence, military resistance and eventual detention away from Bundelkhand.

### Chronology

- **1857-06**: Mobilized forces and declared independence in the .... Mobilized forces and declared independence in the Bundelkhand uprising
- **1858**: Captured/imprisoned after defeat of the rebellion.. Captured/imprisoned after defeat of the rebellion

### Evidence claims

- Raja Mardan Singh of Banpur became an ally of the Rani of Jhansi during the 1857 uprising. (high confidence)
- He mobilized forces around Lalitpur/Chanderi and declared his state independent in June 1857. (high confidence)
- After the defeat of the uprising he was imprisoned and barred from returning to Bundelkhand. (high confidence)
