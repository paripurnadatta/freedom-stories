## Research notes

Advisor to Nana Sahib and associated with rebel diplomacy.

### Overview

Political aide, emissary and writer associated with Nana Sahib whose pre-1857 diplomatic work and 1857 nationalist activity connected Kanpur resistance with a wider political vision.

### Chronology

- **early 1850s**: Served Nana Sahib as an aide/emissary and travelled to Britain over the pension claim
- **1857**: Active in the political leadership/network around Nana Sahib during the uprising
- **1857**: Associated with nationalist writing/publication activity around Payam-e-Azadi

### Evidence claims

- Azimullah Khan was closely associated with Nana Sahib and was sent to Britain in connection with Nana Sahib's pension claim. (HIGH confidence)
- He played a significant political role during the 1857 uprising in the Kanpur/Nana Sahib network. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry profile associates him with nationalist poetry and the publication 'Payam-e-Azadi'. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Kanpur / Cawnpore: Nana Sahib political and 1857 resistance network
- London: Pre-1857 pension/diplomatic mission
