## Research notes

Led resistance around Bulandshahr/Malagarh.

### Overview

Nawab of Malagarh and a major civil-rebellion leader in Bulandshahr whose position linked landed, rural and military resistance during 1857.

### Chronology

- **1857**: Led anti-Company resistance from the Malagarh/Bulandshahr region
- **1857-10**: British forces attacked and suppressed the Malagarh stronghold

### Evidence claims

- Walidad Khan was the Nawab associated with Malagarh in Bulandshahr and became an important leader of the 1857 rebellion there. (HIGH confidence)
- Scholarly analysis treats his leadership as a key link between local civil/rural rebellion and the broader 1857 uprising. (HIGH confidence)
- Malagarh became an important center of resistance before British forces suppressed the stronghold. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Malagarh / Bulandshahr: Regional 1857 leadership and civil rebellion
