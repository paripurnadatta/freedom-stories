## Research notes

Revolutionary convicted in the Alipore case.

### Chronology

- **1908-1909**: Alipore Bomb Case prosecution period
- **1909-11-23**: Calcutta High Court appellate judgment imposed transportation for life

### Evidence claims

- Ullaskar Dutta was associated with the manufacture of high explosives. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested in connection with the Alipur/Alipore Bomb Case. (HIGH confidence)
- A sessions court sentenced him to death; on appeal the sentence was commuted to transportation for life. (HIGH confidence)
- He was deported to the Andaman penal settlement. (HIGH confidence)
- On appeal in November 1909, the Calcutta High Court sentenced Ullaskar Dutt to transportation for life after the trial court had sentenced him to death. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Andaman penal settlement: transportation/imprisonment
