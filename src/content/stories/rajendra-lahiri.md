## Research notes

Revolutionary executed in the Kakori case.

### A bridge between Bengal and the United Provinces

Lahiri's biography links revolutionary currents in Bengal with the HRA network centred in north India.

### Kakori as organization, not spectacle

The profile should explain the action as a fundraising and political operation by a revolutionary organization rather than romanticize violence.

### Chronology

- **1920s**: HRA organizer in Banaras. Lahiri becomes part of the Hindustan Republican Association's revolutionary network
- **1925-08**: Kakori action. He takes part in the train action intended to seize government funds for revolutionary work
- **1925-11**: Dakshineswar arrest. Police arrest Lahiri in Bengal while he is associated with bomb-making activity
- **1927-12-17**: Execution at Gonda. Lahiri is hanged following conviction in the Kakori case

### Evidence claims

- Government reference material identifies Rajendra Nath Lahiri as a member and organizer of the Hindustan Republican Association in Banaras. (high confidence)
- He participated in the Kakori train action of 1925 and was later tried in the Kakori conspiracy case. (high confidence)
- He was also arrested in connection with revolutionary activity at Dakshineswar. (high confidence)
- Lahiri was executed at Gonda jail on 17 December 1927. (high confidence)
