## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Allahabad uprising of 1857.

### Overview

Religious teacher and local political leader who emerged as the principal rebel authority in Allahabad in June 1857 and operated from Khusro Bagh.

### Chronology

- **1857-06**: Assumed rebel leadership in Allahabad and operated from Khusro Bagh
- **1857-06-07 to 1857-06-17**: Organized a short-lived rebel administration in Allahabad

### Evidence claims

- Maulvi Liaquat Ali emerged as a principal leader of the 1857 uprising in Allahabad. (HIGH confidence)
- He made Khusro Bagh his operational headquarters and established a short-lived rebel administration. (HIGH confidence)
- The Ministry account describes support for his leadership among multiple local religious and social communities. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Allahabad / Prayagraj: 1857 rebel leadership
- Khusro Bagh: Rebel headquarters
