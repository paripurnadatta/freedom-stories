## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Sannyasi–Fakir resistance in Bengal.

### Overview

Raja of Huseypur in the old Saran region who became one of the most persistent early opponents of East India Company authority in Bihar. His resistance predates 1781 but intensified during the wider crisis triggered by Chait Singh's revolt. The profile should emphasize refractory zamindari politics, Company revenue expansion and long-running armed resistance rather than projecting later nationalist organization backward into the eighteenth century.

### Evidence claims

- Fateh Bahadur Shahi was the zamindar/ruler of Huseypur in the Saran region and resisted East India Company authority in the late eighteenth century. (high confidence)
- His resistance was already active before 1781 and became entangled with the wider Bihar repercussions of Chait Singh's revolt. (high confidence)
- The conflict must be understood in the context of Company revenue consolidation and the resistance of Bihar zamindars. (high confidence)
