## Research notes

Led Travancore's resistance against the Company.

### From minister to rebel

Velu Thampi’s story is a transformation from state office to anti-Company resistance.

### The crisis of subsidiary power

The conflict emerged from the changing balance between Travancore sovereignty and expanding Company authority.

### Kundara, 1809

The proclamation gave political language to the armed confrontation.

### Chronology

- **1801-1809**: Dalawa of Travancore. Operates at the centre of Travancore government before relations with the Company collapse
- **1805**: Treaty and mounting tensions. A new political arrangement deepens Company influence and fiscal strain
- **1809**: Kundara proclamation and revolt. Calls for resistance as conflict with the Company becomes open war

### Evidence claims

- Served as Dalawa, or chief minister, of Travancore in the early nineteenth century. (high confidence)
- Relations with the East India Company deteriorated after the 1805 treaty framework and mounting political and financial tensions. (high confidence)
- In 1809 issued the Kundara proclamation and led resistance against Company power. (high confidence)
- The supplied record connects Velu Thampi Dalawa with Travancore as associated with. (high confidence)
