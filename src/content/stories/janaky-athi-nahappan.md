## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Rani of Jhansi Regiment training and mobilisation.

### Overview

Malayan-born Indian nationalist who served in the Rani of Jhansi Regiment of the INA and was officially remembered by India as a major figure in the independence struggle.

### Chronology

- **1940s**: Joined the Rani of Jhansi Regiment of the Indian National Army in Southeast Asia
- **1945**: Served in the late-war INA/Rani regiment phase before the end of the Second World War

### Evidence claims

- Janaky Athi Nahappan is officially remembered by the Government of India as a freedom fighter connected to India's independence struggle. (HIGH confidence)
- Prime Ministerial remarks recorded by PIB identify her as serving as deputy to Captain Lakshmi Sehgal in the Rani of Jhansi Regiment. (HIGH confidence)
