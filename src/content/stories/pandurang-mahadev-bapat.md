## Research notes

Revolutionary who later became associated with Gandhian-era struggles.

### Chronology

- **1908**: Returned to India and connected with revolutionary circles including the Maniktola group
- **1921**: Led the Mulshi Satyagraha
- **1977-11-28**: Government of India commemorative stamp issued

### Evidence claims

- Pandurang Mahadev Bapat was born on 12 November 1880 and died on 28 November 1967. (HIGH confidence)
- While studying in Britain, Bapat became involved in Indian revolutionary politics; after returning to India in 1908 he connected with the Maniktola revolutionary group in Bengal. (HIGH confidence)
- Bapat led the Mulshi Satyagraha, from which he acquired the honorific 'Senapati'. (HIGH confidence)
- He spent substantial periods in prison for activities connected to the freedom struggle. (MEDIUM confidence)
- The Government of India issued a commemorative stamp for Senapati Bapat on 28 November 1977. (HIGH confidence)

### Places

- Parner: birthplace
- Mulshi: site of agrarian satyagraha
