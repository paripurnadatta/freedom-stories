## Research notes

Prominent Manipuri commander who fought British forces in 1891.

### Overview

Major-General of Manipur and the best-known battlefield leader of the 1891 Khongjom confrontation against British forces. Ministry sources give a detailed biography, describe his command of Manipuri soldiers sent to resist the Tamu column and record his death in battle on 23 April 1891.

### Chronology

- **1891-04-23**: Led Manipuri troops at Khongjom and died in battle.... Led Manipuri troops at Khongjom and died in battle
