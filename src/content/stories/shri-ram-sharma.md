## Research notes

Led Non-Cooperation mobilization in Rohtak, promoted Salt Satyagraha and was imprisoned in Individual Satyagraha and Quit India.

### Evidence claims

- Shri Ram Sharma was born on 1 October 1899 at Jhajjar. (HIGH confidence)
- He participated in Non-Cooperation in Rohtak and was sentenced to imprisonment in 1922. (HIGH confidence)
- He was arrested during Individual Satyagraha in January 1941. (HIGH confidence)
- He was imprisoned for an extended period during Quit India. (HIGH confidence)
