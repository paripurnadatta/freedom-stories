## Research notes

### Overview

Adi village leader from Yagrung in present-day Arunachal Pradesh associated with armed opposition to British intrusion in the Abor/Adi hills. The Ministry of Culture account connects his conflict with Assistant Political Officer Noel Williamson to a broader determination to resist British penetration; later repression and burning of villages formed part of the punitive response.

### Evidence claims

- Matmur Jamoh was the Gam/village leader of Yagrung in the Adi hills. (high confidence)
- He became involved in a conspiracy against British intrusion after conflict with political officer Noel Williamson. (high confidence)
- Government commemorative material recognizes him as an Adi tribal freedom fighter who resisted British arrogance and later surrendered amid punitive village burning. (high confidence)
