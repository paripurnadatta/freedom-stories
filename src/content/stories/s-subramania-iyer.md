## Research notes

Documented in the cited source in connection with Madras Mahajana Sabha public politics.

### Historical geography

Madras Presidency: Southern British India (Legal and political base)

### Overview

Madras lawyer and jurist who became an important constitutional nationalist. Ministry of Culture describes him as a founding member of the Indian National Congress, later an advocate of Swadeshi and co-founder with Annie Besant of the Home Rule League in 1916. His 1917 appeal to U.S. President Woodrow Wilson and renunciation of his knighthood after official rebuke make him a strong bridge between early constitutional politics and the more assertive Home Rule phase.
