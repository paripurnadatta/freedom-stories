## Research notes

Brother of Kattabomman; continued resistance.

### Resistance after Kattabomman

Oomaithurai's importance lies in demonstrating that Panchalankurichi resistance continued after the execution of its best-known leader.

### A regional coalition

The 1801 phase links Panchalankurichi to the broader South Indian rebellion rather than leaving it as an isolated fort story.

### Chronology

- **1801-02-02**: Escape from Palayamkottai. Insurgents attack the prison and free Oomaithurai, allowing the Panchalankurichi resistance to revive
- **1801-02 to 1801-05**: Panchalankurichi rebuilt and besieged. Oomaithurai and supporters reorganize around the fort until Company forces capture it in May
- **1801**: Resistance shifts toward Sivaganga. Oomaithurai joins the wider southern anti-Company struggle associated with the Marudhu leadership

### Evidence claims

- Government and university histories identify Oomaithurai as a brother of Veerapandiya Kattabomman and a participant in the Palayakkarar wars. (high confidence)
- He was held at Palayamkottai and was liberated in an insurgent prison attack on 2 February 1801. (high confidence)
- After the escape, the rebels re-established resistance around Panchalankurichi and reconstructed its fortifications. (high confidence)
- After the fall of Panchalankurichi, Oomaithurai moved toward the Sivaganga resistance associated with the Marudhu leadership. (high confidence)
