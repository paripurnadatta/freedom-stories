## Research notes

Remembered in Santhal resistance traditions alongside Phulo Murmu.

### A distinct woman in a family remembered collectively

The profile should preserve Jhano as her own canonical person while showing the family and movement context.

### Do not turn commemoration into false precision

Official remembrance strongly supports participation and leadership memory; it is weaker evidence for exact combat anecdotes.

### Chronology

- **1855**: Santhal Hul. Jhano Murmu participates in the rebellion against exploitation in the Santhal region and is remembered with the Murmu leadership group

### Evidence claims

- The President of India has officially remembered Phoolo-Jhano alongside Sido-Kanhu and Chand-Bhairav as leaders of the Santal Hul of 1855. (high confidence)
- The Ministry of Culture profile treats Jhano and Phulo as sisters participating in the uprising and women's resistance. (medium confidence)
