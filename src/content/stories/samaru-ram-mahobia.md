### Overview

### Evidence claims

- Samaru Ram Mahobia was born in 1909 in Chhuikhadan and became a prominent political organizer in the princely state. (high confidence)
- He helped initiate farmers' agitation and opposed forced labour from the mid-1930s. (high confidence)
- He participated in the 1939 Jungle Satyagraha, was imprisoned, and later joined Quit India underground propaganda activity. (high confidence)
- In 1946-47 he participated in the movement for responsible government/merger in Chhuikhadan. (high confidence)
