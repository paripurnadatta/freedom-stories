## Research notes

Prominent nationalist and Congress president.

### Education outside colonial control

Jamia's founding made education itself a political arena in the Non-Cooperation period.

### Medicine, nationalism and institution-building

Ajmal Khan's career demonstrates that freedom politics could operate through professional authority and durable institutions as well as protest.

### Chronology

- **1920-10 to 1920-11**: Jamia founded and Ajmal Khan becomes Chancellor. A nationalist university is established at Aligarh; Ajmal Khan is elected its first Chancellor
- **1921**: Congress presidency. Ajmal Khan serves as president of the Indian National Congress amid the Non-Cooperation/Khilafat phase
- **1920-1927**: Sustaining Jamia. He continues to mobilize resources and political support for the institution until his death

### Evidence claims

- Jamia Millia Islamia identifies Hakim Ajmal Khan as a founder, its major early inspiration and its first Chancellor from 1920 until his death in 1927. (high confidence)
- Jamia's official history describes him as a nationalist leader, freedom fighter and Indian National Congress president in 1921. (high confidence)
- He was closely associated with Gandhi and with the Khilafat/Non-Cooperation political environment from which Jamia emerged. (high confidence)
- He used personal resources and fundraising efforts to sustain Jamia during its early crises. (high confidence)
