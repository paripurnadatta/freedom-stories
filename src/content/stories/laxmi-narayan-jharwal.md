## Research notes

Documented association with Praja Mandal / anti-begar in Rajasthan.

### Overview

Jaipur Praja Mandal activist who combined Gandhian politics with campaigns against begar, lag-bag levies and discriminatory colonial/princely legislation. His profile adds an important social-rights dimension to the Jaipur people's movement.

### Chronology

- **1938**: Joined Praja Mandal movement activity.. Joined Praja Mandal movement activity
- **1945**: Imprisoned in Ajmer Central Jail.. Imprisoned in Ajmer Central Jail

### Evidence claims

- Laxmi Narayan Jharwal joined Praja Mandal activity in 1938 under the influence of Jaipur movement leaders. (high confidence)
- He protested begar, lag-bag levies and legislation affecting the Meena community. (high confidence)
- He was imprisoned in Ajmer Central Jail in 1945 and remained committed to Gandhian nonviolence and social uplift. (high confidence)
