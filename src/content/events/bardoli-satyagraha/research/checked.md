# Source check — 25 October 2026

The supplied dossier contains four events, including Dandi. It was treated as a starting point, not independent proof. Its preserved copy is at src/content/events/champaran-satyagraha/research/source-dossier.pdf.

## Checks and qualifications

- Desai, chapters V–VI, pp. 27–40: consultations on 4 February, letter of 6 February, renewed meeting on 12 February 1928. Representatives expressed doubts; assent was not assumed.

- Desai, chapter XXX, pp. 170–175: Poona negotiations, disputed wording, restoration of land, release of prisoners, reinstatement applications and payment of old assessment. Do not describe cancellation of every tax.

- Desai, chapter VI, pp. 36–40: campaign centres, village messengers, Jugatram Dave’s bulletin, initial cyclostyling followed by printing in Surat.

- Desai, “Not a Miracle”, pp. 110–111: restrictions on social boycott, the Manekpur Parsi householder and Patel’s intervention. Reports about denial of medical assistance were not established in that case.

- Desai, part II, pp. 185–188: Broomfield and Maxwell’s inquiry and the Afwa discrepancy between gross and scrutinised leased areas. The head clerk recalculated. This is not proof that every official fabricated records.

- Desai, chapter V: representatives of Chorasi wanted to join; Patel declined because campaign resources were limited. The neighbouring taluka later benefited from the inquiry.

## 27 October 2026: Claude rewrite (all six stories written fresh)

Sources opened directly this session: Mahadev Desai, *The Story of Bardoli* (1929), full text from archive.org
(chapters 2, 5, 6, 20, 29, 30, the Governor's 23 July speech in the appendix, and Part II, The Enquiry);
Wikipedia "Bardoli Satyagraha" and "Vallabhbhai Patel". The fact sheet was used as a map.

Fact sheet checks: all quotes, dates and figures held up against Desai's text. Two of its cautions were too strict:
- It says not to give circulation beyond 5,000. Desai himself says more than 10,000 copies went out in Bardoli
  and 4,000 more were subscribed outside. Used, with Desai named.
- It says to leave the "fifth man" unnamed and not to invent; followed.
Added from Desai and not in the sheet: the Gandhi-Kalyanji conversation before the campaign; the Governor's
conditions of 23 July (revenue paid and agitation stopped before any inquiry; fourteen days to answer); Swami Anand
as Patel's secretary (he is the "Swami" in the Poona exchange).

Disagreements shown in "Two versions" notes
- Settlement terms: the year's revenue cancelled (Wikipedia) vs the old assessment paid, no arrears within a month
  (Desai, who was present). Desai followed in the main text.

Other differences noticed, not used
- Wikipedia says the settlement was "brokered by a Parsi member" of the Bombay government. Desai names Sir Chunilal
  Mehta and gives no religion. Left out.
- Wikipedia: the struggle "would not end until all taxes had been cancelled for the year". Not in Desai. Left out.
- Wikipedia: wealthy Bombay sympathisers bought back lands (marked "citation needed"). Desai says Collector Garrett
  persuaded buyers to return them. Followed Desai.
- Wikipedia's 6.03 per cent final figure is used once, in a labelled note in "The Figures at Afwa"; Desai's book was
  written before the final orders and does not give it.

Paintings: the six existing paintings fit the rewritten stories (same slugs).
