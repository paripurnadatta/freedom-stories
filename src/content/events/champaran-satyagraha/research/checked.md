# Source check — 25 October 2026

The supplied dossier contains four events, including Dandi. It was treated as a starting point, not independent proof. Its preserved copy is at src/content/events/champaran-satyagraha/research/source-dossier.pdf.

## Checks and qualifications

- Gandhi describes a police notice during his attempted village visit and a summons at Motihari; chapters 138–139. His account reports withdrawal of the case, not a judicial finding that all planters were guilty.

- Chapter 140 describes cross-questioning farmers and rejecting weak statements. Chapter 141 names volunteer helpers. Chapter 143 describes the later committee.

- Chapter 140 describes dismissing personal cooks and establishing a common vegetarian kitchen. Chapter 141 supplies the team context.

- Chapter 141 describes six village schools, local provision of buildings and food, and volunteer teachers. Chapter 142 says sustained staffing proved difficult.

- Chapter 142 recounts the unnamed woman at Bhitiharva who told Kasturba she owned only one sari. This is Gandhi’s recollection, not a recorded interview transcript.

- Chapter 143 gives Gandhi’s conditions for joining the committee and credits Lieutenant-Governor Edward Gait. Tinkathia ended through subsequent legislation, not merely the court appearance.
