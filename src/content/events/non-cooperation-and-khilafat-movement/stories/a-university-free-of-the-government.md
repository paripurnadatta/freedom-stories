One of the non-cooperation movement's tasks was to leave government schools and colleges. That left a question. If students walked out, where would they go?

One answer was a new university. It was founded on 29 October 1920 at Aligarh, in the United Provinces. It was set up by nationalist leaders and by students of Aligarh Muslim University. Its name was Jamia Millia Islamia.

## Why it began

Students and teachers at Aligarh Muslim University wanted a national Muslim university "free from government influence". They believed that the administration of their own university was pro-British.

The founders were national leaders of that time. Muhammad Ali Jauhar, Hakim Ajmal Khan, Mukhtar Ahmed Ansari and Abdul Majeed Khwaja are named among the founding members. Mahmud Hasan laid the foundation stone. A student of his, Shabbir Ahmad Usmani, read his speech aloud.

Jauhar and Ajmal Khan were both leaders of the Khilafat movement. Jauhar was the first vice-chancellor, from 1920 to 1923. Ajmal Khan was the first chancellor, from 1920 to 1927.

The university was meant to give modern education to students of every community, especially Muslims. It also meant to promote Indian nationalism.

<aside class="note"><b>Who founded it?</b> Some lists name Gandhi and Maulana Azad as founders. The history on the same page names only Jauhar, Ajmal Khan and Ansari, and describes Gandhi as a supporter.</aside>

## Gandhi's help

Gandhi welcomed the new university. He sent his youngest son, Devdas, to teach Hindi there.

Another name often linked to it is Zakir Husain. He is counted as one of its founders and also as one of its later makers. Under his leadership, a teachers' training institute was founded in 1938.

## Moving to Delhi

In 1925 Jamia left Aligarh and moved to Karol Bagh in Delhi. On 1 March 1935 a foundation stone was laid for a school building at Okhla, which was then a village on the southern edge of Delhi. In 1936 most of its institutions moved there.

<aside class="note"><b>Two dates</b>: One part of the page says Jamia moved to Okhla in 1935. Another says the stone was laid in 1935 and the move took place in 1936.</aside>

## A university by law

The University Grants Commission gave Jamia the status of a deemed university in 1962. On 26 December 1988 Parliament passed an act that made it a central university.

## Why it matters

Jamia is one of the places where the non-cooperation movement left something that lasted. Its first vice-chancellor was a leader of the Khilafat movement. Gandhi sent his son to teach there. Both ideas, a school free of the government and a school open to every community, are written into its beginning.
