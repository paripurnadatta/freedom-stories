ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Non-Cooperation and Khilafat
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

Only stories that need a painting are listed. The others use real photographs.
Do not show clear faces of real people in any of these. Show them from behind or at a distance.

1. One year to swaraj  (flagship story)
==============================================================================
FILE NAME: one-year-to-swaraj
Save it as one-year-to-swaraj.png (or .jpg / .webp) in this folder.
Shape: extra wide, 16:6. Generate at 2400 x 900 pixels if your tool lets you choose.
Where it goes: the top of the story "One year to swaraj", and its card everywhere on the site

PROMPT (copy everything between the lines):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A bonfire of imported cloth on an open ground in a 1920s Indian town, men and women in white khadi seen from behind watching the flames, spinning wheels stacked nearby, evening light. Wide composition, 16:9.

Painterly storybook illustration for a children's history book about India's freedom struggle. Rich, warm gouache and oil-paint texture with visible brushwork, cinematic lighting, warm saffron and amber light against deep indigo shadows, touches of leaf green. Historically accurate 1900s–1940s Indian clothing, architecture and objects. Faces calm and dignified. No text, no letters, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no borders. No gore, no blood, no weapons pointed at anyone. Do not include the Indian national flag or the Ashoka Chakra.
