## Research notes

Village-level anti-feudal agitation led through Andhra Mahasabha-linked sanghams expanded across Telangana from 1944. Landlord and state repression escalated, and the killing of Doddi Komuraiah at Kadavendi on 4 July 1946 became a major catalyst for armed resistance.
