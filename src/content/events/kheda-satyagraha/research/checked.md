# Source check — 25 October 2026

The supplied dossier contains four events, including Dandi. It was treated as a starting point, not independent proof. Its preserved copy is at src/content/events/champaran-satyagraha/research/source-dossier.pdf.

## Checks and qualifications

- Chapter 147 gives the solidarity pledge and the quarter-crop threshold. The 26 February 1918 letter to the collector offers an independent inquiry. A quarter of normal yield is not a 25 percent reduction.

- Chapter 149 describes a written undertaking followed by the collector’s confirmation. Gandhi explicitly considered the settlement incomplete; it was not blanket cancellation of all taxes.

- Chapter 148 names Mohanlal Pandya and seven or eight companions, the attached onion crop, convictions and brief imprisonment. The description of attachment as wrongful is Gandhi’s judgment.

- Chapter 147 places headquarters at Nadiad’s Anathashram and describes Patel leaving his legal practice. Chapter 149 evaluates his campaign role retrospectively.

- The Dantali speech of 17 April 1918 criticises treatment of bullocks on Gandhi’s journey from Petlad. The story treats it as his reported rebuke, without inventing the driver’s response.

- Chapter 147 describes local organisers, enquiries and failed requests before resistance. The February letter supplies contemporary evidence of the proposed independent assessment.
