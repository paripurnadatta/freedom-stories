ILLUSTRATIONS NEEDED: Kakori train action
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
How to use this file
  1. Copy one PROMPT into your image generator.
  2. Save the result with its FILE NAME, in this same folder.
  3. Send it to Claude (a zip is fine), or run: node scripts/place-illustrations.mjs
     It shrinks the image for the web, and the site shows it automatically.

No faces of the revolutionaries are needed for this one. The paper must show only
scribbly, unreadable handwriting: no real letters or words.

On a wooden desk lies a big old cloth-bound register, open, its yellowed pages filled with faint, flowing, unreadable ink handwriting. An inkpot and a dip pen beside it. A police clerk in a 1920s khaki uniform and turban sits writing, seen from behind and the side so his face is not shown. Through the open doorway, far away, a steam train passes under a dusky sky. Quiet, serious, mysterious mood. Wide composition, 16:9.
