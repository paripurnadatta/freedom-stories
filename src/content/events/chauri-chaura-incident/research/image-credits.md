# Archival pictures

Two pictures, both from Wikimedia Commons, both with a free licence. Downloaded 27 October 2026.

## 1. The Shaheed Smarak at Chauri Chaura

The memorial built near the police station site. This is the modern memorial, not the 1922 police
station and not the British police memorial.

- Commons record: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chauri_chaura_new_photo.jpg
- Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d6/Chauri_chaura_new_photo.jpg
- Credit: Nitishkumarojha, Wikimedia Commons.
- Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0 (the uploader also released it under GFDL).
- Local derivative: `archive/chauri-chaura-shaheed-smarak.webp`; resized to 1200 px wide without
  enlargement and converted to WebP.
- The file carries a Hindi sign on the memorial itself. Do not crop it into a caption; the picture is
  evidence of the memorial, not of the event.

## 2. Madan Mohan Malaviya

Portrait of the lawyer who argued the Chauri Chaura appeal in the Allahabad High Court in 1923.

- Commons record: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mahamana_Madan_Mohan_Malaviya_Portrait.jpg
- Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/aa/Mahamana_Madan_Mohan_Malaviya_Portrait.jpg
- Credit: Banaras Hindu University, via Wikimedia Commons.
- Licence: public domain, per the Commons record checked 27 October 2026.
- Local derivative: `archive/madan-mohan-malaviya.webp`; converted to WebP without enlargement.

## Pictures looked at and rejected

- `File:Chauri Chaura Land Mark.jpg` (railway station sign, public domain). Readable trilingual text
  on a station board, taken with a phone camera in 2010; poor quality and the text would have to be
  cropped out. Rejected.
- `File:MK Gandhi in 1922.jpg` (public domain). An artist's rendering with a printed caption, not a
  clean photograph of Gandhi in 1922. Rejected; the subject already has better Gandhi portraits.
- `File:Rauniyar-bhawan-chauri-chaura.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0). Only 300 px wide. Too small.
- `File:Chauri Chaura Express at Kanpur Anwarganj.jpg`. A modern train named after the event; it
  says nothing about 1922. Rejected.

## Note for whoever places these

There is no free-licence photograph of the burned police station, of the 1922 crowd, or of any of the
19 hanged men. The paintings have to carry those scenes. These two pictures belong inside the
memorial story and the trial story, not at the top of the subject.
